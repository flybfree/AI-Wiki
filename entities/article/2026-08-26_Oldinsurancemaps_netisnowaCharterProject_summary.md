## Summary  
OldInsuranceMaps.net (OIM) has been officially designated a Charter Project by the OpenStreetMap US organization, providing it with a stable legal and fiscal framework. The project brings together georeferenced Sanborn Insurance Maps from 1867 to the 1960s, making them more accessible for historical mappers and researchers. This move underscores OSM US’s commitment to supporting long‑term community initiatives through charter structures.

## Key Takeaways  
- OldInsuranceMaps.net is now a Charter Project under OSM US, ensuring enduring legal and financial support.  
- The project offers high‑resolution, georeferenced Sanborn maps that capture detailed U.S. geographic data from the mid‑19th century to the 1960s.  
- Charter projects enable developers and organizers to focus on building tools while OSM US handles the sustainable infrastructure.
