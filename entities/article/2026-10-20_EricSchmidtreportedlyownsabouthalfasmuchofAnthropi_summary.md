## Summary
Eric Schmidt, the former CEO of Google, reportedly holds a stake in Anthropic that is approximately half the size of the ownership held by the company’s CEO, Dario Amodei. According to Bloomberg, Schmidt owns about 1% of the AI startup following his investment in its Series A round in 2021, a position that could significantly increase his net worth if Anthropic proceeds with its anticipated initial public offering (IPO) at a valuation of up to $2 trillion.

## Key Takeaways
- **Significant Early Investment:** Eric Schmidt invested tens of millions of dollars in Anthropic’s Series A round in 2021, when the company was valued at $461 million. This early entry has resulted in him owning approximately 1% of the company.
- **Comparative Ownership:** Schmidt’s 1% stake is notably large compared to the 2% held by co-founder and CEO Dario Amodei, highlighting the substantial influence early external investors can have in high-growth AI startups.
- **Potential Financial Upside:** With Anthropic expected to go public by the end of 2026 and top-end market estimates valuing the company at $2 trillion, Schmidt’s stake could add up to $20 billion to his current net worth of $57.5 billion.
- **Active AI Portfolio:** Schmidt continues to deploy capital through various entities like Tomorrow Ventures and Hillspire, with other AI investments including Mistral, General Intuition, and Reflection AI, demonstrating his ongoing commitment to the sector.
