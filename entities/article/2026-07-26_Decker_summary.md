## Summary  
Decker is a multimedia platform that lets users create and share interactive documents enriched with sound, images, hypertext, and scripted behavior. It revives the HyperCard aesthetic while adding modern conveniences such as deep undo history, touch‑screen support, and a powerful scripting language called Lil.

## Key Takeaways  
- Decker blends classic MacOS visual design with contemporary usability features like scroll wheels, bulk editing, and robust undo stacks, making it both nostalgic and functional.  
- Its built‑in Lil language fuses Lua’s imperative style with Q’s functional syntax, offering implicit scalar‑vector arithmetic and an SQL‑like query engine for rapid prototyping without steep learning curves.  
- The platform is deliberately privacy‑first: no ads, telemetry, or gamification, and it stores decks as plain text that works seamlessly with Git and other version‑control systems.
