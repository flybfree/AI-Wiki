## Summary
Nscale and Anthropic are reportedly negotiating a second $45 billion compute deal, following a similar agreement announced just over a month prior. This potential transaction underscores Anthropic’s aggressive strategy to secure massive computational resources for its AI development, despite growing investor concerns about the sustainability of such high-cost infrastructure spending. Both companies are simultaneously pursuing initial public offerings, adding financial pressure to these high-stakes negotiations.

## Key Takeaways
- Anthropic is engaged in talks with Nscale for a second $45 billion compute deal, citing sources familiar with the matter via Bloomberg.
- The negotiations are ongoing, and details may change, but the deal would represent a continuation of Anthropic’s extensive history of securing large-scale compute partnerships over the past year.
- Both Anthropic and Nscale are actively pursuing IPOs, with Nscale facing reported investor pushback regarding its proposed $30 billion valuation, highlighting the financial complexities surrounding these deals.

The AI industry is currently characterized by an arms race for computational power, with leading firms like Anthropic investing heavily in infrastructure to support the training and deployment of frontier models. This trend has sparked debate among investors, who worry that the enormous capital expenditure required for compute deals may not yield proportional profits. The situation is further complicated by the companies’ plans to go public, which requires demonstrating financial viability and growth potential to public market investors. Nscale’s reported valuation challenges suggest that the market is scrutinizing the long-term profitability of AI infrastructure providers.
