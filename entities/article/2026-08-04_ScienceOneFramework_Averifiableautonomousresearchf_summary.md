## Summary  
The Science One Framework introduces a Chain‑of‑Evidence (CoE) system that forces every claim in an AI‑generated research artifact to be backed by traceable, verifiable evidence, thereby eliminating hallucinations such as phantom citations and code‑text mismatches. The accompanying CoE Audit automatically measures the integrity of these artifacts, allowing researchers to compare the text they produce with the underlying code and experimental logs. This approach demonstrates that autonomous AI can generate state‑of‑the‑art scientific papers while remaining fully verifiable.

## Key Takeaways  
- [Critical point 1] The CoE framework guarantees that each claim—whether a reference, number, method description, or conclusion—has an explicit evidence chain, preventing broken links.  
- [Critical point 2] In experiments, the Science One Framework achieved zero phantom references and fully reproducible scores, whereas baseline systems hallucinated up to 21 % of their citations and often misaligned code with described methods.  
- [Critical point 3] Despite these verifiability guarantees, the framework attains top‑tier performance on frontier benchmarks such as MLE‑Bench and Parameter‑Golf.

## Context  
Autonomous AI agents are increasingly capable of conducting end‑to‑end scientific research—literature review, hypothesis generation, experiment execution, and manuscript writing. However, the rapid rise in output quality has exposed a structural flaw: verifiability. Current systems often produce non‑existent citations, misdescribe algorithms, or report scores that cannot be reproduced from the provided code, undermining trust in AI‑generated research.

## Implications  
Ensuring verifiable evidence chains is essential for the credibility of AI‑driven scientific work and its adoption across academia and industry. By making AI papers auditable, the Science One Framework reduces misinformation risk, supports regulatory compliance, and encourages broader reliance on autonomous research tools without sacrificing performance.
