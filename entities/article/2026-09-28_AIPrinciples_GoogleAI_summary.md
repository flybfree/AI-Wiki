# Summary: Google AI Principles

**Source:** [Google AI Principles](https://ai.google/principles/)
**Capture provenance:** The partial 2026-09-28 raw capture is `raw/articles/2026-09-28_AIPrinciples_GoogleAI.md`.

## Summary

Google describes its approach to artificial intelligence (AI) through three principles: bold innovation, responsible development and deployment, and collaborative progress. The policy connects product and scientific ambition with risk management, human oversight, safety, fairness, privacy, and cooperation across the AI ecosystem.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Bold innovation:** Google says AI should assist and empower people, advance science, and address real-world problems. It commits to pursuing uses whose likely benefits substantially outweigh foreseeable risks, applying scientific inquiry, and measuring tangible outcomes.
2. **Responsible development and deployment:** Google frames responsibility as a lifecycle practice—from design and testing through deployment, iteration, and post-launch monitoring. The page names human oversight, due diligence, feedback, safety and security research, rigorous testing and safeguards, bias mitigation, privacy, security, and intellectual-property rights.
3. **Collaborative progress:** Google says it will build tools that let others use AI for individual and collective benefit, collaborate with researchers, engage governments and civil society, and support a broader ecosystem of developers.

## Governance in Practice

The page says Google assesses risks through research, expert input, and pre- and post-launch testing; uses those assessments to inform policies and frameworks; and applies mitigations that adapt as AI systems and uses evolve.

## Context and Limitations
