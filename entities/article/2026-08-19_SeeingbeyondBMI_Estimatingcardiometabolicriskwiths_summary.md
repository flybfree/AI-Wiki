## Summary  
PhotoScan, a deep‑learning model that extracts body‑composition metrics from ordinary smartphone photos, can estimate insulin resistance with accuracy comparable to the gold‑standard DXA scan. By delivering non‑invasive A/G and V/S ratios alongside body‑fat percentage, PhotoScan offers a scalable alternative to costly clinical equipment while leveraging AI to interpret visual data.

## Key Takeaways  
- PhotoScan achieves near‑DXA precision in predicting insulin resistance from 2D smartphone images.  
- The model outperforms smartwatch BIA sensors and can compute A/G and V/S ratios that are unavailable with those devices.  
- Validation on a diverse adult cohort demonstrates clinical feasibility for routine, low‑cost screening.
