## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s newest large‑language model that delivers near‑frontier performance at roughly half the price of Claude Fable 5, becoming the default on Claude Max and the strongest choice on Claude Pro. It excels across a wide range of benchmarks—coding, knowledge work, scientific reasoning, and visual output—while offering efficient token usage for everyday tasks.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 matches or exceeds Fable 5’s performance on many coding and knowledge‑work benchmarks (e.g., Frontier‑Bench, CursorBench) at a lower cost per task.  
- It shows significant gains over its predecessor in scientific research, such as 10.2 percentage points higher scores on organic‑chemistry tasks and 7.7 points on protein‑variation prediction.  
- The model generates high‑quality visualizations (e.g., wind‑tunnel airflow diagrams) and is the default intelligent option for Claude Max.
