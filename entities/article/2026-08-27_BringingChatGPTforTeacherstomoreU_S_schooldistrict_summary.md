## Summary  
OpenAI is expanding its free “ChatGPT for Teachers” program to more than 100 K U.S. K‑12 educators and staff across 30 states, adding a 16‑state National Data Privacy Agreement that provides a common framework for districts to adopt the tool while meeting FERPA requirements. The initiative also includes training, data‑privacy safeguards, and role‑based access controls, with the program remaining free through June 2028.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI is reaching over 100 K educators in 30 states by adding 55 new school districts to its ChatGPT for Teachers rollout.  
- A 16‑state National Data Privacy Agreement, built on the Student Data Privacy Consortium framework, offers a standardized privacy pathway for district adoption.  
- The tool remains free and teacher‑only, with data never used to train models by default and full FERPA‑compliant governance controls.
