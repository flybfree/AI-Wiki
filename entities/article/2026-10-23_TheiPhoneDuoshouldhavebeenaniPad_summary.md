Rebranding it as an "iPad Duo" might have aligned user expectations better with its dual-screen functionality, suggesting that marketing strategy is as critical as hardware engineering in tech adoption.
- **Review Volatility and Evolving Tech:** The article highlights the difficulty of reviewing novel hardware concepts.
