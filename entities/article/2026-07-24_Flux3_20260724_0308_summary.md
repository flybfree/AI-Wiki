## Summary  
Flux 3 is a multimodal foundation model that jointly learns from images, videos, and audio within a unified architecture to capture the underlying reality of physical events, enabling perception, prediction, and action across digital and physical spaces. Early access shows it can generate diverse videos with native audio up to 20 seconds, perform text‑to‑video, image‑to‑video, video‑to‑video tasks, and support multilingual dialogue. The model’s architecture, called Self‑Flow, scales compute and data across modalities simultaneously.  

## Key Takeaways  
- FLUX 3 unifies vision, motion, and sound into a single flow model that respects physical constraints.  
- It can generate high‑fidelity videos with synchronized audio from text or visual/audio references.  
- The architecture enables multimodal generation without separate fine‑tuned pipelines.  

## Context  
This aligns with the industry shift toward unified foundation models that integrate multiple sensory modalities, reducing data silos and enabling richer AI agents capable of real‑world interaction. This trend mirrors efforts by companies like Google and Meta to create single models that handle vision, language, and multimodal tasks.  

## Implications  
By merging perception and generation across modalities, Flux 3 paves the way for autonomous systems that can understand and act on physical environments, accelerating applications in robotics, AR/VR, and immersive content creation.
