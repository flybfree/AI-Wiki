## Summary  
Gemini 3.5 Flash is announced as fully general‑available today, delivering frontier‑level performance that is four times faster than earlier models while maintaining a 1 million‑token context window. The model costs $0.0015 per token (or $9 for 1 M tokens) and scores 76.2% on Terminal‑Bench 2.1, surpassing Gemini 3.1 Pro in coding and agent tasks.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Gemini 3.5 Flash achieves 4× speed improvements over comparable models while keeping a 1 M token context.  
- It is priced at $0.0015 per token (≈$9 for 1 M tokens), making it cost‑effective for high‑throughput applications.  
- Benchmarks show 76.2% Terminal‑Bench 2.1 performance, beating Gemini 3.1 Pro in coding and agent reasoning.  

## Context  
The release reflects the rapid evolution of large language models toward higher throughput, longer context windows, and specialized pricing tiers that cater to both research and commercial use. It also signals a shift where speed and cost become as critical as raw capability, influencing adoption across enterprise AI services.  

## Implications  
This advancement will likely accelerate integration of Gemini‑based agents in real‑time workflows, reduce latency for large‑scale inference, and encourage more developers to adopt Gemini over legacy models like GPT‑4 or Claude 3. The combination of speed, cost, and performance could reshape competitive dynamics among AI providers.
