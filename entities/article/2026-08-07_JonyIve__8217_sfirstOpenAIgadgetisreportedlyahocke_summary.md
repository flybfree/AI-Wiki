The device is described as “hockey puck‑sized,” lacking a display and intended to launch in 2027 for a price exceeding $300. It will be part of a broader family of AI hardware that OpenAI plans to release alongside its chatbot ecosystem.

- The gadget is roughly the size of a hockey puck, with a unique moving‑part design that includes lights and sensors to indicate activity.  
- It is battery‑operated and will not include a screen, operating similarly to ChatGPT’s voice mode but with more advanced interactivity.  
- Bloomberg estimates the device could cost over $300 when it arrives in 2027, positioning it as a premium, non‑Apple alternative.

The announcement fits into OpenAI’s strategy of expanding beyond pure software to create embodied AI experiences that complement its chatbot platforms.
