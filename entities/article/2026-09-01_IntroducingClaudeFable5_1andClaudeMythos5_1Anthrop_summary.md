## Summary  
Anthropic is unveiling Claude Fable 5.1, a more capable and cost‑effective model for coding and knowledge work, alongside Claude Mythos 5.1 that offers tighter safeguards for specialized sectors such as cybersecurity and life sciences; both releases directly address recent customer feedback on price, data retention, and false‑positive rates.

## Key Takeaways  
- Price reductions are significant: Fable 5.1 will cost roughly 25 % less than Fable 5 for typical workloads, with up to about 45 % savings for highly agentic tasks due to lower cache‑read costs.  
- The Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) system provides a zero‑data‑retention option that stores all input data in the customer’s own cloud infrastructure, slated for enterprise rollout later this fall.  
- New safeguards cut false positives by roughly 60 %, enabling Fable 5.1 to discover software vulnerabilities without facilitating exploits and supporting a U.S. government‑backed biology access program for Mythos 5.1.

## Context  
The launch aligns with broader industry moves toward cheaper, privacy‑preserving AI services and the creation of tiered model offerings that balance performance with risk mitigation. It also reflects Anthropic’s strategic emphasis on making advanced reasoning accessible while curbing misuse in high‑stakes domains.

## Implications  
These enhancements could lower entry barriers for developers and researchers, speed up scientific discovery, and establish a benchmark for responsible deployment of powerful language models within enterprise environments.
