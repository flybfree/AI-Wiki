## Summary

It dominates coding and knowledge‑work benchmarks such as Frontier‑Bench, CursorBench, and OSWorld, and it also improves scientific research tasks like organic chemistry and protein prediction.

## Context  
The release follows Anthropic’s ongoing effort to balance frontier capability with affordability in large language models, competing with OpenAI’s GPT series and Google’s Gemini. Opus 5 represents a shift toward “smart efficiency,” where higher performance is achieved without proportionally increasing token usage or price.
