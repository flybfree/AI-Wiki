## Summary  
Brad Lightcap, OpenAI’s chief operating officer since 2022 and a veteran of the firm’s early operations team, has announced his departure to “start something new,” sharing an internal note that described the move as bittersweet. The message hints at a future venture while emphasizing continued belief in OpenAI’s mission and excitement about helping the company advance from a different vantage point.

## Key Takeaways  
- Lightcap spent four years as CFO, then two as COO, overseeing finance, legal, people, corporate security, GTM/Gov, partnerships, and more.  
- His departure coincides with OpenAI’s broader executive reshuffle ahead of a highly anticipated industry‑wide IPO.  
- He signals that he will be launching a new venture focused on “the next horizon,” though specifics remain undisclosed.

## Context  
OpenAI is navigating a period of rapid growth and strategic transition, marked by the exit of other senior leaders such as Fidji Simo (AGI lead) and Bill Peebles (Sora head). The company’s push toward an IPO underscores its ambition to become a publicly traded AI powerhouse, prompting internal realignments that prioritize continuity amid change.
