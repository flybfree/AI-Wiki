## Summary  
The article introduces the Biomarker Discovery Framework, a multi‑agent AI system designed to turn continuous wearable sensor data into reliable clinical biomarkers while preserving statistical rigor and human oversight. By orchestrating hypothesis generation, parallel analysis, adversarial validation, and literature grounding in an iterative loop, the framework recovers known physiological signals across three large cohorts (N = 9,279) and improves downstream prediction when combined with demographic features.

## Key Takeaways  
- The framework integrates deterministic computation for statistical testing with generative reasoning to propose physiologically plausible biomarker candidates.  
- An adversarial pipeline and human‑supervised orchestration prevent spurious correlations, leakage, and brittle feature selection.  
- Across three independent cohorts the system recovered known clinical signals and identified convergent biomarkers, demonstrating real‑world efficacy.

Current AI research often relies on language‑model agents that excel at predictive tasks but struggle with time‑series physiological data, leading to invalid features.
