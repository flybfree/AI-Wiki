- Tamper‑resistant safeguards must persist under downstream fine‑tuning or adversarial attacks intended to remove them.

## Context  
The rapid release of open‑weight AI models creates a dual challenge of enabling innovation while mitigating potential misuse across domains like cybersecurity, chemistry, and biology. Safety research is essential to balance openness with responsible deployment and maintain public trust in the technology.

## Implications  
By funding these specific safety projects, the initiative could set new benchmarks for model governance, encourage industry participation in safety testing, and ultimately shape policies that support both open science and security.
