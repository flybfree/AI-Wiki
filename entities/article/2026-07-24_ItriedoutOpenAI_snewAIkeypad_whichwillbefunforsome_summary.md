## Summary  
OpenAI’s new Micro keypad is a limited‑edition hardware accessory designed to pair with ChatGPT and its coding assistant Codex, offering voice dictation and customizable key assignments that some developers find fun. The device is marketed as an artisanal novelty at $230, but it has drawn criticism for being overpriced, overly complex, and more of a prank than a practical tool.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI’s Micro keypad targets only hardcore coders who can program its six frosted agent keys and command buttons to control ChatGPT sessions.  
- Its high price point ($230) and limited functionality make it an expensive alternative to cheaper, off‑the‑shelf solutions.  
- Early user feedback is mixed: some call it a prank, while others discover enjoyment once they master the customization.
