## Summary  
Relay, an AI‑powered workflow automation startup that launched in 2021 and aimed to become the next Zapier, is shutting down its service on September 14, ending access for paying customers (free users lost access earlier). Founder Jacob Bank will join Google’s Chrome team as VP of Product, bringing his six‑year experience at Google to lead Chrome’s AI initiatives.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Relay shuts down after six years, concluding its independent AI automation offering.  
- Founder Jacob Bank becomes Google Chrome’s VP of Product, merging his background with the company’s AI strategy.  
- The departure signals a broader trend: startups either get acquired or their talent moves onto dominant platform ecosystems to scale impact.  

## Context  
Relay’s launch followed Timeful’s acquisition by Google and reflects the rapid infusion of generative AI into consumer products, as seen with Gemini’s integration into Chrome and its 1 billion‑user milestone. This context underscores a market where niche automation tools compete against embedded AI experiences within major platforms.  

## Implications  
The closure highlights challenges for standalone AI automation services in an era dominated by integrated solutions, while also illustrating how Google leverages its ecosystem to embed AI, potentially setting a benchmark for other companies. For the industry, it may encourage startups to focus on platform partnerships rather than isolated products.
