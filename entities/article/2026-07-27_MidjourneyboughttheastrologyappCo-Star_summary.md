## Summary  
Midjourney has acquired the personalized astrology app Co‑Star, a free service that delivers daily horoscopes using AI combined with human insight and NASA data. The move signals the startup’s shift from generating AI cat images to exploring lifestyle‑oriented AI products, with founder Banu Guler joining Midjourney as chief design officer.

## Key Takeaways  
- Midjourney bought Co‑Star, a free app that blends human expertise, space data, and AI for personalized horoscopes.  
- Co‑Star’s founder Banu Guler will stay in charge of the app while also becoming Midjourney’s chief design officer.  
- The acquisition marks Midjourney’s pivot from image generation to lifestyle‑focused AI applications.

## Context  
Midjourney, known for generating AI cat pictures and full‑body ultrasound scans, is expanding into personalization and wellness. Co‑Star exemplifies a trend where generative AI is being applied to everyday services like horoscopes, merging machine learning with user data and space science. This acquisition reflects broader industry interest in diversifying AI’s use cases beyond visual content.

## Implications  
The deal could produce new AI‑driven astrology tools that integrate generative models with individual user profiles, potentially reshaping consumer expectations for personalized experiences. It may also attract further investment as investors view cross‑functional AI applications—such as wellness and lifestyle services—as promising growth areas within the generative AI ecosystem.
