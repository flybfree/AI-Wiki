## Summary  
Grab and OpenAI have launched a two-year initiative called _GO Forward with AI_ to equip 30,000 drivers, delivery partners, and merchants across Southeast Asia with practical AI skills. The program begins in Singapore and expands regionally, aiming to help participants apply AI tools like ChatGPT to real-world business decisions, from exploring new ventures to optimizing sales and promotions.

## Key Takeaways  
- [Critical point 1] Over half of Grab’s driver-partners in Singapore already use AI tools, with ChatGPT being the most familiar, indicating strong interest but a gap in practical application.  
- [Critical point 2] The _GO Forward with AI_ program provides hands-on workshops where participants learn to use AI for tasks such as business planning, sales analysis, and website development, fostering confidence through guided practice.  
- [Critical point 3] The initiative is part of a broader effort to ensure AI benefits are accessible across Southeast Asia, aligning OpenAI’s mission with Grab’s commitment to economic opportunity.

## Context  
This program operates within the growing trend of applying generative AI like ChatGPT in small business and gig economy contexts. It builds on prior collaborations such as OpenAI for Singapore and Driver AI Assistant, which use AI to support daily operational decisions. The focus is on democratizing access to advanced tools that are currently limited to tech-savvy users.

## Implications  
This matters for the field by demonstrating how AI can be integrated into non-technical workflows without requiring deep expertise. It highlights a scalable model for workforce upskilling in emerging economies, potentially setting a precedent for public-private partnerships in AI education. By emphasizing practical outcomes over theoretical knowledge, the initiative could influence future AI training programs globally.
