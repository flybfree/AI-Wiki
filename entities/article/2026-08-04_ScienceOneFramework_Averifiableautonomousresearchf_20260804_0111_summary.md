## Key Takeaways  
- CoE defines two properties for trustworthy artifacts: completeness of evidence chains and correctness of each chain.  
- The Science One Framework integrates CoE with an automated audit (CoE Audit) to measure integrity, yielding zero hallucinated references.  
- This framework enables autonomous AI research pipelines that are both high‑performing and fully verifiable.  

## Context  
The rapid deployment of large language models as autonomous scientific agents raises concerns about reliability; existing systems often produce non‑existent citations or unreproducible results. The CoE framework addresses these issues by formalizing trustworthy output criteria, similar to ACID for databases.  

## Implications  
For the AI research community, Science One shows that verifiable evidence chains can be built natively into autonomous agents without sacrificing performance, paving the way for responsible AI‑driven scientific discovery. For industry, it offers a model for auditable AI‑generated documentation, enhancing trust in automated content creation and reducing risk of misinformation.
