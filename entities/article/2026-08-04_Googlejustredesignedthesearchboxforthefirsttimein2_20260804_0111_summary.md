## Summary  
Google is retiring its 25‑year‑old static search box in favor of a dynamic, AI‑powered interface that accepts text, images, PDFs, videos and Chrome tabs. The new design merges AI Overviews with AI Mode into one seamless experience while coaching users to formulate detailed queries.

## Key Takeaways  
- [The redesigned search box functions as an open‑ended conversation starter rather than a simple keyword input.]  
- [It supports multimodal inputs such as files, images, videos and Chrome tabs directly from the main interface.]  
- [Google is unifying AI Overviews and AI Mode into a single flow to eliminate friction between results pages and conversational search.]

## Context  
The announcement coincides with Google’s I/O 2026 event where it unveiled Gemini models, its personal AI agent Spark, an intelligent shopping cart, and a reimagined developer platform. All of these innovations signal a broader shift toward multimodal, context‑aware AI experiences within the Alphabet ecosystem.

## Implications  
The new search box reshapes how users interact with Google’s flagship product, potentially driving deeper engagement and richer data collection. It may also influence SEO practices as queries become more complex and multimodal, and it reinforces Google’s strategy to embed conversational AI throughout its services rather than isolate features behind separate modes.
