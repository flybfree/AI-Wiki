## Summary  
The article argues that opening the weights of powerful language models can be a public good if done safely, but it warns that misuse risks—especially in cybersecurity—make indiscriminate release unsafe. It proposes a staged, ecosystem‑focused approach that couples robust safety testing with incremental openness to balance benefits and dangers.

## Key Takeaways  
- Safe open‑weight models require thorough safety testing and research into whether dangerous capabilities can be decoupled from general intelligence.  
- Open weights create real misuse risks, such as accelerating cybersecurity exploits, creating an uncertain offense‑defense balance that is hard to predict.  
- A safe path involves staged releases that build ecosystem readiness, support defenders, and iteratively increase openness based on evidence.
