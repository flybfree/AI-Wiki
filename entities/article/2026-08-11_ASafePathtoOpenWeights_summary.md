## Summary  
The article argues that releasing powerful AI model weights can be a public good if accompanied by rigorous safety testing and a prepared ecosystem, but it also warns that open models pose real misuse risks—particularly in cybersecurity—that could lower the barrier for offensive attacks. To move toward openness safely, Thinking Machines proposes a staged release strategy that balances model safety with incremental ecosystem readiness.
