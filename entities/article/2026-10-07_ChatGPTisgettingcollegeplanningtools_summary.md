## Summary
OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT for Teens mode by introducing a new College Planner tool designed to help high school students manage the complex college application process. This feature consolidates application requirements, deadlines, and financial aid steps into a unified timeline, aiming to streamline planning for students in grades 10 through 12. Additionally, OpenAI is enhancing study aids with new features like multi-photo PDF creation and automated flashcard generation, positioning ChatGPT as a comprehensive academic support system for younger users.

## Key Takeaways
- **Integrated Application Management**: The College Planner centralizes critical data such as school-specific requirements, submission deadlines, and financial aid tasks, allowing students to track progress and plan ahead efficiently within a single interface.
- **Targeted Demographic Focus**: Initially, the tool will prioritize US-based students in grades 10 through 12 who are planning to attend four-year colleges, indicating a strategic focus on the pre-college preparation phase rather than general higher education support.
- **Enhanced Study Utilities**: Beyond planning, OpenAI is introducing practical study tools, including continuous multi-photo capture for creating PDFs from physical notes and the ability to generate digital flashcards from user inputs, directly supporting daily academic workflows.
- **Response to Safety Concerns**: The launch occurs amidst criticism from Common Sense Media, which labeled ChatGPT for Teens an "unacceptable risk" due to concerns about homework assistance and lack of parental alerts; OpenAI counters this by emphasizing that teens spend an average of under 15 minutes per day on the platform.
