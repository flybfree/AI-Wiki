## Summary
Meta has officially launched two new AI models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, positioning them as direct competitors to top-tier industry models from OpenAI and Anthropic. These models are currently available to Muse Code subscribers and via the Meta Model API, with plans to eventually power Meta’s broader Muse AI agent. This launch represents a significant step in Meta’s aggressive strategy to reclaim its status as a leading force in the artificial intelligence race through substantial investments in talent and infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways
- **New Model Launches**: Meta introduced "Muse Wonder" and "Muse Wonder Full," which are accessible to Muse Code subscribers and through the Meta Model API. These models are designed to integrate with Meta’s existing Muse ecosystem, including the Muse agent and Muse Image capabilities.
- **Competitive Claims**: Meta asserts that these new models rival leading competitors such as OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1. The company cites internal testing on industry benchmarks to support these claims, though comparisons with Google’s Gemini 4 Argon remain unclear.
- **Strategic Integration**: The models are intended to eventually power the Muse AI agent for all users, not just subscribers. Meta plans to develop specialized models to enhance the utility of its AI agents for the general public, although specific timelines and technical details for this broader rollout have not been disclosed.
