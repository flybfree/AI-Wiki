Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest large‑language model that delivers state‑of‑the‑art performance on coding and knowledge‑work benchmarks while offering roughly half the price of its predecessor. It also shows notable gains in scientific research tasks, visual generation, and overall cost‑effectiveness compared with other Claude models.

The Claude series (Opus 4.8 → Opus 5) competes with Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models in a market where cost‑performance trade‑offs drive enterprise adoption. Opus 5 is positioned as the default model for Claude Max and the strongest choice on Claude Pro, reflecting Anthropic’s strategy to provide high‑value AI at accessible prices.
