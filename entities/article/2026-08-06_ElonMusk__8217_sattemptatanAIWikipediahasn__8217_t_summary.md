## Summary  
Elon Musk’s AI‑driven encyclopedia Grokipedia, which was launched in October 2025 and now hosts over six million articles, has not received any updates since April 24, 2026. A report from Lawfare examined 34,519 pages with suggested edits and found zero accepted or rejected corrections within the last three months, leaving users on Reddit and X wondering if the project is effectively dead.

## Key Takeaways  
- [Grokipedia’s live page shows “No live edits available right now,” indicating a complete halt in content updates.]  
- [The site still allows suggestions and edits, but none have been processed since April.]  
- [Elon Musk’s last X post mentioning Grokipedia was from February 2026, underscoring the lack of recent engagement.]

## Context  
Grokipedia is part of xAI’s effort to create an AI‑generated knowledge base that promises a “massive improvement” over traditional Wikipedia. By October 2025 it had already amassed 885,000 articles and reached version v0.2 with more than six million entries, positioning itself as a competitive alternative in the open‑source encyclopedia space.

## Implications  
The stagnation of Grokipedia raises concerns about the reliability and relevance of AI‑generated content, potentially eroding user trust in machine‑curated knowledge sources. It also highlights a broader industry challenge: ensuring that AI platforms remain dynamic, community‑driven, and responsive to real‑world updates.
