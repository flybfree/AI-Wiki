## Summary

This comparison frames the 2026 LLM landscape as a buyer’s guide: it compares the major models across reasoning, coding, multimodality, and price rather than treating every benchmark as equally important. The main point is that “best” depends on workload, because a model that wins on raw intelligence may lose on cost, context window, or tool reliability.

## Key Takeaways
- The article is useful as a side-by-side shortlist, not as a single universal ranking.
- Reasoning, coding, multimodal capability, and price are the four main axes to compare.
- The practical choice depends on whether you value frontier quality, long context, or lower cost.
- Model selection should be tied to the workload, not to headline benchmark wins alone.
