## Summary  
The Science One Framework introduces Chain‑of‑Evidence (CoE), a verifiable framework that ties every claim in AI‑generated research to concrete, reproducible evidence, eliminating hallucinated references and misaligned code. By pairing CoE with the autonomous prototype Science One, the system produces fully auditable manuscripts that achieve state‑of‑the‑art performance on benchmark tasks.

## Key Takeaways  
- Verifiable evidence chains guarantee that AI claims are traceable to real data or code.  
- The framework eliminates phantom references and misaligned methods, achieving zero hallucinations.  
- Science One maintains top‑tier results on frontier benchmarks like MLE‑Bench and Parameter‑Golf.

## Context  
Current autonomous research agents (e.g., AI‑Scientist, AutoResearchClaw) generate papers iteratively, often producing non‑existent citations or scores that cannot be reproduced. This undermines trust in machine‑produced scientific output as the field pushes toward fully self‑directed workflows.

## Implications  
Ensuring verifiable evidence is essential for AI to become a credible contributor to science; it prevents misinformation and accelerates reproducibility, fostering broader adoption of autonomous research tools across academia and industry.
