## Summary  
The Science One Framework introduces Chain‑of‑Evidence (CoE), a verifiable framework that forces every claim in an AI‑generated research artifact to be backed by a traceable evidence chain, eliminating hallucinations and misalignments. By pairing CoE with the autonomous prototype Science One and its audit tool CoE Audit, the system achieves zero phantom references while maintaining state‑of‑the‑art performance on benchmarks such as MLE‑Bench and Parameter‑Golf.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Zero hallucinations**: The framework guarantees that every reference, number, method description, or conclusion links to real evidence, removing up to 21 % of phantom citations reported by baseline systems.  
- **Fully reproducible scores**: Evidence chains are tied directly to executed code and logs, ensuring scores reappear when the experiment is rerun.  
- **Verifiable performance**: The CoE Audit provides measurable metrics that quantify integrity, making trustworthy AI‑driven research auditable.

## Context  
Autonomous AI agents now perform full scientific workflows—literature review, hypothesis generation, experiment execution, and manuscript writing—yet their outputs often suffer from verifiability flaws. Existing solutions lack a systematic way to prove that claims are grounded in actual data or code, leading to unreliable citations and non‑reproducible results.

## Implications  
This work establishes a new standard for trustworthy AI research, enabling developers to deploy autonomous agents with confidence that their outputs are credible and reproducible. For the broader field, it reduces risk of misinformation, accelerates peer review, and opens pathways for AI‑generated science to be integrated into academic publishing and industry R&D pipelines.
