## Summary  
Ringg, a voice and chat agent platform serving large consumer businesses in India, has integrated OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6 models into its enterprise AI agent system to resolve up to 65% of customer service calls automatically. By combining high-efficiency language models with an orchestration layer that triggers tools across CRM, scheduling, payment, and internal APIs, Ringg delivers real-time, multichannel support at scale while maintaining a 4.8‑star CSAT score.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6 provides the optimal balance of quality, latency, instruction following, tool use, and cost for enterprise workloads, outperforming alternatives across multiple dimensions.  
- Ringg’s platform routes tasks to specific models—GPT‑4.1 handles real‑time voice and chat traffic, while GPT‑5.6 Luna is used when its performance or price‑performance improves the request; GPT‑5.6 Terra processes post‑call analysis and GPT‑5.6 Sol supports evaluation and prompt improvement.  
- The orchestration layer coordinates multi‑step workflows across voice, chat, WhatsApp, and web channels, ensuring consistent customer experience and accurate escalation to human specialists.
