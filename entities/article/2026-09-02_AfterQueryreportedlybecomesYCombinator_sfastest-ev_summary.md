## Summary  
AfterQuery, an AI‑training‑data startup that specializes in encoding the reasoning of top practitioners rather than merely answering factual questions, has reportedly become Y Combinator’s fastest‑ever unicorn, achieving a $3.2 billion valuation after its latest funding round. The increase represents more than a tenfold jump from its April Series A valuation of $300 million and marks the quickest path to unicorn status for any startup that has gone through YC’s Winter 2025 cohort.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Valuation surge:** AfterQuery’s valuation jumped over tenfold in less than six months, from $300 M to $3.2 B, underscoring rapid market confidence.  
- **YC milestone:** The company is now the fastest startup ever to transition from launch to unicorn status within Y Combinator’s history, according to partner Gustaf Alströmer.  
- **Professional‑focused AI:** AfterQuery trains models on how experts—doctors, lawyers, etc.—solve tasks, a niche approach that differentiates it from pure QA‑oriented competitors.

## Context  
The broader AI training‑data market is expanding rapidly as large labs seek high‑quality, domain‑specific knowledge to improve model performance. Recent examples include Mercor and Scale, which also recruit specialist professionals for model training. AfterQuery’s $100 million annualized revenue run rate and notable customers such as Nvidia, Legora, and Motif Technologies illustrate the growing demand for specialized AI expertise beyond generic chatbot capabilities.

## Implications  
This rapid valuation jump signals that investors view niche, expert‑driven AI solutions as high‑growth opportunities, potentially accelerating funding rounds for similar startups. It may also shift talent toward building domain‑specific training pipelines rather than focusing solely on large language models, reshaping the competitive landscape of AI development.
