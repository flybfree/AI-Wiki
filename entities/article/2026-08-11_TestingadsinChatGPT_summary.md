OpenAI has launched a pilot program to test ads within ChatGPT for logged‑in adult users on the Free and Go subscription tiers, expanding the rollout to the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea. The initiative aims to fund infrastructure that supports broader access while preserving user trust, answer independence, and privacy, guided by OpenAI’s advertising principles.

- The March 26 2026 U.S. pilot shows no measurable impact on consumer trust metrics or answer relevance, confirming the independence of ChatGPT’s responses.  
- Core principles—user control, privacy, and unbiased answers—remain unchanged as OpenAI refines the model.
