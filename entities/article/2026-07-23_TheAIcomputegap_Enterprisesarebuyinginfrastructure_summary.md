## Summary  
Enterprises are rapidly investing in AI infrastructure, but they lack the visibility to measure its true cost and utilization. This creates a compute gap where spending outpaces understanding of economics.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Enterprises plan to adopt AI‑specialized clouds (45 % intent) while current GPU utilization is low (<50 %) and cost tracking is limited.  
- Decision drivers are integration and total cost of ownership, not headline token price; only 8 % consider token pricing.  
- High churn intent: 64 % intend to switch providers within a year, 38 % within the next quarter.  

## Context  
The article references VentureBeat Pulse Research survey of 107 mid‑market firms (101–1,000 employees) highlighting that AI compute investment is outpacing economic insight; the industry faces challenges in measuring GPU utilization and total cost of ownership, while hyperscalers dominate today but specialized clouds are emerging.  

## Implications  
If enterprises cannot quantify compute costs, they risk overspending, reduced ROI, and difficulty justifying further investments. The high churn suggests a need for standardized cost‑tracking tools and integration frameworks to align spend with performance, preventing the AI compute gap from widening into unsustainable financial strain.
