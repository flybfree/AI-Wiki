## Summary
Midjourney has acquired Co-Star, a personalized astrology application known for combining human insight, NASA data, and AI to provide daily horoscopes and compatibility checks. As part of the acquisition, which reportedly closed in spring 2026, Co-Star founder Banu Guler will remain in charge of the app while simultaneously joining Midjourney as its chief design officer. This move marks a significant strategic shift for Midjourney, as it aims to leverage Guler’s expertise to develop its first dedicated mobile applications, moving beyond its current reliance on web and Discord interfaces.

## Key Takeaways
- **Strategic Acquisition:** Midjourney has officially acquired Co-Star, an app that offers personalized astrological advice using a blend of AI, human insight, and astronomical data from NASA. The financial terms of the deal remain undisclosed, but reports indicate the transaction was finalized in spring 2026.
- **Leadership Integration:** Banu Guler, the founder of Co-Star, will continue to lead the astrology app while taking on a new role as Midjourney’s chief design officer. This dual responsibility highlights Midjourney’s intent to integrate Co-Star’s user-centric design philosophy into its broader product strategy.
- **Product Expansion:** Midjourney plans to use Guler and her team to build its first standalone apps, including a dedicated application for image generation. Currently, Midjourney’s AI models are accessible primarily through web interfaces and Discord bots, so this acquisition signals a pivot toward native mobile app development.

## Context
This acquisition occurs within a broader trend of AI companies seeking to diversify their product offerings and user engagement strategies beyond core generative capabilities. Midjourney has previously expanded its use cases from artistic image generation to more specialized fields like full-body ultrasound scans, indicating a willingness to apply its technology across various industries. By acquiring Co-Star, Midjourney enters the consumer lifestyle and entertainment sector, an area where AI is increasingly used for personalized content delivery. The integration of NASA data and human insight in Co-Star’s model represents a hybrid approach to AI application, combining hard scientific data with subjective interpretation, which contrasts with purely algorithmic outputs often seen in generative AI tools.

## Implications
The acquisition suggests that Midjourney is preparing to transition from a niche tool for artists and developers into a broader consumer-facing platform. By hiring Guler as chief design officer, Midjourney acknowledges the importance of user experience and app-based accessibility in scaling its reach. This move could pressure other AI startups to consider similar acquisitions or partnerships to enhance their mobile presence and user retention strategies.
