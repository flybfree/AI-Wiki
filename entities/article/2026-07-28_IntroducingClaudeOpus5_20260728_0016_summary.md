## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest large‑language model that delivers state‑of‑the‑art performance on coding, knowledge‑work and scientific benchmarks while offering up to half the cost of its predecessor, Claude Fable 5. It is positioned as the default for Claude Max and the strongest option in Claude Pro, excelling in tasks such as code generation, problem solving, and visual illustration.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 outperforms all other models on Frontier‑Bench v0.1 and CursorBench 3.2 at a lower cost per task, making it the most efficient coding agent available.  
- It shows significant gains over Opus 4.8 in life‑science evaluations (e.g., organic chemistry and protein prediction), with improvements of up to 10 percentage points on internal benchmarks.  
- The model generates high‑quality visual outputs, such as wind‑tunnel flow diagrams and interactive cell illustrations, enhancing its multimodal capabilities.

## Context  
The rapid evolution of generative AI models is driven by the need for tools that balance raw capability with economic viability. Claude Opus 5 exemplifies this trend by delivering frontier‑level performance at a reduced price point, reflecting industry pressure to make advanced AI accessible to developers and researchers without prohibitive costs.
