## Summary  
SymptomAI is a conversational AI prototype that conducts end‑to‑end symptom interviews and differential diagnosis for everyday patients, evaluated in a large‑scale randomized study of 13,917 participants across the United States. The research compares the AI’s diagnostic accuracy with human clinicians’ assessments and physiological data from Fitbit wearables to assess real‑world performance.

## Key Takeaways  
- [SymptomAI demonstrates clinically relevant differential diagnosis abilities when interacting with everyday patients, not just curated vignettes.]  
- [The AI’s diagnoses align with physiological trends (e.g., immune response markers) that precede confirmed infectious disease outcomes.]  
- [A randomized national study shows the AI can be integrated into a symptom‑checking app without compromising patient privacy or safety.]

## Context  
Current language models excel at differential diagnosis on synthetic medical cases, yet they have not been tested in natural, unstructured conversations with real patients. This gap limits confidence that conversational AI can replace or augment clinical interviews in routine healthcare settings.

## Implications  
The findings suggest that conversational AI agents like SymptomAI could become a scalable first‑line tool for symptom assessment, reducing barriers to care and enabling early detection of infectious diseases through combined linguistic and physiological signals.
