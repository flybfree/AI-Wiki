## Summary  
Google’s Gemini AI is now capable of handling entire phone calls with businesses on Pixel 11 devices, allowing users to delegate tasks like making reservations, checking product availability, or rescheduling appointments without waiting on hold. The feature operates as an “early experiment,” enabling users to initiate calls through the Gemini app while remaining in control via a live transcript view and the ability to take over at any moment.

## Key Takeaways  
- Gemini can now manage full business phone interactions, including navigating automated menus and handling conversations autonomously.  
- Users retain full oversight with real-time transcript access and the option to intervene during calls.  
- The feature is an early preview limited to Gemini paid subscribers on Pixel 11 in the U.S., following a similar “Call for me” function introduced last year.

## Context  
This development aligns with broader industry trends toward AI-driven customer service automation, where conversational agents aim to reduce human intervention and improve efficiency.
