This approach enables researchers to focus on defining high-level goals and evaluation criteria while the AI handles the intricate execution and recursive improvement of predictive models.

- **Autonomy in Long-Horizon Tasks:** GPT-6 Astra enables agents to perform complex, multi-day workflows that require flexible coordination and persistence, reducing the need for frequent human guidance and allowing agents to self-correct and redirect efforts based on initial proposals.
- **Recursive Improvement and Analysis:** The system can now merge and stack incremental wins, analyzing findings against agreed-upon criteria to create comprehensive analyses that interrelate data from multiple sources, moving beyond simple code generation to holistic problem-solving.
- **Safety and Compliance in Regulated Environments:** Jump Trading emphasizes that while AI can enhance quality and security, strict system design, clear constraints, observability, and human review are critical to managing risks and ensuring compliance in the heavily regulated financial sector.
