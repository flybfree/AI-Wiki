## Summary
This article highlights how UC San Diego researchers are leveraging artificial intelligence to drive transformative breakthroughs across health, science, and public safety. By integrating advanced machine learning with traditional scientific methods, these initiatives are accelerating drug discovery, uncovering hidden disease mechanisms, and enhancing diagnostic precision. The piece underscores the rapid transition of AI from a theoretical tool to a practical engine for solving complex global challenges.

## Key Takeaways
- AI models have revealed that the gene PHGDH, previously considered merely a biomarker, actively disrupts gene regulation in brain cells, identifying it as a direct cause of Alzheimer’s disease.
- The development of MycoBCP, an AI-powered tool combining deep learning with bacterial cytological profiling, allows researchers to detect subtle changes in tuberculosis cells, potentially fast-tracking treatments for drug-resistant strains.
- Engineers are utilizing AI to reconstruct internal signals of heart muscle cells based on external recordings, enabling non-invasive monitoring of cardiac activity that was previously inaccessible.
