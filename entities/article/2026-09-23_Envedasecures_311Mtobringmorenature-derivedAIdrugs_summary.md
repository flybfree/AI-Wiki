## Summary  
Enveda, a biotech startup founded in 2019 by former Recursion Pharmaceuticals employee Viswa Colluru, has raised $311 million in its Series E round to advance nature-derived AI-discovered drugs into clinical trials. The funding, led by Catalio Capital Management and valued at $2 billion, doubles the company’s valuation from a prior $1 billion level achieved 12 months earlier. Enveda is currently testing two AI-designed therapeutics: one for severe skin conditions and another to support weight maintenance after GLP-1 therapy cessation.

## Key Takeaways  
- Enveda secured $311 million in Series E funding, doubling its valuation to $2 billion, underscoring strong investor confidence in the company’s mission.  
- The startup focuses on leveraging AI to identify and develop drugs from natural sources such as plants and microbes, bypassing traditional synthetic methods.  
- Enveda is among the first companies to bring nature-derived AI drug candidates into human clinical trials, marking a significant milestone in translational AI biotech.
