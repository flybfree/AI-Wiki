## Summary
The article discusses a new AI model named "Jev," released by TypeSafe AI, founded by former OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida—one of the key figures behind Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). Unlike traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) that generate human-like text, Jev is designed to produce "calibrated decisions" or probabilities. This shift from linguistic output to probabilistic data aims to solve issues of high cost and hallucination in automated software workflows.

## Key Takeaways
- **Shift from Language to Probability:** Instead of generating prose, the model outputs specific probabilities. This allows for more reliable automation because users can define expected outputs in advance, effectively eliminating the risk of creative "hallucinations."
- **Extreme Efficiency and Cost Savings:** Because it eschews language generation, Jev is significantly cheaper and faster than current LLMs. Testing by companies like Vercel showed that Jev could perform classification tasks 5 to 18 times faster than OpenAI's models while maintaining high accuracy.
- **Reliable Confidence Scores:** A major differentiator for developers is the model’s ability to provide a "real probability." This allows engineers to set thresholds (e.g., only act if the model is 95% certain), making it ideal for automated workflows where reliability is paramount.
- **Versatile Utility in Agentic Workflows:** Beyond replacing LLMs for simple tasks, Jev can be used to monitor other AI agents, helping to detect and prevent "jailbreaks" or errors by acting as a low-cost oversight layer.

## Context
The current state of AI development is dominated by Large Language Models (LLMs) that excel at human communication but often struggle with the precision required for industrial automation. Diogo Almeida’s critique suggests that while we have mastered human-like conversation, we haven't yet perfected "computer-to-computer" logic because we are still optimizing for human language rather than functional utility. Jev represents a move toward "functional AI," where the goal is to provide a reliable decision-making engine rather than a conversational companion.

## Implications
This shift suggests a maturing of the AI industry from "generative" phases toward "agentic" and "reliable" phases. By providing a way to automate tasks at a fraction of the cost, Jev allows for the deployment of complex, multi-step agentic workflows that were previously too expensive or unpredictable to run reliably. Furthermore, it enables real-time model routing—where an AI decides which specific model is best suited for a task—making the entire ecosystem of artificial intelligence more scalable and economically viable for enterprise applications.
