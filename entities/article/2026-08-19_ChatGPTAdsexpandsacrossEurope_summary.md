## Summary  
ChatGPT Ads is rolling out to 31 European countries, giving marketers a new way to reach users who are actively exploring options on the Free and Go plans while keeping Pro and Enterprise subscriptions ad‑free. The expansion follows OpenAI’s commitment to privacy‑first advertising, with ads clearly labeled, non‑influential, and only shown to those who have opted for free access.  

## Key Takeaways  
- ChatGPT Ads will be available in 31 EU nations, initially via the OpenAI Ads Solutions team, agency partners, and technology partners; self‑service through Ads Manager will follow later this summer.  
- Advertising respects user privacy: conversations are never sold to advertisers, ads do not affect ChatGPT’s answers, and users can choose ad‑free paid plans.  
- The platform now supports conversion‑optimized bidding, geo‑targeting, custom audiences, and richer measurement through the OpenAI Pixel, Conversions API, and third‑party integrations.  

## Context  
The broader AI advertising landscape is moving from traditional keyword‑based campaigns to conversational, intent‑driven outreach that aligns with users’ real‑world goals—such as planning trips or choosing software. This shift reflects a trend toward democratizing access to powerful AI tools while integrating ads in ways that feel native and respectful of user experience.  

## Implications  
For the field, this rollout signals that conversational AI can serve as an effective advertising channel without compromising trust or data security, setting a new benchmark for ethical integration across platforms. It also empowers marketers to target users at critical decision moments, potentially increasing conversion rates while reinforcing OpenAI’s mission of broad, responsible access to generative AI.
