## Summary  
NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a high‑efficiency open model for long‑running agentic AI workloads, and NeMo Switchyard, an open routing library that lets enterprises direct requests to the best model across their own mix of models. Together they enable smarter, faster, and more controllable AI deployments.

## Key Takeaways  
- Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is a 30‑billion‑parameter mixture‑of‑experts model that delivers up to four times faster output and completes agentic tasks 30% quicker than comparable models.  
- NeMo Switchyard provides intelligent routing across open, proprietary, and NVIDIA models without requiring application rewrites.  
- Together they enable always‑on agents as modular systems of specialized models running on PCs, workstations, data centers, or the cloud.  

## Context  
In today’s AI landscape, organizations are moving from static chatbots to autonomous agents that operate continuously and require fine‑grained control over performance, cost, and security. The rise of model ensembles—where a large reasoning engine coordinates smaller task‑specific models—exemplifies this shift, demanding tools that can orchestrate diverse model types seamlessly.  

## Implications  
This integration lowers deployment complexity, reduces latency, and enables enterprises to tailor AI behavior to specific domains such as cybersecurity, legal services, or code review. By keeping models open and customizable, NVIDIA empowers a broader ecosystem of developers to innovate responsibly while meeting performance targets.
