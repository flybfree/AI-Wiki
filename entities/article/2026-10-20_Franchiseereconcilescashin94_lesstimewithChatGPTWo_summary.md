## Summary
This article details how Ted Taylor, an independent franchisee operating multiple quick-service restaurants, leverages OpenAI’s ChatGPT Work to streamline complex operational tasks. By integrating AI into his workflow, Taylor achieved a 94% reduction in time spent on cash reconciliation and a 95% decrease in menu repricing duration. The case study highlights how AI tools can transform labor-intensive administrative processes into efficient, data-driven decision-making activities for small business owners.

## Key Takeaways
- **Significant Efficiency Gains in Pricing:** Taylor reduced the time required for annual menu repricing from five days to just one or two hours. ChatGPT Work analyzed complex variables such as recipe costs, competitor pricing, and delivery economics to generate optimized pricing strategies that maintained competitiveness while improving margins.
- **Enhanced Labor Management and Cash Flow:** The AI tool assisted in optimizing labor costs by comparing scheduled, budgeted, and actual hours against sales forecasts. This analysis helped identify inefficiencies across three locations, saving four hours per payroll run and freeing up cash flow by ensuring staffing levels aligned with actual demand.
- **Superior Analytical Depth:** Contrary to manual methods, ChatGPT Work provided deeper local market comparisons and more thorough item-level analysis. Taylor noted that the AI’s recommendations were often superior to his initial manual attempts, allowing him to avoid uncompetitive price increases and better position add-ons for customer retention.
