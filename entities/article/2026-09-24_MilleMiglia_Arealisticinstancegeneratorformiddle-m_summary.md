## Summary  
MilleMiglia is an open-source C++ instance generator designed to create realistic, privacy-preserving benchmarks for middle-mile logistics problems, which are often overlooked in AI and operational research due to the scarcity of high-quality data. The tool addresses a critical gap by simulating complex network topologies and demand patterns across regional or continental scales, enabling researchers to develop more robust and efficient supply chain optimization algorithms without relying on proprietary company information.

## Key Takeaways  
- [Critical point 1] MilleMiglia generates realistic middle-mile logistics instances that bridge the gap between academic theory and industrial practice.  
- [Critical point 2] The tool avoids privacy risks by not using real-world data, instead simulating anonymized network structures and demand volumes.  
- [Critical point 3] By focusing on the understudied middle mile, MilleMiglia enables targeted AI research that can improve global supply chain efficiency.

## Context  
This work is particularly relevant in the broader AI context where machine learning models are increasingly used to optimize logistics networks. However, most existing datasets focus on first-mile (e.g., delivery from warehouse to hub) or last-mile (e.g., final customer delivery), leaving middle-mile — the bulk movement between regional centers — underrepresented. As AI-driven supply chain systems grow more complex, realistic data is essential for training and validating models.

## Implications  
MilleMiglia matters because it provides a scalable, reproducible foundation for research in network optimization and AI applications to logistics. By enabling better modeling of middle-mile challenges such as time-sensitive or temperature-controlled transport, the tool can lead to innovations that reduce costs, improve delivery reliability, and enhance sustainability across global supply chains.
