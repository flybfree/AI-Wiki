## Summary
Extend engineer Bo Lau has developed a highly detailed, interactive website that simulates the digital workspace of former Theranos CEO Elizabeth Holmes, allowing users to navigate her iPhone, MacBook, and the Theranos Edison machine using real data from the *United States v. Elizabeth Holmes* criminal trial. While the project serves as a humorous and immersive way to explore the infamous tech fraud case, it also functions as a promotional tool for Extend’s document parsing technology and an upcoming documentary about Holmes. The site leverages public court exhibits to create a nostalgic 2016 tech environment, offering a more accessible alternative to sifting through thousands of legal PDFs.

## Key Takeaways
- **Immersive Historical Simulation:** The website recreates the specific technological context of 2016, including OS X El Capitan on a MacBook Air and iPhones with physical home buttons, providing a realistic user experience that mirrors the era of Theranos’ peak deception.
- **Real Data Integration:** The interactive elements, such as texts, emails, slides, and machine records, are not fictional but are sourced directly from public exhibits in the Holmes criminal trial, highlighting the transparency of the American legal discovery process.
- **Dual Purpose of Technology:** The project serves as both an educational tool for navigating complex legal documents and a marketing vehicle for Extend, a B2B SaaS company specializing in document parsing and extraction, as well as promoting Nathan Fielder’s documentary, *You Can See Everything*.
