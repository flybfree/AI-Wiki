## Summary  
Cursor, an AI coding startup, is launching a ₹649‑per‑month “Cursor Start” subscription ahead of its rumored SpaceX acquisition, aiming to capture the large developer market in India. The plan targets developers who need more than the free tier but less than the full $20 Pro offering, while using VPN checks to restrict access to Indian users only.

## Key Takeaways  
- Cursor’s new “Cursor Start” costs ₹649 (about $7) per month, far below its $20 Pro plan, to attract India’s large developer base.  
- The subscription includes Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.5 models with higher usage limits but excludes frontier AI models and advanced features like Bugbot or Auto Mode.  
- Cursor plans the plan to be India‑only initially, mirroring OpenAI’s earlier Go model and potentially expanding later if successful.

## Context  
India hosts over 27 million developers on GitHub, making it a top developer hub; global AI firms are tailoring pricing to capture this market. Cursor’s move aligns with broader trends of localized subscription models in fast‑growing regions.

## Implications  
Localized pricing can lower entry barriers for AI tools, fostering wider adoption among developers and reshaping competitive dynamics as more companies adopt region‑specific plans; however, it may also limit scalability if not adaptable to other markets.
