## Summary
Barclays is significantly expanding its strategic partnership with Anthropic to integrate Claude’s enterprise-grade AI systems across its global operations, aiming to modernize legacy infrastructure and enhance operational efficiency. This initiative targets a widespread adoption of Claude Code among software developers, with projections indicating that half of the developer population will utilize the tool by late 2026. By embedding agentic AI capabilities into their workflows, Barclays seeks to transform how technology is built, secured, and managed while delivering superior customer experiences.

## Key Takeaways
- **Strategic Expansion and Adoption Goals**: Barclays is scaling its use of Claude beyond pilot programs to a bank-wide deployment. The institution has set ambitious targets for the adoption of Claude Code, expecting 50% of developers to use it by the end of 2026, with majority usage among software engineers anticipated in 2027. This move underscores a commitment to accelerating software development cycles and modernizing outdated legacy systems through reliable AI assistance.
- **Operational Efficiency via Knowledge Assistants**: The partnership has already yielded tangible results through tools like the Colleague Knowledge Assistant, which utilizes retrieval-augmented generation to help employees find information rapidly. Since its launch in 2025, over 16,000 colleagues have used the assistant to handle more than one million searches, directly contributing to faster support for Barclays’ 20 million UK retail customers and reducing time spent on routine inquiries.
- **Agentic AI in Global Markets**: Within the Global Markets division, Claude models are being employed to streamline client inquiry processing. These AI systems classify, enrich, and route incoming emails, ensuring that requests are prioritized correctly and processed efficiently. This application highlights a shift toward agentic capabilities where AI actively participates in decision-making workflows rather than just providing passive information retrieval.
