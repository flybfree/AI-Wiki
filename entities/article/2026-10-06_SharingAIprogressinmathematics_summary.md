## Summary
OpenAI has released a broad range of new mathematical results generated by an internal frontier model, marking a significant step in AI-driven scientific discovery. To ensure these findings are integrated responsibly into the academic community, OpenAI collaborated with the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at the Institute for Advanced Study to establish best practices for publication and citation. The release emphasizes transparency by providing formal proofs in Lean, detailed reasoning summaries, and compute usage statistics, while also committing to future improvements in paper quality and community engagement.

- **Collaborative Best Practices:** OpenAI actively consulted with the independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AGMAI) to align its release protocols with established academic standards, ensuring that AI-generated results are credible and properly cited.
- **Transparency and Formalization:** The results are published in a GitHub repository that includes formalizations of proofs in Lean, a computer-verified programming language. Additionally, OpenAI disclosed internal metrics, such as reasoning summaries and compute estimates (averaging three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking), to demystify the discovery process.
- **Commitment to Community Empowerment:** Beyond mere publication, OpenAI plans to fund workshops and conferences to help scientists understand these major results. The company is also working toward responsibly releasing the underlying model to directly empower researchers with state-of-the-art tools for advancing mathematical knowledge.
