## Summary  
Spotify will introduce “AI Persona” badges on profiles that appear to be generated by artificial intelligence and will deliberately exclude the music of those artists from both editorial and algorithmic recommendations. The labels will appear across profile banners, About sections, search results, and playlist rows, allowing only users who explicitly follow an AI‑Persona artist to hear their tracks.

## Key Takeaways  
- Spotify will label AI‑generated artist profiles with “AI Persona” tags and ban their music from recommendations unless the user follows the artist.  
- The labeling system is based on a review of profile appearance, not on how the music was created, and users can appeal if they believe it’s incorrect.  
- The move expands Spotify’s existing AI policy to combat low‑quality AI content and protect the platform from “AI slop” that could harm user experience.

## Context  
The announcement is part of a broader industry effort to manage the proliferation of AI‑generated music, which can flood platforms with low‑quality, deceptive content. Other services have introduced similar labeling mechanisms, such as industry‑standard AI detection techniques and deepfake bans. Spotify’s approach aligns with these trends while emphasizing user agency through explicit follow options.

## Implications  
For the music ecosystem, this policy may improve authenticity and trust by clearly distinguishing human artists from synthetic ones, potentially deterring mass churn caused by poor content quality. However, it also raises concerns about labeling accuracy, artist autonomy, and the impact on discovery algorithms that could limit exposure for genuine creators who use AI as a tool.
