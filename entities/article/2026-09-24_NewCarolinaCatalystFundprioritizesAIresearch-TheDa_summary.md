## Summary  
The University of North Carolina has launched a new internal research fund called the New Carolina Catalyst Fund, designed to support high-risk, high-reward AI-related projects through two tiers: Catalyst Spark ($10,000) and Catalyst Accelerate (up to $75,000). The program aims to accelerate early-stage innovation by reducing financial risk, with applications due October 15 and awards expected in December for projects starting January 2027.  

## Key Takeaways

- [The program includes internal seed funding and administrative support from university offices, enabling early-stage collaboration and proof-of-concept development.]
