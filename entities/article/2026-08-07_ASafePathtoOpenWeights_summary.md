## Summary

## Key Takeaways  
- Robust safety testing is essential for any model release; it must identify harmful capabilities and assess accessibility even if guardrails are removed.  
- Open‑weight models carry real misuse potential, as seen in the 2026 Anthropic exploit case, so a one‑size‑fits‑all release strategy is unsafe.  
- A safe path to openness depends on both model safety and ecosystem readiness, requiring staged releases, support for defenders, and collaboration with safety researchers.
