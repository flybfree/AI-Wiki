## Summary
Amazon’s Alexa Plus smart home speakers have been reported to exhibit a bizarre and unsettling bug where the devices repeatedly sing or say "lalala" for minutes at a time, often interrupting ongoing user conversations. This glitch, which has been documented across multiple Echo speaker models on social media platforms like Reddit, leaves the AI assistant seemingly unaware of its own erratic behavior. Amazon has acknowledged the issue, stating that it affects a small subset of users and that a fix is currently in development.

## Key Takeaways
- **Unintended Repetitive Behavior**: Users have reported that Alexa Plus devices enter a loop of singing or saying "lalala" for extended periods, disrupting normal functionality and user interaction.
- **Lack of Self-Awareness**: When users question the device about this behavior, Alexa Plus appears completely unaware that it has been performing these actions, highlighting a disconnect between the AI's output and its internal state tracking.
- **Official Acknowledgment and Fix**: Amazon has confirmed the bug exists within the Alexa Plus ecosystem, labeling it as affecting a "small number" of users, and has assured the public that a technical solution is being actively worked on to resolve the issue.
