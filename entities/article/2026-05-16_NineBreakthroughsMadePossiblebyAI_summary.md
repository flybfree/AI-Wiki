## Summary
This article highlights how UC San Diego researchers are leveraging artificial intelligence to drive transformative breakthroughs across health, science, and public safety. By integrating advanced machine learning models with traditional scientific methods, these innovations are accelerating disease diagnosis, uncovering hidden biological mechanisms, and developing new therapeutic tools. The piece underscores the rapid evolution of AI from a theoretical concept to a practical engine for solving complex global challenges.

## Key Takeaways
- AI models have revealed that the gene PHGDH, previously considered merely a biomarker, actively disrupts gene regulation in brain cells, identifying it as a direct cause of Alzheimer’s disease.
- Researchers have developed MycoBCP, an AI-powered tool that combines deep learning with bacterial cytological profiling to detect subtle changes in tuberculosis cells, potentially fast-tracking treatments for drug-resistant strains.
- Engineers are utilizing AI to reconstruct internal cellular signals from external heart muscle recordings, enabling non-invasive monitoring of cardiac activity with unprecedented precision.
