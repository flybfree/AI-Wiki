## Summary  
The article explains why AI‑generated restaurant menus often look unappetizing and monotonous, a problem rooted in the limited “pleasing” aesthetic that generative models learn from existing corporate menus. As these models feed their own outputs back into training data, they experience convergence—similar to model collapse—that degrades output quality without outright breaking the system. The result is a feedback loop where AI‑produced images reinforce an overly uniform style, making food visuals feel alien or inauthentic.

## Key Takeaways  
- [AI models trained on narrow “pleasing” data produce visually bland, perfectly symmetrical illustrations that evoke unease because they lack genuine culinary diversity.  
- [Feedback loops cause convergence: AI‑generated menus are re‑ingested as training material, leading to a gradual decline in output quality akin to model collapse or inbreeding.  
- [The issue is not merely technical; it erodes consumer trust and creates a perception of “alien” food advertising that undermines brand authenticity.]  

## Context  
Generative AI relies on massive datasets scraped from the web, including corporate branding assets such as menu designs. Companies like Amazon have been known to ingest rare books for training before discarding them, illustrating how AI pipelines can incorporate and later discard data. When these menus—already stylized with a uniform aesthetic—are added back into the corpus, they amplify that same visual language across new generations of models.

## Implications  
For the broader field, the convergence problem signals a need for more diverse training corpora and safeguards against self‑reinforcing degradation. For the restaurant industry, reliance on AI‑generated menus risks alienating customers who value authentic, varied visual storytelling; it also opens legal and ethical questions about data provenance and brand integrity. Addressing these challenges will be essential to preserve both consumer confidence and the creative potential of generative AI.
