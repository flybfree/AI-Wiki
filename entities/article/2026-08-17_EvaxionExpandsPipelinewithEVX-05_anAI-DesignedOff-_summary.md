## Summary  

Evaxion, a biotech company specializing in AI‑driven oncology therapeutics, has announced the launch of **EVX‑05**, an off‑the‑shelf glioblastoma (GBM) vaccine that was designed entirely by artificial intelligence. The platform leverages deep learning models to predict optimal antigen combinations and delivery vectors, resulting in a ready‑to‑use therapeutic product that can be administered within weeks rather than months or years. In early clinical trials conducted at leading academic hospitals, EVX‑05 demonstrated a statistically significant increase in median survival (from 13.4 months to 22.7 months) and a reduction in tumor burden by 68 % compared with standard of care alone. The company also highlighted that the vaccine’s manufacturing process is fully automated, enabling rapid scale‑up for global distribution.

The EVX‑05 case study illustrates how AI can replace the traditional “hit‑and‑run” approach in vaccine design. By automating antigen selection, adjuvant optimization, and formulation, companies can bring novel immunotherapies to market faster—potentially shrinking the time from discovery to patient benefit from years to months. ### 2. Shift Toward “Off‑the‑Shelf” Immunotherapy  
Traditional cancer vaccines often require personalized manufacturing (e.g., neoantigen sequencing). EVX‑05’s off‑the‑shelf nature reduces cost, simplifies logistics, and lowers the barrier for hospitals to adopt immunotherapy. This model may become a template for other disease areas, such as infectious diseases or autoimmune disorders, where rapid response is critical.

Evaxion’s commitment to transparent model documentation and third‑party audits will be crucial for maintaining public trust and ensuring equitable outcomes across diverse patient populations.
