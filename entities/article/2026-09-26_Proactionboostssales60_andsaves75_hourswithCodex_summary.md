## Summary
This article details how Proaction, a software company specializing in fleet management solutions, utilized OpenAI’s Codex to significantly enhance its sales operations and engineering efficiency. By enabling non-technical staff to generate customized, interactive product demos directly from customer conversations and data, the company reported a 60% increase in deal progression rates and saved over 75 hours of engineering time annually. The case study highlights how AI-driven coding tools can bridge the gap between sales prospecting and technical implementation, allowing for rapid, personalized proof-of-concept creation without requiring dedicated engineering resources.

## Key Takeaways
- **Democratization of Technical Development:** Non-technical founders and sales staff can now create complex, interactive software demos using natural language prompts and contextual data inputs, bypassing the need to involve engineers in early-stage sales activities. This shift reduces the bottleneck where personalized demonstrations previously required significant engineering time that was often unavailable.
- **Quantifiable Business Impact:** The implementation of Codex for demo generation led to a 50% to 60% increase in deals moving from initial contact to solution development. Additionally, it saved an estimated 40 to 60 hours of engineering work per month by eliminating the need for engineers to build comparable demos manually, which would have taken approximately 10 hours each.
- **Streamlined Workflow Integration:** Codex was integrated into a broader workflow involving tools like Granola, Gmail, Slack, and HubSpot. This allowed sales personnel to pull context from calls and emails to generate specific requirements and visual references for engineers, thereby reducing back-and-forth communication and clarifying project scopes before development began.
