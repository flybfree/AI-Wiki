## Summary  
ChatGPT has launched an Apple Messages plugin that lets users have the chatbot send, edit, delete, or search through their iMessage conversations directly from within the Messages app. OpenAI emphasizes that the plugin operates locally on the user’s device and does not create a central index of all messages, though the exact data handling remains unclear. The feature also disables “persistent approval,” meaning users must manually review each outgoing message before it is dispatched.

## Key Takeaways  
- The plugin enables ChatGPT to act as an intermediary for iMessage, allowing users to automate routine text exchanges such as drafting replies or removing unwanted messages.  
- OpenAI claims the system runs entirely on‑device, avoiding a cloud‑based indexing of the user’s entire message history and thereby addressing early privacy fears about AI services harvesting personal communications.  
- Persistent approval is turned off by default; users must approve each outgoing message, preserving the final human review step before it leaves their phone.
