## Summary
Fanatics and OpenAI have partnered to launch an enhanced "Shop the Look" feature across Fanatics’ digital sports platforms, utilizing AI models to recommend coordinated merchandise for game-day outfits. By leveraging ChatGPT Work and Codex, Fanatics rapidly developed custom recommendation tools that enrich product data with team and inventory information, resulting in a more personalized shopping experience. This collaboration demonstrates how enterprise-scale AI integration can significantly improve retail engagement metrics while streamlining the development and testing of complex recommendation systems.

## Key Takeaways
- **Rapid Development Cycle:** Using OpenAI’s Codex, Fanatics and its engineering team built custom recommendation tools to enrich product data and generate outfit looks in under 24 hours for the initial 50 products, allowing for quick iteration and approval within a single week.
- **Significant Engagement Boost:** After testing the new AI-powered recommendations across approximately 30,000 MLB products, the updated "Shop the Look" feature achieved a 10% higher engagement rate compared to the previous version, demonstrating tangible business value from AI integration.
- **Enterprise-Wide AI Adoption:** Fanatics utilizes OpenAI’s ChatGPT Work and Codex across multiple divisions, including Corporate, Commerce, Collectibles, and Betting and Gaming, highlighting a comprehensive strategy for embedding AI into core operational workflows rather than isolated experiments.
