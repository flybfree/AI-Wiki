## Summary
Third Moment Research has developed a self-improving AI system that leverages OpenAI’s latest models, including Codex and the GPT-6 series, to orchestrate 10,000 concurrent agents for extended research tasks lasting 36 to 48 hours. By using AI to analyze agent behavior and automatically generate synthetic scenarios, the company identifies and resolves recurring weaknesses in agent performance, significantly reducing runtime and token waste in complex domains like quantitative finance.

## Key Takeaways
- **Self-Improving Architecture:** Third Moment Research utilizes a feedback loop where AI models analyze agent trajectories to identify bottlenecks and automatically create synthetic scenarios to train and improve agents, ensuring they become more effective over time.
- **Massive Scale and Duration:** The system successfully runs 10,000 agents simultaneously for investigations spanning 36–48 hours, demonstrating the capability of current AI infrastructure to handle long-horizon, complex computational tasks without losing coherence.
- **Optimization via Codex:** By using Codex to profile and optimize research environments, the team reduced a specific backtesting process from three hours to just 3–5 minutes, drastically cutting token usage and preventing context pollution from status-checking loops.
