## Summary  
Google is introducing a “Preferred Sources” button that lets readers flag their favorite publishers, boosting traffic for those sites amid AI‑driven search changes. The feature builds on May’s rollout of Preferred Sources for AI experiences such as AI Mode and Overviews, and it gives users a simple way to counteract the traffic decline caused by these features.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Readers can boost preferred publisher visibility by clicking a “Preferred Sources” button on their device.  
- Early data shows twice as many clicks when a source is marked favorite compared with non‑preferred sources.  
- The tool complements Google’s broader AI‑driven feed customization, letting users fine‑tune Discover and News content in real time.  

## Context  
Google has been expanding Preferred Sources into its AI experiences, integrating the option into Search, Discover, and News. This mirrors a wave of user‑controlled algorithm tweaks by social media platforms that let people adjust what is recommended to them. The broader trend reflects an industry shift toward giving users agency over personalized content pipelines.  

## Implications  
This gives publishers a direct lever to recover lost traffic while reinforcing the power dynamics between AI personalization and commercial incentives; it may reshape how AI‑driven feeds balance relevance with revenue generation for content creators.
