## Summary  
SK Telecom has launched a joint AI graduate program with Seoul National University, beginning in the second semester of 2026. The 15‑week course will be taught by current SK Telecom executives and researchers to teach students about AI applications, modeling, and infrastructure.  

## Key Takeaways  
- The program integrates industrial AI expertise directly into university curricula, moving beyond theory toward real‑world implementations.  
- It spans three thematic areas—AI apps/services, AI modeling, and AI infrastructure—to mirror SK Telecom’s full‑stack strategy.  
- The initiative marks the 10th anniversary of a partnership that has been delivering hands‑on AI education at South Korean universities.  

## Context  
The collaboration reflects a growing trend in Asia where tech firms sponsor university AI research to bridge talent gaps. By involving current executives, the program emphasizes practical knowledge transfer and aligns with national goals for expanding Korea’s AI ecosystem and securing domestic AI talent. This initiative also supports the government's strategy to reduce reliance on foreign AI expertise.  

## Implications  
This model can serve as a blueprint for other corporations seeking to embed industry experience into academic training, fostering faster innovation cycles and stronger R&D pipelines. It also signals confidence in South Korean universities’ ability to produce skilled AI professionals capable of meeting commercial demands. Moreover, such partnerships may accelerate the commercialization of university research by providing immediate market feedback.
