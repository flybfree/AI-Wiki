- Open source LLMs provide full code and training transparency, enabling secure, private deployment. This visibility allows auditors to verify compliance with regulations such as GDPR.  
- They eliminate per‑token costs and reduce reliance on external vendors, cutting operational expenses. This model also reduces the need for dedicated hardware, further lowering infrastructure budgets.  
- Community collaboration accelerates improvements and customization beyond what closed models can offer. Open contributions often lead to specialized variants tailored for niche domains like medical or legal text processing.
