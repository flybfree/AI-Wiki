## Context  
AI data centers generate highly variable electricity loads: training models can draw massive power for short periods, while inference runs at lower levels. Traditional on‑site natural‑gas generators cannot respond instantly enough to these spikes, leading to costly utility fees or generator overloads. Megapack batteries—large‑scale lithium‑ion packs capable of delivering power in seconds—are designed precisely to absorb those peaks and discharge them quickly when demand drops, thereby stabilizing the grid and reducing operational costs.
