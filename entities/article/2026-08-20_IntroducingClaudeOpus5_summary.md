## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest AI model, offering state‑of‑the‑art performance in coding and knowledge work at a fraction of the cost of its predecessor, Opus 4.8, while remaining competitive with Claude Fable 5 on many benchmarks. It excels across diverse tasks—from scientific research to visual generation—making it the most efficient model for everyday use on both Claude Max and Claude Pro tiers.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 delivers up to double the performance of Opus 4.8 at lower cost per task, especially on coding benchmarks like Frontier‑Bench v0.1 and CursorBench 3.2.  
- It outperforms other models in scientific research tasks, improving organic chemistry and protein‑sequence prediction scores by several percentage points compared with Opus 4.8.  
- The model generates high‑quality visualizations (e.g., wind‑tunnel airflow maps and interactive cell diagrams) that surpass previous offerings.

The rapid evolution of large language models has shifted focus toward cost‑effective, high‑impact solutions for developers, researchers, and enterprises. Anthropic’s Opus 5 exemplifies this trend by delivering frontier‑level capabilities without the prohibitive expense of top‑tier models such as Fable 5, thereby widening access to advanced AI tools across industries.
