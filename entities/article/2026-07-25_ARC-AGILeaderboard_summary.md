## Summary  
The ARC‑AGI leaderboard ranks AI systems on efficiency and performance across three versions (ARC‑AGI‑1, 2, 3), emphasizing cost‑per‑task as a key metric. It compares passive fluid‑intelligence models with reasoning‑enhanced LLMs and competition‑grade Kaggle submissions.

## Key Takeaways  
- Efficiency matters: the scatter plot shows that lower cost per task correlates with higher performance, indicating true intelligence includes resource optimization.  
- Reasoning vs. raw models: systems using extended reasoning (e.g., o1) outperform single‑shot base LLMs despite longer computation time.  
- Kaggle competition drives innovation: submissions are constrained to a $50 compute budget for 120 tasks, fostering efficient, purpose‑built solutions.

## Context  
The ARC‑AGI challenge is part of the broader AI research effort to benchmark general‑purpose artificial intelligence (AGI) capabilities beyond static benchmarks. It reflects industry trends toward cost‑effective, scalable models and the push for real‑time reasoning in interactive environments.

## Implications  
This leaderboard underscores that AGI progress must balance performance with computational efficiency, influencing model design, deployment strategies, and investment priorities in AI research and commercialization.
