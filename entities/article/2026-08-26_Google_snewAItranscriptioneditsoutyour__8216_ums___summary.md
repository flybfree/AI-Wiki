## Summary  
Google has rolled out a suite of Gemini Audio updates, most notably the new **Gemini 3.5 Transcribe** model, which automatically transcribes voice recordings while eliminating filler words such as “um” and “ah.”  The feature also supports up to three speakers with word‑level timestamps, adapts to custom vocabularies and specialized jargon, and works across more than 85 languages.  These updates are being delivered alongside Gemini 3.5 Live and its experimental variant, which focus on real‑time conversation handling.

## Key Takeaways  
- The new transcription model automatically removes filler words like “um” and “ah,” producing cleaner text output.  
- It can attribute speech to up to three speakers and provides precise word‑level timestamps for each speaker.  
- Users can supply a custom vocabulary, allowing the AI to preserve specialized jargon without manual editing.
