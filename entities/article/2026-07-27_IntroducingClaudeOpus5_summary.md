## Summary  
Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5, a cost‑effective state‑of‑the‑art model that significantly improves performance over its predecessor Opus 4.8 across coding and knowledge‑work benchmarks while also delivering strong visual outputs; it becomes the default model on Claude Max and is the strongest choice on Claude Pro.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 delivers near‑frontier performance in coding tasks (e.g., surpasses all models on Frontier‑Bench) at a lower cost.  
- It excels across diverse AI benchmarks, including ARC‑AGI 3, Zapier AutomationBench, OSWorld 2.0, and life‑science evaluations, showing higher scores than Opus 4.8.  
- The model is the default for Claude Max and strongest on Claude Pro, offering a balance of intelligence and cost efficiency.
