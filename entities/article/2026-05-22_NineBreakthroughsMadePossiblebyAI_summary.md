## Summary
This article highlights how UC San Diego researchers are leveraging artificial intelligence to drive transformative breakthroughs across healthcare, specifically in understanding disease mechanisms and developing new treatments. By utilizing advanced AI models to analyze complex biological data, scientists have uncovered hidden causes of Alzheimer’s disease and created novel tools to combat drug-resistant tuberculosis. These innovations demonstrate the rapid acceleration of medical science through the integration of deep learning and biological profiling.

## Key Takeaways
- **Alzheimer’s Disease Mechanism Uncovered**: UC San Diego bioengineers used AI to model 3D protein structures, revealing that the gene PHGDH has a previously unknown "moonlighting" role that disrupts gene switching in brain cells, directly contributing to Alzheimer’s pathology rather than merely serving as a biomarker.
- **AI-Driven Tuberculosis Treatment Development**: Researchers developed MycoBCP, an AI-powered tool that combines bacterial cytological profiling with deep learning to detect subtle changes in TB cells. This technology allows for the rapid identification of how potential drugs interact with the pathogen, significantly accelerating the search for effective therapies against drug-resistant strains.
- **Non-Invasive Cardiac Monitoring**: Engineers have successfully utilized AI to reconstruct internal cellular signals from external recordings of heart muscle cells. This breakthrough enables the monitoring of intracellular heart activity without invasive procedures, offering a safer and more detailed view of cardiac function.
