## Summary  
OpenAI Academy, launched in September 2024, has expanded access to practical AI training by hosting over 250 events and engaging more than 4 million people worldwide. The program now includes self-paced courses, workshops, and large-scale events like AI Skills Jams, with a new focus on empowering communities through hands-on learning and peer collaboration.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI Academy has reached over 4 million learners across diverse fields such as education, small business, and software development.  
- The program emphasizes practical, real-world applications of AI tools like ChatGPT, Codex, and custom workflows tailored to individual roles.  
- A new Community Trainer Program is being piloted to train local organizations to deliver ongoing, community-based AI education.

## Context  
The initiative aligns with broader efforts in the AI industry to democratize access to artificial intelligence by moving beyond technical training toward practical skill-building and inclusive learning ecosystems. It reflects a shift from one-way knowledge transfer to collaborative, community-driven models that integrate AI into everyday professional and personal tasks.
