## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s newest large‑language model that delivers performance on par with Claude Fable 5 while costing roughly half as much, and it serves as the default for Claude Max and the strongest option for Claude Pro. The article highlights how Opus 5 excels across coding benchmarks (Frontier‑Bench, CursorBench), scientific research tasks (organic chemistry, protein prediction), knowledge‑work evaluations (ARC‑AGI 3, Zapier AutomationBench), and visual generation, all while maintaining a low token cost.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 provides state‑of‑the‑art coding performance at a lower price than its predecessor, surpassing all other models on Frontier‑Bench v0.1 and achieving near‑Fable 5 scores on CursorBench 3.2 with half the cost per task.  
- The model makes significant gains in scientific reasoning, outscoring Opus 4.8 by double‑digit percentage points on organic chemistry and protein‑sequence prediction tasks across life‑science evaluations such as ARC‑AGI 3, GDPval‑AA v2, OSWorld 2.0, HLE, AutomationBench, DeepSearchQA, and more.  
- Opus 5 also excels in visual output generation, producing detailed wind‑tunnel simulations and interactive cell illustrations that rival the best of its class.
