## Summary  
The article introduces “Did They Ghost You?”, a public ledger that records when companies stop responding to candidates after an interview without providing a clear rejection. It highlights both the prevalence of such silence—564 approved reports so far—and the recognition of firms that communicate transparently throughout the hiring process.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Anonymous moderated reporting:** The platform never asks for candidate or interviewer names, and each report is vetted by a moderator before it becomes part of the public record.  
- **Leaderboard impact limited to approved entries:** Only moderator‑approved submissions affect company totals; unverified reports are excluded.  
- **Definition of ghosting:** A “ghost” occurs when a hiring team ceases communication after an interview and fails to give a definitive answer, not merely an unanswered application.

## Context  
In the broader AI and machine‑learning industry, recruitment practices often rely on opaque algorithms or manual reviews that can leave candidates in limbo. This article’s focus on human‑centric hiring signals a shift toward more transparent, data‑driven accountability mechanisms—an area where AI tools are increasingly used to streamline interviews but also risk creating silent failures if not properly monitored.
