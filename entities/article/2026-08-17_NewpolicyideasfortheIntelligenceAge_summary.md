## Summary  
OpenAI is launching a $1 million grant program that funds 14 independent research projects across the United States, European Union, Brazil, Singapore and South Korea to explore how artificial intelligence can expand economic opportunity and strengthen societal resilience. The initiative builds on OpenAI’s earlier “Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age” call, emphasizing that policy must be ambitious, inclusive, and shaped through democratic institutions rather than corporate decisions alone.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI is providing direct funding to non‑profit and academic organizations to turn high‑level AI policy ideas into actionable research.  
- The selected projects address both the economic impact of AI on jobs and the need for societies to adapt as capabilities grow.  
- Success will depend on collaborative, democratically driven policy development that challenges existing assumptions.

## Context  
Artificial intelligence is rapidly becoming ubiquitous—ChatGPT alone is used by over a billion people worldwide across diverse demographics. While broad access expands opportunity, it also raises questions about distribution of gains and the resilience of social systems. Existing policies often focus on incremental adjustments rather than bold, forward‑looking strategies that could harness AI’s potential for inclusive growth.

## Implications  
This funding model sets a precedent for public‑private partnerships in AI governance, encouraging other labs to allocate resources to independent research. It may influence future regulatory frameworks by demonstrating how experimental policy solutions can be tested and scaled responsibly, ultimately shaping the trajectory of AI development toward broader societal benefit.
