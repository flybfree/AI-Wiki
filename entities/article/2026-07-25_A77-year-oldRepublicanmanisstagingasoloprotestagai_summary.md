## Context  
Flock cameras, a private‑sector system launched in St. Petersburg in 2022, employ computer‑vision AI to capture continuous video, extract license‑plate details, and populate a searchable archive that can be queried across the country’s 100,000+ units. The technology enables rapid identification of suspects, but it also creates a permanent digital footprint for every vehicle passing by, raising questions about consent and data ownership.
