## Summary
Amplitude, a product analytics platform, has significantly accelerated its product development cycle by integrating OpenAI’s advanced models into its proactive product agent, Wave. This integration allows teams to identify improvement opportunities and ship code changes in just 1–2 days, compared to the previous timeline of approximately one month. The article highlights how Amplitude leverages specific OpenAI models for distinct tasks, such as deep investigation, low-cost scanning, and visual mockup generation, to streamline the workflow from data insight to implementation.

## Key Takeaways
- **Drastic Reduction in Development Time:** By using Wave to automate the identification of product opportunities and generate technical plans, Amplitude reduced the time required to ship product improvements from roughly a month to 1–2 days. This speed is achieved by seamlessly handing off prioritized tasks to coding agents like Codex with clear acceptance criteria.
- **Specialized Model Utilization:** Amplitude does not rely on a single model but strategically deploys different OpenAI models for specific functions. GPT‑6.1 Sol handles complex opportunity management and deep code investigations, GPT‑6 Luna performs frequent, lower-cost tasks like code scanning, and GPT‑Image‑2.5 Flare generates visual mockups based on session replays, optimizing both cost and performance.
- **Proven Impact on User Engagement:** A real-world test on Amplitude’s homepage demonstrated the effectiveness of this approach. Wave analyzed session replays to discover that users ignored generic suggestion cards. By proposing personalized suggestions based on user context and implementing them via coding agents, Amplitude achieved a 160% lift in card-driven sends, validating the agent's ability to drive measurable business outcomes.
