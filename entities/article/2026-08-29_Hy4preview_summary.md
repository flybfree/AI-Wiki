## Summary  
Tencent has released Hy4 preview, an open‑source large language model with 770 B total parameters (49 B active) and a context window exceeding one million tokens, designed to boost productivity across coding, office work, scientific research, and game development. The model is accessible via WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, Tencent Cloud TokenHub, OpenRouter APIs, and offers free two‑week access with extended Hy3 support until September 30.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Hy4 preview scores higher than GLM‑5.3 (2.92/4.0) and Kimi K3 (2.94/4.0) in an internal blind evaluation of 163 experts and 203 engineering tasks, reaching an average of 2.99/4.0.  
- It supports long‑context tasks up to a million tokens, enabling complex coding, multi‑document workflows, and seamless integration with game engines for prototype generation.  
- The model is co‑designed with Tencent experts across software engineering, finance, gaming, and scientific domains, using high‑quality training data that drives real‑world performance gains.  

## Context  
Hy4 preview exemplifies the industry shift toward open‑source LLMs that prioritize practical productivity over isolated benchmark scores. Its multi‑modal capabilities—spanning coding, office tasks, game development, and scientific research—reflect a broader trend of embedding AI assistants into enterprise and creative tools to deliver unified workflows.  

## Implications  
This model shows that open‑sourced LLMs can rival proprietary systems in specialized productivity domains, encouraging other companies to adopt cost‑effective open models for deployment. It also underscores the value of co‑design with domain experts to improve training data relevance—a practice likely to become standard as AI products evolve toward real‑world integration.
