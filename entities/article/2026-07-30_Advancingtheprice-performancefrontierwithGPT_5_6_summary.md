## Summary  
OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6 family now offers cheaper, faster options—Luna at 80 % lower cost, Terra at 20 % less, and Sol paired with Fast mode that delivers up to 2.5× speed for twice the price—while preserving high performance. These updates aim to make advanced AI more abundant and affordable, letting businesses obtain frontier‑level intelligence without a steep expense.

## Key Takeaways  
- Luna’s price is cut by 80 %, delivering near‑frontier quality at roughly six cents per task and nine times the speed of earlier models.  
- Fast mode provides up to 2.5× faster processing for Sol, priced at twice the Standard rate with no loss in intelligence.  
- The new pricing reflects years of efficiency gains across model architecture, inference systems, and the agentic harness that connects them to tools.

## Context  
OpenAI’s mission is to ensure artificial general intelligence benefits all humanity; these price‑performance improvements embody a broader industry shift toward making large‑scale AI more accessible. By lowering per‑task costs, the company helps democratize access to powerful models for developers and enterprises worldwide.

## Implications  
For developers and organizations, the reduced cost per task enables higher volume usage, encourages experimentation with complex workflows, and lowers barriers to deploying multi‑step AI tasks such as coding, testing, and tool integration. This trend accelerates innovation across sectors that rely on scalable, high‑quality AI solutions.
