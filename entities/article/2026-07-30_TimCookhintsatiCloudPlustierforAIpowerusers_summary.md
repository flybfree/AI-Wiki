## Summary  
Apple’s CEO Tim Cook hinted that the iCloud Plus subscription could be expanded to include higher‑capacity tiers for users who want to rely heavily on Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, allowing customers to “buy up the stack” of AI services. The upcoming launch of Siri AI with iOS 27 will introduce daily usage limits tied to server models, but Cook suggested that additional upgrade options may be offered this fall.

## Key Takeaways  
- Apple is considering tiered pricing within iCloud Plus to accommodate heavy AI users.  
- Tim Cook said upgrades will let customers purchase more of the “stack” of AI features.  
- The new Siri AI in iOS 27 currently has daily usage caps, and expanded access may be tied to higher‑priced plans.

## Context  
Apple’s services business generated $30.74 billion in revenue recently, a figure that includes subscriptions such as iCloud Plus, Apple One, and Apple TV. AI features like image generation are limited because they depend on powerful cloud models, prompting the company to consider ways to increase access without overburdening its servers.

## Implications  
Introducing paid tiers for AI usage could accelerate adoption of Apple’s AI stack but may also create a two‑tier market where power users pay more while casual users stay on basic plans. This shift raises questions about fairness, sustainability of server costs, and the broader competitive landscape of subscription‑based AI services.
