## Summary  
Thinking Machines argues that releasing powerful AI models safely requires balancing model‑level safety testing and ecosystem readiness, rather than opening weights indiscriminately. The authors propose a staged, evidence‑driven approach where safety is continuously evaluated and the community’s defenses are strengthened before each release. This path aims to preserve both the benefits of open‑weight collaboration and the mitigation of misuse risks.

## Key Takeaways  
- Robust safety testing must be a mandatory prerequisite for any model release, probing harmful capabilities and accessibility.  
- Ecosystem readiness—including staged releases, defender support, and researcher collaboration—is essential to manage real‑world misuse potential.  
- Open‑weight models can democratize AI development but also amplify offensive cybersecurity and dual‑use risks; the net effect depends on who controls the technology.
