# Summary: New LLM Releases April 2026: Every Major Model Launch This Month - Fazm Blog (2026-06-11)

**Source**: [Original Article](https://fazm.ai/blog/new-llm-releases-april-2026)

## Summary

This roundup tracks the major LLM launches from April 2026 and frames them around the practical question that matters most: which model is worth using for a real workload. The month’s release wave was split between two frontier closed-model updates—GPT-5.5/Spud from OpenAI and Claude Opus 4.7 from Anthropic—and a crowded open-weight field led by Gemma 4, GLM-5.1, Qwen 3.6-Plus, Llama 4, and Arcee Trinity. The article emphasizes that the big differentiators were no longer just raw benchmark scores, but context length, token price, agent/tool reliability, and whether a model could actually be run locally or under permissive licensing.

## Key Takeaways
- GPT-5.5 shipped late as a renamed GPT-5.5/Spud family, with 1M context, stronger long-context recall, and a sharp price increase that makes it a premium choice.
- Claude Opus 4.7 delivered a coding-focused jump at unchanged pricing, making it the steadier default for agentic coding and tool-heavy workflows.
- Gemma 4, GLM-5.1, Qwen 3.6-Plus, Llama 4, and Arcee Trinity show how quickly open-weight options are closing the gap on capability, context, and deployment flexibility.
- Context windows are now a major product differentiator: 200K, 1M, and even 10M-token windows matter as much as benchmark deltas for codebase-scale tasks.
- The article’s practical recommendation is to choose by workload: GPT-5.5 for long-context frontier work, Claude Opus 4.7 for steadier coding agents, and the open-weight models when licensing, local deployment, or cost matter more than a single top score.
