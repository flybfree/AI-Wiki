## Summary  
The article introduces SymptomAI, a conversational AI agent designed to conduct everyday symptom assessments and differential diagnosis at scale. A randomized national study of 13,917 participants compared the AI’s diagnoses with those of clinicians and wearable biosignals, showing that the system can generate clinically relevant predictions in real‑world patient conversations.

## Key Takeaways  
- SymptomAI can generate differential diagnoses comparable to human clinicians when evaluating natural, unstructured patient interviews.  
- The AI’s predictions align with physiological trends (e.g., immune response signals) that precede infectious disease diagnoses, indicating integration with biometric data.  
- Current large‑scale evaluations of medical LLMs rely on synthetic vignettes; SymptomAI demonstrates performance in real everyday interactions.

## Context  
The broader context is the growing interest in AI for clinical decision support, where language models are being tested against real‑world patient interviews. However, most benchmarks use curated cases that lack variability and incomplete information typical of everyday patients.

## Implications  
This research suggests conversational AI could become a viable tool for early symptom triage, especially when combined with wearable data to improve accuracy and accessibility, potentially reducing barriers to timely medical care.
