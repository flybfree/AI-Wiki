## Summary  
OpenAI Academy, launched in September 2024, has delivered two years of practical AI training to over 4 million people across diverse communities through workshops, courses, and events. The program emphasizes hands-on learning using tools like ChatGPT and Codex to solve real-world problems, with a new focus on empowering community members to teach others via the Community Trainer Program.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI Academy has reached 4 million learners across 250+ events, demonstrating broad accessibility and impact.  
- The program’s success lies in providing dedicated time for participants to apply AI tools to meaningful tasks, fostering peer learning and mentor support.  
- A new Community Trainer Program is being piloted to train local organizations to deliver Academy content, enhancing grassroots capacity.

## Context  
OpenAI Academy operates within a growing ecosystem of AI education initiatives aimed at democratizing access to artificial intelligence. As AI tools become embedded in daily workflows—from education to small business operations—the need for accessible, practical training has increased. This program aligns with broader trends toward community-driven learning and localized AI adoption.

## Implications  
The expansion of OpenAI Academy signals a shift from one-way instruction to collaborative knowledge sharing, reinforcing the importance of human-centered AI implementation. By enabling communities to lead their own training efforts, it strengthens resilience in local economies and education systems, positioning practical skill-building as central to sustainable AI integration.
