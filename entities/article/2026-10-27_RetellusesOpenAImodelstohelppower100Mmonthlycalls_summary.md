## Summary
Retell AI, a voice AI platform for contact centers, has scaled to handle over 100 million monthly calls by leveraging OpenAI’s advanced models, including GPT-6 Astra and Codex. The company reports significant operational efficiency, achieving an annual revenue run rate of $1.6 million per employee and resolving more than 80% of customer inquiries autonomously. This partnership highlights the critical role of high-performance language models in powering large-scale, real-time enterprise voice automation systems.

## Key Takeaways
- **Massive Scale and Adoption:** Retell AI has grown from handling 30 million calls in early 2026 to over 100 million monthly calls by September, serving more than 4,000 businesses. This growth is supported by the consumption of over 1 trillion OpenAI tokens, demonstrating the substantial computational resources required for enterprise-grade voice AI.
- **Superior Model Performance:** In Retell’s proprietary vCX benchmark, which tests models on real-world, multi-turn production scenarios, GPT-6 Astra ranked first in instruction following with a score of 89.7%. Previous evaluations also showed GPT-5.5 leading in non-hallucination rates and tool-call correctness, outperforming competitors like Gemini-3.1-Pro and Claude-4.6.
- **Operational Efficiency via Codex:** Retell’s engineering team utilizes OpenAI’s Codex for development tasks such as debugging, feature building, and code research. This integration of AI coding assistants into the development workflow contributes to a highly efficient operational model, evidenced by the $1.6 million annual revenue run rate per employee.
