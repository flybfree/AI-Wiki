Dubbed "PHP2Shell" by security firm Wiz, these flaws affect PHP versions dating back nearly 20 years, causing widespread outages for major platforms like Wikipedia, WordPress, and the Internet Archive. The situation is exacerbated by the fact that many older, unmaintained websites cannot be patched, leading to permanent shutdowns and a potential loss of historical digital content.
