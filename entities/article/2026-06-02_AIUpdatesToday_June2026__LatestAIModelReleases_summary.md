## Context  
The AI industry is characterized by rapid iteration, where each week brings new model versions that compete on benchmark scores measured via the sigma‑normalized Quality Index. This metric reflects how far a model’s performance deviates from its baseline over a 30‑day period, providing a standardized way to compare models across arenas and track progress.

Consequently, organizations must balance performance expectations with cost, customization needs, and ethical considerations when selecting AI solutions.
