## Summary  
Anthropic has unveiled Claude Opus 5, a model that is reported to be “close” to the capabilities of its earlier Claude Fable 5 while offering stronger cybersecurity safeguards and a price point that matches its predecessor Opus 4.8 ($5 input / $25 output). The release positions Opus 5 as an enterprise‑focused alternative, cheaper than both Fable 5 and OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6, and includes features such as “Fast mode” for higher throughput and automatic fallback to lower‑tier models when safeguards trigger.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 matches Claude Fable 5 in many domains and excels at complex coding tasks.  
- Anthropic has added enhanced cybersecurity measures after government scrutiny, making it the most aligned Opus model and least susceptible to misuse.  
- Pricing remains $5 per 1,000 input tokens and $25 per 1,000 output tokens, identical to Opus 4.8, cheaper than Fable 5 and GPT‑5.6.
