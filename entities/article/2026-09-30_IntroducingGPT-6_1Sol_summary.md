## Summary
The article introduces GPT-6.1 Sol, an upgraded version of the GPT-6 Sol model designed to deliver intelligence comparable to the high-end GPT-6 Astra model for agentic coding, computer use, and professional tasks at approximately one-fifth of Astra's standard pricing. By significantly reducing input token costs, particularly through cached inputs priced at $0.10 per million tokens, the model aims to provide developers with a cost-effective solution for building capable agents that reuse context across multiple requests. The text claims substantial performance improvements over its predecessor, GPT-6 Sol, across various benchmarks including software engineering and business workflow automation.

## Key Takeaways
- **Cost-Efficiency:** GPT-6.1 Sol offers near-Astra level intelligence at one-fifth of Astra’s standard token prices, with cached input costs reduced by 95% compared to standard pricing and 50% lower than the previous GPT-6 Sol cached rates.
- **Performance Benchmarks:** The model matches GPT-6 Astra on complex software engineering tasks (DeepSWE v1.1) while outperforming its predecessor, GPT-6 Sol, by 6.4 percentage points at a lower reasoning effort and cost.
- **Professional Workflow Capabilities:** In evaluations like GDP.pdf and AutomationBench, GPT-6.1 Sol scores higher than competitors such as Opus 5.5 and approaches Astra’s state-of-the-art performance at significantly reduced costs per task, demonstrating strong capabilities in handling complex documents and multi-step business workflows.

## Context
This release highlights a strategic shift in the AI industry toward optimizing the balance between high-level reasoning capability and operational cost. By targeting "everyday work" tasks such as debugging code, understanding professional documents, and executing business workflows, GPT-6.1 Sol positions itself as a practical tool for enterprise applications where budget constraints are critical. The emphasis on cached input pricing suggests a focus on long-running agent sessions that require maintaining context over multiple interactions, addressing a common bottleneck in agentic AI deployment.
