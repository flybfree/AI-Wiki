## Summary
OpenAI has released ChatGPT Images 2.5, a significant update to its image generation capabilities that introduces a new feature called "Sketch." This tool allows users to create images by drawing rough doodles directly within the chat interface and providing text instructions for refinement. The update also claims improvements in lighting realism, texture richness, and processing speed compared to previous versions.

## Key Takeaways
- **Introduction of Sketch Feature:** Users can now activate a drawing window by typing `@Sketch` into the ChatGPT prompt box. This allows for the creation of images based on user-drawn doodles, which AI then interprets and renders into detailed visuals based on accompanying text instructions.
- **Enhanced Editing Capabilities:** The update supports direct comments on specific parts of generated images to request targeted changes, such as altering eye color or other details, improving the iterative editing process across multiple turns.
- **Performance and Quality Improvements:** OpenAI states that ChatGPT Images 2.5 offers "more natural lighting and richer textures" and reduces image generation latency by up to 50% compared to the previous Images 2.0 model. The feature is available for ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex users across desktop, mobile, and web platforms.

By integrating drawing tools directly into the chat workflow, OpenAI is lowering the barrier to entry for users who may struggle to articulate visual concepts through text alone. The release date of September 8, 2026, places this update in a competitive landscape where AI providers are increasingly focusing on intuitive, hybrid input methods to enhance user control and output fidelity.
