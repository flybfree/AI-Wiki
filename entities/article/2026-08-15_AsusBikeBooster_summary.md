## Summary  
The Oxiis Intelligent Bike Booster is a universal friction‑drive e‑bike system that adds 500 W peak power, wireless cadence sensing and a smart brake‑detecting taillight to any conventional bicycle. It transforms ordinary bikes into smart e‑bikes with instant assistance for steep climbs while keeping the installation wire‑free and unmodified.

- Delivers 500 W peak power with instant burst for steep climbs.  
- Features wireless cadence sensor and smart brake‑detecting taillight for safety.
