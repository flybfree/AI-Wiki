## Summary  
Spotify has launched a new feature called “Taste Profile” in the U.S., allowing Premium users to view and adjust how its AI understands their music, podcast, and audiobook preferences through natural language commands. This tool essentially gives listeners control over their personalized recommendations across Spotify’s Home feed, Discover Weekly, Made for You, and even annual Wrapped summaries.

## Key Takeaways  
- [Taste Profile lets users fine-tune AI-driven recommendations using simple text prompts to increase variety or reduce specific genres.]  
- [The feature addresses a common pain point: repetitive or unwanted suggestions by enabling direct edits to the algorithm’s input data.]  
- [It expands beyond track and playlist exclusions by allowing broad, natural-language adjustments across music, podcasts, and audiobooks.]

## Context  
Taste Profile is an evolution of Spotify’s long-standing use of AI for personalization, first introduced at SXSW in 2026. While the algorithm has been central to user engagement, it often fails to adapt as tastes evolve. The beta version previously limited users to New Zealand; now it targets U.S. listeners aged 18+, marking a major scale-up of this AI-driven feature.

## Implications  
This development signals a shift toward more transparent and user-controlled AI systems in entertainment platforms. By letting users shape their experience, Spotify reduces friction between human preference and algorithmic output, potentially increasing satisfaction and retention. For the broader AI field, it demonstrates how natural language interfaces can make complex recommendation models more accessible and responsive to individual needs.
