## Summary  
The article argues that releasing powerful open‑weight AI models is a double‑edged sword; while openness democratizes development and enables safety research, it also creates irreversible misuse risks such as cyber attacks. To move toward safe openness, the authors propose a staged release process that couples rigorous safety testing with ecosystem readiness. By iteratively choosing the most open option the evidence supports while building up the ecosystem's resilience, we can move toward openness safely.

## Key Takeaways  
- Open‑weight models can accelerate both beneficial AI progress and harmful cyber‑attack capabilities.  
- Safe release requires rigorous safety testing to assess dangerous capabilities before public distribution.  
- A phased, collaborative rollout builds ecosystem resilience alongside model openness.
