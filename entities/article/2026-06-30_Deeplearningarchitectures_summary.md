## Summary  
The article surveys the principal deep‑learning neural architectures—RNNs, LSTM/GRU networks, CNNs, DBNs, and DSN—and highlights how modern frameworks streamline their implementation. ## Key Takeaways  
- RNNs are designed for sequential data but suffer from vanishing gradients; LSTM/GRU variants mitigate this issue.  
- CNNs dominate image‑ and video‑processing tasks due to their hierarchical feature extraction capabilities.  
- Frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Keras accelerate development by providing pre‑built modules for all listed architectures.
