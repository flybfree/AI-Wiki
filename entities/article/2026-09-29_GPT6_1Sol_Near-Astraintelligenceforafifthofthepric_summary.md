## Summary
The article introduces GPT-6.1 Sol, a new model positioned as an upgrade to GPT-6 Sol that offers near-equivalent intelligence to the high-end GPT-6 Astra for agentic coding and professional tasks at approximately one-fifth of Astra’s cost. It highlights significant improvements in benchmark performance across software engineering, document analysis, and computer use workflows while drastically reducing input token costs through aggressive caching discounts. The model aims to provide developers with a more cost-effective option for building capable agents that require extensive context reuse.

## Key Takeaways
- GPT-6.1 Sol achieves performance on agentic coding benchmarks like DeepSWE v1.1 that matches GPT-6 Astra at roughly one-fifth of the cost, surpassing its predecessor GPT-6 Sol by 6.4 percentage points.
- Cached input pricing is set at $0.10 per million tokens, representing a 95% reduction compared to standard input pricing and a 50% decrease relative to GPT-6 Sol’s cached rates, facilitating cheaper context reuse for agents.
- In professional workflows measured by GDP.pdf and AutomationBench, the model outperforms competitors like Opus 5.5 in specific metrics while maintaining costs significantly lower than Astra, demonstrating strong capabilities in multi-step business tasks and complex document understanding.

## Context
This release reflects a strategic shift in the AI industry toward optimizing the price-performance ratio for agentic systems rather than solely pursuing maximum raw intelligence. By targeting "everyday work" such as debugging code, analyzing PDFs, and executing business workflows, OpenAI is addressing the practical needs of developers building autonomous agents. The emphasis on cached input pricing suggests a focus on long-running agent sessions where context retention is critical for efficiency. This move competes directly with other high-performance models like Opus 5.5 by offering comparable utility at a fraction of the operational cost, potentially lowering barriers to entry for deploying sophisticated AI agents in enterprise settings.
