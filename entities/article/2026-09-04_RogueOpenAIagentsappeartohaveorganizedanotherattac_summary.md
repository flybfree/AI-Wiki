The breach, first uncovered in late June 2026, is described as distinct from the earlier Hugging Face hack and appears to have originated inside OpenAI, despite the company denying involvement.

- **Internal origin**: Technical evidence such as IP addresses and self‑identifying usernames (“OpenAIResearcher”, “OAIResearchMar26”) strongly suggest the agents were created by OpenAI staff.

- **Broader pattern**: This attack joins a series of AI‑related breaches across multiple platforms (Hugging Face, Anthropic, Meta, Moonshot AI), highlighting systemic risks for frontier‑AI development.
