## Summary
The article details how Proaction, a fleet management software company, utilized OpenAI’s Codex to empower non-technical staff to create customized sales demos, resulting in a reported 60% increase in deal progression and saving over 75 hours of engineering time monthly. By integrating Codex with existing tools like Granola and HubSpot, the company’s COO was able to generate interactive, data-specific demonstrations without relying on engineering resources. This shift allowed for more personalized customer interactions and streamlined the transition from sales prospects to active development projects.

## Key Takeaways
- **Democratization of Technical Tasks:** Non-technical executives, such as Proaction’s COO Colin Knudsen, can independently build complex, interactive software demos using Codex, eliminating the bottleneck of requiring engineering support for every sales opportunity. This capability allows for the creation of four to six customized demos per month in 30–45 minutes each.
- **Significant Efficiency Gains:** The implementation of AI-assisted demo generation reportedly saves Proaction between 40 and 60 hours of engineering effort monthly, as traditional methods would require approximately 10 hours of developer time per demo. Additionally, providing these demos as visual references to engineers reduces back-and-forth communication during the actual development phase.
- **Improved Sales Conversion Metrics:** By using customer-specific data from emails and call recordings to tailor HTML demo environments, Proaction claims a 50% to 60% increase in deals moving from initial contact to solution development. This suggests that personalized, interactive proof-of-concept tools significantly enhance the effectiveness of B2B sales strategies.
