## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest AI model, offering state‑of‑the‑art performance on coding and knowledge tasks at a lower price than its predecessor, while still lagging only slightly behind the flagship Claude Fable 5. It is positioned as the most cost‑effective option for daily use, serving as the default on Claude Max and the strongest choice on Claude Pro.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 delivers up to double the performance of Opus 4.8 on coding benchmarks such as Frontier‑Bench at a fraction of the cost.  
- It excels in knowledge‑work evaluations (ARC‑AGI, OSWorld) with scores up to three times higher than the next best model and passes more tasks even at low effort settings.  
- The model improves scientific research tasks, especially organic chemistry and protein prediction, by several percentage points compared with Opus 4.8.  

## Context  
The release occurs amid a competitive AI landscape where companies are releasing increasingly specialized models for coding, cybersecurity, and multimodal tasks. Anthropic’s focus on cost‑effective high performance reflects broader industry trends toward democratizing advanced AI through pricing strategies that balance capability with affordability.
