## Summary  
France announced a ban on unsolicited telemarketing calls effective August 11, 2026, requiring telecom operators to block such calls and penalize violations. The measure aims to protect consumers from intrusive marketing attempts and align with EU consumer‑protection standards.  

## Key Takeaways  
- [The law mandates that all unsolicited telemarketing calls be blocked at the network level by August 11, 2026.]  
- [Violations will incur fines proportional to the number of affected calls and the duration of non‑compliance.]  
- [Consumers can report illegal calls through a dedicated portal, which may trigger automated detection systems.]
