## Summary  
Amazon Prime Video has introduced an AI‑driven lip‑sync feature that aligns actors’ mouth movements with “human‑dubbed” English audio, currently limited to the German series *Maxton Hall* and its English dub. The technology combines AI processing with visual effects to create seamless lip‑sync for dubbed dialogue, a capability Prime Video plans to roll out to additional titles later this year.

## Key Takeaways  
- The feature is only available with the English dub of *Maxton Hall*, but will expand to more content in the future.  
- It uses a blend of AI and visual‑effects technology to match lip movements to translated speech, similar to Meta’s auto‑dubbing tool for creators.  
- Prime Video has already experimented with AI‑aided dubbing on 12 movies and TV shows since last year.

The rapid growth of AI‑powered dubbing reflects a broader industry shift toward automated content localization. Companies like YouTube, Meta, and Netflix are deploying machine learning models to generate subtitles and lip‑sync automatically, reducing production costs and enabling multilingual distribution at scale. This trend is driven by demand for personalized streaming experiences across diverse audiences.
