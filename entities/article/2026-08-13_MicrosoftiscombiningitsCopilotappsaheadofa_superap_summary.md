## Summary  
Microsoft is consolidating its Copilot apps into a single unified “super app” interface, merging the personal and work versions to eliminate duplicate icons and improve user experience. The new design will replace the two separate Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot icons across Windows, Mac, mobile, and web platforms.

## Key Takeaways  
- The new unified Copilot app will replace the two separate Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot icons on Windows, Mac, mobile, and web.  
- Existing chats and content from both account types remain separate but are merged into one interface starting mid‑August for mobile/web and mid‑September for desktop.  
- Features like Podcasts, Deep Research, Group Chat, and some shopping functions will be retired or temporarily unavailable during the rollout.

## Context  
This move reflects a broader trend in AI product integration where companies aim to deliver seamless, cross‑device experiences by consolidating multiple AI assistants into one unified platform. It also aligns with Microsoft’s push to embed generative AI within its productivity suite and cloud services, aiming to streamline user workflows.

## Implications  
The consolidation could simplify user adoption of Copilot while reducing UI friction, but it may also limit access to niche features for power users. For the industry, it signals a shift toward integrated AI experiences that prioritize continuity over feature diversity, potentially influencing how other vendors design their AI assistants and their impact on user engagement.
