## Summary
The San Francisco Onion Futures Company provides a platform for purchasing and trading private, transferable contracts for the future physical delivery of yellow onions. The service operates as a direct-to-consumer model where contracts are sold up to six months in advance, with pricing determined by a proprietary algorithm updated daily.

## Key Takeaways
- **Contract Structure:** The company offers private contracts rather than an open exchange; they specifically note that they do not operate as a "board of trade" to navigate specific legal interpretations regarding onion futures.
- **Logistics and Delivery:** Delivery is initiated during the first week of the contract's designated month, with primary delivery zones including San Francisco, Toronto, Seattle, and Chicago.
- **Transferability and Security:** Contracts are transferable via unique keys; providing a key to another party allows them to update ownership information, while the system ensures that previous owners cannot revert the changes or view private delivery data.
- **Payment and Pricing:** The platform accepts payments through Stripe, and prices fluctuate between 3 and 12 USD per onion depending on the delivery month and current market conditions.
