## Summary  
The article introduces the Science One Framework, a verifiable autonomous research system that eliminates hallucinations by constructing chain‑of‑evidence (CoE) proofs for every claim in AI‑generated scientific papers. By pairing CoE with an automated audit tool called CoE Audit, the framework guarantees both completeness and correctness of evidence, achieving zero phantom references while matching state‑of‑the‑art performance on benchmark tasks.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Verifiable evidence chains replace subjective quality judgments**, ensuring that every reference or result can be traced back to its source.  
- **Zero hallucinated citations** are possible because the system records actual code executions and peer‑reviewed papers alongside claims.  
- **Performance remains high**: the framework matches top benchmarks (MLE‑Bench, Parameter‑Golf) despite its strict verifiability constraints.

## Context  
Current autonomous research agents generate manuscripts iteratively, leading to errors that propagate through the pipeline—phantom citations, mismatched methods, and non‑reproducible results. These issues undermine trust in AI‑produced science and limit adoption of fully automated research workflows.

## Implications  
The Science One Framework demonstrates that rigorous verification can coexist with cutting‑edge problem solving, paving the way for reliable AI‑driven scientific discovery and fostering confidence among researchers, publishers, and industry stakeholders.
