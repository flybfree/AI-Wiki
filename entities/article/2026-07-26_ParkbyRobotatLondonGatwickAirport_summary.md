## Summary  
London Gatwick Airport has launched robotic parking, a first in the UK, allowing passengers to drop off their cars in secure cabins near the South Terminal without handing over keys. The autonomous robots lift and store vehicles using Stanley Robotics technology, enabling efficient use of limited parking space while enhancing passenger experience.

## Key Takeaways  
- Robotic parking allows passengers to keep their keys, eliminating the need for valet services or key handover.  
- The system maximizes parking capacity by storing cars in tight spaces without requiring access, improving airport infrastructure efficiency.  
- The service is integrated with flight schedules, ensuring vehicles are collected automatically upon passenger return.
