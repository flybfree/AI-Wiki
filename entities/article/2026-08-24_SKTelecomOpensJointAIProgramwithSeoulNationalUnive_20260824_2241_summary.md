## Summary  
SK Telecom has launched a 15‑week graduate AI program in partnership with Seoul National University, featuring CEO Jung Jae‑heon and senior executives Yoo Kyung‑sang and Kim Tae‑yoon teaching a course that covers AI applications/services, modeling, and infrastructure. The initiative aims to embed industrial AI expertise into university education and marks the 10th anniversary of their collaboration.

## Key Takeaways  
- The program is a 15‑week graduate course in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering starting next month.  
- It emphasizes practical AI technologies—apps/services, modeling, infrastructure—using real SK Telecom R&D examples rather than theory.  
- Teaching includes Jung Jae‑heon delivering an opening lecture on “Choices and Opportunities in the AI Era” followed by senior executives leading subsequent sessions.

## Context  
This reflects a broader South Korean trend of industry‑university alliances to accelerate AI talent pipelines, with long‑term partnerships like this one underscoring the commitment to merging cutting‑edge research with commercial application. The 10th anniversary partnership highlights sustained investment in both academic training and real‑world AI development.

## Implications  
Embedding industrial AI expertise into curricula strengthens Korea’s local AI ecosystem, improves workforce readiness for rapid advances in large language models and infrastructure, and creates a direct pipeline from research to commercialization that benefits the entire sector.
