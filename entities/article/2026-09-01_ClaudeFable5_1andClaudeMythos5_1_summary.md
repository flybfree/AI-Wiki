## Summary  
Anthropic has released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, which are two access tiers of the same underlying model tailored for different use‑cases. Fable 5.1 is generally available at a reduced price and with zero data retention via Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS), while Mythos 5.1 offers enhanced safeguards for cybersecurity and life‑science research through a government‑backed access program. Both versions deliver higher accuracy versus cost than their predecessor, especially in low‑ to medium‑effort tasks.

## Key Takeaways  
- The model’s price is cut by about 25 % (up to 45 % for highly agentic workloads) thanks to cheaper cache reads.  
- Enterprise Frontier Safeguards provide true zero data retention, giving customers full privacy while preserving strong adversarial protection.  
- Mythos 5.1 unlocks advanced biology capabilities via a US‑government partnership, and safeguards now block 60 % fewer false positives.

## Context  
The release reflects broader industry trends toward more efficient, secure AI services that balance performance with cost and privacy. By separating general‑purpose access (Fable) from highly regulated domains (Mythos), Anthropic aims to meet diverse enterprise needs without compromising safety or data governance. The emphasis on reduced false positives and lower token costs also aligns with the push for more reliable, production‑ready AI tools.

## Implications  
For developers and researchers, Fable 5.1 offers a cost‑effective, privacy‑preserving option that can be deployed at scale without sacrificing accuracy. Mythos 5.1 may accelerate breakthroughs in cybersecurity vulnerability discovery and biomedical research by providing vetted, high‑risk‑free access to specialized capabilities. Together, these models signal a shift toward AI services that are both economically viable and ethically responsible, encouraging broader adoption of large language models across regulated sectors.
