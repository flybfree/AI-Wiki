## Summary  
The planetary prediction engine (PPE) is an autonomous AI system that turns natural‑language geospatial queries into complete predictive models, handling data discovery, feature engineering, training and report generation in minutes. By orchestrating the workflow with large language models, PPE eliminates weeks of manual curation, delivering rapid insights for public health, food security, environmental risk and socio‑economic analysis.

## Key Takeaways  
- PPE automates the full geospatial modeling pipeline from data selection to final report generation using LLM orchestration.  
- It cuts model development time from weeks of manual effort to minutes, enabling swift response during humanitarian crises.  
- The system seamlessly integrates established repositories such as Google Earth Engine and Data Commons while performing live web discovery for missing signals.

The research demonstrates how autonomous AI can streamline complex workflows that currently rely on fragmented human expertise.
