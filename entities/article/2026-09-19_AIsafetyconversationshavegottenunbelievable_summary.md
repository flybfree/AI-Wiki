## Implications
The primary implication is that the "fear factor" surrounding AI safety may be outpacing the actual evidence of catastrophic risk. If the public and policymakers accept every speculative leap—such as the idea that an air-gapped computer could somehow coordinate a global takeover via heat fluctuations—it could lead to over-regulation based on "Rip van Winkle" style doomsday scenarios rather than tangible, manageable risks. For the AI field, this highlights the urgent need for clear, evidence-based communication about what is actually possible versus what is merely theoretically imaginable in an extreme, laboratory setting.
