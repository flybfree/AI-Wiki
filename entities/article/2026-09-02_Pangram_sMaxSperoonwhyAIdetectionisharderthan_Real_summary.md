## Context  
The broader AI landscape is marked by an increasing reliance on generative models for writing, image creation, and even code generation. This proliferation fuels a market for “trust layers” that aim to verify authenticity, yet the rapid evolution of AI capabilities creates a constant arms race between detection systems and evasion techniques.

## Implications  
For the field, these insights suggest that effective detection must account for nuanced usage patterns rather than relying on simplistic binary judgments.
