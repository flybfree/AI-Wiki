## Summary  
SK Telecom has announced a 15‑week “SKT‑Seoul National University AI Joint Program” for graduate students in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, beginning this second semester of 2026. The course blends three practical tracks—AI applications and services, AI modeling, and AI infrastructure—and is introduced with a CEO lecture by Jung Jae‑heon followed by teaching from senior executives such as Yoo Kyung‑sang (head of AI CIC) and Kim Tae‑yoon (head of foundation models). The partnership marks its 10th year in 2026, aiming to integrate SK Telecom’s industrial research and commercialization experience into university education.

- The program launches for the second semester of 2026, offering a 15‑week intensive course rather than a theoretical lecture series.  
- Its curriculum is built around real‑world AI technologies that SK Telecom has developed, emphasizing hands‑on implementation over abstract theory.  
- This marks the tenth anniversary of the joint initiative, highlighting long‑term commitment to merging industry expertise with academic instruction.
