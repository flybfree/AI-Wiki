## Summary  
Google has unveiled a major redesign of its search box, transforming it into an AI‑driven conversational interface that accepts multimodal inputs and merges AI Overviews with AI Mode. This marks the first such overhaul in 25 years and signals a shift from keyword‑only queries to open‑ended, rich‑media interactions.

## Key Takeaways  
- The search box now supports text, images, PDFs, videos, Chrome tabs, etc., enabling multimodal input.  
- It merges AI Overviews and AI Mode into a single flow, eliminating user friction between results pages and conversational mode.  
- AI‑powered query suggestions coach users toward detailed questions that leverage the new AI capabilities.

## Context  
The redesign reflects Google’s broader strategy to embed generative AI across its ecosystem, exemplified by Gemini models, Spark agent, intelligent shopping cart, and a reimagined developer platform. It signals a move from traditional keyword‑based search to open‑ended multimodal interaction as the core user experience.

## Implications  
This integration could reshape how users discover information, making Google’s product more powerful but also raising concerns about data privacy, content overload, and the erosion of classic search results. For the industry, it sets a precedent for AI‑driven interfaces that blend search with conversational AI, influencing competitors’ roadmaps.
