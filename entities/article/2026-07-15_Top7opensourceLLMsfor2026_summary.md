- Community‑driven development accelerates innovation, delivering rapid improvements that closed‑source rivals cannot match.

## Context  
The AI landscape is shifting toward open source as enterprises prioritize data privacy, cost efficiency, and flexibility. Researchers increasingly rely on publicly available models to conduct experiments, while businesses seek solutions that avoid the hidden expenses of commercial APIs. This trend reflects a broader move away from walled‑garden ecosystems toward collaborative, auditable AI development.
