US District Judge Zachary Bluestone has dismissed the sexual abuse lawsuit filed by Ann Altman against her brother, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, with prejudice, meaning the case cannot be refiled in the same court. The dismissal was granted after Ann Altman failed to appear for an independent medical examination or meet other critical deadlines despite prior warnings from the court. While the judge denied Sam Altman’s request for attorney fees, the ruling effectively concludes the legal proceedings regarding these allegations, which the Altman family had previously publicly denied.
