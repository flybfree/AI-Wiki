## Summary
OpenAI, in partnership with WAN-IFRA and the Association of Independent Regional Press Publishers of Ukraine (AIRPPU), has launched a joint initiative to support Ukrainian independent news publishers by enhancing their artificial intelligence capabilities. The program aims to strengthen the sustainability, efficiency, and resilience of journalism in Ukraine during ongoing conflict through two main components: the Newsroom AI Masterclass Series for knowledge sharing and the Newsroom AI Catalyst for hands-on implementation support. This effort focuses on practical AI adoption across editorial workflows, business transformation, and responsible innovation for selected news organizations.

## Key Takeaways
- The initiative is a collaborative effort between OpenAI, WAN-IFRA, and AIRPPU designed to build practical AI capabilities within Ukrainian newsrooms, specifically targeting independent regional and local publishers.
- The program consists of two distinct phases: the Newsroom AI Masterclass Series, which provides theoretical knowledge and case studies on topics like editorial workflows and revenue generation, and the Newsroom AI Catalyst, which offers deep, hands-on support to ten selected organizations for implementing specific AI solutions.
- Participating news organizations will receive API credits from OpenAI to facilitate the development of bespoke newsroom tools, alongside access to international experts and implementation roadmaps tailored to their specific operational needs.
