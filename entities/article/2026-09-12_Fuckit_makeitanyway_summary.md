## Implications
For the field, this piece highlights a tension between productivity and ownership: AI can accelerate output but may erode the sense of authorship that makes work meaningful. It suggests that human-made artifacts could become more valuable as signals of intentionality, taste, and craft, especially if AI-generated content becomes ubiquitous. For practitioners, it raises practical questions about how to maintain motivation, professional identity, and peer recognition when machines can imitate many routine tasks.
