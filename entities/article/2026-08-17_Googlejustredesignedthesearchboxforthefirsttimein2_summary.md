## Summary  
Google has finally overhauled its iconic search box after a quarter‑century, turning it into an AI‑driven, multimodal interface that accepts text, images, PDFs, videos and Chrome tabs. The redesign merges AI Overviews with the conversational AI Mode, delivering a single seamless experience where users can type detailed queries or upload files directly from the search field.

## Key Takeaways  
- [The new search box supports multimodal inputs such as text, images, PDFs, videos and Chrome tabs, expanding beyond simple keyword entry.]  
- [Google is unifying AI Overviews and AI Mode into one integrated flow, eliminating the need to switch between results pages and conversational mode.]  
- [An AI‑powered query suggestion system now coaches users toward complex, detailed questions that leverage the AI’s capabilities.]

## Context  
The change reflects a broader industry shift from keyword‑centric search to open‑ended, multimodal interactions powered by generative AI. As competitors like Bing and DuckDuckGo experiment with conversational interfaces, Google’s move signals its confidence in positioning its flagship product as an all‑in‑one AI assistant rather than a static results engine.

## Implications  
This redesign will reshape user expectations, forcing content creators to produce richer, more detailed information that can be consumed through visual and file inputs. It also raises questions about SEO relevance, data privacy with multimodal uploads, and the competitive race for integrated AI experiences across search platforms.
