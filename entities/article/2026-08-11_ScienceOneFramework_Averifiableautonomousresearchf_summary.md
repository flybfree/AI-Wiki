## Summary  
The Science One Framework introduces a Chain‑of‑Evidence (CoE) system that forces every claim in an autonomous research artifact to be backed by a verifiable chain of evidence, and pairs it with the CoE Audit, an automated protocol that quantifies how well those chains hold. By guaranteeing completeness and correctness of evidence links, the framework eliminates hallucinated references, misaligned code‑text descriptions, and non‑reproducible scores while still delivering state‑of‑the‑art results on benchmarks such as MLE‑Bench and Parameter‑Golf.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Verifiable evidence chains eliminate hallucinated references, achieving zero phantom citations.  
- The CoE Audit provides measurable proof that each claim is correctly supported by its underlying code or literature.  
- Science One attains state‑of‑the‑art performance on frontier research benchmarks while maintaining full verifiability.  

## Context  
Current autonomous AI researchers generate papers end‑to‑end, yet their outputs often contain broken evidence links—phantom citations, mismatched methods, and scores that cannot be reproduced. These issues undermine trust in AI‑produced scientific work and create a risk of disseminating false or misleading results. The broader industry is moving toward fully automated research pipelines, making verifiable integrity a critical prerequisite for adoption.  

## Implications  
For the field, Science One Framework establishes a new standard that couples high‑performance problem solving with provable correctness, enabling automated peer review and regulatory compliance in AI‑driven science. For industry, it reduces reputational risk, accelerates trustworthy deployment of research outputs, and opens pathways for AI agents to act as credible scientific collaborators rather than unreliable content generators.
