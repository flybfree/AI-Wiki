## Summary  
Midjourney has acquired the personalized astrology app Co‑Star, a service that delivers daily horoscopes and compatibility insights using human insight, NASA data, and AI. The deal reportedly closed in spring 2026, and Co‑Star’s founder Banu Guler will remain as the company’s chief design officer while also helping Midjourney develop its first applications, including an image‑generation tool.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Midjourney’s acquisition of Co‑Star signals a strategic move into the wellness and lifestyle market beyond AI art generation.  
- Banu Guler will lead both the design direction for Co‑Star and the creation of Midjourney’s initial apps, merging astrology expertise with AI development.  
- The terms of the deal remain undisclosed, leaving the financial scope and integration timeline unclear.  

## Context  
The acquisition reflects a broader trend where AI startups are diversifying into non‑technical domains such as astrology to attract new user bases and generate revenue streams that complement their core technology. Midjourney’s earlier pivot from generating AI cat images to full‑body ultrasound scans illustrates an ambition to expand beyond visual art, suggesting a willingness to explore adjacent fields where AI can add value through personalization and data integration.  

## Implications  
This move underscores the growing role of AI in delivering hyper‑personalized lifestyle content, potentially blurring lines between entertainment, wellness, and data analytics. It also raises questions about user privacy, as Co‑Star’s use of NASA data and AI could involve sensitive information handling. Moreover, by appointing Guler as chief design officer, Midjourney aims to fuse creative design with technical execution, setting a precedent for how AI firms may structure cross‑functional leadership in future acquisitions.
