The move aims to capture India’s rapidly expanding developer base—now the company’s third‑largest market with user numbers tripling in a year—to drive growth while the SpaceX deal is still pending.

- Cursor has introduced localized pricing in India, offering a low‑cost plan that includes Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.5 models but excludes premium features like Bugbot and Auto Mode.  
- The Indian market’s large pool of software talent and its status as a top developer hub justify the regional subscription model, which could be expanded to other countries if successful.  
- Cursor plans to restrict access to individual users in India only, using checks against VPNs, reflecting concerns about abuse.
