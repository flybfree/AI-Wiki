## Summary  
Keenable, a startup backed by Accel, is creating an AI‑centric web index that enables chatbots and large language models to retrieve and cite source documents in real time. The company has amassed over 100 billion indexed pages and already supplies its API to several AI labs, while also partnering with voice AI firm Gradium for live information retrieval.

## Key Takeaways  
- Keenable’s index is purpose‑built for AI agents, allowing them to ground responses in verifiable web sources.  
- The startup addresses the “innovators’ dilemma” by offering a cost‑efficient alternative as Google and Microsoft restrict their search APIs.  
- Its upcoming WebQueryLanguage product will let AI systems synthesize answers from fragmented documents without requiring exact matches.

## Context  
The rapid adoption of generative AI has shifted web usage from human‑centric queries to agentic tasks that demand massive, upfront data access. Traditional enterprise search solutions are costly at scale, and tech giants are consolidating their APIs rather than exposing them broadly, creating a vacuum that startups like Keenable aim to fill.

## Implications  
For AI developers, Keenable’s infrastructure reduces latency and improves factual grounding, enhancing model reliability. For the broader industry, it signals a move toward specialized, cost‑optimized search engines tailored for autonomous agents, potentially reshaping how organizations integrate web data into their AI pipelines.
