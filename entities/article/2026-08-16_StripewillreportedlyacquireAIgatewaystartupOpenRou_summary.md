## Summary  
Stripe is reportedly poised to acquire AI‑gateway startup OpenRouter for a price exceeding $7 billion, marking one of the largest deals in the AI infrastructure space. The acquisition would give Stripe direct access to OpenRouter’s platform that aggregates over 400 AI models and serves roughly eight million global users, effectively turning it into a “Stripe for AI.”  

## Key Takeaways  
- Stripe is set to buy OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, the highest valuation disclosed so far.  
- OpenRouter provides a single access point to 400+ AI models and has 8 million users, positioning it as an essential AI gateway.  
- A Stripe spokesperson declined to comment on the rumor, leaving the deal details unconfirmed.  

## Context  
The AI startup ecosystem is rapidly consolidating around platforms that simplify access to diverse machine‑learning services. OpenRouter’s Series B round raised $113 million at a $1.3 billion valuation, backed by Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures and Alphabet Capital G. This acquisition aligns with broader trends where large tech firms are buying niche AI infrastructure to integrate generative‑AI capabilities into their payment or cloud services.  

## Implications  
The deal could reshape how developers obtain AI models, reducing lock‑in by offering a unified interface similar to Stripe’s payment ecosystem. For the industry, it signals that AI gateways will become core components of major corporations’ product stacks, potentially accelerating adoption while raising concerns about vendor concentration and pricing power in the rapidly expanding generative‑AI market.
