## Summary  
This article explores the underlying algorithms and real-world complexities of elevator systems, revealing how seemingly simple technology involves sophisticated coordination between multiple cars and passengers. It examines how wait times are measured through statistical distributions rather than averages, highlighting the critical importance of minimizing long delays during peak usage periods like morning rush hour.

## Key Takeaways  
- The LOOK algorithm optimizes elevator movement by stopping only at requested floors instead of going to the top floor, significantly improving efficiency.  
- Wait time distribution (p90) is more important than average wait times as it reflects rider frustration during long delays.  
- Morning rush hour traffic creates a worst-case scenario for elevator performance due to high concentration of upward trips from the lobby.
