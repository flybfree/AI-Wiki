## Summary  
Inkling is an open‑weights Mixture‑of‑Experts transformer released by Thinking Machines that combines 975 B total parameters (41 B active) with multimodal support and a 1 M token context window, pretrained on 45 trillion tokens of text, images, audio and video. A lighter sibling, Inkling‑Small, also appears for cost‑effective use cases. The model is made available for fine‑tuning through the Tinker platform and includes an interactive Playground UI that lets developers chat with it.

## Key Takeaways  
- Inkling is an open‑weights foundation model with 975 B total parameters (41 B active) supporting multimodal reasoning.  
- The release enables anyone to fine‑tune it on Tinker, lowering cost and latency compared to closed models.  
- A self‑fine‑tuning demo demonstrates the platform can automate training pipelines and evaluate improvements.

## Context  
In AI research, most large foundation models remain proprietary, limiting accessibility and customization. Inkling breaks this pattern by offering a high‑performance, open‑weights model that can be fine‑tuned locally, thereby promoting democratization of advanced AI capabilities.

## Implications  
By making a multimodal, 975 B parameter model freely customizable, Inkling could accelerate niche applications, reduce dependence on closed APIs, and set a new benchmark for transparent fine‑tuning workflows in the field.
