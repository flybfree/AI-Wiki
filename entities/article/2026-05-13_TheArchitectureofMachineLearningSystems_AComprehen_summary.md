## Summary
The article introduces the foundational architecture of machine learning systems, emphasizing that modern deep learning capabilities are rooted in the mathematical principles of representation learning and non-linear function approximation. It highlights how contemporary deep learning frameworks have evolved to abstract the intricate complexities of differentiation and computation graphs, thereby simplifying the development process for engineers and researchers. This guide serves as the first part of a comprehensive series aimed at demystifying the structural components that enable efficient model training and deployment.

## Key Takeaways
- **Mathematical Foundations**: The core of machine learning systems relies heavily on advanced mathematical concepts, specifically representation learning and the ability to approximate non-linear functions, which allow models to capture complex patterns in data.
- **Abstraction via Frameworks**: Modern deep learning frameworks play a crucial role by abstracting the tedious and error-prone tasks of manual differentiation and managing computation graphs, allowing developers to focus on model design rather than low-level calculus.
- **Systematic Architecture**: Understanding the architecture is not just about code but involves a holistic view of how mathematical theories are translated into computational structures that can scale and perform efficiently in real-world applications.
