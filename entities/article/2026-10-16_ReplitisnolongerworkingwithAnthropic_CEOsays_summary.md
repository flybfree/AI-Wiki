## Summary
Replit CEO Amjad Masad announced at TechCrunch Disrupt that the AI coding platform has ended its partnership with Anthropic, opting instead to rely on OpenAI’s models for its development tools. Masad cited superior performance, lower costs, and better benchmark results as the primary drivers for switching to OpenAI, stating that ChatGPT and other OpenAI models now outperform Anthropic’s Claude models in building applications from scratch. However, the announcement faced immediate pushback from insiders who claimed the partnership with Anthropic remained active, creating a discrepancy between public statements and internal operational realities.

## Key Takeaways
- **Strategic Shift to OpenAI:** Replit has officially moved away from using Anthropic’s Claude models, favoring OpenAI’s technology due to perceived advantages in cost efficiency, partnership dynamics, and performance metrics on internal and public benchmarks.
- **Performance-Based Decision:** Masad emphasized that Replit’s decision was data-driven, noting that OpenAI’s models consistently performed better on benchmarks specifically designed to test the creation of "vibe-coded" applications from scratch.
- **Disputed Partnership Status:** Despite the CEO’s public declaration of ending the collaboration, sources familiar with the partnership contradicted this claim, and evidence from September 2026 indicated that Replit was still utilizing Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 models in its production environment.
- **Enterprise Growth:** Masad highlighted a significant shift in Replit’s business model, with enterprise contracts growing from 2% to 15% of revenue, and B2B revenue now accounting for over 50% of the company’s total income.
