## Summary  
Ramp’s latest analysis shows that OpenAI has reclaimed a modest edge over Anthropic among U.S. businesses, reversing the May lead that Anthropic held at 41 % versus OpenAI’s 39 %. The data reflects a volatile market where new model releases and regulatory concerns drive shifting user preferences.

## Key Takeaways  
- OpenAI’s share has slipped from its May peak to about 40 % of Ramp’s paying business users, while Anthropic sits at roughly 44 %.  
- OpenAI is currently growing faster among this segment in Q3, though the quarter still has a month left for the trend to reverse.  
- Enterprise AI adoption among Ramp customers continues to rise, reaching nearly 56 % of users by July.

## Context  
The report covers over 70,000 U.S. businesses that use corporate credit‑card and expense‑management tools from providers like Ramp; the sample is skewed toward tech firms but still representative of broader enterprise AI spending. Overall, the percentage of companies paying for AI has climbed steadily, surpassing 50 % in March and nearing 56 % by July.

## Implications  
The shifting market share underscores that enterprise AI adoption is cyclical rather than stable; investors should view growth as potentially short‑term and driven by product releases (e.g., OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6 Sol vs Anthropic’s Fable). Regulatory data‑retention concerns also influence user choice, highlighting the need for both companies to balance innovation with compliance in their enterprise offerings.
