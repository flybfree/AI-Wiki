## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest AI model that delivers state‑of‑the‑art performance on coding and knowledge‑work benchmarks while offering roughly half the price of its predecessor, Claude Fable 5. It outperforms all other models across a wide range of evaluations—including Frontier‑Bench, CursorBench, ARC‑AGI 3, OSWorld 2.0, and several life‑science tests—making it the most cost‑effective option for both developers and researchers.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 surpasses all other models on Frontiers‑Bench v0.1 and CursorBench 3.2 at a lower cost per task, delivering near‑Fable 5 performance with half the expense.  
- It excels in scientific research tasks such as organic chemistry inference and protein sequence analysis, showing double‑digit percentage gains over Opus 4.8.  
- The model’s visual generation capabilities (e.g., wind‑tunnel airflow and interactive cell diagrams) are markedly stronger than those of earlier versions.

## Context  
The rapid evolution of large language models has shifted focus toward cost‑effective, high‑performance solutions for enterprise and research users. Claude Opus 5 represents a strategic move by Anthropic to provide a flagship model that balances frontier intelligence with affordability, aligning with broader industry trends toward scalable AI deployment.

## Implications  
For developers, Opus 5 lowers the barrier to entry for sophisticated coding assistance and problem‑solving tools without sacrificing quality, potentially accelerating product innovation. For researchers, its superior performance on life‑science benchmarks could shorten experimental design cycles and reduce reliance on costly external services, thereby democratizing access to advanced AI capabilities in scientific workflows.
