## Summary  
The article revisits Apple’s short-lived Cards app, which allowed users to create and send custom letterpress cards via iOS in 2011—a project that became a notorious case of corporate miscommunication and operational chaos. Despite its brief existence, the initiative was deeply embedded in Steve Jobs’ vision for premium user experiences, yet it unraveled due to poor product-market fit, internal disagreements, and logistical nightmares involving international printing partners.  

## Key Takeaways

- [The project involved massive international operations, including print facilities in Japan and the Czech Republic, underscoring its scale but also its fragility.]
