## Summary  
Smallest.ai, a startup founded late 2024, has secured $13 million in Series A funding to develop an ultra‑fast voice AI that produces speech indistinguishable from human conversation. The company’s approach centers on creating tiny, specialized models that simulate simultaneous listening, thinking, and speaking, aiming to pass the Turing test for voice agents.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Smallest.ai prioritizes minimalist, domain‑specific voice models over scaling large language models.  
- Its real‑time intelligence layer delivers virtually zero latency and delegates out‑of‑scope queries to a larger LLM with a brief “research” hold.  
- Competitors such as ElevenLabs focus on broader use cases like dubbing, whereas Smallest.ai targets enterprise conversational agents.  

## Context  
The voice AI industry is dominated by large language models that generate text‑based responses, often resulting in noticeable latency and unnatural pauses. As customer support demand grows, the need for seamless, low‑latency human‑like interaction becomes critical.  

## Implications  
By separating real‑time conversational processing from problem‑solving LLM calls, Smallest.ai could redefine how AI agents operate, reducing reliance on massive models and enabling more natural, efficient voice experiences across enterprises.
