## Summary  
Meta is introducing two new AI-powered development tools—Horizon Create and Horizon Studio—to enable users to build games directly on their phones, leveraging AI prompts to generate complete 2D or 3D experiences with progression systems, multiplayer, and art direction. These tools are part of a strategic effort to revitalize the struggling Horizon social VR platform by making it more accessible and engaging through AI-driven game creation.

## Key Takeaways  
- Meta is launching Horizon Create (a mobile app) and Horizon Studio (a browser-based tool) in early access, allowing users to generate full games from simple AI prompts.  
- Published games made with these tools will be recommended on Facebook and Instagram, enabling players to join multiplayer sessions instantly without downloading apps or leaving social feeds.  
- The initiative aims to boost user engagement by integrating Horizon into platforms where users already spend time, such as Instagram, despite Horizon’s limited current user base.

## Context  
This move aligns with a broader industry trend of using AI to democratize game development, similar to Roblox’s recent rollout of AI-powered tools. Meta is also shifting focus from its long-term VR ambitions to mobile-first experiences, reflecting a pivot in its metaverse strategy. The use of AI in Horizon follows Google’s Project Genie, which produced brief but often unengaging interactive outputs, highlighting the potential and pitfalls of AI-generated content.

## Implications  
This development could lower the barrier to entry for game creators, fostering innovation and community-driven content on Horizon. However, it also raises concerns about quality control and the sustainability of AI-generated games in competitive markets like Roblox’s. If successful, Meta may redefine how social platforms integrate gaming, turning passive consumers into active participants through seamless, AI-assisted experiences.
