## Summary  
OpenAI is constructing a large‑scale data center called Project Camellia in Effingham County, Georgia, powered by 3.2 GW of electricity supplied by Georgia Power between 2028 and 2032. The project includes commitments to keep resident rates unchanged, use minimal water through a closed‑loop system, fund $80 million in community benefits, and provide up to $71 million in Codex credits for eligible students, all overseen by an annual independent audit.

## Key Takeaways  
- Electricity rates will not go up for residents because the project’s full cost is borne by OpenAI under state rules.  
- Water use is minimal and non‑diverting, employing a closed‑loop system that recirculates water like a car radiator.  
- The initiative delivers $80 million in direct community benefits plus up to $71 million in education credits, creating thousands of local jobs.
