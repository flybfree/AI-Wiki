## Summary
Gilbert + Tobin, a leading Australian corporate law firm, has successfully integrated AI into its operations by progressing from individual experimentation to structured, agentic workflows using OpenAI’s ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex. The firm’s leadership emphasizes that the primary value of AI is not cost reduction or headcount reduction, but rather freeing legal professionals to focus on high-value human-centric tasks such as client engagement, mentoring, and strategic judgment. By fostering a culture where leaders actively use the technology and governance frameworks ensure responsible usage, the firm achieved an exceptionally high adoption rate of 87% among enabled users.

## Key Takeaways
- **Strategic Phased Adoption:** The firm followed a deliberate three-phase approach: initial unconstrained experimentation with strict data privacy rules, followed by secure enterprise deployment (ChatGPT Enterprise) with robust governance controls like Australian data residency and role-based access, and finally, the implementation of multi-step agentic workflows where humans retain final review authority.
- **Human-Centric Value Proposition:** Contrary to common fears about AI displacing jobs, Gilbert + Tobin found that AI tools significantly enhanced employee satisfaction and productivity by automating routine tasks, allowing lawyers to dedicate more time to complex problem-solving, client relationships, and mentoring junior staff.
- **Leadership-Led Cultural Transformation:** High adoption rates were driven not by mandates or targets, but by leadership actively using the tools themselves, demonstrating relevance through tailored workflow showcases, and creating safe spaces for innovation such as "AI bounty" challenges that surfaced creative applications from across the organization.

## Context
This case study is part of OpenAI’s "Executive Function" series, highlighting how traditional industries are navigating the transition to AI-driven operations. It serves as a critical example for highly regulated sectors like law, finance, and healthcare, where data security, confidentiality, and professional judgment are paramount. The narrative underscores a shift in enterprise AI strategy from simple tool adoption to embedding AI into core business processes while maintaining strict compliance and ethical standards.

## Implications
For the legal industry and other knowledge-based professions, Gilbert + Tobin’s approach offers a blueprint for sustainable AI integration. It demonstrates that successful adoption relies less on technical deployment alone and more on cultural change management, where leadership visibility and clear governance frameworks build trust. The implications suggest that future competitive advantage in professional services will depend on how effectively firms can augment human expertise with AI, rather than replacing it. This model provides a roadmap for other CEOs to drive transformation by focusing on empowering employees to leverage AI as a tool for enhanced judgment and creativity, ensuring that technology serves to elevate the human element of professional work.
