Meta is unveiling a new generation of VR glasses, the Meta VR Glasses, set to launch in Spring 2027 at a price point of $1,299. Early promotional content features video clips showing users interacting with the glasses in gaming scenarios using Xbox Game Pass, suggesting integration with existing entertainment ecosystems.
