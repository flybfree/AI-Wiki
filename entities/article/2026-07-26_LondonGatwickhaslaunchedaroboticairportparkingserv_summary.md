## Summary  
London Gatwick has launched a robotic parking service that uses autonomous robots to store passenger cars in private drop‑off cabins near the South Terminal, allowing travelers to keep their keys and avoid searching for spaces. The system automatically lifts the vehicle with its tyres once it is parked and later retrieves it when the passenger’s return flight arrives. This innovation aims to maximise parking capacity without expanding physical infrastructure.

## Key Takeaways  
- Robotic parking stores cars in secure storage, eliminating the need for valet services and key surrender.  
- The service relies on pre‑booked return flight details to trigger automated retrieval, providing a seamless experience.  
- By packing vehicles tightly together, the system increases usable parking density, boosting capacity without new construction.
