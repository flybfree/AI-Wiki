By integrating advanced machine learning models with traditional scientific methods, these initiatives are accelerating the discovery of disease mechanisms and developing novel therapeutic interventions. The piece underscores the rapid evolution of AI from a theoretical concept to a practical tool capable of solving complex biological challenges.

## Key Takeaways
- Researchers at UC San Diego utilized AI to model 3D protein structures, revealing that the gene PHGDH plays a causal role in Alzheimer’s disease by disrupting gene regulation in brain cells, rather than merely serving as a biomarker.
- A new AI-powered tool called MycoBCP, developed by the Pogliano team, applies deep learning to bacterial cytological profiling, enabling the detection of subtle changes in tuberculosis cells to accelerate the development of treatments for drug-resistant strains.
- Engineers are employing AI to reconstruct internal signals of heart muscle cells based on external recordings, demonstrating the technology's potential to monitor complex physiological processes non-invasively and improve cardiac diagnostics.
