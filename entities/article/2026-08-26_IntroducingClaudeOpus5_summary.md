## Summary  
Claude Opus 5, launched on July 24 2026, is Anthropic’s latest large‑language model that delivers state‑of‑the‑art performance in coding, knowledge work, and creative tasks while costing roughly half of Claude Fable 5. It outperforms all other models across a suite of benchmarks such as Frontier‑Bench, CursorBench, ARC‑AGI 3, OSWorld 2.0, and several life‑science evaluations, offering the best value for both intelligence and token efficiency.

- Opus 5 surpasses Claude Fable 5 on many high‑effort coding benchmarks while using half the cost per task.  
- It achieves superior scientific research results, notably 10.2 pp higher in organic‑chemistry inference than Opus 4.8 and 7.7 pp higher in protein‑sequence variation prediction.  
- The model’s visual generation capabilities (e.g., wind‑tunnel airflow diagrams and interactive cell illustrations) are markedly stronger than previous versions.
