## Summary  
OpenAI’s GPT‑5.6 model series—Sol, Terra, Luna—has been integrated into Kiro, a software‑development agent that couples AI with engineering rigor to boost the price‑performance of developer workflows. By grounding code generation in structured requirements and team standards, Kiro enables developers to achieve higher‑quality output at lower token costs, delivering more finished work per dollar spent.

## Key Takeaways  
- **Cost efficiency:** GPT‑5.6 Terra reduces task completion cost by roughly 82 % on Terminal‑Bench 2.1 compared with earlier models in Kiro.  
- **Spec‑driven development:** The agent converts high‑level intent into clear technical designs and executable tasks, minimizing missteps and iteration cycles.  
- **Contextual awareness:** Kiro supplies the full codebase and team standards as context, allowing GPT‑5.6 to produce consistent, property‑tested solutions.

## Context  
The integration reflects a broader trend in AI‑assisted software engineering where large language models are embedded into end‑to‑end development pipelines.
