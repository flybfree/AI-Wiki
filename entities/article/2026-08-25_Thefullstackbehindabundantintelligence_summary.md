## Summary  
OpenAI argues that AI progress compounds fastest when software, hardware, frontier models, developer tools, consumer products and AI‑native devices evolve together as a single integrated system. Their custom inference chip Jalapeño shows measurable gains in throughput per kilowatt and token latency across multiple model families, illustrating how tightly coupled layers can create a virtuous cycle of performance and efficiency.

## Key Takeaways  
- Custom inference chip Jalapeño delivers higher peak throughput per kilowatt and lower token latency than comparable commercial accelerators, as verified on GPT‑OSS 120B and other models.  
- An integrated portfolio that matches workloads to the strongest silicon balances capability with cost, giving OpenAI a credible first‑party path alongside partner accelerators.  
- Data‑center projects such as Camellia demonstrate how infrastructure can be designed around specific workloads while generating community benefits.
