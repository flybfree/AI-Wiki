## Summary
OpenAI Academy, in collaboration with AARP’s Older Adults Technology Services (OATS), is launching a free, in-person "Older Adults AI Skills Jam" to help seniors use ChatGPT confidently and safely for everyday tasks. This initiative is part of a multi-year partnership aimed at building practical AI skills among older adults while emphasizing online safety and scam prevention. The program targets specific life challenges, such as understanding complex bills, planning trips, and maintaining social connections through technology.

## Key Takeaways
- **Growing Adoption Among Seniors**: Data indicates that the share of ChatGPT messages associated with users aged 55+ in the U.S. increased from 6% to nearly 10% within a single year, highlighting a significant rise in AI adoption among older demographics for practical guidance and information retrieval.
- **Focus on Digital Safety**: A core component of the workshop is educating participants on how to identify scams by recognizing warning signs like urgent language or suspicious links. The program teaches users to use ChatGPT as an additional layer of scrutiny, reinforcing a simple "pause, think, and ask" rule to combat fraud.
- **Nationwide Community Partnership**: The Skills Jam will be held in ten communities across the United States, partnering with local organizations such as Senior Planet centers, public libraries, and community service agencies to ensure accessible, localized support for older adults in diverse regions.

## Context
This initiative fits into OpenAI Academy’s broader strategy to make AI literacy practical and accessible to all demographic groups. It follows similar "Jam" events previously hosted for nonprofits, small businesses, and K–12 educators. By targeting older adults, OpenAI addresses a critical gap in digital inclusion, recognizing that this demographic often faces unique barriers to technology adoption but has significant potential to benefit from AI tools in daily life.

## Implications
This effort is crucial for the industry as it demonstrates a commitment to inclusive AI development beyond just technical capability. By focusing on safety and practical application, OpenAI helps mitigate risks associated with digital fraud, which disproportionately affects older adults. Furthermore, this program serves as a model for how tech companies can collaborate with non-profits to bridge the digital divide, ensuring that the benefits of advanced AI technologies are not limited to younger or more tech-savvy populations but extend to all age groups, thereby fostering broader societal trust and adoption of AI tools.
