## Summary  
Okta has agreed to acquire Permiso Security, an AI‑focused identity security startup, for about $200 million in cash, with the deal expected to close in Q3 of its fiscal 2027. The acquisition is intended to integrate Permiso’s continuous threat‑detection platform that monitors machine identities and AI agents into Okta’s broader identity management ecosystem.

## Key Takeaways  
- Okta is investing heavily in AI‑focused security by buying a startup that specializes in detecting attacks using compromised or stolen machine identities.  
- The $200 million cash deal, valued at just under $200 million, reflects strong market confidence and the perceived growth of AI identity threats.  
- Permiso’s platform, such as SandyClaw, enables continuous monitoring of cloud access beyond traditional login verification.

## Context  
In an era where enterprises embed AI agents into daily operations, securing non‑human identities has become a critical challenge. Identity management firms are shifting from user authentication to ongoing threat detection for applications and AI systems, intensifying competition among security startups that offer machine‑identity monitoring solutions.

## Implications  
This acquisition signals that mainstream identity providers view AI‑driven threats as an existential risk, prompting broader integration of machine‑identity monitoring into enterprise security strategies. It may accelerate the development of unified solutions that protect both human and synthetic identities, reshaping how organizations manage the security surface of their automated workflows.
