## Summary  
FLUX 3 is a multimodal foundation model that simultaneously learns from images, videos and audio within a unified architecture, aiming to capture the underlying reality of physical events as perceived by different sensors. By training on all modalities together, it can generate coherent video‑plus‑audio outputs from text or visual references, advancing real‑world visual intelligence beyond single‑modality models.  

## Key Takeaways  
- FLUX 3 integrates image, video and audio into a single architecture using the Self‑Flow framework to align generation and understanding.  
- It enables multimodal creation: text‑to‑video, image‑to‑video (continuation or reference), video‑to‑video, and video‑audio continuation with native audio.  
- Early evaluations show strong performance in content creation and physical AI tasks, suggesting a viable path for embodied visual intelligence.  

## Context  
The development reflects the industry shift toward multimodal foundation models that unify perception, prediction and action across digital and physical domains. Self‑Flow is an emerging technique to efficiently align modalities within one model, reducing redundancy and improving coherence.  

## Implications  
This work matters because it demonstrates a practical route to AI systems that can perceive, predict and act on real‑world events, opening possibilities for autonomous agents, immersive media and safe physical interaction, thereby accelerating the convergence of generative AI with embodied robotics and smart environments.
