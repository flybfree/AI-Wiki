## Summary  
The Science One Framework introduces a verifiable autonomous research system built on Chain‑of‑Evidence (CoE), which records every claim in an AI‑generated paper and ensures that the evidence genuinely supports it, eliminating hallucinations. An accompanying automated protocol, CoE Audit, measures the integrity of these papers by checking for missing citations, code mismatches, or unreproducible results.

## Key Takeaways  
- The framework guarantees zero phantom references by requiring each claim to have a recorded evidence chain.  
- It achieves state‑of‑the‑art performance on frontier benchmarks such as MLE‑Bench while maintaining full verifiability.  
- CoE Audit provides automated metrics that quantify errors like missing citations or code misalignment.

## Context  
Current large language models are being used to perform end‑to‑end scientific research, yet their outputs often contain hallucinated references and non‑reproducible results. This creates a trust gap for both researchers and the broader AI ecosystem, where reproducibility is essential for reliable knowledge generation.

## Implications  
For the field of AI research, Science One demonstrates that autonomous agents can produce high‑quality papers without sacrificing verifiability, encouraging wider adoption of self‑directed systems. In industry, it offers a pathway to trustworthy AI‑generated documentation and automated quality control, reducing risk in scientific publishing and data‑driven decision making.
