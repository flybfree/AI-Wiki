## Summary
LegalOn Technologies successfully reduced its AI development costs by 50% without sacrificing development speed by implementing a strategic model selection framework. The company moved away from unlimited access to high-performance models, instead utilizing a tiered approach that matches specific GPT-6 variants (Luna, Sol, and Astra) to task complexity. This governance strategy, managed by an internal Center of Excellence, allows engineers to independently select the most cost-effective model for each specific coding or design challenge.

## Key Takeaways
- **Tiered Model Selection Strategy:** LegalOn Technologies replaced a blanket "highest-capability" approach with a nuanced system where developers start with lightweight models (GPT-6 Luna) for simple implementation and escalate to more capable models (GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra) only as task complexity increases.
- **Governance via Center of Excellence:** An internal AI-powered Development CoE (AID CoE) was established to test models, monitor usage, and define guidelines, enabling engineers to make informed choices while maintaining organizational oversight through monthly usage limits.
- **Cost Reduction Without Performance Loss:** By aligning model capabilities with specific business needs and development stages, the company achieved a 50% reduction in costs while maintaining the same speed of development, demonstrating that unrestricted access to premium models is not necessary for high productivity.

This case study highlights a growing trend in enterprise AI adoption: the shift from experimentation phases, characterized by unlimited access to powerful models, to operational maturity, which requires strict cost governance. As organizations integrate AI into core business functions like legal and software development, they face the dual challenge of maximizing productivity gains while controlling expenditures. The strategies employed by LegalOn Technologies suggest that future enterprise AI deployments will rely heavily on sophisticated resource allocation and model routing rather than brute-force usage of the most powerful models.
