## Summary  
April 2026 witnessed a rapid surge of large‑language‑model releases, with OpenAI’s GPT‑5.5 (released April 23) and Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.7 (April 16) emerging as the dominant publicly available options for developers. Meanwhile, Google’s Gemma 4 series, Zhipu AI’s GLM‑5.1, and Meta’s Llama 4 models offered free open‑source alternatives, while several other models remain in preview or beta phases.

## Key Takeaways  
- GPT‑5.5 (Spud) and Claude Opus 4.7 are the two frontier public models with 1M and 200K context windows respectively, priced at $5‑in/$30‑out and $5‑in/$25‑out.  
- OpenAI’s recent price hike on GPT‑5.5 Pro ($30‑in/$180‑out) signals a shift toward higher‑value usage tiers, while Claude continues to keep Opus 4.7 at the same cost as its predecessor.  
- Open‑source models such as Gemma 4 (31B dense), GLM‑5.1 (744B MoE), and Llama 4 (400B) remain freely available under Apache 2.0, reinforcing competition between proprietary and community‑driven solutions.

## Context  
The AI industry is experiencing a “two‑horse race” for the public‑API top spot, driven by the release of GPT‑5.5 and Claude Opus 4.7 just six weeks apart. This acceleration reflects both companies’ strategic push to capture market share before rivals catch up. Additionally, the proliferation of open‑source models like Gemma 4 and GLM‑5.1 underscores a growing trend toward community‑driven innovation that can bypass costly proprietary licensing.

## Implications  
For developers, the new pricing landscape means cost per token is no longer the sole differentiator; model capability (e.g., context length, verification scores) now heavily influences adoption decisions. The continued availability of free open weights encourages experimentation and reduces entry barriers for startups, while the rise of local agents like Fazm demonstrates a shift toward privacy‑preserving AI usage on macOS. Overall, these releases will shape competitive dynamics, influence funding priorities, and accelerate the integration of LLMs into everyday workflows.
