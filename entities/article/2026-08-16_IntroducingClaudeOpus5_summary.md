## Summary  
Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic’s latest flagship model that delivers state‑of‑the‑art performance on a wide range of AI benchmarks while costing roughly half as much as the more expensive Claude Fable 5. It outperforms all competing models in coding, knowledge‑work, and scientific evaluation tasks, and it also generates high‑quality visualizations, making it the most efficient option for developers, researchers, and enterprises seeking powerful AI assistance without prohibitive expense.

## Key Takeaways  
- Opus 5 is the new state‑of‑the‑art model on Frontier‑Bench v0.1 and CursorBench 3.2, delivering performance that exceeds all others at a lower cost per task than its predecessor Opus 4.8.  
- The model excels across multiple knowledge‑work evaluations (ARC‑AGI 3, Zapier AutomationBench, OSWorld 2.0) and even surpasses Fable 5 on certain computer‑use tasks while using only one‑third the cost.  
- Opus 5 shows significant gains in scientific research domains such as organic chemistry and protein prediction, with up to 10 percentage points higher scores than Opus 4.8.

## Context  
The Claude series represents Anthropic’s effort to compete with leading generative AI models by balancing capability, safety, and price. Frontier‑Bench and related benchmarks are industry‑standard metrics for evaluating reasoning, coding, and problem‑solving abilities in large language models. As the market pushes toward cheaper yet high‑performing assistants, Opus 5 illustrates how incremental improvements can close gaps with premium models like Fable 5.

## Implications  
For developers and businesses, Opus 5 lowers the barrier to accessing cutting‑edge AI tools, encouraging wider adoption of Claude’s Max tier for everyday tasks. For researchers, its superior performance on scientific benchmarks may accelerate progress in fields such as drug discovery and materials science. Overall, the release signals a shift toward cost‑effective, high‑capability AI that can be integrated into production workflows without sacrificing quality.
