## Summary  
OpenAI is rolling out a series of upgrades to ChatGPT that enhance both the reliability and depth of its AI responses while widening access for free users. The new GPT‑5.6 Sol model delivers more focused, fact‑checked answers with an optional slider that lets Plus/Pro users control how much “thought” the system invests in each reply; Free users receive unlimited text chats powered by GPT‑5.6 Luna and a Think button that triggers deeper reasoning for complex queries.

## Key Takeaways  
- The updated GPT‑5.6 Sol reduces factual errors by roughly 68 % compared with the previous version, improving trustworthiness across domains such as finance, medicine, and law.  
- A user‑adjustable slider now lets Plus/Pro subscribers balance speed versus thoroughness, creating a consistent experience between Instant and Thinking modes.  
- Free users gain unlimited chat capacity and a Think button that unlocks higher‑effort reasoning without requiring a paid upgrade.

## Context  
These changes reflect the broader AI industry’s push to make advanced language models more accessible while maintaining quality. As competitors release similar tiered offerings, OpenAI’s focus on balancing cost, performance, and user experience sets a new benchmark for commercial deployment of generative AI.
