## Summary  
Amazon is rolling out an AI update for Alexa Plus that expands its ability to handle complex, multi‑step instructions across a broader set of smart home devices. The preview launch lets users issue tasks like “my kid’s soccer jersey could use a deep clean, but the tag says cold wash only,” and the system will route commands to the correct appliance automatically.  

## Key Takeaways  
- Alexa Plus now integrates with dozens of third‑party smart‑home brands (Bosch, Delta, Ecovacs, etc.) via Amazon’s new AI developer toolkit.  
- The update leverages the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to enable richer interactions and future payments through Amazon Wallet.  
- It builds on the February launch that added multi‑step routines but limits third‑party services; now it adds more capabilities.  

## Context  
This rollout reflects a broader industry shift toward open AI standards such as MCP, which allow devices and apps to expose APIs for seamless voice‑driven workflows. By standardizing how models connect to external tools, manufacturers can deliver richer experiences without Amazon having to build proprietary bridges each time.  

## Implications  
The expansion of Alexa Plus’s ecosystem could accelerate adoption of smart home automation, reduce friction between users and device makers, and set a precedent for other AI assistants to adopt similar open protocols, potentially reshaping the competitive landscape in consumer IoT.
