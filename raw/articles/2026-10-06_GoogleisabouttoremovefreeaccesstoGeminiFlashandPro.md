Starting on October 9th, anyone using Google Gemini on a free plan will be
limited to the Flash Lite model
. Free users can currently choose from Gemini Flash Lite, Flash, and Pro, but now you’ll need a $4.99/month Google AI Plus subscription to access the standard Flash model. However, that subscription is also getting a downgrade – it will soon no longer include Gemini Pro. Google says AI Plus subscribers will receive an email letting them know when they’ll lose access to it.
After these changes, access to the top-tier Gemini Pro model and a “Deep Think” advanced reasoning option will be restricted to users with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription, which cost $19.99 or $99.99 per month, respectively.
Google says users will still be able to choose from low, medium, and high effort levels for each model, but using higher effort levels could
burn through your Gemini usage limit
faster.
