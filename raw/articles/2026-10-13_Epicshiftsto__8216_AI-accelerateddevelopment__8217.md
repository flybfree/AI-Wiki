Epic Games plans to use AI to generate code and art for an unannounced Disney game, which is now delayed from its original March release window, according to a report from
Bloomberg
. The project’s developers have been given an “AI-first” mandate directing them to use tools like Anthropic’s Claude more to finish the game.
According to
Bloomberg
, one of the game’s directors said in a message to staff, “No more hand-written code.” Staff were also told that all of the game’s art will be “generated with full AI workflows.” In a statement given to
Bloomberg
, Epic Games spokesperson Jake Jones said the company is “embracing AI-accelerated development.”
Epic Games and Disney did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
The upcoming game is reportedly a shooter featuring characters from Marvel and
Star Wars
. It’s part of
a multiyear collaboration
between Epic and Disney, which also invested $1.5 billion in Epic in 2024. Epic already
uses AI in
Fortnite
, which has previously featured Disney characters like an
AI Darth Vader
. But it sounds like Epic may be aiming to have AI do most of the heavy lifting on its new Disney game.
AI game development tools have been popping up from other large companies, like
Meta
and
Google
, so Epic isn’t alone here. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney has also pushed for Steam to
drop its “made with AI” tag
, arguing that “AI will be involved in nearly all future production.” Earlier this year, Epic laid off
over 1,000 of its employees
, following
a “downturn” in engagement on
Fortnite
since 2025.
