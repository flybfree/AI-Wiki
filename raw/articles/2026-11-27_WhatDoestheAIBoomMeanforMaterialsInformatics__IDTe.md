# What Does the AI Boom Mean for Materials Informatics? : IDTechEx Webinar

## Full Article

What Does the AI Boom Mean for Materials Informatics?
Materials informatics applies machine learning and other data-driven methods to accelerate the discovery and development of materials. Recent advances in generative AI and foundation models are opening up new research opportunities, but established machine learning approaches continue to deliver value in industrial R&D. The suitability of materials informatics varies considerably by application, and formulation-based materials are among the first to see widespread commercial adoption.
The AI boom is also changing the competitive landscape. Big Tech companies are expanding into scientific R&D, and established engineering software providers are integrating materials AI capabilities into their platforms. These developments create opportunities for wider industrial adoption but also raise questions about the scalability and profitability of specialist materials informatics providers, especially as larger materials companies develop their own internal capabilities.
In this 30-minute webinar, IDTechEx Technology Analyst Roberta Salciute will discuss:
•	Established machine learning approaches and recent advances in AI for materials informatics.
•	The characteristics that make certain materials applications particularly well suited to data-driven development.
•	Industrial applications of materials informatics and their commercial progression.
•	The changing competitive landscape and the commercial challenges facing specialist providers.
•	IDTechEx's market outlook and revenue forecast for external materials informatics providers through 2037.
This webinar draws on research from the newly released IDTechEx market report, "
Materials Informatics 2027-2037: Markets, Strategies, Players
