# Bristol Myers Squibb accelerates life-changing scientific pursuits with Microsoft AI

## Full Article

Global biopharmaceutical company Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) has a mission is to bring more medicines to more patients faster. Aiming to speed up the science behind its medicines, BMS rolled out Microsoft 365 Copilot across its 30,000-person enterprise.
BMS treated AI adoption as a change management exercise, launching a learning academy to build fluency with Microsoft 365 and Copilot. Teams share AI knowledge via Viva Engage and Insights, with Purview and Microsoft Agent 365 supporting governance.
Today, there are 400+ AIQ Champions at BMS and Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption is at 96%. 25,000 agents are in use including one that’s produced up to 20% time savings for project managers. BMS also uses AI in core business areas like R&D.
