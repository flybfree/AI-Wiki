On Tuesday, US District Judge Zachary Bluestone dismissed the
lawsuit
Ann Altman
filed last year alleging that her brother, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, had sexually abused her
throughout her childhood. As reported earlier by
The Guardian
, the judge wrote that over the past year, Ann had failed to appear for an independent medical examination or meet other deadlines despite warnings that it would result in the case being dismissed.
When the case was filed last year, a statement shared by Sam Altman, his mother, and his brothers said, “Annie has made deeply hurtful and entirely untrue claims about our family, and especially Sam… All of these claims are utterly untrue. This situation causes immense pain to our entire family.”
Bluestone granted Sam Altman’s motion to dismiss the case with prejudice, meaning it can’t be refiled in that court, while denying Sam’s request for attorney fees and costs. Santos Perez, a lawyer who had represented Ann until withdrawing this month, told
The Guardian
in a statement that “I am no longer authorized to comment on this case but I continue to believe in Annie.”
