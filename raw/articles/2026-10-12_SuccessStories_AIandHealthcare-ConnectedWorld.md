# Success Stories: AI and Healthcare - Connected World

## Full Article

Technology like AI (artificial intelligence) is opening new opportunities in healthcare, from analyzing medical images to helping organizations collaborate while keeping sensitive patient information protected. As these technologies continue to advance, researchers are also finding new ways to make sure AI systems are secure, accurate, and reliable.

One example comes from Florida Atlantic University, where researchers in the SPEED Lab are studying the security of AI systems used in healthcare. The recent research examined federated AI, an approach that allows hospitals and other organizations to work together to train AI models without directly sharing sensitive patient data. The research found that while this approach can help protect patient privacy, the AI models themselves can still be vulnerable to cyberattacks.

Here is how this can help advance healthcare technology:

*   Protect sensitive patient information while allowing healthcare organizations to collaborate on AI research and development.
*   Strengthen AI security by identifying vulnerabilities before these systems become more widely used in clinical settings.
*   Support more reliable medical AI that can assist with analyzing medical images and identifying important clinical information.

Looking to the future, we are going to see AI play an increasingly important role across healthcare—and with that growth comes the need to build security into these technologies from the beginning. Research like this will be important in making sure the next generation of medical AI is not only innovative, but also secure, accurate, and dependable.
