Business development teams are operating in a more demanding buying environment: Prospects have less time to give, more information at their fingertips and longer to make a final decision. AI has accelerated research and changed when buyers engage with sellers, often pushing the first meaningful conversation further down the decision-making process.
As a result, business development priorities are shifting from simply generating more outreach and information to creating immediate value, building credibility and earning trust throughout a longer buying journey. Here, members of
Forbes Business Development Council
share how shorter attention spans, AI-powered research and extended buying cycles are changing the way organizations engage prospects.
1. Strong Storytelling Can Win And Hold Attention
Shrinking attention spans make storytelling more important than ever before. The key is to hook prospects quickly with authentic, pain-point-driven narratives, then use long-form written and video content to build trust during longer buying cycles. As AI reshapes discovery, the brands that resonate most deeply with people will also become the easiest for algorithms to recommend to the right audience. -
Jonathan Brierre
,
GTIF Capital
2. Buyers Need Insight After AI Has Done The Research
The research is done before the first conversation even happens. Buyers arrive informed, vendor-aware and often already skeptical. AI accelerated that shift, but it cannot build trust, resolve uncertainty or demonstrate you understand their specific business needs. In high-value enterprise sales, that is now the entire game. The organizations winning are investing earlier in relationships and leading every interaction with insight, not information. -
Shariq Rehman
,
Visionet Systems Inc.
3. Companies Must Become Discoverable In AI-Powered Research
The biggest shift isn’t necessarily shorter attention spans. It’s where attention happens. More research now happens in AI before buyers ever visit your website or reach out to sales. The companies that stand out will be the ones that create credible, AI-discoverable content with consistent messaging that works just as well in AI tools as it does in sales conversations. -
Lauren Newman
,
Button

4. Business Development Must Focus On Retaining Knowledge
The biggest thing for me is the ability to retain knowledge when the work of acquiring it is already done for you. We are used to gaining expertise from doing research to find the information. With that done, business development needs to shift away from research and focus on retention techniques. -
Seth Marrs
,
Sandler
5. Strategic Relationships Are Replacing Volume-Driven Outreach
Shrinking attention spans require concise, personalized outreach with immediate value. AI improves prospecting and research but raises buyer expectations for deeper insights. Longer sales cycles demand consistent engagement, stakeholder alignment and trust-building. Business development is shifting from volume-driven outreach to strategic, data-informed relationship building. -
Damini Sood
,
KBS International LLC
6. Judgment Matters More When Information Is Everywhere
Business development must create value earlier and earn trust faster. Buyers arrive more informed, but not always better informed, so the role shifts from sharing information to providing judgment. Focus on solving real business problems, demonstrating measurable value and staying engaged throughout longer buying cycles. -
Michael Fritsch, PMP
,
Confoe
7. Every Buyer Interaction Must Deliver Something AI Cannot
Shrinking attention spans and AI-driven research are creating a double-edged sword for business development teams. Buyers are more informed, but they're also spending more time researching options before ever engaging a seller. That makes every interaction count. Teams should focus on building authentic relationships, delivering insights from their own expertise and creating high-value interactions beyond what AI can provide. -
Matt Bartels
,
Alexander Group
8. Credibility Is Becoming More Important Than Visibility
AI has made information easier to find, but harder to trust. That shifts the priority from creating more content to building more credibility. Independent media, real client results and authentic expertise now influence buying decisions more than simply being visible online. -
Valeri Manziuk
,
UFIRST Production
9. AI Discovery Is Becoming A New Business Development Priority
Effective professionals expect instant answers because AI has transformed how research is conducted. Curiosity about effective solutions can be quickly explored and validated. Business development starts with ensuring your brand appears prominently in LLM results and that those results reinforce credibility, relevance and trust. AI isn't just shrinking attention spans—it's raising expectations for every interaction. -
Chris Vriavas
,
pharosIQ
10. Buyers Are Using Conversations To Vet, Not Discover
Because attention spans are shorter, it's taking longer to earn trust, which is a significant shift from even a couple of years ago. It's essential to realize that because of LLMs, people already did their homework in a few minutes before they ever talk to you. By the time they reach out, they've finished discovering and are looking to vet you. -
Wayne Elsey
,
Funds2Orgs
