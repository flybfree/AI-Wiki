AI companies have been talking about superintelligent AI like it’s inevitable, but recent
safety incidents
like OpenAI’s
Hugging Face breach
are demonstrating the potential dangers of deploying AI systems that are more capable than humans. So what happens when we can’t reliably control what these systems do?
On this episode of TechCrunch’s
Equity
podcast, Rebecca Bellan is joined by
Connor Leahy
, an AI researcher, entrepreneur, and now the U.S. Executive Director of nonprofit
ControlAI
, which is pushing for a far more radical approach to AI safety: stopping companies from developing superintelligence altogether. Leahy explains why he thinks the risks have become too great to manage through alignment and containment alone, and how what sounded far-fetched six months ago is now being backed by a wave of new legislation.
