a big, new idea
about the most popular smartphone in history.
On
this episode of
The Vergecast
, David and Nilay debate whether the Duo deserved the score it got, and try to work through how this device might change over time. Is the outer screen, as Daring Fireball’s John Gruber suggested,
the worst iPhone ever
