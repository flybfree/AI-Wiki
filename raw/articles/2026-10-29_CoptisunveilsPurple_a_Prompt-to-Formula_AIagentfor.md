# Coptis unveils Purple, a “Prompt-to-Formula” AI agent for cosmetic R&D

## Full Article

Industry buzz
Coptis unveils Purple, a “Prompt-to-Formula” AI agent for cosmetic R&D
[Coptis unveils Purple, a “Prompt-to-Formula” AI agent for cosmetic R&D]
At its annual Users Club, held in Paris on October 13, 2026, software publisher
Coptis
, which specializes in formulation and regulatory compliance, unveiled two AI-powered innovations:
Purple
and
Document Reader
. Designed for R&D, formulation and regulatory affairs teams, the tools aim to accelerate cosmetic product development and streamline technical data management.
"Prompt-to-Formula” AI agent
Integrated into
Coptis
solutions, Purple draws on each company’s own raw materials and structured data, alongside
Coptis
’ knowledge base. This approach embeds regulatory compliance from the outset of product development while enabling teams to build on their expertise and internal data. Furthermore, the new tool does not rely on external sources, such as general-purpose LLMs.
“Our customers (cosmetic brands, manufacturers and contract manufacturers, etc..) need to innovate faster, personalize their products, reduce waste and ensure compliance,” said Coptis in a press release.
Intelligent processing of raw material documents
Furthermore,
Coptis
also presented Document Reader, which automatically creates a raw material record in the
Coptis
solution from a supplier document. The tool extracts relevant information, populates the record fields, and then renames and files the document in the library.
The AI reads supplier PDF documents, extracts the relevant data, and cross-checks it against the customer’s settings and
Coptis
reference data, including properties, solubility, and alerts. It then automatically pre-populates the
Coptis
raw material record with key information such as the INCI breakdown and traces, alerts, and natural origin indicators under ISO 16128 and COSMOS.
Planned enhancements include source traceability, with references to the relevant pages and passages, support for multiple documents and file formats for a single raw material, and the ability to update existing records.
“Our User Club is where we actively shape the future of our solutions together with our customers. With Purple and Document Reader, we put AI at the service of formulation and regulatory experts: helping them innovate and work more efficiently, without ever compromising on reliability or compliance,”
Pauline Caron, Chief Product Officer at
Coptis
.
