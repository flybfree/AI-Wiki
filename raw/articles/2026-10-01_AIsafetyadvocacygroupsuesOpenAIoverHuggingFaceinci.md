# AI safety advocacy group sues OpenAI over Hugging Face incident

## Full Article

A technology and
artificial intelligence
safety group has sued
OpenAI
over a July incident in which the company’s technologies
autonomously gained access
to the systems of AI developer hub Hugging Face.
Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology filed the lawsuit on Thursday in the Superior Court of
California
in San Francisco, arguing the company’s conduct violated the state’s anti-hacking laws. LASST argues that OpenAI’s defense that “artificial intelligence autonomously caused the harm” in the Hugging Face incident is insufficient, and claims the company is “on the hook for harms” caused in the hacking.
