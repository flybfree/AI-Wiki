Anthropic reportedly plans to move forward with IPO plans
Launching a major tech IPO would seem risky right now, shortly after one of Nasdaq’s biggest sell-offs and amid revelations that a CEO central to the AI boom had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. But that isn’t deterring Anthropic, which is moving ahead with its listing plans, according to
The Wall Street Journal
.
Anthropic plans to set a valuation range this week and begin trading on Thursday or Friday of next week, the Journal reported.
Investors will look to Anthropic’s valuation to gauge the strength of the AI boom. Some institutional investors are now pushing for a valuation below the $1.5 trillion target discussed as recently as last week, a figure that was itself considerably below the $2 trillion floated a few months ago, the report said.
OpenAI, Anthropic’s main rival, was reportedly valued at
$1.25 trillion
last week after the ChatGPT maker raised $11 billion in fresh capital, short of the $20 billion it had initially sought.
