# Oldinsurancemaps.net is now a Charter Project

## Full Article

OldInsuranceMaps.net is now a Charter Project!
Aug 25, 2026
·
OpenStreetMap US Staff
OSM US is incredibly excited to announce the latest Charter Project:
OldInsuranceMaps.net
(OIM)!
If you’re a map nerd you’ll remember ever seeing a Sanborn Insurance Map in person. They often come in absolutely massive tomes that were once perhaps the closest you could get to OpenStreetMap before the digital age; over the years, each page becomes a collage of small cutouts of paper pasted over streets since renamed, or buildings since demolished. They are both beautiful pieces of artwork and one of the most accurate references for geographic data in the United States from 1867 into  the 1960s. Sanborn Maps are a valuable resource for historical mappers and researchers (as briefly mentioned in our
OpenHistoricalMap Mapper Highlight
post), and thanks to OIM their georeferenced information is much more accessible.
