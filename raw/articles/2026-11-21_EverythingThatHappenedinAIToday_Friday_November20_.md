Everything That Happened in AI Today (Friday, November 20, 2026)
Anthropic made its record-breaking market debut; Meta's Muse faced privacy concerns; Google brought Gemini computer use to Mac; Apple reportedly developed a rival agent; and an AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted European Commission systems.

Anthropic just pulled off the biggest IPO in history, creating thousands of new AI millionaires while one of its earliest investors admitted he wasn't entirely thrilled about the windfall.
Welcome, humans!
The race to control your computer got interesting yesterday. Google launched a Gemini agent that can operate Mac apps, Apple is reportedly developing its own competitor, and Meta's Muse is facing complaints about personal information surfacing in the wrong conversations.
Apparently, giving AI more independence comes with a few problems nobody wants to discover firsthand.
Meanwhile, the European Commission was investigating an AI-assisted cyberattack, and Google announced plans to send AI servers into orbit. There's plenty to unpack, but first, we have a historic day on Wall Street. Let's get into it.

Anthropic has officially made history. The Claude developer completed a record-breaking stock-market debut, with its
IPO raising $81.3 billion
through the sale of 980 million shares. Another $26 billion in private financing brought the combined proceeds to $107.3 billion. The public offering valued Anthropic at roughly $1.5 trillion before its shares began trading.
And the money isn't only going to the company. The IPO has reportedly
created thousands of new millionaires
, along with several billionaires. Yet one of Anthropic's earliest backers, Facebook co-founder Dustin Moskovitz, reportedly
feels uneasy about his investment windfall
.
Imagine watching your early investment become one of the biggest fortunes in tech and still having mixed feelings about the outcome.
The financial milestone also puts Anthropic's philosophy under a brighter spotlight. In a
New York Times opinion piece
, a writer who spent a year interviewing Anthropic employees explored their belief that the company has a special responsibility to develop safe superintelligence. Public shareholders now have an enormous financial stake in Anthropic's growth. The next test is whether its revenue can eventually justify the company's valuation and mounting AI infrastructure expenses.

ð TOP 5 NEWS (Around the Horn)
Meta's Muse reportedly mixed up private conversations
, with employees encountering unfamiliar childcare messages, personal reminders, and bank balances. Meta said employees test versions with features unavailable to the public.
An AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted the European Commission
, temporarily affecting staff payroll and medical reimbursement systems. Investigators reportedly identified ARTEX, an AI-assisted security testing tool, while officials examined whether personal information was compromised.
Apple is reportedly developing its own computer-controlling AI agent
, capable of operating applications, browsing websites, and writing code.
The project remains in early development
as Apple prepares additional permission warnings for competing agents.
Over 500 Google employees urged the company
to let schools use traditional Google Search separately from its AI features. Staff members argued that schools should be able to choose whether students encounter AI-generated answers.
Google selected Blue Origin to launch its AI server satellites
in the first quarter of 2029. Project Suncatcher aims to test solar-powered AI computing in orbit, with each satellite designed to provide computing capacity comparable to a data center rack.

Honorable Mentions
China's Moonshot AI reportedly canceled a $150 million Nvidia chip deal
involving a data center in Indonesia after new U.S. rules restricted remote access to advanced chips by Chinese companies.
Google built a simulated internet
called Mirror Dimension to train Chrome browsing agents on realistic websites while keeping experimental actions away from real accounts and services.
Meta's upcoming Muse Charm may cost $300
. The portable AI device could include up to a year of free Verizon 5G service, with availability expected around the December holidays.
Anthropic's IPO prompted new scrutiny in Washington
, where four senators introduced bipartisan legislation that would require publicly traded companies to disclose more information about AI.

ðª TOP TREATS TO TRY
Google Gemini for Mac
can organize local files, complete forms, and operate Mac apps while you continue working. Initially available in English to U.S. Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers.
Google Genie 4 API
creates interactive 720p worlds at 24 frames per second with native audio, live keyboard controls, and the ability to change environments through text prompts.
ChatGPT Live Dictation
transcribes your speech as you talk, shows editable text before submission, and removes the previous 10-minute recording limit. Rolling out globally across ChatGPT plans.
Canva's AI off switch
disables many generative AI tools, including Magic Write and Magic Expand. Users can also reduce AI-generated content in search results, although some AI-assisted editing tools remain available.
Microsoft's seven new MAI models
cover coding, cybersecurity, image generation, and real-time speech. Microsoft claims several offer competitive benchmark performance at substantially lower costs than frontier rivals.
Tencent Hy4 Flash
is now downloadable for local deployment. The text-and-image model supports up to one million tokens of context, allowing developers to process unusually long documents.
Orange's five Wolof language models
build on Alibaba's Qwen3 and support a language spoken by millions in West Africa. The models are available free for noncommercial use through Hugging Face.
DaisyRose-27B
is RadixArk's downloadable model for reasoning and coding tasks. Its developers report improved coding-agent test performance after training with just 1,200 curated tasks.
OpenAI GPT-Live-1.1
is available through the API, with improved accent consistency across languages, more reliable task completion, and a lower-cost standby mode for voice applications.
TestSprite 4.0
automatically explores frontend and backend applications, creates software tests, identifies failures, and checks future code changes for regressions.
Antinuous
is a research platform for running AI-agent experiments and simulations at scale. Its creators claim support for 100 times more experiments at one-thousandth the cost.
