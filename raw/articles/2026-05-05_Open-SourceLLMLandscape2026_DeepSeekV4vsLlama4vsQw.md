# Open-Source LLM Landscape 2026: DeepSeek V4 vs Llama 4 vs Qwen …

## Summary

Compares the 2026 open-weight LLM landscape, emphasizing that frontier open models now compete with closed models on capability while shifting the real decision criteria to cost, licensing, latency, and deployment constraints.

## Key points

- DeepSeek, Llama, Qwen, Gemma, and Mistral define the major open-weight families
- MoE models dominate the frontier because active parameters control inference cost
- License terms matter as much as benchmark scores for real deployment decisions
- Context window size is less important than retrieval quality and budget
