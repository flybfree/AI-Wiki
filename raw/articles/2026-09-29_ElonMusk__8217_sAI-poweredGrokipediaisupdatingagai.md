Grokipedia, the AI-powered online encyclopedia from SpaceXAI, appears to be updating articles once again after
a months-long pause
. In August,
Lawfare
reported that
articles on Grokipedia hadn’t reviewed edits since April, but the
platform’s live updates site
is now showing various recent changes to pages — though as I write this, many are just a note that says “recheck all references and sources.”
The site seems to have become active again relatively recently. The page for
President Barack Obama
has a note that says it was “Fact-checked by Grok” two days ago, and the page for
Elon Musk
was fact-checked while I was writing this, within the last hour. However, the page for
Honolulu
, which I clicked on from Obama’s page, was last fact-checked 7 months ago, so it appears that not every page has a recent scan.
While writing this article, I suggested an update to the
Gears of War: E-Day
page to note the game’s October 6th release date, and Grokipedia accepted the suggestion and now lists the date. I also requested an article about Meta’s Muse chatbot, and that request is “In Review.”

SpaceXAI didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment, and Elon Musk still hasn’t posted on X about Grokipedia
since February
. (Musk claimed about a year ago that Grokipedia would be
“a massive improvement over Wikipedia.”
) However, Benji Taylor, the
head of design
at X and SpaceXAI, posted
last week
about Grokipedia,
saying that
“We haven’t forgotten about Grokipedia” and that “v0.2 will be better than ever” (even though v0.2 technically launched
in November
).
