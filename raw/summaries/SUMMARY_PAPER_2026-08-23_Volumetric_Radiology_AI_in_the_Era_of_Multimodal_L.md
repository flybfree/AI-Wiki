## Summary
The paper reviews over 200 publications up to July 2026 on volumetric radiology AI within multimodal large language models, focusing on representation mismatches and integration across clinical workflows. It categorizes literature into model-level, system-level, and application-level analyses, introducing a Claim-Design-Validation framework for assessing technical, workflow, and clinical claims.

## Key Takeaways
- Volumetric radiology demands full 3D spatial context and quantitative data that current MLLMs often ignore by using only 2D images or text.  
- The review distinguishes tasks where selected views suffice from those requiring native volumetric modeling.  
- A Claim-Design-Validation framework is proposed to ensure technical, workflow, and clinical claims are matched by appropriate design and validation.

## Context
Multimodal large language models have advanced beyond image classification to integrate diverse data types, yet their limited handling of 3D medical volumes hinders precise radiology interpretation. This paper highlights the gap between model capabilities and the need for volumetric fidelity in clinical settings.

## Implications
Practitioners must prioritize AI systems that preserve 3D information and support agentic workflows rather than relying on compressed views. The framework can guide rigorous validation, improving trust and adoption of volumetric radiology AI.
