## Summary
The paper proposes a platform that enables the exchange and management of AI models and related datasets in industrial settings by using an ontology to eliminate semantic gaps. It demonstrates how this platform supports transparent tracking, comparison, and reuse of assets. The study shows practical benefits through a real-time critical system use case.

## Key Takeaways
- The ontology provides a common vocabulary that aligns model and dataset descriptions across organizations, reducing ambiguity in asset exchange.
- The platform automates the discovery of relevant AI assets for a given task, improving efficiency and traceability.
- Real‑time critical systems can benefit from integrated model‑dataset management, enabling reliable performance under strict constraints.

## Context
AI research increasingly relies on sharing models and data, yet current workflows suffer from inconsistent definitions and fragmented repositories. This paper addresses those gaps by introducing an ontology‑driven framework that standardizes asset representation in industrial environments.

## Implications
For practitioners, the ontology simplifies integration of external AI components into existing pipelines without costly re‑engineering. Industries can adopt this platform to accelerate innovation while ensuring compliance and traceability across model lifecycles.
