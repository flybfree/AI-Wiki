## Summary
The paper introduces HyperANFIS, a hyperbolic extension of ANFIS that improves rule representation and interpretability. It achieves higher predictive accuracy while preserving the ability to generate clear IF-THEN rules. Experimental results show consistent superiority over standard ANFIS variants across multiple datasets.

## Key Takeaways
- HyperANFIS performs rule‑prototype learning, activation, and consequent aggregation in hyperbolic space, which strengthens fuzzy inference.
- The model retains the original IF-THEN rule generation capability, enhancing interpretability.
- Experiments demonstrate improved predictive performance and inter‑rule collaboration compared to Euclidean ANFIS baselines.

## Context
ANFIS is widely used for transparent AI reasoning but limited by Euclidean geometry constraints. Hyperbolic geometry offers richer representational capacity, making this work relevant for scalable interpretable systems.

## Implications
This approach could enable more accurate and explainable AI models in domains where rule transparency matters. Industry practitioners may adopt HyperANFIS to improve model performance without sacrificing interpretability.
