## Summary
This paper proposes TaxoScale, an automated pipeline that constructs a privacy‑and‑security taxonomy from mobile app reviews. By filtering millions of reviews and applying hierarchical clustering with LLM assistance, the system builds a scalable taxonomy that outperforms existing baselines on path, level, coverage, and novelty metrics.

## Key Takeaways
- The method filters over 600K app reviews to create a comprehensive corpus for taxonomy construction.
- TaxoScale extends an expert‑defined taxonomy using recursive hierarchical clustering and LLM‑generated node names.
- The pipeline achieves superior performance on path, level, coverage, and novelty metrics compared to automatic‑taxonomy baselines.

## Context
Mobile app review data represent a dynamic source of user feedback on privacy and security concerns. Existing taxonomies are manually curated and cannot adapt quickly to the volume and diversity of modern reviews. This work addresses the need for scalable AI‑driven taxonomy generation in large, real‑world datasets.

## Implications
Automated taxonomies can improve content analysis and recommendation systems by providing structured insights into user concerns. Practitioners can leverage TaxoScale to monitor emerging privacy issues and refine app security measures proactively.
