## Summary
The paper introduces HealthBench-Psych, a mental‑health subset of OpenAI's HealthBench that isolates performance in this high‑need domain. It reports 610 conversations (12.2% of the original corpus) and finds frontier models tied across judges with τ≥0.92.

## Key Takeaways
- The subset was derived using an LLM‑applied rubric to screen 5,000 physician‑rubric conversations for mental‑health relevance, resulting in 610 items.
- Two frontier models exhibit measurable refusal behavior during evaluation.
- Cross‑vendor judges rank models almost identically with τ≥0.92 indicating high consensus.

## Context
Mental health support is a growing use case for LLMs, yet existing benchmarks lack domain specificity and integration ease, making it difficult to compare performance across models in this critical area.

## Implications
This reusable resource helps developers benchmark mental‑health LLM performance fairly and transparently, guiding responsible deployment and informing industry standards.
