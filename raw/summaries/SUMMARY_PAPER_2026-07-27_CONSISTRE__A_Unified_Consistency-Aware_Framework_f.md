## Summary
The paper introduces CONSISTRE, a consistency-aware framework for document-level relation extraction that tackles the issue of contradictory triples generated by large language models. It combines constraint-aware prompting and iterative self-reflection to refine predictions without fine-tuning, and also injects consistency knowledge into open-source models via distillation and reinforcement learning. Experiments on DocRED show both tracks outperform baselines.

## Key Takeaways
- The inference-time track uses constraint-aware prompting and verification to reduce relational contradictions in black-box LLMs.
- Knowledge distillation from a powerful teacher combined with GRPO alignment improves consistency in smaller open-source models.
- Both approaches achieve higher F1 scores on DocRED while maintaining low deployment cost.

## Context
Document-level relation extraction is crucial for summarizing multi-entity interactions, yet current LLM outputs often violate logical constraints like transitivity. This work addresses a fundamental weakness of LLMs by integrating explicit consistency modeling into both inference and training pipelines.

## Implications
Practitioners can deploy consistent relational predictions using off-the-shelf models at minimal cost, while developers benefit from more reliable open-source alternatives. The framework sets a new standard for reliability in LLM-based information extraction tasks.
