## Summary
The paper introduces SmartWeatherAgent, a unified three-stage architecture designed to resolve contextualization and generalization deficits in tourism meteorological services by fusing rule-based methods with large language models. This system integrates intent recognition, hazard prediction using a LightGBM model enriched with highland-specific features, and reasoning-enhanced generation, achieving an F1-Macro score of 0.605 with 1.60 ms latency while employing a self-optimizing prompt loop that elevates warning quality scores by over 100%.
