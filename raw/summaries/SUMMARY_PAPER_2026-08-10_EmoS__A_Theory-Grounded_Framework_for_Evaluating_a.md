## Summary
The paper introduces EmoSBench and the EmoS evaluator to assess emotional intelligence in spoken language models using a theory‑grounded four‑branch framework. It demonstrates that even top models like GPT‑4o‑Audio score only 52.6% on human tasks, while EmoS reaches 83.8% accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- EmoSBench provides the first comprehensive benchmark covering perceiving, understanding, using and managing emotion across ten sub‑tasks.
- The evaluator model EmoS achieves near‑human performance with 83.8% accuracy through SFT and GRPO optimization.
- A reward system combining SEAR and RFR enforces accurate ordinal scoring and valid reasoning.

## Context
Emotional intelligence evaluation in AI remains fragmented, relying on limited paralinguistic metrics that do not reflect cognitive processes. This work establishes a systematic framework that aligns model behavior with human emotional reasoning.

## Implications
The findings suggest that current SLMs lack genuine EI capabilities, prompting industry to prioritize theory‑driven benchmarks. Practitioners can leverage EmoS as a reliable metric for developing emotionally intelligent dialogue systems in real‑world applications.
