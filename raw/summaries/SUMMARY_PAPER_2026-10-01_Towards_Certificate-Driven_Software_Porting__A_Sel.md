## Summary
The paper introduces Certificate-Driven Evolutionary Search (CDES), a novel framework designed to automate the porting and optimization of legacy scientific codebases by leveraging enforceable restrictions derived from failed LLM-generated candidates. Applied to CPU-to-GPU translation of Geant4 particle-simulation functions, CDES achieves significant performance gains, including speedups
