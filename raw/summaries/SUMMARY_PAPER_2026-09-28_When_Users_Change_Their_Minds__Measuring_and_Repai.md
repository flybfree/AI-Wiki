## Summary
This paper investigates "intent drift," a failure mode where LLM agents persistently rely on outdated user instructions even after those instructions are superseded during multi-turn interactions. The authors introduce IntentFlux, an executable benchmark designed to measure this phenomenon by embedding controlled intent changes into verifiable tasks while preserving standard evaluation metrics.
