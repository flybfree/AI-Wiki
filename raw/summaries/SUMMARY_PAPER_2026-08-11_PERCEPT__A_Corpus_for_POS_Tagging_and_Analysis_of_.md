## Summary
This paper introduces PERCEPT, a large-scale Persian‑English code‑mixed corpus of 6,800 social media posts annotated with Universal Dependencies part‑of‑speech tags for mixed words. The study shows that nouns dominate the POS distribution and that positional patterns are stable across platforms while triggering effects vary.

## Key Takeaways
- PERCEPT provides the first publicly available large‑scale Persian‑English code‑mixed dataset with UD POS annotations, enabling systematic linguistic analysis of mixed terms.
- Nouns account for the majority of code‑mixed word tags, indicating their primary role in mixing strategies across social media platforms.
- The positional distribution of mixed words remains consistent across X, Instagram, and Digikala, whereas triggering effects are most pronounced on Digikala.

## Context
The paper addresses a gap in multilingual NLP where existing resources lack UD annotations for code‑mixed terms, limiting the development of syntax‑aware models. By supplying annotated data, it supports research on language contact, dependency parsing, and topic modeling in mixed linguistic contexts.

## Implications
For AI practitioners, PERCEPT offers a benchmark dataset to evaluate POS tagging and dependency parsing on real‑world code‑mixing scenarios. Its availability encourages further studies that improve cross‑lingual models and understand user communication patterns in multilingual digital spaces.
