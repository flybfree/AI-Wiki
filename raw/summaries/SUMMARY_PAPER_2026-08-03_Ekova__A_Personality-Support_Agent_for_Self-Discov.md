## Summary
The paper introduces Ekova, a personality-support agent that helps users achieve self‑discovery rather than merely alleviating distress. It presents the DSD dataset, DeepSupport model with OrthoTune training, and the unified Ekova system, achieving an average relative gain of 16.3% over prompt‑based baselines.

## Key Takeaways
- The authors define Personality Support (PS) as a distinct paradigm focused on cognitive clarity and self‑articulation rather than emotional symptom relief.
- DeepSupport uses OrthoTune, a PS‑tailored framework with style‑specific adapters and a consistency regularizer, to train multi‑persona models that outperform baselines.
- Ekova unifies five personas into a persistent agent with cross‑session memory, enabling adaptive routing and user‑selected persona styles.

## Context
Current emotional support AI systems prioritize immediate distress alleviation, leaving self‑reflection untouched. This work expands the paradigm to include cognitive clarity, aligning with broader trends toward holistic user assistance in conversational agents.

## Implications
For practitioners, Ekova demonstrates that multi‑persona, memory‑aware agents can improve engagement and insight generation. The field may shift from purely symptom‑focused support to integrated self‑discovery tools, influencing design of AI companions for mental wellness.
