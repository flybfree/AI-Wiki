## Summary
This paper proposes a fast and high‑performance fluid antenna (FA) reconfiguration strategy for low‑altitude wireless networks using multi‑agent reinforcement learning. The authors demonstrate that joint optimization of FA positions and beamforming can boost the system sum‑rate by 118.5% compared with fixed‑position operation, thanks to millisecond‑scale dynamic reconfigurations and adaptive steering toward mobile aerial users.

## Key Takeaways
- The electromagnetic digital twin (EM‑DT) framework enables a two‑stage transfer learning approach that bridges the sim‑to‑real gap for FA network simulations.  
- Multi‑agent reinforcement learning is employed to jointly optimize both antenna positions and beamforming, achieving significant performance gains under dynamic air‑ground interference.  
- The proposed method supports millisecond‑scale reconfiguration of FA arrays, allowing rapid adaptation to abrupt blockages and heterogeneous interference.

The integration of fluid antennas into low‑altitude networks represents a frontier in AI‑driven wireless system design, where reinforcement learning is used for real‑time control.
