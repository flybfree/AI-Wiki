## Summary  
The paper evaluates the IT security risks inherent to agentic AI coding, outlines a mitigation concept that preserves its benefits, and presents an implementation overview as a step toward responsible use. The assessment identifies potential vulnerabilities such as unauthorized code execution and supply chain contamination.

## Key Takeaways  
- Agentic AI introduces severe new risks that outweigh its advantages, including the possibility of autonomous agents performing malicious actions without human oversight.  
- Mitigation must be implemented to prevent these risks while preserving productivity gains, ensuring that security controls do not hinder development speed.  
- The proposed framework offers a concrete approach for early evaluation.

## Context  
Agentic AI represents the next evolution beyond LLM‑driven assistance, offering autonomous code generation. This shift accelerates development but also expands attack surfaces in software supply chains. The rapid adoption of agentic AI is outpacing existing security tooling and policies.

## Implications  
Practitioners must adopt security‑aware practices to avoid costly breaches and reputational damage. Failure to implement safeguards could lead to widespread breaches affecting multiple organizations.
