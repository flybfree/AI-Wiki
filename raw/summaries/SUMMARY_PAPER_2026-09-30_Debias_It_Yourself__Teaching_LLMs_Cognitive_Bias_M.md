## Summary
The paper introduces "Debias It Yourself" (DIY), a cognitively grounded framework that adapts five validated human debiasing interventions for large language models using Show, Train, and Revise paradigms. Experimental results demonstrate that the Train+Revise and Revise approaches achieve state-of-the-art performance across multiple bias benchmarks while maintaining high reasoning accuracy, effectively minimizing stereotypical outputs without compromising model capabilities.

## Key Takeaways
- The DIY framework bridges social psychology and AI by translating five validated human debiasing interventions into LLM procedures, delivered through three distinct paradigms: Show (in-context examples), Train (instruction tuning), and Revise (guided self-revision).
- Evaluation across three models, five bias benchmarks, and eleven baselines reveals that the Train+Revise combination and Revise alone achieve the top average ranks, leading in the bias-reasoning tradeoff with results as low as 2% bias while preserving 90% reasoning accuracy.
- The approach demonstrates strong generalization
