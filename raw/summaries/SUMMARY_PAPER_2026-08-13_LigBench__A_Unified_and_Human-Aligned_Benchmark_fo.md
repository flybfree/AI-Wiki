## Summary
This paper introduces LigBench, a unified evaluation benchmark for measuring the quality of AI‑generated research ideas, and PAIR‑IQ, a dataset designed to train models that can judge idea pairs objectively. Experiments show that LigBench provides stable, interpretable scores aligned with expert judgments, while models trained on PAIR‑IQ achieve better ranking accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- LigBench offers a consistent scoring framework across diverse idea distributions, reducing reliance on ad‑hoc LLM evaluations.
- The PAIR‑IQ dataset enables fine‑grained pairwise comparisons that improve model robustness and ranking performance.
- Model training on PAIR‑IQ leads to higher alignment with human expert judgments, establishing a benchmark for objective research idea assessment.

## Context
The rapid growth of large language models has spurred interest in automating research idea generation. However, existing evaluation methods lack standardization, making it difficult to compare results across studies or tools. This work addresses that gap by creating a shared metric and dataset.

## Implications
LigBench sets a new standard for evaluating AI‑driven creativity, encouraging developers to adopt objective benchmarks. Practitioners can leverage PAIR‑IQ to fine‑tune models, leading to more reliable idea generation pipelines in academic and industry settings.
