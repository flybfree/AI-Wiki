## Summary
The paper introduces a deep learning framework combining CNNs, LSTMs and recurrence plot analysis to automatically characterize EEG biomarkers in Fragile X Syndrome. It demonstrates that integrating alpha and gamma components with hybrid CNN‑LSTM architecture yields superior performance over single‑modality baselines.

## Key Takeaways
- The hybrid model leverages spatial‑spectral textures from CNNs, temporal dynamics from LSTMs and nonlinear recurrence structures encoded by RP images to capture complex EEG patterns.
- Gamma frequency features alone provide strong discriminative power for FXS patients compared with alpha or other modalities.
- Joint alpha‑gamma integration yields the highest overall classification accuracy, highlighting synergistic benefits of multimodal representation.

## Context
This work advances AI applications in neuroimaging by demonstrating that nonlinear time‑frequency representations can be processed end‑to‑end without external feature engineering. It aligns with trends toward interpretable deep models and closed‑loop biomarker pipelines.

## Implications
For clinicians and researchers, the framework offers a scalable tool for early diagnosis, patient stratification and longitudinal monitoring of FXS using routine EEG recordings. The method could be integrated into clinical decision support systems to personalize treatment strategies.
