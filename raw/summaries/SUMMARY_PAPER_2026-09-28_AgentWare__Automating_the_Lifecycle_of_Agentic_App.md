## Summary
AgentWare is an AgenticOps framework designed to automate the entire lifecycle of LLM-enabled agentic applications across heterogeneous Edge-to-Cloud infrastructures, addressing challenges related to hardware diversity, deployment complexity, and fragmented tooling. The system provides a unified pipeline for provisioning execution environments, distributing agent components, collecting comprehensive telemetry, and performing automated semantic evaluation via LLM-as-a-Judge workflows. Demonstrations on real-world infrastructure show that AgentWare significantly reduces manual operational overhead while enabling reproducible analysis of correctness, performance, resource usage, and energy consumption.
