## Summary
The paper introduces Document-Mediated Reinforcement Learning, a framework that treats skill document optimization as a sequence of structured editing actions guided by an upper-level agent. It demonstrates that DMRL improves advertising recommendation metrics compared to existing approaches.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework separates the editing decision from evaluation using A/B testing to enable credit assignment.
- Dual-Relative Policy Optimization provides robust advantage estimation, reducing risk in long-term outcomes.
- Long-term Reward Predictor models population heterogeneity with disentangled representations and cross-attention for accurate future reward forecasting.

## Context
This work advances skill-based AI by formalizing how document edits are optimized through reinforcement learning. It bridges the gap between large language model prompting and systematic performance measurement, offering a scalable method for continuous improvement in recommendation systems.

## Implications
For advertising platforms, DMRL can automate content refinement while preserving user experience and commercial goals. Practitioners can adopt this framework to reduce manual tuning effort and adapt quickly to market changes.
