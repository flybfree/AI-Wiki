## Summary
This paper introduces Jiuge‑Tuiqiao, an interactive human‑AI system that refines classical Chinese poetry by combining user control with AI assistance. The triadic model—user‑driven, ancient‑guided evidence, and AI‑assisted generation—enables users to lock characters or lines while receiving real‑time prosody feedback and interpretable refinement suggestions based on high‑frequency collocations and PPL‑ranked classical lines.

## Key Takeaways
- The system shifts the AI role from autonomous generator to supportive assistant, preserving user creative agency.  
- Real‑time prosody feedback is provided using structured knowledge extracted from classical encyclopedias, making refinements transparent and grounded in linguistic patterns.  
- Preliminary results show improved controllability, interpretability, and higher user engagement compared with one‑shot generation models.

## Context
Current AI poetry tools often produce complete poems in a single pass, limiting the collaborative flow that traditional Tuiqiao practices require. This work addresses the gap by designing an iterative interface that respects classical constraints while leveraging modern machine learning for targeted suggestions.

## Implications
For scholars and practitioners of classical literature, Jiuge‑Tuiqiao offers a bridge between historical poetic methods and contemporary AI, fostering deeper user involvement. In industry, such interpretable systems could inspire new human‑AI collaboration frameworks that balance automation with creative control.
