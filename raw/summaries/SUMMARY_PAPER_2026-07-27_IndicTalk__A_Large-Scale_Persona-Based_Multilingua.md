## Summary
This paper introduces IndicTalk, a large‑scale multilingual conversational corpus that gathers over 1.3 million event‑grounded multi‑turn dialogues across 18 varieties of the 9 major Indic languages. The dataset demonstrates fluent code‑mixed dialogue in both native script and Romanized forms, addressing a critical gap for low‑resource Indic language AI.

## Key Takeaways
- The corpus spans more than 132 8604 conversations, showcasing extensive coverage of Indian languages such as Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and Malayalam.  
- It was generated via an automated pipeline that merges real‑world news grounding with persona‑conditioned generation using multilingual LLMs, ensuring natural code‑mixing patterns.  
- Both automatic metrics and human evaluations confirm high fluency, coherence, and authenticity across script variants.

## Context
The surge of large language models has driven demand for diverse dialogue data, yet most resources focus on English or widely spoken languages. IndicTalk fills this void by providing a comprehensive, multilingual dataset that can be used to train and benchmark conversational AI for under‑represented Indian languages.

## Implications
Researchers and industry practitioners can now evaluate model performance on realistic code‑mixed interactions without relying on limited monolingual corpora. This advancement supports the development of inclusive chatbots that serve India’s linguistic diversity, fostering broader adoption of multilingual conversational systems.
