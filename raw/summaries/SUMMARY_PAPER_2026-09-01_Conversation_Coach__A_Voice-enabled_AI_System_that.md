## Summary
The paper introduces Conversation Coach, a voice‑enabled AI that lets managers rehearse tough workplace dialogues in realistic spoken form. It tackles low latency, adaptive conversational agents, and personalized feedback while comparing two technical architectures. The end‑to‑end speech‑to‑speech model delivers three times lower median (P50) latency with barge‑in capability at an estimated eightfold cost reduction.

## Key Takeaways
- The system achieves 3× lower median (P50) latency compared to a cascaded approach, enabling real‑time interaction and native barge‑in functionality.
- It uses configurable bot personalities to simulate diverse employee types, providing adaptive conversations that mimic realistic workplace dynamics.
- Deployment with the cascaded architecture reached 40,000+ managers over six months, showing selective adoption for high‑stakes conversations.

## Context
Voice‑first conversational AI is emerging as a scalable alternative to text‑based chatbots for training and coaching. This work demonstrates that end‑to‑end speech pipelines can balance performance and cost while supporting nuanced human‑like dialogue.

## Implications
Managers gain confidence through realistic rehearsal, potentially reducing turnover of high performers and improving employee development outcomes. The technology also lowers the financial burden on organizations by cutting implementation costs significantly.
