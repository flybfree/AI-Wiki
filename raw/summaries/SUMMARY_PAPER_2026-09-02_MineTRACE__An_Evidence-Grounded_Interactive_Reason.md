## Summary
MineTRACE is a web‑based platform designed to provide evidence‑grounded prospectivity maps for eight mineral commodities, allowing users to explore data and query locations through natural language. The system combines multi‑source geochemical, geophysical, and geological evidence into interpretable scores using a transparent expert tree.

## Key Takeaways
- The system integrates heterogeneous evidence into interpretable prospectivity scores using a transparent expert tree.
- It achieves high spatial accuracy with AUC up to 0.917 across different test scenarios.
- Users can query locations via natural language and receive grounded explanations from the analysis pipeline.

## Context
This work advances AI‑driven geoscience by merging machine learning with geological knowledge, making complex data sets accessible for exploration teams worldwide.

## Implications
Mineral prospectors gain transparent scoring that reduces reliance on opaque heatmaps, fostering trust in automated assessments and supporting more efficient exploration decisions.
