## Summary
The paper introduces SafeStep, an AI-powered travel assistance system designed to help elderly individuals with frailty or dementia navigate urban environments safely. It combines a novel travel graph representation with predictive failure modeling and personalized interventions to maximize destination reachability. Field testing on 26 journeys showed that integrating Anticip8 for failure prediction with GPT-based intervention evaluation yields the most reliable performance, while users reported higher confidence during travel.

## Key Takeaways
- SafeStep uses a travel graph that links route planning with predictive modelling to anticipate potential failures and suggest targeted interventions.
- The system evaluates interventions using GPT models to estimate their impact on outcome probabilities, selecting those that maximize success likelihood.
- User feedback indicates improved perceived safety and confidence during travel, though interface usability remains a challenge for the target demographic.

## Context
The integration of large language models with behavioral prediction engines like Anticip8 represents an emerging trend in assistive AI, aiming to personalize support based on individual risk profiles. ## Implications
SafeStep demonstrates a scalable framework that could be adapted to other domains such as mental health, career coaching, or addiction treatment where personalized risk assessment is needed. Its success suggests that AI‑driven intervention selection can enhance outcomes and user confidence across various assistive applications.
