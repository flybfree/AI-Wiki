## Summary
The paper introduces AgentPanel, a multi‑agent forum that enables human researchers to explore scientific questions through asynchronous discussion among heterogeneous AI agents. Offline experiments demonstrate that the system generates higher‑quality ideas and broader exploration than a centralized multi‑agent baseline. A human study with twenty participants shows strong preference for the platform’s perspective diversity and usability.

## Key Takeaways
- AgentPanel outperforms a centralized multi‑agent debate baseline in idea quality, as measured by offline experiments.
- Human participants value the platform for providing diverse research perspectives and supporting exploratory brainstorming.
- In experience comparisons, 65% of users prefer AgentPanel for both breadth of directions and suitability for early‑stage exploration.

## Context
Current AI tools often centralize reasoning in a single model or limited group chats, restricting the range of ideas that can be surfaced. This work addresses that limitation by distributing discussion across multiple agents within a forum interface.

## Implications
The platform suggests a scalable way to harness collective intelligence for scientific brainstorming, potentially accelerating discovery in fields like biology and physics. Practitioners may adopt AgentPanel as an alternative to single‑LLM or ad‑hoc collaboration methods.
