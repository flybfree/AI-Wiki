## Summary
This paper introduces Diffusion Layer Integrated Gradients (DLIG), a novel token attribution framework designed for diffusion language models that extends the principles of Integrated Gradients across arbitrary network layers and denoising steps. DLIG quantifies how these models progressively commit to specific completions, offering a mathematically rigorous method that satisfies key axioms such as completeness, implementation invariance, linearity, and symmetry preservation. The authors demonstrate DLIG's utility in analyzing generation dynamics across tasks like word-sense disambiguation and multi-hop reasoning, providing an efficient tool for probing mechanistic hypotheses without the cost of full interventional analysis.
