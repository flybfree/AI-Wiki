Title: China's DeepSeek releases preview of long-awaited V4 model as AI
Article text:

## Summary
DeepSeek unveiled a preview of its V4 large language model, an open‑source upgrade that builds on the earlier R1 breakthrough. The release highlights lower inference costs and stronger performance in agent tasks, positioning Chinese AI as a competitive alternative to Western models. Analysts note that while V4 may not shock markets like R1 did, it intensifies domestic competition and signals broader shifts in global AI spending.

## Key Takeaways
- DeepSeek’s V4 model is open‑source, allowing developers to download, run locally, and modify the code, similar to its previous releases.  
- The model offers lower inference costs than earlier versions and achieves strong performance on agent‑based tasks, knowledge processing, and inference.  
- Chinese chipmaker Huawei confirmed that its Ascend AI processors can support V4, underscoring a move toward domestic AI hardware.  
- Analysts predict V4 will not have the same market impact as R1 but will increase pressure on global AI spending strategies.

## Context
The rapid rise of Chinese open‑source LLMs such as DeepSeek’s R1 and V3 has challenged U.S. leadership in AI, prompting investors to reassess spending priorities. This article follows a year of model releases that have reshaped market dynamics and highlighted the importance of cost efficiency and local chip usage.

## Implications
For developers, V4’s native compatibility with domestic chips could accelerate AI sovereignty and reduce reliance on Nvidia hardware. For investors, the heightened competition signals potential volatility in Chinese AI stocks while prompting global firms to diversify their AI infrastructure strategies.
