## Summary
The paper presents a prototype that merges classical econometric methods with AI techniques to forecast interest rates from multiple viewpoints for banking asset management. It demonstrates how Bayesian vector autoregression combined with topic modeling and sentiment analysis can generate transparent scenario analyses. The system is tested in a European bank, showing improved precision and flexibility compared to single‑model forecasts.

## Key Takeaways
- The prototype integrates AI-driven document analysis such as topic modeling and sentiment analysis to detect early monetary policy trends.
- Bayesian vector autoregression enables simulation‑based multi‑perspective scenario planning that consolidates separate economic signals.
- The integrated platform provides interpretable, transparent outputs that enhance evidence‑based decision making for risk managers.
