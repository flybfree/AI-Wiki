## Summary
Guixu introduces a valuation-driven data discovery system that enables autonomous agents to evaluate and procure datasets in a budget‑aware manner while ensuring trustworthy feedback. The paper demonstrates how Guixu moves beyond simple keyword retrieval by integrating proxy‑label propagation, multi‑round knapsack optimization, and on‑chain attestation signals for verifiable transactions.

## Key Takeaways
- Guixu uses a three‑phase valuation pipeline that propagates proxy labels to estimate task utility and selects cost‑effective datasets under budget constraints.  
- The system incorporates an agentic payment protocol that allows budget‑constrained procurement workflows on an on‑chain data market.  
- On‑chain attestation signals provide verifiable feedback, allowing agents to trust the provenance and performance of retrieved data.

## Context
