## Summary
AlgoEvo introduces a unified agentic framework that transforms automated algorithm discovery into an interactive, knowledge-accumulating process driven by large language models. By dynamically inspecting, diagnosing, and editing code based on runtime feedback, the system overcomes the rigid control flows of traditional search pipelines. Across six benchmark tasks, AlgoEvo matches or exceeds specialized methods while requiring significantly fewer evaluations and reduced token consumption, demonstrating strong intra-task learning and cross-paradigm transfer capabilities.
