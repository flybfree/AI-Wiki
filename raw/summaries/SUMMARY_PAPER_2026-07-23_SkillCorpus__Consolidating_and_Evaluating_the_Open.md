## Summary  
The paper introduces SkillCorpus, a framework that consolidates and evaluates the open skill ecosystem for LLM agents. It filters millions of crowd‑sourced skills into a curated corpus and demonstrates consistent performance gains across benchmarks.

## Key Takeaways  
- The pipeline reduces 821,000 crawled skills to 96,401 organized by taxonomy and quality metrics.  
- Retrieval‑based matching yields task‑relevant skill pairs that improve agent behavior on three evaluation sets.  
- Gains are bounded by coverage limits of the corpus and by harness constraints.

## Context  
The rapid growth of open skill repositories has created a fragmented landscape where agents cannot reliably reuse procedures. This work addresses the need for a unified, high‑quality resource to guide practical deployment.

## Implications  
Practitioners can now access a vetted skill set that directly lifts agent performance, while developers must monitor coverage gaps to avoid diminishing returns.
