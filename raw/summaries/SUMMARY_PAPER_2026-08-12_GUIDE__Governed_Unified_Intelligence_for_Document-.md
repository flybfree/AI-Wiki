## Summary
The paper presents GUIDE, a governed multi‑agent framework that automates the creation of enterprise guideline artifacts from heterogeneous documents. By integrating rule‑based parsing, vision‑language models, and provenance tracking, it achieves high accuracy and rapid turnaround compared to manual processes.

## Key Takeaways
- The system reaches 96% document success rate across 120 real‑world guidelines, indicating robust handling of complex multimodal content.  
- It extracts a total of 3,896 rules with 71.4% auto‑approved, showing strong rule detection and confidence scoring.  
- Turnaround time drops to 40–125 minutes per document, a dramatic reduction from the typical 2–3 days required manually.

## Context
Current AI systems excel at text or image extraction but struggle with combined narrative tables and embedded images in enterprise guidelines. Most solutions stop at extraction without validation or workflow governance, leaving organizations with fragmented outputs and manual reconciliation.

## Implications
Automated guideline generation can streamline compliance across departments, lower operational costs, and ensure consistent artifact quality. Practitioners can adopt GUIDE to scale rule‑driven processes while maintaining auditability and traceability.
