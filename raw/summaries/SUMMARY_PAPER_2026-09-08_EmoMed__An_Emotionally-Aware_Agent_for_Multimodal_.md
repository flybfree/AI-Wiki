## Summary
EmoMed is a multimodal medical consultation agent that tailors its responses to users’ emotional states while preserving clinical accuracy. The system detects affect indicators such as anxiety or urgency from text and images, then adjusts tone, structure, and detail accordingly. Evaluation across multiple large language models shows emotionally adaptive answers outperform neutral baselines without sacrificing factual reliability.

## Key Takeaways
- EmoMed integrates real‑time affect detection to modify response style based on user emotions, ensuring communication remains supportive yet appropriate for the clinical context.  
- The agent employs a dual retrieval pipeline—web fact‑checking and an API‑driven medical knowledge base—to guarantee that any information presented is up‑to‑date and verifiable.  
- Controlled human studies indicate participants perceive higher empathy and clarity in emotionally adaptive interactions while still trusting the factual correctness of the advice.

## Context
The rapid expansion of large language models in healthcare has focused on delivering accurate, concise answers, yet few address how emotional cues influence therapeutic communication. EmoMed bridges this gap by demonstrating that affective awareness can be safely incorporated into AI‑driven medical support without compromising safety standards.

## Implications
For clinicians and developers, EmoMed suggests a path toward more human‑like patient interactions where AI respects both emotional needs and clinical precision. This could enhance adherence to treatment plans and improve overall patient satisfaction in digital health platforms.
