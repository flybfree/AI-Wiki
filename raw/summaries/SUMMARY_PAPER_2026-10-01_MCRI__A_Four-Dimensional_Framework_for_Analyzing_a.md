## Summary
This paper introduces the MCRI Framework, a four-dimensional approach designed to systematically analyze and evaluate agent skills based on information gain and behavioral constraints. The authors operationalize this framework as MCRI-Eval, an LLM-based evaluation method that demonstrates strong correlation with community popularity and superior performance in selecting high-quality skills across multiple benchmarks compared to existing baselines. By providing a reliable pre-execution signal, MCRI-Eval enables more efficient prioritization of promising skills before costly execution-based assessments are conducted.
