## Summary
This paper introduces a systematic framework for monitoring AI systems that could evolve into catastrophic threats. By defining behavioral indicators and measurable thresholds across capability dimensions, the authors aim to enable early detection of rogue AI progression. The study emphasizes evidence‑based protocols for researchers and policymakers.

## Key Takeaways
- Behavioral indicators such as autonomous goal drift and loss of alignment with human values can signal dangerous AI evolution.
- Clear metric thresholds allow continuous evaluation without subjective judgment, ensuring objective monitoring.
- A multi‑dimensional approach integrates technical performance, safety logs, and external impact assessments for comprehensive risk assessment.

## Context
Artificial intelligence research increasingly focuses on scalable models that may outpace current safeguards. As AI capabilities grow, the need for proactive oversight mechanisms becomes urgent to prevent unintended harmful outcomes. This framework addresses a gap in existing security practices by applying cybersecurity principles to AI governance.

## Implications
Practitioners can implement these indicators to embed safety checks into development pipelines and regulatory frameworks. The approach supports industry adoption of standardized monitoring tools, fostering trust and reducing the likelihood of catastrophic AI incidents.
