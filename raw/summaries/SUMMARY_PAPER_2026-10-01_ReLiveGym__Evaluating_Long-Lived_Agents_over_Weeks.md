## Summary
ReLiveGym introduces a novel diagnostic evaluation environment designed to test large language model agents over extended periods using chronologically replayed real-world data streams. The study reveals that action timing mechanisms and hindsight feedback are critical design factors for maintaining agent performance in dynamic, long-horizon tasks where environments continuously evolve.
