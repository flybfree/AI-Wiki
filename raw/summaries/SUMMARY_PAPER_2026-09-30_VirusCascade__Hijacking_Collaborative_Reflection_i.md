## Summary
The paper introduces VirusCascade, a novel attack against LLM-powered agentic recommender systems that exploits the collaborative reflection mechanism to hijack recommendations by rationalizing adversarial evidence across autonomous agents. By leveraging reflective persistence and cross-agent propagation, this black-box targeted promotion attack achieves state-of-the-art exposure metrics while maintaining stealth, revealing critical vulnerabilities in recurrent multi-agent amplification pathways.

## Key Takeaways
- LLM-powered agentic recommender systems utilize collaborative reflection to dynamically refine semantic states through recurrent interactions, creating a systemic vulnerability where adversarial evidence injected into a single agent is rationalized and written back to memory, allowing it to propagate to other agents via interaction contexts in a process termed "collaborative-reflection hijacking."
