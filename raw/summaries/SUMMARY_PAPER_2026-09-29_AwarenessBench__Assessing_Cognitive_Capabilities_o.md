## Summary
This paper introduces AwarenessBench, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate the cognitive capabilities of large language models across metacognition, self-awareness, social awareness, and situational awareness. The study assesses 18 state-of-the-art models using over 14,000 samples covering 15 cognitive functions, revealing that while advanced models generally outperform random baselines and surpass human averages overall, significant gaps remain in metacognitive and self-aware tasks compared to human performance. Furthermore, the research demonstrates that improvements in language modeling or reasoning do not automatically equate to enhanced cognitive awareness, establishing awareness as a distinct capability requiring specific evaluation.
