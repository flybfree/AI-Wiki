## Summary
This paper introduces HABIT, a comprehensive taxonomy of 23 behavioral axes across five categories designed to characterize how LLM agents perform everyday tasks beyond mere task success. Using this framework, the authors develop AgentHABIT, a benchmark that profiles the behavioral tendencies of 18 models on 86 tasks, revealing distinct patterns such as GPT and Claude models explicitly stating assumptions while Qwen and Google models often omit them. The study demonstrates that these behavioral profiles are robust across different task sets and persist even after interventions
