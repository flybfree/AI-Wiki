## Summary
This paper introduces a five‑stage AI framework for automatically harvesting Indonesian cultural heritage from the open web, aiming to overcome coverage, integrity and completeness gaps in the existing manual digital library. The framework combines deterministic orchestration with agentic decision‑making to ensure each entry is enriched with deeper metadata while preserving human curation roles.

## Key Takeaways
- The model uses a subcritical branching crawl process that requires periodic re‑seeding to maintain coverage across languages and sites, ensuring no region of knowledge is left untouched.  
- Bayesian fusion of multiple sources raises publication thresholds for sacred categories, preventing the AI from overwriting authentic content with low‑quality or inappropriate data.  
- Idempotent upserts guarantee exactly‑once updates, so human contributions remain intact while machine actions are logged in a transactional outbox.

## Context
The work situates AI‑driven cultural preservation within the broader challenge of scaling open knowledge without eroding authenticity, highlighting how algorithmic efficiency can complement rather than replace human expertise. It reflects current trends toward autonomous data pipelines that balance breadth and depth in digital archives.
