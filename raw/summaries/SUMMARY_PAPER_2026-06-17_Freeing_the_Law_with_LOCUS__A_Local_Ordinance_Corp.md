# Summary: 2026-06-17 Freeing The Law With Locus  A Local Ordinance Corp


## Summary
This paper introduces LOCUS, a large-scale corpus of U.S. local ordinances that fills a gap in legal AI training data by providing machine‑readable access to municipal and county codes. The raw dataset covers 9,239 jurisdictions, while the count­y‑harmonized layer serves the most populous 2,309 counties. Researchers can download LOCUS‑v1 from Hugging Face for analysis.

## Key Takeaways
- The corpus aggregates nearly all publicly available ordinance codes, enabling a comprehensive view of everyday regulations such as zoning and animal control.
- OCR preprocessing handles the diverse document formats that previously prevented bulk extraction of local law.
- ModernBERT classifiers are trained to score ordinances on dimensions like opacity and paternalism, offering new research metrics for legal AI.

## Context
Legal artificial intelligence relies heavily on large, structured text corpora; however, most existing datasets focus on statutes and case law. Local ordinances remain fragmented across vendor sites, limiting their utility for scalable legal analysis. LOCUS addresses this imbalance by creating a unified, count­y‑harmonized resource that can be leveraged for both research and practical applications.

## Implications
The availability of a machine‑readable local law corpus will accelerate the development of AI tools that interpret municipal regulations, improving transparency and reducing bias in automated decision‑making. Practitioners can integrate these models to offer citizens clearer guidance on compliance with neighborhood codes.
