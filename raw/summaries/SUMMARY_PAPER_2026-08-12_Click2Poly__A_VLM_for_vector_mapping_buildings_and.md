## Summary
Click2Poly is a human‑in‑the‑loop AI assistant that speeds up the manual editing of building and wall vector layers in GIS workflows. By extending the Florence-2 Vision Language Model (VLM), it allows users to click on map features and have the model directly modify the corresponding vector layer, reducing reliance on labor‑intensive quality control.

## Key Takeaways
- Click2Poly integrates a VLM with QGIS, enabling real‑time editing of building and wall vectors through user clicks.  
- The system automates the correction of complex extraction cases that previously required manual intervention.  
- Implementation as a plugin demonstrates practical deployment in production GIS environments.

## Context
The field of geospatial AI faces a persistent bottleneck where automated vector mapping is still dependent on human oversight for accuracy and compliance with cartographic standards. Click2Poly addresses this by embedding language‑model capabilities directly into the editing interface, merging vision understanding with spatial data manipulation.

## Implications
For GIS practitioners, Click2Poly promises faster project turnaround times and lower error rates, making high‑quality vector mapping more scalable. The approach may inspire similar human‑assisted AI tools across other domain‑specific workflows that combine image analysis with precise editing tasks.
