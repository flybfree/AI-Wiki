## Summary
This paper introduces the eROI framework, a method for evaluating AI projects by separating value if successful, likelihood of success, and investment required. The authors demonstrate that coarse business-level ratings can identify strong versus weak bets without precise ROI calculations.  

## Key Takeaways
- Teams must ask how valuable the product would be if it worked before judging its probability of success or cost.  
- The framework breaks the circular problem of estimating ROI by evaluating each component separately, allowing a clear decision loop.  
- After ranking ideas, the approach encourages building a portfolio rather than funding only the top-ranked project.  

## Context
AI adoption in enterprises often stalls because executives cannot reliably predict which projects will deliver value. Traditional ROI models assume certainty about outcomes, creating a chicken‑and‑egg dilemma that discourages experimentation. This paper offers a pragmatic alternative for navigating uncertainty at the business level.  

## Implications
The eROI framework enables managers to prioritize AI initiatives based on qualitative insights rather than speculative forecasts. By focusing on portfolio strategy and separating value, likelihood, and cost, firms can reduce risk while still pursuing innovative projects.
