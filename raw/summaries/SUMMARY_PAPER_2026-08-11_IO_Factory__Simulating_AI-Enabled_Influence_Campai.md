## Summary  
IO Factory is an AI‑driven framework that simulates information and influence campaigns as continuous, traceable processes involving coordinated agents. The paper demonstrates that the system can run up to 100 000 simulated agents while producing inspectable evidence of exposure and measured belief changes across planning, platform action, interpretation, measurement, and adaptation phases.

## Key Takeaways  
- IO Factory integrates actor roles, platform actions, exposure records, structured model‑based evaluations, and configurable population changes to capture the full lifecycle of a campaign.  
- The framework enables reproducible research by recording all parameters—actors, objectives, action constraints, exposure paths, and measurement rules—used in each simulation run.  
- Experiments show that IO Factory can execute long timelines at scale and reliably track belief variable movement, providing inspectable evidence of influence.

## Context  
The rise of AI swarms challenges traditional detection methods for coordinated digital manipulation, which often rely on isolated messages. This work addresses the need for a systematic tool that models the entire campaign lifecycle within a controlled environment, reflecting real‑world complexities of platform feedback and adaptive behavior.

## Implications  
For researchers, IO Factory offers a reproducible sandbox to test hypotheses about influence dynamics without ethical concerns. Practitioners can leverage its traceable outputs to design defenses against AI‑enabled manipulation, informing policy and industry practices in the era of automated social media campaigns.
