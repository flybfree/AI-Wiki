## Summary
The paper introduces a hospital‑specific, compliance‑first agentic AI architecture that integrates three interoperable layers to overcome siloed point solutions in healthcare AI. Using a synthetic yet realistic dataset and an open prototype, it shows end‑to‑end orchestration of triage risk prediction, workflow optimization, and compliance logging while cutting turnaround times and manual effort.

## Key Takeaways
- The architecture adds an Agent Orchestration Layer that enables multi‑agent workflows across clinical, operational, and financial domains.  
- A Compliance and Policy Layer centralizes policy‑as‑code covering HIPAA, GDPR, the EU AI Act, DISHA Act, India’s DPDP Act, and ISO/IEC standards.  
- The Privacy‑Preserving Data Fabric combines federated learning, differential privacy, and secure enclaves within the Hospital Information Management System to maintain policy‑guarded data access.

## Context
Healthcare AI is expanding rapidly but most implementations remain isolated, leading to duplicated effort and compliance gaps. This research addresses those silos by proposing a layered framework that aligns technical capabilities with regulatory requirements, offering a scalable model for hospitals moving beyond ad‑hoc tools.

## Implications
Hospitals can adopt this blueprint to build ROI‑focused AI platforms that are globally compliant and adaptable across on‑premise, hybrid, or cloud environments. The approach reduces risk of pilot failure by embedding governance into the system design, encouraging broader enterprise adoption of AI in healthcare.
