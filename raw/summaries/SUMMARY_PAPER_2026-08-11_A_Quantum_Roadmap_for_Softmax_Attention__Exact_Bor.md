## Summary
The paper proposes a quantum realization of softmax attention where inputs and outputs lie on the probability simplex, showing that each component can be realized exactly via quantum operations. It identifies attention scores as Hadamard‑test statistics derived from amplitude‑encoded inputs. The exponential softmax corresponds to a cosine‑squared family generated by Born‑rule measurements with exact bijections.

## Key Takeaways
- Attention scores are Hadamard‑test statistics on block‑encoded projections of amplitude‑encoded inputs.
- The exponential softmax is the interior of a cosine‑squared family produced by Born‑rule measurement under an exact bijection, with sparse attention expressed by zeros at finite parameters.
- Learnable parameters act as rotation‑gate angles and value aggregation uses deterministic column‑loading to dilate a column‑stochastic matrix.

## Context
Modern transformer models rely on softmax attention but treat it classically. This work shows that the same operation can be performed exactly with quantum circuits, linking classical probability geometry to quantum measurement theory.

## Implications
Quantum implementations could reduce computational cost and enable exact modeling of attention in AI systems. The framework may inspire hybrid quantum‑classical architectures where probabilistic constraints are enforced via quantum operations.
