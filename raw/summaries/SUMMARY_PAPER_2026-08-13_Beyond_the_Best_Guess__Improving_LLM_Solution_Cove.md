## Summary
The paper investigates how post‑training reinforcement learning can narrow the diversity of solutions generated by large language models in discovery tasks, and it proposes Evolution Strategies as an alternative that directly perturbs model weights to broaden output space. Experiments on standard math benchmarks show that Evolution Strategies consistently achieve higher pass@k scores than RL because they produce a more varied solution distribution. This broader coverage translates into better overall performance.

## Key Takeaways
- Evolution Strategies improve pass@k by generating a wider range of candidate solutions, unlike RL which concentrates outputs around high‑reward answers.
- The method directly modifies model weights through random perturbations, avoiding the need for reward gradients and thus preserving solution diversity.
- This increased solution coverage leads to higher scores on standard math benchmarks compared with conventional RL approaches.

## Context
LLMs are widely used in scientific discovery where generating multiple plausible solutions is essential. Traditional post‑training methods often sacrifice diversity for efficiency, limiting their usefulness. Evolution Strategies offer a population‑based, gradient‑free alternative that can be integrated into existing pipelines without retraining from scratch.

## Implications
For researchers and industry practitioners, adopting Evolution Strategies means better solution coverage and higher benchmark performance with minimal additional compute. This could accelerate the adoption of LLMs in real‑world discovery applications where diverse answers are critical.
