## Summary
This study investigates the spatiotemporal evolution of land cover and vegetation in Dhaka District from 2019 to 2024 using high‑resolution Sentinel‑2 and Landsat 8 satellite imagery combined with supervised machine learning classifiers. The analysis reveals a 59.5% expansion of urban built‑up areas, alongside an 8.46% reduction in vegetation and a 7.77% loss of water bodies.

## Key Takeaways
- Urban built‑up increased by 59.5%, indicating rapid conversion of natural habitats into infrastructure.  
- Vegetation cover declined by 8.46%, reflecting intensified land use that reduces green space.  
- Water body area fell by 7.77%, highlighting growing pressure on local water resources.

## Context
The integration of remote sensing with machine learning exemplifies how AI can process large spatial datasets to uncover patterns invisible to manual analysis. ## Implications
