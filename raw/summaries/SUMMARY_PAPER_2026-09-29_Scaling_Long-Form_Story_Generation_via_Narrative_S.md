## Summary
This paper introduces NstAgent, a training-free agentic framework designed to enable Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate full-length novels by maintaining narrative consistency over extended text spans. The authors demonstrate that their approach effectively tracks structured narrative states, including characters, past events, and future requirements, allowing for the systematic generation of stories ranging from 10,000 to 100,000 words. Experimental results indicate that NstAgent sustains high writing quality and narrative coherence as story length increases, addressing a critical gap in scaling creative text generation beyond short-form content.

## Key Takeaways
