## Summary  
The Adobe Creators' Toolkit Report surveys 16,000 global creators and finds that 86 percent use creative generative AI, reporting it boosts business growth, personal brand development, and the creator economy. The study also highlights strong interest in agentic AI and mobile‑first workflows.

## Key Takeaways  
- 76 percent of creators say creative generative AI has helped grow their business and personal brand.  
- 85 percent would turn to an AI agent that learns their creative style, indicating a high expectation for personalized assistance.  
- Creative generative AI is now integrated across workflows: 55 percent edit/upscale, 52 percent generate new assets, 48 percent ideate; 60 percent use multiple tools.  
- Top concerns include content‑training without permission (69%) and barriers like cost (38%), quality (34%), training transparency (28%).

## Context  
The report reflects a broader shift where generative AI moves from experimental to essential for creators, driven by demand for speed, personalization, and mobile productivity. Adobe’s focus on agentic AI aligns with industry trends toward proactive assistance while preserving human control.

## Implications  
For creators, the findings suggest that tools offering style‑learning agents and multi‑task integration will dominate adoption, reducing reliance on single‑purpose apps. For brands, this signals a need to respect creator consent and provide transparent training data practices to avoid backlash. The mobile‑first trend also pushes developers toward lightweight, high‑performance AI experiences.
