## Summary
This paper introduces a benchmark and comparative study of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for French immigration law. The authors evaluate retrieval augmentation against a parametric LLM baseline on synthetic profiles, finding that retrieval improves administrative guidance, especially permit-type accuracy, at both model scales.

## Key Takeaways
- Retrieval significantly boosts permit‑type recommendation accuracy across Qwen3.5‑9B and Qwen3.5‑27B models.  
- Dense retrieval augmentation provides a more reliable legal citation coverage than the parametric baseline alone.  
- The study demonstrates that hybrid strategies are essential for robust administrative guidance in this domain.

## Context
Legal AI systems often rely on large language models without grounding, leading to hallucinations and inaccurate advice. This work addresses the gap by creating a focused benchmark for French immigration law, showing how retrieval can mitigate such risks.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt retrieval‑augmented pipelines to produce more trustworthy legal guidance, reducing errors that could affect real‑world compliance. The findings encourage further research into hybrid approaches tailored to complex regulatory domains.
