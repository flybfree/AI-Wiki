## Summary
This study evaluates the Sola Security Brain against general-purpose coding agents, such as Claude Code, to assess their effectiveness in enterprise cloud-security investigations involving population tasks. The results demonstrate that purpose-built security intelligence layers significantly outperform live coding agents, achieving 0.693 coverage compared to 0.387 for Claude Code, while delivering a 79.2% relative gain and drastically lower reasoning costs across the majority of tested scenarios.

## Key Takeaways
- The Sola Security Brain achieves superior performance on population-based cloud security tasks by resolving its relational substrate offline, resulting in 0.693 coverage versus 0.387 for Claude Code operating via live CLI; this represents a relative gain of 79.2% and a win rate of 25 out of 2
