## Summary
The paper surveys instruction-based image editing (IIE) research, organizing it into five dimensions and highlighting progress from GANs to diffusion models. It introduces the CDD-IIE benchmark for evaluating multiple aspects of model performance across open-source solutions. The authors find that recent advances in LLMs and VLMs enable one-sentence editing but also expose new challenges.

## Key Takeaways
- The survey categorizes IIE tasks into a hierarchical taxonomy, clarifying how operations are defined and grouped.
- It emphasizes the shift from GAN-based to diffusion and autoregressive architectures as a key technological milestone in recent years.
- The proposed CDD-IIE benchmark provides a comprehensive evaluation framework that assesses task diversity, model robustness, and practical applicability.

## Context
Instruction-based image editing sits at the intersection of natural language understanding and visual generation, reflecting broader trends toward multimodal AI systems.
