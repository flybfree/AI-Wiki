## Summary
FinanceHarness is a specialized harness that automates end‑to‑end financial deep research by integrating environment and data construction, an agent execution loop, and reward modeling. The authors also introduce FinanceGym, a thesis‑driven benchmark with pre‑cutoff and post‑cutoff criteria, which achieved 82% expert pass rate while leading LLMs scored below 40%. Using the same open‑weight backbone improves the overall rubric score from 25.3% to 32.4%.
