## Summary
The paper introduces PaperBanana-Interact, a multi‑agent system that refines scientific diagrams through iterative human feedback. It addresses quality drift and forgetting by using an internal critique‑and‑refine loop. The system improves diagram quality across turns, outperforming baselines by 11.9‑18.6 points.

## Key Takeaways
- All participants requested further revisions after the initial draft, indicating a persistent need for iterative improvement.
- Refined diagrams were rated more satisfactory, with 86% of users preferring them over the first version.
- The system reduces forgetting and quality drift, achieving consistent gains in quality scores.

## Context
Scientific diagram generation from text remains an active research area as authors seek visual outputs that match their intent. Automated systems often produce single‑turn results that fail to capture nuanced preferences, highlighting a gap in multi‑turn workflows. This work contributes a scalable benchmark and a solution framework for iterative refinement.

## Implications
For researchers, PaperBanana-Interact offers a template for building robust dialogue between AI and human feedback loops. Practitioners can leverage the approach to deliver higher quality visual content, improving user satisfaction and trust in automated diagram tools.
