## Summary
This paper disproves Carlson’s associated‑prime depth conjecture by constructing a finite group of order 128 whose cohomological depth is exactly two while none of its rank‑two elementary abelian subgroups have centralizers with that depth. The authors provide an exact presentation, enumerate all relevant subgroups, and supply algebraic certificates confirming the absence of any associated prime of dimension two.
