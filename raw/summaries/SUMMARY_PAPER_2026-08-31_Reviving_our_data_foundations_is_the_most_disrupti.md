## Summary
The paper argues that the most disruptive step for enterprises of small‑medium size and moderate maturity to achieve AI data maturity is to rebuild or revive a solid knowledge foundation layer rather than chasing new hype technologies. It proposes a low‑impact, modular strategy that integrates knowledge graph techniques while respecting existing data flows.

## Key Takeaways
- Establishing a robust knowledge foundation is essential for leveraging advanced AI tools and must be communicated with clear evidence to executives.
- Knowledge graphs should be designed as modular, dynamic structures that can adapt to evolving processes without disrupting current operations.
- The implementation of this strategy requires minimal impact on existing workflows, focusing instead on incremental integration.

## Context
The rapid advancement of AI technologies has created pressure for organizations to adopt cutting‑edge solutions quickly. However, many firms lack a coherent data architecture that supports these tools, leading to fragmented insights and limited ROI. This paper situates the revival of knowledge foundations within this competitive landscape.

## Implications
For practitioners, adopting a modular knowledge graph approach can accelerate AI adoption while preserving operational stability. The field will likely shift toward evidence‑driven decision making, where foundational data structures become non‑negotiable components of any mature AI strategy.
