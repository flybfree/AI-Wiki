## Summary
The paper describes ORBIT, an agentic AI toolkit integrated into ServiceNow to automate routine NOC tasks and improve incident resolution for ESnet 7. It achieved six initial tasks plus two new ones with high reliability and rapid adoption of its chat interface and lightweight model gateway.

## Key Takeaways
- ORBIT’s modular skill architecture delivers reliable, bounded automation that reduces task steps and eliminates error modes observed in unconstrained agents.
- The system integrates directly into ServiceNow via MCPs, enabling seamless access to siloed ESnet data without operator retraining.
- High request volumes from external users demonstrate strong organic adoption of the chat interface and LiteLLM gateway.

## Context
This work addresses a growing need for AI that augments rather than replaces human operators in complex, real‑time environments. By applying versioned skill design, ORBIT aligns with industry practices aimed at predictable performance and maintainable toolchains.

## Implications
For NOC teams, the findings suggest that structured agentic tools can cut resolution time and lower cognitive load without extensive infrastructure changes. Practitioners should adopt modular skill frameworks to balance autonomy with reliability in AI‑driven operations.
