## Summary
This paper introduces a QUBO‑inspired computational framework to diagnose traffic bottlenecks and dynamically dispatch resources at airport landside. The study shows that the method can cut passenger queues by up to 29 % during peak arrivals at Shanghai Pudong International Airport and Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport.

## Key Takeaways
- The five‑minute state model links passenger arrivals, vehicle supply, pickup berth service, storage, and road capacity, enabling a composite congestion severity index that pinpoints dominant bottlenecks.  
- In strong‑peak scenarios the QUBO‑inspired dispatch reduces final queues from 3445 to 2477 passengers at Shanghai Pudong and from 2053 to 1482 at Hangzhou Xiaoshan, demonstrating significant queue‑reduction benefits.  
- Robustness tests under demand, supply, service, road‑capacity, modal‑share, and random‑noise perturbations retain the queue‑reduction advantages across varied uncertainties.

## Context
