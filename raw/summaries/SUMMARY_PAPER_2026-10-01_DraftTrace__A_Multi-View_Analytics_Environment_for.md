## Summary
This paper introduces DraftTrace, a writing environment that captures three complementary views of AI-integrated writing: the final product, the step-by-step process, and interactions with an integrated AI assistant. Deployed in a graduate NLP course with 81 students, the system enables instructors to reconstruct document development over time through submission, longitudinal, and class-level analytics, revealing how combined product and process measures can distinguish between text formulation styles and detect behaviors like copy-typing that single-view analysis might miss.
