## Summary
The paper introduces MobileGroupVis, a mobile visual analytics system that enables teachers to monitor multiple group discussions simultaneously and intervene quickly during class. It provides six coordinated views on a small screen using streamlined audio-to-data pipeline and dialogue analysis. Evaluation shows it helps teachers understand processes and identify groups needing attention.

## Key Takeaways
- The system converts audio into structured discussion data via lightweight streaming, enabling real-time extraction of interaction patterns.
- Six coordinated views—including a compact glyph for cross-group comparison and detailed views for opinion evolution and topic coverage—are designed for glanceable insight on mobile devices.
- Evaluation through case studies and expert interviews demonstrates that MobileGroupVis supports teachers in diagnosing groups and facilitating interventions.

## Context
This work addresses the need for real-time visual analytics in educational settings where physical monitoring is essential. It contributes to AI-driven assistive tools that transform raw audio into actionable pedagogical insights without requiring heavy desktop infrastructure.

## Implications
For educators, MobileGroupVis offers a practical bridge between AI analysis and classroom practice, reducing cognitive load during walk‑around teaching. In industry, the approach demonstrates scalable dialogue analytics on constrained devices, informing future smart classroom deployments.
