## Summary
This paper introduces BACKDROP, a benchmark designed to evaluate how well AI agents maintain their capabilities when operating in dynamic environments populated by external hazards like unauthorized messages, injected text, boundary violations, and write faults. The study reveals that while static benchmarks suggest high performance, the presence of these real-world disruptions causes average pass rates to plummet from 69.5% to 31.3%, with even top-tier models experiencing substantial degradation in reliability.

## Key Takeaways
- BACKDROP evaluates agents against four specific hazards planted in the execution environment—Authority, Injection, Boundary, and Fault—while keeping the core instruction and correct end state constant
