## Summary
The paper introduces L3Cube‑IndicQuest v2, a large‑scale multilingual benchmark designed to test the factual knowledge of Large Language Models across Indian languages. It evaluates six LLMs using three answer‑verification protocols and finds that the commercial model leads while an open‑weight model outperforms a dedicated Indic specialist.

## Key Takeaways
- The benchmark contains 3,471 English question–answer pairs from nine educational domains and is translated into 20 languages, creating 69,420 multilingual items.  
- A hybrid construction method that mixes LLM‑generated questions with human verification ensures high annotation quality while enabling scalable data creation.  
- All evaluation protocols produce nearly identical rankings, indicating robustness of the results to the choice of judge.

## Context
This work addresses a critical gap in evaluating AI models on regionally specific knowledge, where existing benchmarks often focus on global or English‑only content. By providing a comprehensive Indic‑language dataset, it supports fair and meaningful comparisons across diverse linguistic contexts within the rapidly evolving LLM landscape.

## Implications
The benchmark offers practitioners a reliable tool to assess model performance in Indian languages, informing both research priorities and commercial product development. Its robustness across multiple verification methods suggests that such benchmarks can become standard practice for evaluating factual knowledge in multilingual AI systems.
