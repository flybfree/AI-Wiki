## Summary
This paper presents a multimodal dataset of 485 scanned student examination answers linked to expert‑designed Outcome‑Based Education rubrics, enabling criterion‑level assessment. The collection includes handwritten text, equations, tables, code and diagrams from four institutions across nine subjects, with each answer mapped to detailed performance criteria and total marks.

## Key Takeaways
- The dataset comprises 485 unique answer PDFs paired with a randomized identifier system that preserves the original scanned content while allowing secure access.  
- Expert‑designed rubrics define 47 criteria across 12 templates, each providing performance‑level descriptions and maximum marks for rigorous criterion‑based grading.  
- The data has been validated for integrity: every total mark equals the sum of its criterion marks, ensuring consistency between automated evaluation and human grading.

## Context
The work addresses a growing need in AI research to evaluate models that understand both visual document content and educational rubrics simultaneously. By integrating multimodal inputs with structured outcome criteria, it supports the development of privacy‑aware, rubric‑aware evaluation systems for OBE assessment.

## Implications
For educators, this data enables automated feedback generation aligned with specific learning outcomes. Industry practitioners can leverage the dataset to build robust document understanding pipelines that respect privacy constraints while delivering accurate criterion‑level scores.
