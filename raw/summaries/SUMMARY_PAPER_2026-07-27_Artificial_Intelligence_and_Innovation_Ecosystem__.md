## Summary
This paper investigates how artificial intelligence reshapes the Innovative Ecosystem (IE) by proposing a spatial framework for Artificial Intelligence IE (AIIE). It outlines AI’s contributions to IE across physical, social, and thinking dimensions while identifying evolutionary roles of AI during different development phases. The analysis also highlights future challenges that could limit AIIE’s effectiveness.

## Key Takeaways
- AI introduces unique spatial characteristics to the Innovative Ecosystem, influencing its integration in physical spaces through infrastructure and digital platforms.  
- The evolutionary perspective reveals distinct AI roles: early AI acts as a catalyst for digitization, mid‑stage AI drives collaborative advancement, and late‑stage AI focuses on shared achievements within IE.  
- Future challenges include ethical constraints, data privacy issues, and the risk of AI dependency that may hinder sustainable innovation.

The rapid expansion of AI technologies has transformed global processes of digitization, informatization, and intelligence, prompting scholars to reconsider how these forces interact with existing ecosystems.
