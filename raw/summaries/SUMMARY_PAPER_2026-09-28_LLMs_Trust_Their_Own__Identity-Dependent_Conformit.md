## Summary
This study investigates how social identity influences Large Language Models (LLMs) in multi-agent environments, revealing that models exhibit bidirectional conformity based on whether peer agents share their group identity or not. The research demonstrates that LLMs increase agreement with incorrect answers from in-group consensus while diverging from out-group consensus, a pattern distinct from human behavior where a single ally typically breaks conformity.
