## Summary
The paper introduces JieZi, a large-scale expert-audited dataset and benchmark for ancient Chinese character exegesis, consisting of two resources: the JieZi-Dataset with over 500K VQA pairs and the JieZi-Bench evaluation set. Experiments show that multimodal LLMs excel at basic identification but falter on glyph analysis, semantic reasoning, and diachronic understanding; fine‑tuning on JieZi improves performance across all levels.

## Key Takeaways
- The dataset is built using expert-designed templates and source‑text references to reduce factual errors, ensuring scholarly accuracy.
- Human verification is applied at each generation stage to guarantee that the QA pairs reflect authentic exegesis processes.
- Current models perform well on basic identification but struggle with glyph analysis, semantic reasoning, and diachronic understanding.

## Context
This work addresses a gap in AI research where multimodal models lack structured benchmarks for scholarly tasks involving historical linguistics. By providing a rigorously curated dataset, JieZi enables systematic evaluation of how AI can interpret ancient Chinese characters beyond simple recognition.

## Implications
For scholars and practitioners, JieZi offers a reliable benchmark to advance AI’s ability to perform nuanced cultural and linguistic analysis. For industry applications such as heritage preservation and digital archiving, the model can be fine‑tuned to support accurate interpretation of traditional texts.
