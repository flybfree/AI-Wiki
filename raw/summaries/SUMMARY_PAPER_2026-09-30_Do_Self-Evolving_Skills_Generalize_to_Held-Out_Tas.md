## Summary
This study investigates whether self-evolving skills, which are iteratively refined during training, effectively generalize to unseen tasks compared to static or one-shot approaches. The authors evaluate five self-evolving methods across six benchmarks and find that while many skills improve on training data, a significant portion fails to retain this improvement on test tasks, with no single method dominating all scenarios. Ultimately, the research introduces Generalizable Skill Optimization (GSO), a novel approach that generates task-specific skills guided by general principles, achieving superior performance
