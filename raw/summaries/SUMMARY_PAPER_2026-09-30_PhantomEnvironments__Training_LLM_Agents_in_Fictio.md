## Summary
This paper introduces PhantomEnvironments, a method for training LLM agents via reinforcement learning using synthetic, rule-based environments set in fictional worlds that require no LLM involvement for generation and incur zero marginal cost. Despite containing no real-world facts, these simple multi-turn search tasks enable agents to learn generalizable skills that transfer effectively to real-world multi-hop benchmarks, often surpassing performance achieved with actual training data. The study further demonstrates emergent behaviors where models adaptively scale their search budgets based on question difficulty and generalize across unseen fictional universes.
