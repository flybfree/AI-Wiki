## Summary
This paper presents AMPLIFAI, the first publicly available multiphase abdominal CT dataset annotated with LI‑RADS categories and segmented for arterial phase hyperenhancement, washout, and enhancing capsule. The dataset enables researchers to benchmark AI models that aim to automate liver lesion diagnosis without biopsy. Key findings include a diverse collection of high‑quality scans covering multiple patient groups and reliable segmentation masks.

## Key Takeaways
- The dataset provides a large, publicly accessible repository of multiphase CT scans with complete LI‑RADS annotations, addressing the scarcity of such resources in AI research.  
- Each scan is segmented into three distinct features—arterial phase hyperenhancement, washout, and enhancing capsule—allowing precise evaluation of model performance on each component.  
- The annotation pipeline follows a transparent, reproducible workflow documented in a datasheet, ensuring trustworthy data quality for downstream studies.

## Context
Current AI development for liver lesion characterization is hampered by the lack of large, well‑curated datasets that mirror real clinical practice. Without such resources, models cannot be reliably tested or deployed. AMPLIFAI fills this gap by offering a comprehensive benchmark that aligns with established LI‑RADS standards and supports reproducible research.

## Implications
For clinicians, the dataset offers a practical tool to evaluate AI tools against standardized criteria, potentially accelerating adoption of automated diagnosis pathways. For industry stakeholders, it enables more robust model validation, reducing risk in clinical deployment and improving patient outcomes through evidence‑based decision support.
