## Summary
This paper investigates critical measurement boundaries in evaluating LLM financial agents, demonstrating how execution conditions and audit metrics can distort performance assessments. Through two studies, the authors reveal that comparing independent runs conflates model behavior with execution artifacts, while standard recall-based auditing fails to capture precision issues and false positives in defect detection. The work emphasizes that fixed-tape execution and multi-defect auditing controls are essential to bound the validity of evaluation scores.
