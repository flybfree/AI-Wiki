## Summary
The paper introduces temporal-causal unity, a framework that links the philosophical idea of time as causal unfolding with an operational model for cognitive and social dynamics. It defines a measurable causal‑progress coordinate τ(t) and shows how agents evolve through interaction, drift, and external input. The analysis predicts synchronization thresholds and polarization conditions under specific network models.
