## Summary
This paper presents a theorem that links two organizing mechanisms of statistical physics in one dimension: collective behavior emerging spontaneously from competing interactions and that is induced by an external field. By establishing a temperature‑independent Hamiltonian mapping, the authors show that a complex open‑chain model reduces to a simpler equivalent with only nearest‑neighbor coupling and axial potentials, yielding exact solutions for both Ising, XY, Heisenberg and Potts $J_1$-$J_2$ chains.
