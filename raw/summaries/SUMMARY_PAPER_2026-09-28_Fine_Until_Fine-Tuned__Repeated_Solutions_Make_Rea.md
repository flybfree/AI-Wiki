## Summary
This study examines how repeated exposure to identical solution traces during reasoning training impacts a model's robustness during subsequent fine-tuning phases. The authors find that drilling models on the same problems leads to significant performance degradation when later subjected to instruction tuning, whereas models trained with diverse solutions remain stable, indicating that fragility arises from overfitting to specific texts rather than data scarcity. Furthermore, the research demonstrates that this reasoning suppression is reversible and preventable
