## Summary
The paper aims to map the ocean’s submesoscale energy cascade using a generative deep‑learning reconstruction of kilometer‑scale surface currents from satellite data and simulations. It demonstrates that submesoscale fronts regulate both an upscale cascade above 10 km and a downscale cascade below 10 km, concentrating cross‑scale transfer within these features. The study shows that despite their limited spatial extent, fronts dominate the domain‑integrated cascade. The generative framework also validates that physically plausible dynamics can be learned from limited data, reinforcing the utility of AI in ocean science.

## Key Takeaways
- Submesoscale fronts act as efficient conduits for energy transfer both upscale and downscale, increasing cross‑scale efficiency by an order of magnitude compared with other regions.
- The upscale cascade above 10 km supplies the seasonal vigor of mesoscale eddies in systems like the Agulhas Current.
- Downscale convergence at submesoscale fronts drives dissipation, completing the energy cycle.
