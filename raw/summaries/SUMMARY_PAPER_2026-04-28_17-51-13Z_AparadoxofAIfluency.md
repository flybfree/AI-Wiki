## Summary
The paper investigates how users’ fluency with AI influences the outcomes of their interactions, using a large annotated dataset. It finds that fluent users engage actively while novices are passive, leading to more visible failures but also higher success on complex tasks.

## Key Takeaways
- Fluent users experience more visible failures than novices because they stay engaged and notice problems.
- Their failures often lead to partial recovery and accompany greater success on complex tasks.
- Novices suffer from invisible failures where conversations end successfully yet miss the mark.

## Context
AI fluency research highlights that user behavior, not just model capability, determines real‑world performance. Understanding these dynamics is essential for designing systems that match human expectations.

## Implications
Designers should encourage deep engagement rather than frictionless experiences to improve overall success rates. This shifts focus from seamless interaction to fostering active collaboration with AI.
