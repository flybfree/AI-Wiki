## Summary
This paper addresses a longstanding question in finite group theory by proving that a specific 3‑group of order \(3^{84}\) is an E‑group. The authors show that every element commutes with all its endomorphic images, confirming the nilpotency class three property for this large group.

## Key Takeaways
- The nine power relations of the group define a linear map \(q:V\to\Lambda^2 V\) where \(V=P/\Phi(P)\cong\mathbb{F}_3^9\), and no nonzero proper subspace satisfies \(q(U)\subseteq\Lambda^2 U\).  
- Consequently, any endomorphism acts on \(V\) either invertibly or trivially, reducing the tensor rigidity to a finite calculation over 9841 points of \(\mathrm{PG}(8,3)\).  
- In the trivial case the image lies in \(\Phi(P)=P'\) and then into \(Z(P)\), ensuring commutation with all endomorphic images.
