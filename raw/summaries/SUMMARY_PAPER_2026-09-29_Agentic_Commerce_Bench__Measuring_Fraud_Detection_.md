## Summary
This paper addresses the emerging risk of pricing fraud in agentic commerce, where AI agents autonomously settle payments to legitimate-looking counterparties that overcharge for services without triggering traditional identity-based security checks. The authors introduce a comprehensive taxonomy, the Agentic Commerce Bench (ACB) with twenty fraud classes
