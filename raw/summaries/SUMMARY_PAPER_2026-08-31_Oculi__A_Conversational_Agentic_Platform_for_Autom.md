## Summary
Oculi is a conversational agentic platform that converts natural‑language queries into full credit risk analyses, including SQL queries, statistical tests and interactive visualizations. The system uses a three‑layer architecture to separate reasoning, execution and presentation, enabling analysts to uncover high‑risk portfolio segments quickly while keeping the process auditable.

## Key Takeaways
- Oculi integrates LLM‑driven reasoning with deterministic statistical methods to select features that reveal material risk segments in large mortgage datasets.  
- The platform automates the entire workflow from question formulation to visual output, cutting manual query writing and computation time dramatically.  
- Evaluation on a portfolio of over 200 features shows Oculi can identify previously intractable risk groups, preserving statistical rigor throughout.

## Context
The paper contributes to AI‑assisted finance by demonstrating how large language models can act as intelligent agents that orchestrate complex data pipelines without exposing their inner workings. This aligns with trends toward explainable AI and automated decision support in regulated industries where transparency is essential.

## Implications
For financial institutions, Oculi offers a scalable way to democratize credit risk analysis across teams, reducing reliance on specialized analysts. Practitioners can leverage the platform’s audit trail to meet compliance standards while exploring novel segment insights that were previously inaccessible through manual methods.
