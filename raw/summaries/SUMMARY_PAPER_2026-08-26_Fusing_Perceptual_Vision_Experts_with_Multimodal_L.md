## Summary
The paper introduces the Hybrid Hierarchical Multi-Agent Framework (H$^{2}$MAF) that fuses EfficientNet-B3 and ConvNeXt-Tiny with open‑weight multimodal large language models to generate explainable plant disease diagnoses in real‑world field conditions. The framework is evaluated on benchmark and robot‑acquired datasets, achieving high accuracy and low disagreement rates.

## Key Takeaways
- Gemma improves accuracy from 63.9% to 68.5% on PlantDoc, gaining +7.6 points on the CNN‑conflict subset.
- Cornell datasets show accuracies of 99.3% and 98.9%, with only 1.7–4.1% disagreement, demonstrating that MLLM arbitration reduces conflict errors.
- Qwen overflags by 3.5–14.4 points, highlighting calibration challenges in large language models.

## Context
This work tackles the need for multimodal AI that can combine uncertain visual and textual evidence under uncontrolled field conditions, moving beyond static benchmark evaluations to practical robotics applications.

## Implications
The fusion approach provides a scalable, explainable diagnostic tool for agricultural robots, reducing false alarms and improving treatment decisions, thereby supporting sustainable farming practices.
