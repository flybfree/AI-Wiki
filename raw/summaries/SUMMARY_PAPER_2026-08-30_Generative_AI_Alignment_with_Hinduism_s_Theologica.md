## Summary
This paper investigates how Bangladeshi Hindu users interact with generative AI to answer personal religious questions. It finds that while AI can assist scriptural inquiry and devotional visualization, it also raises ethical concerns such as theological flattening and cultural misrepresentation. The study draws on fifteen semi‑structured interviews with Bangladeshi Hindu participants to explore their experiences with AI‑generated scriptural explanations, devotional visualizations, and synthetic religious media.

## Key Takeaways
- The study shows AI supports scriptural inquiry but risks reducing complex theology to a single interpretation.
- It highlights the danger of cultural misrepresentation when AI generates religious media without community input.
- The research warns against simulating sacred authority, which could undermine genuine devotional practice. Future research should incorporate community feedback loops to ensure AI respects cultural nuance and avoids reinforcing stereotypes.

## Context
Generative AI is being deployed across diverse domains, yet most alignment frameworks ignore non‑Western religions.
