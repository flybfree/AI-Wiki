## Summary
This paper introduces ESCRAG-R1, a unified framework that merges retrieval‑augmented psychological guidance with Group Relative Policy Optimization for Emotional Support Conversation. By embedding external knowledge into the reinforcement learning loop, the model generates more coherent and empathetic responses while avoiding artificial splicing of clinical advice and generic reassurance.

## Key Takeaways
- Retrieval‑based psychological guidance functions as a robust learning signal that prompts explicit internal reasoning before each generated response.
- The ESC-Preference dataset provides precise, empathy‑aware reward signals that enable the reinforcement learning process to incorporate professional expertise effectively.
- Experiments show that ESCRAG-R1 markedly outperforms existing baselines by achieving natural integration of clinical strategies and empathetic expression.

## Context
The work addresses a growing need for AI agents capable of delivering therapeutic support through structured reasoning and genuine empathy. It exemplifies how retrieval mechanisms can be woven into reinforcement learning pipelines to improve dialogue quality in human‑AI interaction systems.

## Implications
For the field, ESCRAG-R1 demonstrates that RL can be enhanced with knowledge grounding to produce more reliable conversational partners.
