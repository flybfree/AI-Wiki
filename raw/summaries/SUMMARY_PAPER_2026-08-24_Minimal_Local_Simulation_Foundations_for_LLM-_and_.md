## Summary
The paper introduces two minimal simulation platforms that allow locally hosted large language models and vision-language models to interact with 2D or 3D agent environments. It demonstrates how these lightweight setups can be used for education and rapid prototyping without requiring cloud resources.

## Key Takeaways
- SD-AgentFoundry-2D creates a two-dimensional multi-agent setting where locally hosted LLM agents move, communicate, respond to place occupancy, and react to spatially localized fire events. - The 3D version provides a digital twin where a locally hosted VLM receives first-person images and generates natural-language movement instructions. - Both codebases are deliberately open for modification and run on macOS Windows Linux.

## Context
These minimal foundations address the gap between powerful generative models and accessible simulation environments, enabling researchers to experiment with complex social dynamics without heavy infrastructure. The focus on local execution aligns with trends toward edge AI and democratizing access to multimodal agents.

## Implications
Educators can use these tools to teach concepts of agent behavior and emergent phenomena in a tangible way. Industry practitioners may leverage the open codebases to prototype domain-specific simulations that integrate generative intelligence directly into physical or virtual spaces.
