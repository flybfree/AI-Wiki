## Summary
The paper introduces D2VBench, a benchmark of 10,000 daily dilemma scenarios designed to evaluate the value alignment of large language models. Evaluation combines multiple-choice and open-ended questions on eight mainstream LLMs, revealing high reliability and robustness in reflecting their alignment across various value categories.

## Key Takeaways
- D2VBench includes 10,000 instances built through a multi-stage collaboration between LLMs and humans, grounded in 158 manually annotated fine-grained value concepts.  
- The evaluation paradigm uses both multiple-choice questions and open-ended questions to capture complex value conflicts and assess alignment.  
- Experimental results demonstrate that D2VBench exhibits high reliability and robustness, effectively reflecting the LLMs' alignment across different value categories and dimensions.

## Context
Current LLM benchmarks often lack realistic daily dilemmas and employ simplistic evaluation methods, limiting their ability to measure how models handle competing values in everyday situations. This gap hampers progress toward truly aligned AI systems that can navigate nuanced human preferences.

## Implications
D2VBench provides researchers and industry practitioners with a fine-grained tool to assess value alignment, guiding safer deployment in sensitive applications where conflicting values may arise.
