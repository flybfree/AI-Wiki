## Summary
The paper introduces InstructMesh, an interactive tool that refines generative 3D models to improve fabrication readiness by repairing flaws such as voids and thickness issues. Users can select regions and apply edits via natural language prompts or sliders, operating on the latent representation. User studies show novices successfully perform relevant repairs.

## Key Takeaways
- InstructMesh targets specific fabrication failures like voids and thin sections that degrade print quality.
- It allows precise local adjustments without requiring expert modeling expertise.
- The hybrid interface combining natural language with slider controls is preferred by users for ease of use.

## Context
Generative AI models produce visually appealing 3D outputs but often lack geometric fidelity required for physical fabrication. This gap can lead to costly rework and user frustration, highlighting a need for post‑generation correction tools that are accessible to non‑experts.

## Implications
InstructMesh bridges the visual generation and manufacturing pipelines, enabling designers to produce printable models directly from text or image prompts. Its adoption could reduce prototyping costs and accelerate product development across industries such as additive manufacturing and rapid prototyping.
