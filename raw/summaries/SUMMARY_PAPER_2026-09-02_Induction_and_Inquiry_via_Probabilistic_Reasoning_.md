## Summary
The paper proposes a computational model that encodes symbolic knowledge as mental programs blending natural language with source code, using LLM‑guided Bayesian learning to infer these programs from streaming noisy data. The model reproduces key human inductive learning phenomena such as anchoring and garden‑pathing while being more data‑efficient and computationally tractable than pure LLMs or classic Bayesian methods.

- The model captures gradations of uncertainty through sequential Bayesian updates across language‑like and program‑like hypotheses, enabling intelligent inquiry.
- It achieves data‑efficiency and compute‑efficiency, avoiding the exorbitant computational cost that plagues other approaches.
