## Summary
Andy is an autonomous mathematical research agent that formulates, solves, and verifies problems without human intervention. The paper demonstrates its capability by tackling a self‑triggered impulsive consensus problem on delayed heterogeneous networks with switching communication topologies, establishing global exponential synchronization conditions while excluding Zeno behavior.

## Key Takeaways
- Andy formulates a global exponential leader‑follower synchronization problem for delayed heterogeneous networks with switching communication topologies.  
- The hybrid control combines self‑triggered impulses with execution delay and recovery‑phase feedback that cancels the delayed error channel during a recovery window.  
- Sufficient conditions for global exponential synchronization are established, Zeno behavior is excluded for both sampling and impulse sequences, and a numerical example confirms the result.
