## Summary
PANDA introduces a decentralized architecture for LLM-based multi-agent systems that overcomes scalability, fault tolerance, and governance limitations inherent in centralized designs by enabling heterogeneous agents to self-organize into specialized teams via a web-of-trust model. The system decouples collective from team communication to support load balancing, concurrent scheduling, and simultaneous team participation across thousands of agents. Evaluations on HotPotQA demonstrate that PANDA assembles teams in milliseconds, matches state-of-the-art accuracy at up to 8x the efficiency of existing systems, and sustains 100% task completion under failure conditions where other architectures fail.
