## Summary
The paper surveys Physical AI governance from scientific and operational viewpoints, proposing a unified framework and five-stage lifecycle. It links governance principles to each stage with concrete practices.

## Key Takeaways
- Governance must be integrated across the entire physical AI lifecycle, not just at deployment.
- Safety constraints require real-time monitoring and adaptive policies that respond to dynamic environments.
- A unified framework bridges scientific theory with engineering workflows.

## Context
Physical AI extends artificial intelligence to embodied systems that perceive, interact with, and act in the physical world. Traditional AI governance assumes static screen‑based models, which is insufficient for these dynamic interactions.

## Implications
This framework offers researchers a structured reference for building safe Physical AI systems. Practitioners can apply its principles to ensure trustworthiness and societal alignment across research, design, data, model development, and deployment stages.
