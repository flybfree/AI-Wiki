## Summary
This paper introduces DAYJOB, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents on long-horizon professional work tasks across healthcare and finance domains. The benchmark consists of 130 complex, multi-hour tasks evaluated through containerized environments with strict expert rubrics requiring perfect adherence to binary criteria. Experimental results reveal that even state-of-the-art models struggle significantly, with the best-performing configuration passing only about a quarter of attempts, while median systems fail almost entirely.
