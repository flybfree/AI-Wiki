## Summary
The paper tackles the core optimization problem behind current best bounds for matrix multiplication exponent ω by reformulating it, applying machine learning techniques, and refining with AlphaEvolve. The authors achieve an upper bound of 2.371177 which is slightly lower than the previous record 2.371339.
