## Summary
The paper introduces AgenticTwin, an agentic framework that combines large language models with digital twin anomaly detection to improve interpretation and mitigation of cyber‑physical system anomalies. Experiments on a weather sensor dataset show that structured collaboration between LLM reasoning and classifier outputs enhances diagnostic quality and operator satisfaction.

## Key Takeaways
- The integration of LLM explanations grounded in digital twin classification results enables natural‑language queries from operators, making anomaly analysis more accessible.
- Structured agent collaboration improves both the accuracy of diagnosis and the effectiveness of mitigation actions across varied anomaly scenarios.
- Lightweight open‑source LLMs can be deployed in real‑world cyber‑physical environments without sacrificing performance.

## Context
Large language models are being explored for automated reasoning and explanation generation, yet their deployment in operational systems like digital twins remains limited by integration challenges. This work addresses that gap by aligning LLM outputs with domain‑specific anomaly data, creating a bridge between high‑level reasoning and low‑level sensor streams.

## Implications
For industry practitioners, AgenticTwin offers a practical path to embed AI‑driven insights into existing monitoring pipelines without costly overhauls. The approach could reduce downtime and improve safety in critical infrastructure where timely anomaly detection is vital.
