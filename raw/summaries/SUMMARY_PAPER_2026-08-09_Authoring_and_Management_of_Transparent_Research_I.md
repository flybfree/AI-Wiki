## Summary
This paper introduces INSPECT-AI, an LLM‑driven interactive tool that helps human reviewers evaluate the research integrity of published randomised controlled trials using the community‑approved INSPECT‑SR framework and the Research Integrity Provenance and Evidence ontology (RIPE‑O). The system also generates a knowledge graph documenting the provenance of each assessment. The authors report that 140 expert assessments were produced for 95 RCT publications, providing an initial evidence base for transparent integrity evaluation.

## Key Takeaways
- LLM‑based interactive tools can reduce manual effort and subjective variance in research integrity reviews by aligning human reviewers with a standardized framework.
- The RIPE‑O ontology enables detailed documentation of the provenance of each assessment, creating a traceable knowledge graph that enhances transparency.
- The generated 140 expert assessments for 95 RCTs demonstrate that AI can support large‑scale, reproducible integrity evaluations.

## Context
The integration of artificial intelligence into complex evaluation tasks such as research integrity assessment is gaining traction to complement human expertise and improve consistency. This work illustrates how LLMs can be combined with established ontologies to produce structured, auditable outputs for scientific evidence.

## Implications
Transparent AI‑assisted assessments help ensure that guideline development relies on high‑quality, trustworthy RCT evidence, reducing the risk of low‑quality or false research influencing clinical care. Practitioners and industry stakeholders benefit from a reliable provenance record that supports accountability and reproducibility in research evaluation.
