## Summary
The paper proposes BLAZE, a socialized AI paradigm that integrates persistent knowledge, collective reasoning, empirical validation, and human judgment into a continuous scientific discovery cycle. Its core finding is that scientific intelligence emerges from sustained interaction among these elements rather than computation alone. The framework demonstrates that integrating these components yields a coherent discovery process.

## Key Takeaways
- BLAZE treats AI as organizational infrastructure, not an isolated task assistant.
- It emphasizes traceable, reproducible, cumulative research by linking knowledge, hypotheses, experiments, and verification.
- The paradigm preserves human creativity while extending scale, depth, and continuity of scientific inquiry.

## Context
Current AI systems are often designed for specific tasks, limiting their contribution to long-term scientific progress. This work addresses that gap by proposing a framework that unifies multiple stages of discovery.

## Implications
BLAZE could transform how research teams collaborate, enabling more robust data integration and iterative refinement. Practitioners may adopt it to improve reproducibility and foster continuous learning across disciplines.
