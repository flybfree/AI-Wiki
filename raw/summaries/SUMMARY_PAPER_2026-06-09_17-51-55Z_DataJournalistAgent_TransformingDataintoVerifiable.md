## Summary
The paper introduces Data2Story, a multi‑agent framework that turns raw data into verifiable multimedia stories. Evaluation on 18 articles shows it matches expert work in evidence and interactivity while lagging slightly in editorial flair.

## Key Takeaways
- Claims are evidence‑grounded: every number, angle, asset is linked to code or an external reference, ensuring traceability.
- Articles are multimodally generative: the system reasons about reader preferences and uses interactive maps, audio, etc., beyond static text and charts.
- Human articles retain an edge in editorial angle, creative design, and presentation.

## Context
This work advances the field by integrating specialized agents into a unified newsroom pipeline, addressing the gap between isolated tools and end‑to‑end storytelling.

## Implications
For journalists, Data2Story offers an auditable, interactive reporting tool that boosts transparency; for researchers, it demonstrates scalable multimodal generation with verifiable claims.
