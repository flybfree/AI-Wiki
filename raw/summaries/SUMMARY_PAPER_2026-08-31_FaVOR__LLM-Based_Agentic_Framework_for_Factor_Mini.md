## Summary
This paper introduces FaVOR, a framework that validates factor mining by linking mathematical forms to economic hypotheses through an agentic three‑stage loop. The authors demonstrate that FaVOR generates interpretable, regime‑robust factors on CSI 500 and S&P 500 data in 2025, outperforming return‑focused baselines while preserving economic meaning.

## Key Takeaways
- Decomposition of a broad hypothesis into observable conditions ensures each factor can be checked against its intended signal.  
- Validation step confirms that the extracted factor truly reflects those conditions before integration.  
- Integration merges validated factors into a composite structure, maintaining interpretability across market regimes.

## Context
Automated factor discovery using large language models has accelerated finance research but often sacrifices economic rationale for short‑term returns. This work addresses the gap by embedding hypothesis grounding within the generation pipeline, aligning AI outputs with traditional financial theory.

## Implications
Practitioners can rely on factors that are transparent and resilient to regime shifts, reducing risk of spurious correlations. The framework also sets a new standard for AI‑driven finance, encouraging rigorous validation before deploying automated models.
