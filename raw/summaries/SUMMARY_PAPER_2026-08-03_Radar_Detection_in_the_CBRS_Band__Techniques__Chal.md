## Summary
The paper surveys radar detection techniques for the CBRS band focusing on regulatory requirements and performance metrics. It compares traditional energy‑based and pattern‑matching methods with modern machine learning approaches that learn from datasets. The authors conclude that combining both can meet high accuracy and low latency needs.

## Key Takeaways
- Detection must achieve a 99% radar overlap recall meaning almost all radar signals are identified without missing any.
- False alarms caused by interference from modern wireless systems remain a major challenge limiting reliability.
- Real‑time operation within 60 seconds is required to allow commercial users to adjust transmissions promptly.
