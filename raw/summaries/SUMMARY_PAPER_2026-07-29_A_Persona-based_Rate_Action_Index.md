## Summary
This paper introduces a persona-based rate action index that predicts the U.S. Federal Open Market Committee’s decision to hike, hold, or cut the federal funds target rate by modeling how multiple digital personas respond to current market conditions. The index is built from nearly 25,000 retrievable data chunks and shows strong alignment with actual policy outcomes, outperforming traditional baselines in accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- The average member‑conditional recall of persona behavior is eight times chance, indicating highly attributable responses that closely match real market content.  
- Generated personas are nearly indistinguishable from held‑out real content (detection error 0.23 vs a floor of 0.15), suggesting strong authenticity.  
- Query‑conditioned persona representations capture the hawk–dove monetary policy stance with Kendall’s τ = 0.63, p < 0.001, and improve prediction accuracy to 0.69 versus a base rate of 0.47.

## Context
The work advances AI‑driven market forecasting by leveraging generative personas as dynamic proxies for collective behavior, highlighting how structured digital avatars can encode temporal shifts in policy preferences beyond static data retrieval.

## Implications
For financial institutions and policymakers, the index offers a novel tool to anticipate rate moves with higher confidence, potentially reducing reliance on opaque statistical models. Practitioners may integrate persona‑based signals into risk management frameworks, though careful validation is needed to ensure robustness across market regimes.
