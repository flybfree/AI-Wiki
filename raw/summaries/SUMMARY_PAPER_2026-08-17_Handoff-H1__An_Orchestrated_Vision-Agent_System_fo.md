## Summary
Handoff-H1 is a system that converts architectural blueprints into accurate material quantity takeoffs by integrating computer‑vision primitives, tool‑using agents, and a construction knowledge base. The authors report that the end‑to‑end pipeline achieves 81.6 % composite score, surpassing both frontier models and independent estimators.

## Key Takeaways
- Handoff-H1 combines specialized vision models with agentic tools to perform counting, detection, and plan decomposition on blueprints, enabling multi‑step reasoning beyond simple image extraction.  
- The system is grounded in a curated construction knowledge base, allowing it to infer unstated conventions that drive material selection and quantity calculations.  
- Evaluation shows the pipeline reaches 86.1 % coverage and 78.8 % Precision@25%, delivering roughly 20‑point gains over the strongest frontier agent.

## Context
This work advances AI construction estimating by moving beyond static image classification toward end‑to‑end, reasoning‑capable pipelines that mimic human trade expertise. It demonstrates how hierarchical knowledge integration can improve both coverage and precision metrics in a benchmark setting.

## Implications
For industry practitioners, Handoff-H1 suggests a path to automated takeoffs that reduce manual errors and accelerate estimate generation. For researchers, the open framework invites further exploration of AI‑driven construction workflows and the impact of knowledge grounding on performance.
