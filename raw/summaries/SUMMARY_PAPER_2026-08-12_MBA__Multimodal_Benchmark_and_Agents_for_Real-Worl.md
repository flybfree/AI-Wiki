## Summary
This paper introduces MBA-Bench, a multimodal benchmark for training and evaluating business ideation agents that incorporate visual cues beyond text. Experiments show that agents trained with creativity and feasibility rewards outperform caption‑only baselines by 63.9% and 77.1%, while multimodal approaches achieve near closed‑source performance.

## Key Takeaways
- MBA-Bench provides 30K samples across six domains, each featuring visual cues not fully conveyed by text alone.  
- Agents trained with creativity and feasibility reward objectives surpass caption‑only baselines by 63.9% and 77.1%, respectively.  
- Multimodal agents achieve near closed‑source performance, outperforming multimodal baselines by 25.6% and 35.8%.

## Context
Current AI research on business ideation relies heavily on text‑only models, ignoring the multimodal richness of real‑world contexts such as images and videos. This limitation hampers the generation of practical, actionable ideas that leverage visual information.

## Implications
The results demonstrate that integrating visual data into LLM‑driven ideation can significantly boost performance, encouraging developers to adopt multimodal architectures. Practitioners should consider these benchmarks when designing or evaluating business AI solutions.
