## Summary
ServeLearnBench introduces a benchmark for evaluating whether LLM agents can learn from serving experience in evolving environments where required knowledge is hidden, implicit, and changing over time. It formalizes an evolving-environment streaming dataset and tests multiple learning harnesses and models across retail support, banking, and sales-pitch generation tasks. The main finding is that current agents still struggle to reliably infer, apply, and revise latent environment knowledge from interaction and outcome feedback.
