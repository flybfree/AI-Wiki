## Summary
The paper introduces Agentic Technical Debt (AgTD) as a new construct for autonomous AI systems, mapping existing technical debts to emergent liabilities that arise from dynamic multi‑agent collaboration and persistent memory.

## Key Takeaways
- AgTD captures how static AITD evolves into system‑level liabilities such as memory inconsistencies and orchestration fragility due to autonomy.  
- The transformation framework shows conventional debts manifest as unsafe autonomous decision‑making, cascading failures, and tool coordination breakdowns.  
- Technical debt now includes agent behaviors, coordination mechanisms, and interactions among agents, tools, and execution environments.

## Context
Agentic AI systems introduce dynamic, multi‑agent collaboration that traditional static software models cannot capture. This research bridges the gap between static software debt analysis and the evolving landscape of self‑directed AI agents.

## Implications
Practitioners can use AgTD to prioritize interventions that safeguard trustworthiness, reduce risk exposure, and enhance long‑term system sustainability.
