## Summary
The paper introduces CEAA, a modular cognitive architecture that integrates high‑level reasoning models with real‑time embodied execution for interactive virtual agents. It bridges the gap between abstract cognition and low‑level game engine constraints by providing a reusable template. The framework combines Sense‑Think‑Act and Belief‑Desire‑Intention principles into an implementation‑oriented pipeline.

## Key Takeaways
- CEAA offers a modular, implementation‑focused framework that can be plugged into existing 3D computing environments without requiring custom low‑level code.
- It aligns high‑level cognitive models with real‑time agent behavior, enabling adaptive and explainable decision making in complex virtual worlds.
- The architecture reuses established frameworks such as Sense‑Think‑Act and Belief‑Desire‑Intention to simplify deployment of embodied IVAs.

## Context
The field of intelligent virtual agents is hampered by incompatible architectures that either rely on proprietary game engines or lack real‑time execution capabilities. This work addresses the need for a unified, scalable solution that can be applied across diverse interactive systems and research domains.

## Implications
CEAA will streamline development pipelines for researchers and industry teams seeking embodied AI agents. By providing a clear template, it reduces integration time and fosters reproducibility in cognitive computing projects.
