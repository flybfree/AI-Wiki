## Summary
This paper introduces SFgen, an agentic recognition and generation system that creates electronic component symbols and footprints using multimodal large language models. The model achieves 86% accuracy for symbol generation and 80% accuracy for footprint generation, forming the basis of a database called SFnet with 1000 components.

## Key Takeaways
- SFgen combines recognition and generation to produce PCB symbols and footprints, reducing manual design effort.
- The system reaches high precision rates, generating symbols with 86% accuracy and footprints with 80% accuracy.
- The resulting database SFnet contains 1000 components and is designed for continuous expansion.

## Context
The integration of multimodal large language models into PCB design workflows represents a shift toward automated generation that leverages natural language understanding. This approach addresses longstanding challenges in symbol creation, which are traditionally labor‑intensive and prone to human error.

## Implications
For designers, SFgen can accelerate prototyping by providing accurate symbols and footprints instantly. Industry adoption could lower costs, improve consistency, and enable rapid iteration of printed circuit board layouts.
