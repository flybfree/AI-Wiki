## Summary
The paper proposes PonsRAG to address cognitive islanding and cross‑layer evidence disconnection in long narrative reasoning; it outperforms baselines with an 11.56% relative improvement on multi‑choice tasks.

## Key Takeaways
- Triple-Layer Indexing creates a connected knowledge structure that bridges cognitive islands.
- Coordinated Reasoning retrieves evidence across distinct layers and integrates cross‑layer information into a unified context.
- The framework achieves an 11.56% relative accuracy boost over the strongest baseline on four long‑context narrative benchmarks.

## Context
Current RAG systems struggle to maintain coherence when processing extended narratives, leading to fragmented reasoning; this work introduces a biologically inspired architecture that organizes knowledge in layers and coordinates retrieval across them.
