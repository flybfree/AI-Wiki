## Summary
The paper introduces Atria Dawn Preview, a foundation agentic language model engineered to enhance scientific research and engineering workflows through a Verifiable Experience Pipeline that links tool-mediated interactions with executable environments. Evaluated across sixteen real-world benchmarks, the model demonstrates competitive performance against state-of-the-art agents and achieves top scores on five tasks. Furthermore, the authors analyze extensive human-AI collaboration logs to demonstrate how AI assistants are increasingly enabling complex research endeavors while humans maintain strategic oversight and final decision-making authority.
