## Summary
CompMat-Bench introduces a novel benchmark comprising 94 tasks derived from recent computational materials studies to evaluate AI agents without the prohibitive cost of running expensive simulations during assessment. By reproducing research steps in advance, the benchmark allows agents to be graded on input preparation and output analysis using fixed rules and ground truth data, eliminating the need for LLM judges or repeated computations. Results show that while agents achieve high pass rates (66.0-90.4%) on single tasks with full guidance, performance degrades under complex workflows or reduced guidance, with failures primarily stemming from scientific reasoning errors rather than software misuse.
