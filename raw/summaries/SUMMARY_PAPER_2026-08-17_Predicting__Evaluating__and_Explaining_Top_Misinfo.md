## Summary
The paper aims to predict, evaluate, and explain the most influential misinformation spreaders by analyzing three behavioral archetypes: amplifiers, super‑spreaders, and coordinated accounts. It finds that super‑spreader traits dominate the top rankings while later positions reflect mixed archetypal behavior.

## Key Takeaways
- Super-spreader traits consistently dominate the top ranks among most influential misinformation spreaders.
- The interplay of multiple archetypes becomes more prominent as ranking moves down.
- Temporal dynamics are critical for predictive performance and methods reduce data requirements by minimizing the observation window needed.

## Context
This work contributes to AI research on content moderation, showing how behavioral patterns can be captured with machine learning. It highlights the importance of timely data and explainable models in addressing misinformation challenges.

## Implications
These results give platforms actionable tools for identifying accounts that pose a risk, enabling more precise monitoring and intervention strategies against harmful spreaders.
