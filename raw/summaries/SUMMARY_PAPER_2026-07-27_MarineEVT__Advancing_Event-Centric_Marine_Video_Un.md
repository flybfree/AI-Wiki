## Summary
The paper introduces MarineEVT, a dataset and an event-centric visual tool reasoning (EVT-R1) framework for marine video understanding. It demonstrates that EVT-R1 outperforms leading vision-language models by 5.22 and 11.09 points respectively.

## Key Takeaways
- The MarineEVT dataset contains 20K multi-task, video-level visual question-answering pairs covering diverse marine understanding tasks.
- EVT-R1 leverages visual tools to localize and interpret sparse, unpredictable marine events, improving model performance.
- The approach achieves significant gains over top open-source and commercial VLMs in both settings.

## Context
Recent vision-language models excel on static images but struggle with video due to temporal demands and limited annotated data. Marine video understanding adds domain complexity requiring expert knowledge and rare event localization, making this work a step toward robust multimodal video AI.

## Implications
This research enables ecological discovery by enabling models to reason about marine interactions, supporting education and sustainable ocean analysis. Practitioners can adopt the EVT-R1 framework to enhance video understanding in resource-constrained settings where event data is scarce.
