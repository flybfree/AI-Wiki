## Summary
The paper introduces a method for generating spinning conformal fields using neural networks and embedding formalism, computing two-, three- and four-point functions in concrete examples. It builds on scalar conformal field techniques from Halverson 2024 and shows that an i.i.d. neuron ensemble recovers the four‑dimensional Maxwell CFT as width goes to infinity.

## Key Takeaways
- The authors construct spinning conformal fields directly from neural network architectures, demonstrating a bridge between machine learning models and theoretical field theory.
- They compute non‑trivial two-, three- and four‑point functions for these fields in specific examples, providing empirical validation of the construction.
- In the infinite-width limit of an i.i.d. neuron ensemble the model reproduces the Maxwell conformal field theory, confirming the theoretical prediction.
