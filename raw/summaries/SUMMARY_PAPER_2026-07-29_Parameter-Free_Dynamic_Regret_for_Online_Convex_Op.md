## Summary

The method combines restarted AdaGrad experts with a geometric block length pool and a pathwise meta‑algorithm to achieve tight bounds.

## Context
Online convex optimization addresses learning from noisy sequential data where each observation is generated by an unknown convex function. Heavy‑tailed noise is common in real‑world signals and makes traditional moment‑based methods unreliable. Achieving parameter‑free guarantees is essential for robust algorithms that can operate without hyperparameter tuning.

## Implications
