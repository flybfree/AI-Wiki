## Summary
La Agente Óptima is an agentic framework that runs Bayesian optimization campaigns across computational and physical systems while keeping a persistent state. It separates LLM reasoning from executed experiments, allowing continuous loops without specialist intervention. The approach maintains an auditable decision log for reproducibility and demonstrated success in contact angle and flow‑chemistry tasks.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework separates LLM reasoning from executed campaigns, enabling continuous optimization loops without specialist intervention.
- In a contact angle experiment, Óptima detected a measurement failure, corrected the target, and recommended formulation change when the goal became unattainable.
- Over five days, Óptima raised flow‑chemistry yield from 30% to 59% using less starting material than a human campaign.

## Context
This work shows that large language models can autonomously orchestrate multi‑step scientific experiments, reducing reliance on expert labor and enabling rapid iteration across computational and physical platforms. Such autonomy could reshape how labs integrate AI with traditional experimental workflows.

## Implications
For researchers, the system lowers barriers to entry in self‑driving labs, democratizing access to advanced optimization tools. For industry, it promises faster discovery cycles with minimal material waste, aligning AI capabilities with sustainable manufacturing practices.
