## Summary
The paper introduces Eco3S, a framework that uses agent-based modeling to simulate socio-economic systems with evolving agents and environments. It demonstrates the framework’s ability to replicate known economic studies and shows its scalability for policy analysis.

## Key Takeaways
- Co-evolving Environment Design creates bidirectional feedback where agents shape the environment and vice versa, generating realistic emergent behaviors.
- Structural Causal Simulation provides an SCM‑inspired counterfactual mechanism that lets researchers test diverse causal interventions flexibly.
- The Simulation‑Analysis‑Refinement Paradigm iteratively improves experimental designs based on simulation outcomes, enabling self‑corrective research workflows.

## Context
Agent‑based modeling has become a cornerstone for exploring complex socio‑economic dynamics, but existing LLM‑driven ABM tools struggle with flexible counterfactuals and automated workflow refinement.
