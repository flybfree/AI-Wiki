## Summary
MILO introduces a meta-evolutionary framework that co-evolves agent harnesses and their discovery strategies to overcome the combinatorial complexity of harness design in agentic systems. By leveraging hierarchical lineage memory, island-based evolution, and an adaptive orchestrator, MILO automatically generates superior execution controls without relying on narrow optimization or fixed search heuristics.
