## Summary
SkillSeek addresses the critical bottleneck of selecting from over 230,000 open-source agent skills by proposing an efficient two-stage retrieval system based on standard information retrieval techniques rather than expensive LLM-mediated loops. Evaluated across a comprehensive grid on the SkillsBench benchmark, SkillSeek achieves performance parity with costly LLM-based selection methods while significantly reducing costs, demonstrating that a bi-encoder and cross-encoder pipeline can serve as a highly effective default for skill retrieval at marketplace scale.
