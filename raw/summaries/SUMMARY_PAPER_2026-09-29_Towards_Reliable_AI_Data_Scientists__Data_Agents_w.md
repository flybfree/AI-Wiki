## Summary
This paper introduces a harness-centric framework for Data Agents to automate the end-to-end data science lifecycle with enhanced reliability, moving beyond general-purpose LLM reasoning and ad hoc tool use. The authors propose a taxonomy organizing agents across five functional stages—perception, planning, execution, verification, and repair—and analyze 15 technical routes within these stages. Key findings highlight four critical open reliability problems that cause silent failures, emphasizing the necessity of rigorous workflow harnesses and shared resources to ensure robust data analysis automation.
