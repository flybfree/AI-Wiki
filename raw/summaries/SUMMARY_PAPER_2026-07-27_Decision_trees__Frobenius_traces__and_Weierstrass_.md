## Summary
The paper investigates how the reduced minimal Weierstrass coefficients of an elliptic curve over the rationals can be derived from its Frobenius traces. By constructing a decision tree, it shows that the first two coefficients are recovered perfectly using the traces at primes 2 and 3, while the third requires the conductor parity as additional information. The authors also provide explicit formulas linking these coefficients to the Frobenius data and prove that all three coefficients are determined solely by the isogeny class of the curve.
