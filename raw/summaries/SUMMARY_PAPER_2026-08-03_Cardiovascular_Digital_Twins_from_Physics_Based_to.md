## Summary
The paper proposes a review of cardiovascular digital twin approaches that combine physics-based mechanisms with data-driven learning, aiming to balance interpretability and computational efficiency. It outlines how hybrid graph-based models integrate physical constraints while leveraging relational learning on vascular networks. The authors highlight progress in data assimilation and validation challenges toward clinical deployment.

## Key Takeaways
- Mechanistic models offer physiological insight but are computationally heavy, limiting scalability for many patients.
- Data-driven methods improve speed but may lack robustness without explicit physics guidance.
- Hybrid graph-based frameworks combine physical constraints with relational learning to achieve both interpretability and scalability.
