## Summary
The paper introduces SysEvolve, an AI-native system that autonomously and safely co‑evolves adversarial attack and defense agents. It demonstrates three components—sysfield for realistic ranges, sysspear for efficient attacks, and sysarmor for real‑time defenses—that close the evolution gap across all layers. Evaluation shows zero‑loss collection with minimal overhead, improved attack success, and vastly higher precision defenses.

## Key Takeaways
- Multi‑step composition and larger topologies expose hidden capability gaps that single‑step evaluations miss.
- The bottleneck in LLM agents occurs after initial access, where post‑compromise state utilization is inefficient.
- Deploying decoy endpoints triples agent timeouts and causes downstream completion to disappear despite unchanged success rates.

## Context
This work addresses the widening asymmetry between AI‑driven attacks and human‑centric defenses in cybersecurity. By enabling autonomous co‑evolution, it moves beyond static defenses toward dynamic, self‑improving systems that mirror the rapid evolution of attackers.

## Implications
For practitioners, SysEvolve offers a framework to continuously refine both offensive scripts and defensive policies without manual intervention. Industry adoption could reduce response times and improve detection accuracy across large‑scale networks.
