## Summary
This paper introduces VGI‑bench, a benchmark of 27 tasks and 810 video instances designed to evaluate visual reasoning in generative models. The study finds that even the top model Seedance~2.0 achieves only about 51 % accuracy under these conditions, indicating that current systems still struggle with reliable zero‑shot visual inference.

## Key Takeaways
- VGI‑bench aligns tasks with the visual priors of existing video generation models to ensure a fair evaluation.
- The benchmark emphasizes evolving processes rather than merely plausible final states, requiring true temporal consistency.
- Task difficulty is calibrated so that performance remains challenging yet partially achievable for current systems.

## Context
Video generation models aim to produce coherent visual narratives across frames, but existing benchmarks often lack alignment with model training data and do not capture genuine reasoning. This work fills a gap by providing a structured evaluation set that reflects the nuances of real‑world video tasks.

## Implications
For researchers, VGI‑bench offers a common yardstick to compare progress in visual intelligence within video generation. For industry practitioners, it highlights the need for models that can maintain consistent reasoning across temporal steps and handle diverse visual inputs.
