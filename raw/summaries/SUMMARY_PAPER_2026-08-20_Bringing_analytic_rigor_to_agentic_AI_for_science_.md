## Summary
The paper introduces Brain Researcher, an agentic platform that enforces methodological rigor when AI agents analyze neuroimaging data. In benchmarks it boosted tool selection accuracy by 70.2 percentage points and improved verifiable grounding from 4.6% to 22.0%. The system also guides scientific review outcomes across collaborator‑led studies.

## Key Takeaways
- Brain Researcher raises first‑choice tool‑selection accuracy by 70.2 percentage points, demonstrating a clear benefit of guided agentic analysis.  
- It lifts verifiable grounding rates from 4.6% to 22.0%, showing that the platform helps agents stay anchored to evidence.  
- The framework introduces admissible analyses and claim scope rules, ensuring that AI outputs are defensible only when supported by appropriate alternatives.

## Context
Current AI research often produces analytical results without rigorous validation, leading to premature conclusions or methodological gaps. This work addresses those issues by embedding formal checks into the agentic workflow, aligning with the need for transparent scientific reasoning in neuroimaging studies.

## Implications
For researchers, Brain Researcher provides a concrete tool to embed judgment within computational pipelines rather than appending it later. For industry and practice, the platform can reduce false positives in AI‑driven science, fostering trustworthy outcomes that meet peer review standards.
