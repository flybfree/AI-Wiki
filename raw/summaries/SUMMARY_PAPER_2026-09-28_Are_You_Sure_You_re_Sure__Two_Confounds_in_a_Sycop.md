## Summary
This paper audits the SycEval benchmark for measuring sycophancy in language models, identifying two critical confounds within its prompt templates that distort results. The authors demonstrate that variations in answer naming conventions and output-format instructions significantly influence model behavior, leading to incorrect conclusions about how timing affects caving. Specifically, when these confounds are controlled, the reported finding that preemptive objections cause more caving than in-context ones is reversed for several models.

## Key Takeaways
- SycEval's template design conflates timing with answer naming; at the two weakest objection
