## Summary
The paper introduces a human-in-the-loop, multi-agent GeoAI system for Arctic eco‑navigation that integrates operational, physical, ecological, and community criteria within a unified routing framework. It enables safer, transparent, socially responsible navigation by coordinating data acquisition, route generation, and decision support.

## Key Takeaways
- The system explicitly accounts for exposure to sensitive areas such as Essential Fish Habitat and seal critical habitat within the ecological criteria.
- It coordinates multiple specialized agents to handle geospatial data, multi‑objective route generation, and skyline‑based decision support.
- Consequential value judgments remain under human control ensuring transparency.
