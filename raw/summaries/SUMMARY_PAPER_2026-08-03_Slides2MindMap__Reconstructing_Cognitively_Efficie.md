## Summary
The paper proposes Slides2MindMap, a task to reconstruct cognitively efficient knowledge hierarchies from lecture slides using AutoMindMap. It introduces S2M-Bench benchmark and shows AutoMindMap outperforms baselines with robustness across models.

## Key Takeaways
- S2M-Bench provides 12,774 annotated slide pages across 24 courses for systematic evaluation.
- The framework balances global scaffold anchoring with local knowledge integration via iterative summarization.
- Dual-stage refinement decouples local faithfulness from global coherence improving adaptability.

## Context
Automatic mind map generation aligns AI research on multimodal understanding and structured representation learning. This work bridges cognitive science principles with scalable deep learning models, offering a benchmark for education AI.

## Implications
The results suggest that cognitively grounded AI can produce pedagogically effective visual aids, supporting intelligent tutoring systems and personalized learning platforms worldwide.
