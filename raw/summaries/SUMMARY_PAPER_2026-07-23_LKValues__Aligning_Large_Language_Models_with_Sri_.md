## Summary
The paper introduces LKValues, a resource suite for aligning large language models with Sri Lankan societal values using Sinhala-English news scenarios. It fine‑tunes open‑weight LLMs and shows modest but meaningful improvements in value alignment across English and Sinhala.

## Key Takeaways
- The survey of 205 respondents yields 40 majority‑endorsed values, forming the basis for a culturally grounded dataset.
- LKvaluesIT provides 150k scenario‑based instances derived from news, enabling fine‑tuning; LKvaluesBench offers 1,000 evaluation cases.
- Fine‑tuning reduces invalid outputs and cross‑lingual disparities but gains are still limited by model family.

## Context
In AI alignment research, cultural bias toward Western norms is a known problem. This work addresses it for Sri Lanka, showing that value‑aligned datasets can mitigate such bias in multilingual settings.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt LKValues as a replicable pipeline for low‑resource country‑specific value alignment, improving model relevance and fairness across diverse linguistic contexts.
