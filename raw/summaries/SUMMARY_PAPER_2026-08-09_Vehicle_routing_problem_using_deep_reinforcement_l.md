## Summary
The paper investigates three real‑world logistics cases where deep reinforcement learning (DRL) is applied to solve the Vehicle Routing Problem, comparing its performance against traditional optimization baselines. It reports that DRL‑generated routes achieve a total cost reduction of over 10 % and highlights the potential for extending DRL algorithms to other VRP variations.

## Key Takeaways
- The study demonstrates that DRL can outperform conventional methods by delivering routes with up to ten percent lower total transportation costs.  
- Three distinct external truck network scenarios were evaluated, showing consistent improvement across all cases despite varying constraints and data complexities.  
- The authors argue that the results suggest a promising pathway for generalizing DRL approaches beyond the specific VRP framework explored.

## Context
