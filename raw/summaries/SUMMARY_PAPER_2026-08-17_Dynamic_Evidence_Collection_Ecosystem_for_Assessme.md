## Summary
The paper introduces a “Dynamic Evidence Collection Ecosystem” that redefines assessment by gathering continuous, multi‑source evidence of student learning rather than relying on isolated final products. It integrates an AI‑enabled layer for analytics, formative feedback and transparency while testing the hypothesis that academic integrity should be designed into assessments rather than treated as a separate detection problem.

## Key Takeaways
- The ecosystem emphasizes iterative artefacts, design logs, activity rounds, self‑reflection and peer collaboration to create authentic evidence of learning over time.  
- An AI layer provides analytics, formative feedback and transparent reporting that supports the collection process without compromising integrity.  
- The framework treats academic integrity as a design element within assessments, shifting focus from detection to proactive assessment practices.

## Context
Generative AI can produce convincing essays, code and designs, eroding trust in single‑point product grading. This work responds to the need for assessment models that remain valid amid such capabilities by embedding continuous evidence collection into the learning process, aligning with scholarship on authenticity and AI‑rich educational contexts.

## Implications
Institutions adopting this ecosystem can strengthen credibility of assessments while leveraging AI for supportive analytics rather than punitive detection. Practitioners gain a scalable model to embed integrity into assessment design, fostering trust and more meaningful feedback loops in AI‑enhanced education.
