## Summary
The paper introduces Cognivia, an AI therapist that automatically detects cognitive distortions and generates rational CBT responses. It outperforms baseline methods in both recognition and response generation using a hierarchical quality evaluation framework. The authors release code at the provided URL.

## Key Takeaways
- Cognivia integrates automatic identification of cognitive distortions with evidence‑based rational answer generation, leveraging a fine‑tuned lightweight LLM on an augmented CBT dataset.
- The system uses multi‑stage prompting and expert supervision to ensure domain specificity and avoid overly flattering replies.
- A hierarchical quality evaluation framework combining lexical metrics, LLM judges, and human expert review is proposed as the first of its kind.

## Context
The rapid rise of large language models has opened new possibilities for mental‑health assistance, yet most applications lack domain precision and reliable performance metrics. Cognivia addresses these gaps by grounding responses in established CBT literature and rigorous evaluation protocols.

## Implications
For clinicians, Cognivia offers a scalable tool that can triage patients and provide initial guidance without replacing human expertise. For the AI industry, it sets a benchmark for mental‑health applications through transparent evaluation and open code, encouraging responsible deployment.
