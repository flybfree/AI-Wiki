## Summary
The paper introduces Prove2Me, an open collaborative platform that enables large‑scale math formalization using AI agents and human users. It demonstrates how natural language prompts can generate Lean 4 proofs that are machine‑checked, facilitating crowd‑sourced proof building. The platform is open‑source and accessible via a web interface.

## Key Takeaways
- AI agents can produce complex Lean 4 proofs from natural‑language instructions, reducing the need for specialized verification expertise.
- The platform’s harness allows agents to reference and extend existing formal results, enabling modular proof construction across a community.
- Proofs are automatically checked by the system, guaranteeing correctness for all users.

## Context
The rise of large language models has made it significantly feasible to automate high‑level reasoning tasks that previously required expert coders. Prove2Me extends this capability to mathematical formalization, showing how code‑generation techniques can be applied beyond natural language generation.
