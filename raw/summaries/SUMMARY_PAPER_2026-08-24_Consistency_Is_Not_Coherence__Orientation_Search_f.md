## Summary
This paper introduces a method to create certified alignments between four 4D defence ontologies—Information Exchange Standard (IES), Higher Quality Data Model (HQDM), Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), and the UK defence data infrastructure. By merging IES and HQDM through a hand‑curated crosswalk and reasoning with HermiT, three significant alignment results are demonstrated.
