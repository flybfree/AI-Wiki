## Summary
This paper introduces a comprehensive benchmark aligned with the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) to evaluate large language models' performance on middle and high school science topics, addressing a gap in K-12 assessment tools. The study evaluates nine open-weight LLMs using a rigorous pipeline involving synthetic data generation, multi-judge validation, and psychometric analysis, revealing that smaller, locally deployable models can achieve high accuracy across diverse scientific domains. Furthermore, the inclusion of human reviewers highlights limitations in automated item alignment with educational standards, underscoring the necessity of human-in-the-loop processes for developing reliable assessment materials.
