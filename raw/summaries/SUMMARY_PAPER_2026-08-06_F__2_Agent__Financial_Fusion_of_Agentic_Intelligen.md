## Summary  
The paper introduces F^2Agent, a multimodal agentic system that fuses financial data across modalities to generate robust trading signals. Experiments on six stocks and crypto assets show it outperforms 16 baselines with an average annualized return improvement of over 20%.

## Key Takeaways  
- The hierarchy of specialized agents extracts modality-specific signals, enabling comprehensive feature capture.  
- A modality‑aware adaptive fusion mechanism captures fine‑grained inter‑modality dependencies dynamically.  
- Noise‑robust consistency regularization makes the trading signals resilient to market noise.

## Context  
Current AI research focuses on integrating heterogeneous data into single models, yet most approaches lack explicit handling of cross‑modal interactions and robustness. This work advances multimodal fusion by treating it as an adaptive process within an agentic framework.

## Implications  
For traders and developers, F^2Agent offers a practical solution to improve portfolio performance while mitigating noise, encouraging adoption of modular, robust AI agents in financial systems.
