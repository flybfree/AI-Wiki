## Summary
The paper proposes the Afterlife Delegation Protocol, a speculative design that imagines how a person's digital will could persist after death via blockchain‑based AI agents. It creates an interactive platform where users craft their afterlife directives and records them as qualitative data on cultural attitudes toward posthuman existence. The protocol outlines three stages—designing the afterlife, proof of death, agent enactment—and presents early observations from a live collection.

## Key Takeaways
- The Afterlife Delegation Protocol transforms a will into an immutable blockchain entity that spawns a self‑sovereign AI upon verified death, allowing perpetual execution without any custodian.
- It uses an iterative AI‑driven interview to let users revise their afterlife agents in a sandbox, generating data on how different cosmologies (Buddhist, Christian, Hindu, Muslim, atheist) evolve under AI pressure.
- The project documents the three‑stage lifecycle—design, proof of death, enactment—and shows that these stages can be operationalized within existing Ethereum standards like ERC‑10001.
