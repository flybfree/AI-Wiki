## Summary
The paper studies developer pain points in federated learning by analyzing 495 Stack Overflow posts and 9,116 GitHub issues from 92 projects. It identifies nine dominant Stack Overflow topics and thirteen GitHub topics with high unresolved rates. The study shows that environment setup, API breakages, training instability, and privacy integration are persistent challenges.

## Key Takeaways
- Environment setup and dependency compatibility problems have high unresolved rates and long resolution times, indicating tooling gaps.
- Training instability under non-IID data is a recurring issue across both platforms, suggesting algorithmic robustness needs improvement.
- How‑type questions dominate the help sought, reflecting developers' need for procedural guidance rather than just answers.

## Context
Federated learning aims to train models collaboratively without sharing raw data, a key approach for privacy‑preserving AI. This research highlights that even with good intentions, technical friction can hinder adoption and progress.

## Implications
For framework designers, the findings suggest prioritizing stable environments and clear migration paths to reduce developer frustration. Educators should address these pain points when teaching FL, making tooling and documentation central to curriculum design.
