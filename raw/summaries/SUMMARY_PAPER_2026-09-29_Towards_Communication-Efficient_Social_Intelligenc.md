## Summary
This paper introduces Teacher-Assisted Communication Training (TACT), a framework designed to enhance social goal attainment in language agents while significantly reducing communication costs by optimizing both action strategy and expression. TACT employs specialized revision modules to refine student-generated actions, selecting optimal revisions based on a balance between goal support and token efficiency, which are then distilled into the agent via on-policy learning.
