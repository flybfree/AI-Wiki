## Summary
The paper introduces BlueLM-GUI, a 35B-parameter mobile GUI agent designed to bridge critical gaps between sandbox training and real-world deployment. By implementing a real-device-centric flywheel grounded in three core principles, the authors demonstrate how continuous learning from actual device interactions can significantly enhance model performance. The system achieves state-of-the-art results on major benchmarks, outperforming leading closed-source models by over five points while maintaining robust transferability to production environments.
