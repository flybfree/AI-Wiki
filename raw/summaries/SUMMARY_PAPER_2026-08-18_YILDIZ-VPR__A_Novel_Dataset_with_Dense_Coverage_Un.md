## Summary
The paper introduces YILDIZ-VPR, a dense visual place recognition dataset gathered by walking through the Davutpasa campus of Yildiz Technical University under varied environmental conditions. The collection combines outdoor scenes captured with GPS‑synchronized GoPro 9 video and auxiliary sensor data to provide precise location labels for each frame.

## Key Takeaways
- Each video is synchronized with GPS coordinates, enabling accurate geo‑referencing of extracted frames.
- The dataset also records gyroscope, speed, and temperature information, offering rich temporal context beyond visual cues.
- Long‑term variability across seasons, times of day, and weather conditions creates a long‑lasting record of real‑world scene changes.

## Context
Visual place recognition is essential for applications such as navigation and mapping where users must identify locations from images. Existing datasets often lack dense pedestrian‑level coverage or fail to reflect diverse environmental factors like seasonality and weather, limiting the realism of training models.

## Implications
This dataset will enable researchers to develop more robust image‑based and temporal visual place recognition systems that generalize across real‑world conditions. Practitioners in smart city planning and augmented reality can leverage YILDIZ-VPR for improved location services and immersive experiences.
