## Summary
This paper outlines progress toward an AI Software Factory designed to accelerate the entire software development lifecycle, addressing the bottleneck where current AI coding tools fail to improve end-to-end efficiency due to Amdahl's law. The proposed system targets all SDLC stages—including targeting, coding, reviewing, and operations—by generating a metadata exhaust that enables continuous self-improvement through model fine-tuning and World Model updates. Scaled deployments within Microsoft demonstrate significant gains, achieving three times engineering efficiency over agentic coding approaches and up to twenty-two times token efficiency on evolutionary coding tasks in data systems.
