## Summary
The paper introduces a meshless domain randomization approach that works on the parameter space of 3D Gaussian Splatting. It replaces traditional polygon meshes with randomized radiance fields generated by perturbing illumination and geometry. The method produces synthetic training datasets for complex organic subjects without extracting textures or meshes.

## Key Takeaways
- Photometric DR modifies baked illumination and color balance by adjusting spherical harmonics coefficients, creating varied lighting conditions.
- Procedural DR replaces original textures with 3D spatial noise, isolating the geometric shape while altering appearance.
- The perturbed radiance fields are composited over stochastically varied backgrounds using a rasterization engine to generate diverse training samples.

## Context
Domain randomization is essential for bridging sim-to-real gaps in AI, especially when dealing with complex organic forms where mesh extraction is costly. This work demonstrates that 3D Gaussian Splatting can serve as a lightweight alternative to traditional mesh-based pipelines, enabling scalable and flexible dataset generation.

## Implications
For practitioners, this approach reduces reliance on expensive texture mapping and mesh reconstruction for synthetic data, accelerating training of vision models. In industry, it opens new pathways for generating diverse organic datasets without compromising geometric fidelity, supporting applications in medical imaging and robotics.
