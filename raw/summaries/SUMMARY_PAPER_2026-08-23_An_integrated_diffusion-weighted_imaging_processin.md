## Summary
The paper presents an integrated platform that processes diffusion-weighted imaging from MR-Linac and generates literature‑grounded clinical interpretation using a retrieval‑augmented generation module, with expert ratings confirming its reliability.

## Key Takeaways
- The platform combines deep learning processing of low‑signal‑to‑noise DWI with a RAG system that cites specific source sections for each statement.  
- Independent experts rated the interpretation on three metrics, achieving a pooled mean of 4.65 / 5 across all cases, indicating strong clinical reasoning and citation quality.  
- Raters agreed within one point on 85 % of paired ratings, highlighting the system’s reliability.

## Context
This work fills a gap where quantitative DWI processing is not linked to actionable, traceable clinical insights in radiotherapy. By merging AI with curated medical literature, it exemplifies RAG as a bridge between raw data and expert knowledge.

## Implications
The platform could become a standard tool for MR‑Linac clinics, improving treatment planning accuracy while providing audit trails. It also shows that LLM outputs can be validated by human experts, paving the way for broader AI adoption in radiation oncology.
