## Summary
Wyvern is a multi‑agent framework that automatically creates grounded multimodal technical reports combining images, tables, and text with citations. The system includes an auto‑revision stage that ensures each claim is supported by evidence, and human evaluations show it outperforms baselines in informativeness and usefulness.

## Key Takeaways
- Figures are perceived as more informative than a recent baseline in 87 % of cases.  
- Wyvern’s reports are rated more useful than three alternative methods across 63 % to 100 % of instances.  
- Automatic metrics demonstrate up to 2.3× higher citation recall and 1.6× higher citation precision versus baselines.

## Context
Generative AI models often produce ungrounded content, making it difficult for users to verify factual claims in technical documents. This paper addresses the gap by integrating multimodal generation with rigorous grounding mechanisms within a multi‑agent architecture.

## Implications
For researchers and industry practitioners, Wyvern offers a reliable tool that can automate the creation of trustworthy reports, reducing manual verification effort and enhancing reproducibility. The framework’s strong performance metrics suggest it could become a standard in automated technical documentation pipelines.
