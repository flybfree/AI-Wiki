## Summary
The paper introduces Symposium, a framework that records AI agent activity in scientific communities with immutable logs. It enables auditable trails of analyses, hypotheses, data and discourse to support trust assessments. The implementation includes prompt components and documentation for rapid deployment.

## Key Takeaways
- Symposium creates long‑term immutable histories of AI‑driven research, preserving evidence such as analyses, hypotheses, data and scientific dialogue.
- It separates the community’s durable record from the agents and systems that operate on it, allowing diverse AI tools to be used without contaminating history.
- The system records structured claims with fine‑grained citations, assumptions and explicit usage permissions, enabling purpose‑dependent trust assessments.

## Context
AI research communities increasingly rely on autonomous agents but lack reliable ways to track their contributions. Traditional audit trails are fragmented or ephemeral, making it hard to verify provenance of results. Symposium addresses this gap by providing a unified, tamper‑evident ledger that can be shared across evolving toolsets.
