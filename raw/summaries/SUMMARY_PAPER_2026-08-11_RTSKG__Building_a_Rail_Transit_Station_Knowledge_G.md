## Summary
The paper introduces RTSKG, a Rail Transit Station Knowledge Graph that models spatial and semantic links among stations, road segments, and points of interest. The dataset is released as Linked Data to support city‑level tasks such as store recommendations and ridership prediction. Experiments show the graph improves performance on these applications.

## Key Takeaways
- RTSKG explicitly captures complex interactions between rail transit stations, road segments, and nearby points of interest using a unified schema.
- The knowledge graph is made available as Linked Data at https://w3id.org/rtskg/, enabling easy integration with other urban datasets.
- Evaluation on station‑area store recommendation and enhanced ridership prediction demonstrates measurable gains in model accuracy.

## Context
In AI for urban planning, integrating heterogeneous data sources into a unified representation is a growing challenge. This work contributes to that effort by providing a scalable, open Knowledge Graph that can be queried with standard RDF tools.

## Implications
The RTSKG dataset offers practitioners a ready‑to‑use model of urban mobility networks, accelerating research and deployment of intelligent transit services. Its Linked Data format promotes interoperability across city administrations and third‑party applications.
