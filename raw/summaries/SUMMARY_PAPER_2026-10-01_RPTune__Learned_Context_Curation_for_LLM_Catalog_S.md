## Summary
RPTune is an end-to-end framework designed to optimize in-context catalog search for small merchant businesses by coupling learned catalog curation with LLM post-training using automatically generated supervision. The method employs an encoder-reorganizer curator to order and prune products based on downstream model feedback, while simultaneously improving the LLM via context-relative rewards derived from curated catalogs. Evaluation across seven real-world merchants demonstrates that RPTune significantly boosts search accuracy for both proprietary and open-weight models, achieving gains of up to 31.4 percentage points through curation alone and an additional average improvement of 10.3 points post-training.
