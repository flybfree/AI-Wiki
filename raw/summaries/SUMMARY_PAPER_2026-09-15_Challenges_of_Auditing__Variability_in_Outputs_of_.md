## Summary
This paper investigates the systematic variability in health-related advice generated by frontier large language models across different access modes, such as public chatbot interfaces versus developer APIs. The authors demonstrate that evaluations conducted via standard API endpoints frequently fail to reflect the actual outputs experienced by end-users through consumer-facing applications. These discrepancies significantly undermine the validity of current AI safety and performance audits, highlighting a critical gap between technical testing and real-world deployment.
