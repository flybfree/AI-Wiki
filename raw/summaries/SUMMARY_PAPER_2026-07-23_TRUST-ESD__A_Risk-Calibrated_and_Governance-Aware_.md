## Summary
This paper introduces TRUST‑ESD, a risk‑calibrated and governance‑aware AI framework designed to support enterprise strategic decisions under uncertainty. The framework integrates predictive utility estimation, conformal uncertainty calibration, CVaR‑based downside‑risk scoring, risk memory retrieval, policy‑as‑code governance, explainability, and human oversight to recommend balanced strategies.

## Key Takeaways
- TRUST‑ESD improves risk‑adjusted utility by 7.95% compared with strong uncertainty‑aware baselines while maintaining competitive predictive accuracy, highlighting its effectiveness in balancing value and reliability.
- The framework reduces CVaR (Conditional Value at Risk) by 23.78%, demonstrating a substantial decrease in downside risk exposure that is critical for conservative decision making.
- Governance compliance rises by 9.76% through policy‑as‑code enforcement, showing how structured governance mechanisms enhance trustworthy enterprise AI.

## Context
Enterprise strategic decisions often rely on AI predictions that ignore uncertainty, leading to overconfidence and non‑compliant outcomes. This work addresses the gap between accuracy and risk awareness in AI decision support systems, aligning with broader trends toward explainable and auditable AI.

## Implications
For industry practitioners, TRUST‑ESD offers a practical roadmap to embed uncertainty calibration and governance into AI pipelines without sacrificing performance. Practitioners can leverage these findings to build more trustworthy, compliant, and resilient strategic decision tools.
