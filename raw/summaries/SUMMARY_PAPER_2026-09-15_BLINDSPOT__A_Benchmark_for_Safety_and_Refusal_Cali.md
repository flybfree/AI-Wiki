## Summary
The paper introduces Blindspot, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate the trajectory-level safety and refusal calibration of long-horizon tool-using large language model agents. By simulating adaptive adversarial interactions across multiple domains and attack families, the authors demonstrate that safety failures frequently emerge only after several initially benign interaction steps. Their evaluation of thirteen models reveals significant disparities in how different systems balance utility with appropriate refusal, underscoring the need to assess agent safety as a dynamic, multi-turn property rather than a static binary outcome.
