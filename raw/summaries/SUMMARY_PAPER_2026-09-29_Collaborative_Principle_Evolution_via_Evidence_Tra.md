## Summary
This paper introduces COEVOLVE, a framework for collaborative scientific discovery that leverages evidence transfer between parallel principle-evolution branches to overcome the limitations of sequential exploration in LLM-based agents. By implementing a coordination core with value-of-information-gated routing and context-discounted likelihood injection, the method enables distinct branches to share measurements while maintaining separate principle posteriors. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in solution quality and wall-clock speedup across multiple scientific tasks compared to single-branch baselines.

## Key Takeaways
- COEVOLVE addresses the inefficiency of sequential principle-evolution methods by formulating discovery as evidence transfer between parallel branches, allowing agents to explore the hypothesis space more broadly while reducing wall-clock time wasted on challenging problems through a centralized coordination core.
