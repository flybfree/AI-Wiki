## Summary
The paper introduces TSN4PI, a unified framework for tracking political ideologies on social media over time. It combines style‑transfer language models to detect ideology and graph neural networks to forecast future shifts. Experiments on platforms X and Truth Social show the model captures polarization trends accurately.

## Key Takeaways
- The PIDN module uses large language models with style transfer and unsupervised domain adaptation to robustly detect political ideologies while filtering out non‑political content from noisy, cross‑domain data.
- The PIPN module employs temporal graph neural networks to predict future ideological shifts, providing insights into ideology presence, intensity, and evolution.
- Two large‑scale datasets for noncommercial research are released to support further analysis of social media political discourse.

## Context
Understanding the dynamics of political ideologies on digital platforms is crucial as AI models increasingly shape public opinion. This work advances methodological tools that combine natural language processing with temporal modeling, reflecting broader trends in multimodal and time‑aware AI systems.

## Implications
For researchers, TSN4PI offers a scalable pipeline for studying ideological change without manual annotation. Practitioners can leverage the framework to monitor polarization risks and design interventions that mitigate extreme discourse on social media.
