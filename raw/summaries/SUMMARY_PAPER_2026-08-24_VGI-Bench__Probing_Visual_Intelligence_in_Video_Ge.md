## Summary
This paper introduces VGI‑Bench, a benchmark designed to evaluate visual reasoning in video generation models with tasks aligned to their visual priors. The study demonstrates that even the leading model Seedance 2.0 performs poorly, achieving only 51.0% accuracy under strict criteria.

## Key Takeaways
- Current video generation models can solve only a subset of visually grounded reasoning tasks.
- Even the strongest model Seedance 2.0 achieves only 51.0% under evaluation criteria.
- Output failures often involve refining early hypotheses rather than correcting errors.

## Context
Video generation research has focused on producing plausible final frames, yet reliable assessment is scarce because benchmarks lack alignment with model priors and do not capture evolving processes. VGI‑Bench addresses this gap by providing a structured set of tasks that test the models’ ability to reason across time.

## Implications
The results highlight the need for more rigorous evaluation before claiming video generation systems are competent. Practitioners should design benchmarks that reflect real visual reasoning challenges and consider them when advancing model capabilities.
