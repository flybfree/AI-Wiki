## Summary
The paper proposes an information‑theoretic model that links autism’s insistence on sameness to a reduction of surprise and uncertainty. It defines autism as a restriction of cognitive functions to discrimination, memorization and prediction of tangible environmental properties.

## Key Takeaways
- The metric D_H(R,M)=H(R|M)+H(M|R) quantifies surprise and uncertainty as conditional entropies that the individual seeks to minimize.

- The framework also maps concepts such as sensory overload and anxiety onto entropy terms, offering a unified quantitative view of autism‑related distress.
