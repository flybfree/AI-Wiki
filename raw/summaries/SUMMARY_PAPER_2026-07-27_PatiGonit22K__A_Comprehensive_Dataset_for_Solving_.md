## Summary
This paper introduces PatiGonit22K, a large Bengali Mathematical Word Problem (MWP) dataset with 22,441 annotated problems that extend the original PatiGonit collection. The authors emphasize that the new set balances simple and multi‑operation equations while ensuring linguistic consistency and mathematical correctness across all entries.

## Key Takeaways
- The dataset comprises 22,441 Bengali MWPs, representing a substantial increase in scale compared to earlier resources.  
- Each problem is translated, culturally adapted, and verified for both linguistic accuracy and mathematical validity.  
- The collection includes a balanced mix of simple and complex equations, providing a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating reasoning performance.

## Context
The scarcity of high‑quality annotated MWPs in low‑resource languages hampers progress in natural language understanding and quantitative reasoning models. This work addresses that gap by delivering a substantial Bengali dataset, enabling researchers to train and compare systems on a common ground.

## Implications
For practitioners, PatiGonit22K offers a ready benchmark for developing robust Bengali NLP solutions, potentially improving educational tools and automated tutoring systems. The broader field benefits from more reliable evaluation metrics that reflect real‑world low‑resource language challenges.
