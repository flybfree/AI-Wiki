## Summary
The paper introduces a machine‑learning based workflow that predicts gas lift performance curves from historical production data, then uses Bayesian optimization to set optimal injection rates within facility limits. The model requires no downhole gauges or multi‑rate well tests. Piloted on 30 wells it delivered over five percent average uplift and is now deployed across two hundred plus lifts.

## Key Takeaways
- The workflow replaces costly physical testing with a data‑driven ML forecast of gas lift curves, enabling rapid optimization.
- Bayesian Optimization finds the best injection rates while respecting well capacity constraints, improving production efficiency.
- Pilot results show a five percent uplift and full deployment across 200+ wells demonstrates scalability in Bakken.

## Context
This approach leverages AI to automate decisions that traditionally rely on expensive downhole instrumentation, aligning with trends toward predictive maintenance and real‑time optimization. It illustrates how unsupervised time series analysis can replace labor‑intensive testing, reducing risk and downtime.

## Implications
For operators lacking access to multi‑rate wells, the method offers a cost effective path to higher yields without new infrastructure. Practitioners can adopt similar pipelines for other unconventional assets where data is limited but production impact matters.
