## Summary
This paper introduces a unified agentic framework designed to automate video editing across tasks that differ significantly in editorial goals, structural complexity, and creative demand. By applying the system to scene previews, video summaries, and cinematic trailers, the authors demonstrate how AI-driven tools can navigate multiple production constraints while preserving narrative coherence. The study evaluates the quality of these automated outputs and explores how agency and automation intersect in modern media workflows.

## Key Takeaways
- The proposed agentic tooling framework successfully automates editing across three distinct tasks that vary widely in editorial objectives, technical complexity, and creative requirements.
- Scene previews, video summaries, and cinematic trailers serve as practical benchmarks to assess how AI agents balance structural constraints with stylistic intent during automated assembly.
- The evaluation emphasizes that automated systems are best positioned to augment human editors by handling repetitive or time-intensive tasks while preserving room for creative decision-making.
