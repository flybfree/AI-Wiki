## Summary
The paper explores using large language models to generate search heuristics for hierarchical task network (HTN) planning, extending prior work on classical HTN methods. Experiments on six benchmark domains show LLM-generated heuristics nearly match the coverage of top planners while cutting search effort by 83%. The study evaluates nine different LLMs under domain-specific prompting.

## Key Takeaways
- LLM-generated heuristics nearly match the coverage of the best HTN planner.
- They reduce search effort on 83% of shared problems.
- The approach extends Corrêa et al.'s methodology to hierarchical planning and evaluates nine LLMs under domain‑specific prompting.

## Context
Hierarchical task network planning is a classic AI technique for decomposing goals into subgoals. Recent advances have focused on improving classical heuristics, but LLM integration remains underexplored in this field.

## Implications
This work demonstrates that LLMs can serve as dynamic heuristic generators, offering scalable solutions for complex planning problems and paving the way for more efficient autonomous agents in robotics and logistics.
