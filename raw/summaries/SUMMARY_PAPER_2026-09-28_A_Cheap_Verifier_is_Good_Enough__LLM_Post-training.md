## Summary
This study investigates whether verifier agreement reliably predicts the success of large language model post-training on semi-verifiable reward tasks across medical, legal, and finance domains. Analyzing over 11,000 H100 GPU-hours using Qwen3 trainees and various verifiers, the authors find that higher verifier agreement does not consistently correlate with optimal training outcomes, challenging the assumption that expensive "golden" verifiers are always superior.
