## Summary
Dr. AGENTONOMICS presents a didactic experiment that uses an AI lecture agent as both subject and teaching tool within a TUM course on AI agents in business administration. The prototype demonstrates how the same system can evolve into multiple roles—avatar lecturer, design consultant, and meta‑agent—while sharing a unified interface and knowledge base.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework treats AI agents as economic entities that can be designed, managed, and governed through an integrated architecture.
- The prototype’s three cumulative roles share the same interface, intelligence layer, tools, knowledge base, and ecosystem connection, orchestrated by a role‑specific algorithm selector.
- This experiment shows that an agent can simultaneously teach, apply, and reproduce its own design framework.

## Context
AI agents are increasingly being integrated into educational settings to personalize learning and demonstrate emerging economic models. The paper situates this integration within a polycentric AI economy concept, where multiple autonomous systems interact through shared platforms.

## Implications
This work suggests that agent‑driven education can become self‑sustaining, reducing reliance on human instructors while maintaining pedagogical quality. It also provides a template for scaling AI governance frameworks across industries.
