## Summary
DeepSeek has released two open‑weight large language models, V4 Flash and V4 Pro, that significantly improve on its previous V3.2 architecture while offering massive context windows of up to one million tokens. The models leverage advanced techniques such as distillation, MoE, and multi‑head latent attention, run on Huawei chips, and are priced competitively against leading frontier AI services.

## Key Takeaways
- Both V4 Flash (≈284 billion parameters) and V4 Pro (1.6 trillion parameters) have context windows exceeding one million tokens, enabling processing of large documents or entire codebases in a single prompt.
- The Pro model is the largest open‑weight LLM available, surpassing models from Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and beating DeepSeek’s V3.2 in parameter count.
- Pricing for V4 Flash is $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens, undercutting many leading open‑weight alternatives such as GPT‑5.4 Nano and Claude Haiku 4.5.
- Performance on reasoning benchmarks rivals or exceeds some closed models like OpenAI’s GPT‑5.2, though it lags frontier models by roughly three to six months.

## Context
DeepSeek’s V4 series represents a major shift in Chinese AI development, demonstrating that open‑weight models can achieve high performance with fewer resources and newer hardware from Huawei. This move challenges the dominance of Nvidia‑centric architectures and highlights the growing importance of cost efficiency and large context windows for enterprise adoption.

## Implications
The release could accelerate adoption of open‑source LLMs in regions where GPU supply is constrained, offering a cheaper alternative to Western models that rely on scarce H200 chips. Companies may integrate V4 Pro’s massive parameter count for specialized tasks while using Flash for lightweight inference, reshaping market dynamics and prompting further innovation in MoE architectures.
