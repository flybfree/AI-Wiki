## Summary
This study introduces an AI‑assisted workflow for requirements elicitation interviews that combines theory‑guided script generation with real‑time topic tracking and on‑demand follow‑up question generation. The evaluation shows the AI‑supported condition outperforms a training‑only approach across multiple metrics, indicating that integrated AI support reshapes interview dynamics.

## Key Takeaways
- AI‑generated scripts score higher (92.8 vs 74.8) according to best‑practice rubrics, reflecting better coverage and refinement of goals.
- The AI‑assisted group covers fewer topics (9.6 vs 14.5) but asks more follow‑ups per topic (3.43 vs 1.15), suggesting deeper exploration rather than breadth.
- Participants rate topic tracking as the most useful feature, with 86% agreement, highlighting its practical value in real‑time interview management.

## Context
The paper addresses a gap where AI support has been applied to isolated tasks like script generation but not integrated into full elicitation workflows. By linking AI outputs to live interview processes, it aligns with broader efforts to embed intelligent tools within human‑centered research.

## Implications
For researchers, the findings suggest that AI can act as an elicitation scaffold, improving efficiency and depth of requirement capture. Practitioners may adopt similar scaffolds to reduce cognitive load and generate more coherent, goal‑aligned artifacts in stakeholder interviews.
