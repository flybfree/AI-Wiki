## Summary
This paper introduces Myopia Prevention and Control 3.0 as an AI‑driven framework that transforms myopia prevention from reactive screening to proactive, personalized management. The authors present a closed‑loop pipeline integrating risk prediction, continuous monitoring, and adaptive interventions, highlighting evidence across each stage.

## Key Takeaways
- AI models can stratify individual myopia risk by analyzing multimodal data such as eye measurements, lifestyle factors, and genetic markers, offering more accurate predictions than traditional population averages.  
- Wearable sensors and smartphone apps enable real‑time monitoring of visual acuity and ocular health, allowing early detection of progression before symptoms appear.  
- Personalized interventions are guided by closed‑loop feedback loops that adjust treatment plans based on continuously updated AI assessments.

## Context
The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence in healthcare has enabled systems to process complex, high‑dimensional data for predictive analytics.
