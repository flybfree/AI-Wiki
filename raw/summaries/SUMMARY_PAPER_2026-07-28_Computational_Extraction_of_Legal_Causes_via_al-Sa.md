## Summary
The paper introduces a set-theoretic formalization of the classical usuli method al‑Sabr wa al‑Taqsim, enabling computational extraction of legal causes within closed chapters of fiqh. By applying a truth‑table based algorithm to juristic verdicts it identifies minimal structural generators and removes logically redundant attributes, producing admissible candidate causes for further evaluation.

## Key Takeaways
- The algorithm computes the minimal set of conditions that generate a ruling from a complete truth table, thereby isolating only the essential operational rules.  
- Redundant attributes are systematically eliminated, leaving structures that represent genuine causal links rather than logical noise.  
- The framework relies on a finite school‑relative concept vocabulary and a fully specified ruling table to function correctly.

## Context
This work bridges classical jurisprudential reasoning with modern computational AI by treating legal inference as a set‑theoretic problem solvable through algorithmic analysis of truth tables.
