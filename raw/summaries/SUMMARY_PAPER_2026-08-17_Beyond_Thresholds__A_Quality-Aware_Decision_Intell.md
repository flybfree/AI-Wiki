## Summary
The paper introduces a Quality-Aware Decision Intelligence framework for cold chain IoT systems that transforms threshold alerts into proactive logistics decisions by modeling product degradation as a structured state S_q. Benchmarks show the framework reduces shelf-life error and spoilage compared to baselines, achieving near-optimal outcomes in most scenarios.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework defines a quality state S_q containing remaining shelf life L, degradation rate Q, estimation uncertainty U, and operational risk R derived from equations.
- Hybrid modeling of microbial kinetics with data-driven correction improves prediction accuracy over physics-only models.
- Retrieval-augmented generation using Phi-4 drives decision optimality and explanation quality.

## Context
Cold chain logistics relies on reactive monitoring that fails to link alerts to cumulative product degradation. Integrating AI reasoning into this domain enables proactive decisions, aligning with broader trends of AI-driven operational intelligence in supply chains.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt QADI to cut spoilage rates and enhance shelf-life forecasts, reducing waste and cost. The framework’s modular design invites integration into existing IoT cold chain platforms, fostering smarter, data‑driven logistics operations.
