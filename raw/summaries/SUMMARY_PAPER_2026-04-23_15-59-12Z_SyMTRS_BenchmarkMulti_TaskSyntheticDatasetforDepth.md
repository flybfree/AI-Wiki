## Summary
The paper introduces SyMTRS, a synthetic multi‑task benchmark for aerial imagery that simultaneously supplies depth estimation, domain adaptation, and super‑resolution tasks with pixel‑perfect geometric ground truth. The dataset includes high‑resolution RGB images (2048 × 2048), night‑time counterparts, and low‑resolution variants at x2, x4, and x8 scales.

## Key Takeaways
- It provides high‑resolution RGB aerial imagery with perfect depth maps for accurate geometric analysis.

- Low‑resolution versions are available at x2, x4, and x8 scales to support super‑resolution tasks.

## Context
Remote sensing research suffers from a severe shortage of high‑quality annotated data, limiting progress in monocular depth estimation and domain adaptation. SyMTRS addresses this gap by delivering a controlled environment with perfect geometric supervision across multiple tasks.

## Implications
