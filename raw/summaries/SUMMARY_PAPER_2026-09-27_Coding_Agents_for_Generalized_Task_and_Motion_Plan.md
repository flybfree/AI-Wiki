## Summary
This paper investigates whether coding agents can automate the synthesis of generalized task and motion planning programs, eliminating the extensive hand-engineering traditionally required to handle complex geometric and dynamic constraints. Through systematic evaluation across nearly 100,000 simulation episodes, the authors demonstrate that agent-synthesized code significantly outperforms classical planners and LLM baselines in both success rate and computational efficiency. The findings establish coding agents as a highly effective, scalable baseline for generalized planning tasks.
