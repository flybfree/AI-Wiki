## Summary
This paper introduces TSDS-Toolbox, a unified framework for measuring and comparing time‑series dataset similarity methods. The toolbox provides systematic, reproducible comparisons, flexible extensibility for custom datasets and tasks, and consistent evaluation via integrated reducers. Experiments across diverse settings demonstrate its effectiveness.

## Key Takeaways
- TSDS-Toolbox enables systematic and reproducible comparisons of time‑series dataset similarity methods, addressing the fragmentation of existing benchmarks.  
- The framework is designed to be extensible, allowing users to add new datasets, similarity metrics, and downstream tasks without altering core components.  
- Integrated reducers provide consistent evaluation for both dataset‑level and series‑level similarity approaches.

## Context
The rapid growth of AI has intensified the need for reliable data selection in time‑series modeling, where dataset similarity influences model performance. Prior work often relies on isolated tools that cannot be combined or extended, limiting reproducibility and scalability across research projects.

## Implications
TSDS-Toolbox offers practitioners a standardized platform to benchmark similarity metrics, accelerating progress in AI forecasting and generation tasks. By simplifying integration of diverse datasets, it will streamline model fine‑tuning pipelines and foster more robust AI systems.
