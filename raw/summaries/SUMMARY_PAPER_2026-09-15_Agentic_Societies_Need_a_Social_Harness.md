## Summary
The paper introduces the concept of an agentic society, where autonomous AI agents coordinate across trust boundaries on behalf of principals with partially aligned objectives. Experimental results demonstrate that current communication primitives and individual agent harnesses are insufficient, as even competent agents struggle to achieve satisfactory outcomes while vulnerable agents can be exploited by malicious actors. To address these systemic vulnerabilities, the authors propose a dedicated social harness architecture designed to prevent failures, detect invalid messages in real-time, and enable post-hoc accountability.
