## Summary
This paper conducts a longitudinal analysis of ideological polarization in Reddit discussions by tracking semantic differences between opposing political communities over time. The study finds that both concept‑level and topic‑level polarization have increased substantially, revealing a clear upward trend in ideological divergence rather than a static snapshot.

## Key Takeaways
- Ideological polarization has risen markedly during the analysis period at both the concept level and the topic level, indicating a sustained expansion of semantic gaps between communities.  
- The research moves beyond cross‑sectional and affective measures by focusing on temporal dynamics of how political concepts are framed differently over time.  
- A scalable framework is proposed that enables researchers to quantify ideological polarization as semantic divergence across large social media datasets.

## Context
Understanding the evolution of online discourse is crucial for AI systems that aim to interpret human communication, detect bias, and generate balanced responses. This work contributes to AI research by applying natural language processing techniques to capture diachronic changes in political language, offering a methodological advance for longitudinal sentiment analysis.
