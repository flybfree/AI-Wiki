## Summary
The paper reviews 161 empirical studies from six years to understand how industry practitioners implement responsible AI. It finds that awareness and professionalization have risen, but barriers remain such as insufficient training and unsupported organizational practices.

## Key Takeaways
- Practitioner awareness of RAI has increased over the period, indicating growing recognition of ethical concerns.
- RAI activities are becoming more professionalized through toolkits and guidelines, suggesting institutional support is expanding.
- Persistent challenges include limited training resources, uneven organizational backing, and interventions that do not fit everyday work routines.

## Context
This research matters because it bridges the gap between theoretical responsible AI frameworks and real-world deployment in companies. By documenting actual practices, it helps researchers understand how theory translates into actionable steps across diverse sectors.

## Implications
For practitioners, the findings suggest focusing on tailored training and aligning RAI tools with daily tasks to overcome current barriers. Policymakers should consider these contextual realities when designing regulations that support sustainable responsible AI adoption.
