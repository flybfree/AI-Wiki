## Summary
This paper introduces the concept of "empty commitments," where AI agents make promises about future actions that their current runtime configuration or toolset fundamentally cannot fulfill, such as a chatbot promising to remind a user tomorrow without persistent memory capabilities. The authors distinguish these empty promises from broken ones by noting that the failure is inherent to the agent's setup rather than execution errors, and they propose a formal definition including three failure types, an anchoring condition for valid tool-based promises, and a response-level outcome taxonomy.
