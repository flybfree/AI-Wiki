## Summary
This paper explores how a large language model can assist in constructing a formal proof that the square root of 2 is irrational within an LP (Logic Programming) framework. The authors use LPTP, a theorem prover based on natural deduction, to state and verify the proof, resulting in a complete formal proof where parts were generated by the LLM and fully checked by LPTP.

- By interacting with an LLM, the authors generate portions of the proof automatically while maintaining full verification by LPTP.
