## Summary
The paper investigates how lexical and pragmatic categories relate to emoji usage in colloquial Arabic posts on X using a fine‑tuned MARBERT model and logistic regression. It finds that nouns are most frequent but verbs exert the strongest influence, as shown by high verb density (η = 0.821, p < .001). The hybrid approach combines machine learning, lexical analysis, and pragmatic modeling.

## Key Takeaways
- Nouns dominate normalized frequency with mean 0.675 and standard deviation 0.161, indicating they are the most common category in emoji‑bearing texts.
- Verbs have a lower mean of 0.083 but show significant statistical association with emoji occurrence (p = .001) due to high verb density (η = 0.821).
- The study demonstrates that combining computational, statistical, and pragmatic methods yields a more accurate explanation than any single approach alone.
