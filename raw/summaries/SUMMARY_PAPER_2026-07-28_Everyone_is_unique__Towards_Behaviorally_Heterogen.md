## Summary
This paper introduces DebtBench, a benchmark that incorporates diverse personas to capture behavioral heterogeneity in debt collection negotiations. It also presents DebtGPT, an agent that balances financial recovery with user experience. Experiments across 16 state‑of‑the‑art LLMs show most models fail, while DebtGPT matches GPT-4o performance.

## Key Takeaways
- The study highlights that existing benchmarks assume static rational agents, ignoring the varied behaviors of real debtors.
- DebtBench is the first public persona‑enriched benchmark designed to expose this behavioral diversity in negotiation dialogue systems.
- DebtGPT achieves state‑of‑the‑art results, outperforming all open‑source baselines and approaching GPT‑4o’s performance.

## Context
The rapid adoption of large language models in customer service creates a need for evaluation methods that reflect real‑world variability. Traditional benchmarks often oversimplify user behavior, limiting insights into how AI can adapt to diverse personalities.

## Implications
For the financial industry, this work demonstrates that effective debt collection systems must account for heterogeneous users to improve both recovery rates and satisfaction. Practitioners should adopt persona‑aware evaluation frameworks to ensure their models perform robustly across real customers.
