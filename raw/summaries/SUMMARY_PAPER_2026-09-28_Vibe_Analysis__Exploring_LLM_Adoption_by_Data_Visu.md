## Summary
This study investigates the adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) among data visualization practitioners through semi-structured interviews with members of the Data Visualization Society. The research identifies an emerging workflow termed "vibe analysis," where designers leverage LLMs for both creative and technical components of visualization creation despite inherent model limitations. Key findings highlight that while LLMs accelerate workflows, they introduce unique challenges such as gaps in domain knowledge and difficulties in verifying chart accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- Vis designers actively integrate LLMs into their workflows for creative ideation and technical execution, giving rise to a new practice called "vibe analysis" that mirrors the rapid, iterative nature of vibe coding but applied to data visualization tasks.
- Participants face challenges similar to those in vibe coding alongside specific visualization issues, including significant gaps in LLMs' understanding of visualization best practices and the critical need for manual verification to ensure charts are accurate and meaningful.
- The study suggests a clear direction for future research and tool development, emphasizing the potential benefits of integrating LLM-mediated workflows with specialized data visualization tools that enforce domain-specific constraints, guidance, and best practices to mitigate error rates.
