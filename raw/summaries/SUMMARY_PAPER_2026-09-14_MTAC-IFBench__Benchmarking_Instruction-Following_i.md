## Summary
The paper introduces MTAC-IFBench, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate how well large language models follow complex instructions across multi-turn agentic coding workflows. While current benchmarks primarily assess final functional correctness or single-turn interactions, this work highlights the critical need for sustained instruction adherence throughout iterative development cycles. Experimental results reveal that existing code agents struggle significantly with long-horizon constraint following, showing rapid performance degradation as interaction sessions lengthen.
