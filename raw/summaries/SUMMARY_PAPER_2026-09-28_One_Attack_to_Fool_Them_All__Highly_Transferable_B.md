## Summary
This paper introduces O-Attack, a novel framework designed to generate highly transferable black-box adversarial attacks against frontier Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). The authors demonstrate that a single adversarial image can consistently mislead diverse MLLMs by exploiting the broad, cross-modally aligned semantic space within surrogate models, achieving significant improvements in attack success rates over existing methods.
