## Summary
ParaAgent introduces a novel framework for language model agents operating in open-world tool environments by addressing the performance-efficiency tradeoff inherent in balancing exploration and exploitation. The method, centered on a structured parallel-action loop called ParaAct, coordinates phase-level exploration with execution alongside action-level parallelism to enhance agent capabilities. Experiments demonstrate that ParaAgent-4B outperforms existing baselines, including GPT-4.1
