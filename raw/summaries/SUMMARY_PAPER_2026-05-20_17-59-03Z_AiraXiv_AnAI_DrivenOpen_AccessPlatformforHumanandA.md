## Summary
AiraXiv is an AI‑driven open‑access platform that enables both human and machine scientists to co‑author, review, and iterate on research preprints. The system integrates interactive UI tools for humans with Model Context Protocol (MCP) interactions for AI agents, and it has been validated as a submission venue for ICAIS 2025, showing rapid processing and inclusive participation.

## Key Takeaways
- AiraXiv combines human‑centric interfaces with MCP‑based AI authoring to create a continuous feedback loop between authors and models.  
- The platform is open‑access and serves as the official submission venue for ICAIS 2025, demonstrating its scalability under real conference loads.  
- Continuous iteration driven by reader feedback improves paper quality without compromising speed or inclusivity.

## Context
The rapid growth of AI‑generated research outpaces traditional publishing pipelines, creating bottlenecks in review and dissemination. AiraXiv addresses this by embedding AI as an active participant rather than a passive tool, aligning with the shift toward collaborative, iterative science.

## Implications
For researchers, AiraXiv offers a new workflow that reduces turnaround time while maintaining open access. For publishers and conferences, it provides a scalable model that can handle high submission volumes without overburdening human reviewers.
