## Summary
The paper introduces Mint-Agent, a family of finance‑native agentic foundation models that combine reliable domain knowledge with long‑horizon execution and auditable evidence. The authors demonstrate that their flagship models Mint-Cu (9B) and Mint-Ag (27B) outperform leading competitors on multiple financial benchmarks, achieving high reliability scores and strong executability metrics.

## Key Takeaways
- Mint-Agent’s data engine creates specialized tasks from real‑world financial sources, enabling precise atomic operations that are grounded in evidence.  
- The MintHarness framework maintains an auditable trail across extended research trajectories, supporting long‑horizon agentic execution with traceable reasoning.  
- Training combines SFT, critical‑step OPD, and RLVR to produce separate financial reasoning and agentic experts that merge into compact general‑purpose agents.

## Context
Finance‑native AI is a growing challenge as models must perform accurate calculations, navigate complex market dynamics, and produce auditable decision trails. This work bridges the gap between narrow domain expertise and scalable, trustworthy agentic behavior in open‑ended financial environments.

## Implications
For practitioners, Mint-Agent offers a reliable tool for automated trading strategies that can be audited and extended over time. The industry will benefit from models that combine high reliability with long‑term execution, reducing risk of costly errors in real‑time finance applications.
