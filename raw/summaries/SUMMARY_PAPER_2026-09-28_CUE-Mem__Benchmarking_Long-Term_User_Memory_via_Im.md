## Summary
This paper introduces CUE-Mem, a novel text-image-audio benchmark designed to evaluate how multimodal agents handle long-term user memory based on implicit cues rather than just explicit statements. The study reveals that while textualized systems struggle significantly with implicit evidence compared to oracle baselines, native multimodal access alone fails to uniformly resolve these challenges due to backbone dependency and retrieval noise introduced by indexing methods.
