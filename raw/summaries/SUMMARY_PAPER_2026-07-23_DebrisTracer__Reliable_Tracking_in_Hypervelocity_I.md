## Summary
DebrisTracer is a framework that improves the reliable tracking of debris in hypervelocity impact fast imaging by extending an existing topology‑based tracker with physical assumptions, leading to more accurate mass and speed estimates. Experiments show significant gains over traditional tools in predicting ejected mass and crater depth profiles. The method also reveals clear regimes within the debris population through statistical summaries.
