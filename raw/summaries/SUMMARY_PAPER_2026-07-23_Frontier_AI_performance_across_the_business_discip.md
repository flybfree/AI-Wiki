## Summary
This paper introduces BusinessCaseBench, a benchmark that measures AI performance on the analytical knowledge work performed by white‑collar professionals across multiple business disciplines. The results show that frontier language models already achieve high scores against expert rubrics and demonstrate substantial improvement within a two‑year period.

## Key Takeaways
- AI performance on analytical knowledge work is currently high, as evidenced by strong alignment with instructor‑written case solutions.
- Capability gains are rapid, with noticeable improvements in model families over just two years of development.
- The benchmark validates the case method’s effectiveness in training professionals and provides a reliable measure for assessing AI progress.

## Context
While existing LLM benchmarks focus on factual recall, coding, and narrow problem solving, they underrepresent the complex, judgment‑heavy tasks that define business analysis. This work fills that gap by applying a rigorous, expert‑driven framework to assess higher‑order reasoning in real‑world scenarios.

## Implications
The findings suggest that AI is already capable of supporting advanced case analysis, which could enhance graduate education and early‑career training programs. For industry practitioners, the rapid progress implies growing reliance on AI tools for strategic decision making and risk assessment.
