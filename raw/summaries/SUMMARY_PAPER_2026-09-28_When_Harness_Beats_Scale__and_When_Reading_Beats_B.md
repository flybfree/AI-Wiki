## Summary
This paper introduces a system for the DocSem document-grounded quantitative reasoning task that leverages Program-of-Thoughts generation and entity enrichment to achieve performance comparable to much larger models, demonstrating that architectural design can outweigh raw parameter scale. However, the study exposes a severe degradation in accuracy when processing watermarked test PDFs, revealing that OCR limitations rather than reasoning deficits are responsible for the bimodal distribution of results on the leaderboard.
