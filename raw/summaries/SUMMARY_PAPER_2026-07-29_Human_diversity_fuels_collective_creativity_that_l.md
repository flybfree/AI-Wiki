## Summary
The paper investigates how human diversity drives creative innovation and shows that large language models cannot replicate or sustain this effect. Experiments with native and non‑native English writers reveal that AI ideation compresses collective diversity while refinement preserves it.

## Key Takeaways
- AI ideation reduces the variety of ideas generated by both L1 and L2 writers, erasing the observed diversity advantage.
- The AI refinement stage maintains higher collective diversity across all participant groups.
- Individual ratings improve with AI ideation, highlighting a conflict between personal gain and group creativity.

## Context
Human creativity thrives on the intersection of varied cultural backgrounds, linguistic habits, and lived experiences. Large language models, despite their scale, lack genuine heterogeneity because they are trained on homogeneous corpora and cannot emulate the spontaneous diversity of real people.

## Implications
Designing collaborative workflows must prioritize preserving human diversity to sustain innovative output. If AI tools only augment rather than replace human input, collective creativity can endure; otherwise, it will be eroded by homogenized outputs.
