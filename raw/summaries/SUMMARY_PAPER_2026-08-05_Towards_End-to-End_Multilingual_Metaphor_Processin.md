## Summary
This paper proposes an end‑to‑end framework that unifies metaphor detection, translation, and evaluation in multilingual natural language processing. By integrating these components into a single computational pipeline, the research aims to improve both human assessment and automatic quality estimation of metaphor‑aware machine translation.

## Key Takeaways
- The study emphasizes robust metaphor detection across languages as a prerequisite for meaningful translation, highlighting that figurative meaning often diverges from literal lexical equivalents.
- It introduces metaphor‑oriented evaluation metrics that can be used by humans and automated systems to gauge translation quality beyond standard BLEU scores.
- A joint modelling approach is advocated to link detection signals with translation performance, creating a cohesive system where each stage informs the next.

## Context
Multilingual NLP continues to grapple with figurative language, which challenges traditional sequence‑to‑sequence models that rely on literal word correspondences. Recent advances in large language models have shown promise for handling context, yet they remain largely untethered from systematic evaluation of metaphorical content across languages.

## Implications
Practitioners will benefit from a unified framework that reduces the need for separate pipelines and improves consistency between detection and translation outputs. This work sets a new benchmark for evaluating multilingual systems on figurative language, encouraging industry adoption of more nuanced performance metrics.
