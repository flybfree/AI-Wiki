## Summary
ARISE addresses the challenge of static learning criteria in long-horizon agentic reinforcement learning by introducing a framework where evaluation rubrics, exploration skills, and task priorities co-evolve based on real-time rollout evidence. This dynamic adaptation allows agents to continuously align training with their shifting capability gaps, rewarding partial progress while actively guiding exploration toward unresolved weaknesses. Experiments demonstrate that ARISE significantly improves both overall task performance and training efficiency across challenging benchmarks like SkillsBench and Terminal-Bench
