## Summary
The paper investigates how large language models can serve as expert assistants for geothermal well array design. It demonstrates that LLMs can generate benchmark data, auto‑parallelize numerical models, and produce decision‑support insights.

## Key Takeaways
- LLMs such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and domain‑specific AskGDR can synthesize complex geothermal data and improve feature capabilities of existing models.  
- The study uses NotebookLM to rapidly create unpublished quantitative benchmarks for evaluating LLM performance in geothermal applications.  
- Auto‑parallelization of numerical codes is shown to be feasible, supporting dynamic digital twin workflows.

## Context
In AI research, the integration of language models with high‑performance computing remains a frontier, and this work bridges that gap by applying LLMs to energy engineering problems. The rapid creation of benchmarks highlights the need for automated evaluation tools in emerging technologies.

## Implications
For geothermal operators, LLM‑driven decision support could reduce design cycles and increase fault tolerance. Industry practitioners can adopt these methods to generate more accurate models without extensive manual coding.
