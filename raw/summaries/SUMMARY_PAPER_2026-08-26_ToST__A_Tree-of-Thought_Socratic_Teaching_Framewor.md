## Summary
The paper proposes ToST, a Tree-of-Thought Socratic Teaching framework enabling multi-path guidance for LLMs. It introduces Parallel Sowing and Multi-Path Adaptive Guidance to support exploring multiple solutions simultaneously. Experiments show improved guidance success rates both automatically and human-evaluated, with parallel thinking leading to higher engagement.

## Key Takeaways
- ToST replaces the one-problem-one-solution paradigm with a one-problem-multiple-solutions approach, allowing parallel exploration of solution paths.
- Parallel Sowing is a questioning strategy that encourages diverse perspectives by prompting students to generate multiple reasoning branches at once and can lead to higher engagement.
- MPSG-Bench provides a benchmark of 31K multi-path teaching dialogues evaluated on five dimensions using SOLO theory.

## Context
Current LLM teaching methods are linear and limited, hindering flexible instruction. This work addresses the need for non-linear, parallel guidance in AI tutoring systems by providing tools that support diverse solution exploration.

## Implications
The framework can be applied to educational AI tools to improve learning outcomes by enabling students to explore multiple solutions, fostering deeper understanding and adaptability in automated tutoring.
