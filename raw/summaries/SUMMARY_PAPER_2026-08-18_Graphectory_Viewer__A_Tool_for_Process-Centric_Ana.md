## Summary
Graphectory Viewer is a web‑based tool that enables interactive analysis of software‑agent trajectories by converting raw execution data into phase‑aware graphs. The system connects low‑level actions with higher‑level behaviors, allowing users to explore individual runs, filter large datasets, and view Sankey‑style summaries of problem‑solving transitions.

## Key Takeaways
- Graphectory Viewer transforms heterogeneous agent trajectories into phase‑aware graphs that link execution details to behavioral structures.  
- The tool supports multiple frameworks, offers node‑level inspection of thoughts, actions, and observations, and provides searchable filtering over large collections.  
- It releases an open‑source artifact together with documentation, precomputed graphs, and a large trajectory corpus to support reproducibility.

## Context
Understanding how agents navigate complex problem spaces is essential for developing reliable autonomous systems. Current methods often focus on final outcomes, overlooking intermediate reasoning patterns that determine success or failure. Graphectory Viewer addresses this gap by visualizing the entire decision process in an accessible format.

## Implications
Researchers can now compare successful versus failed executions to uncover systematic biases or bottlenecks. Practitioners benefit from a reusable interface for diagnosing and improving agent behavior, accelerating iterative development cycles across AI applications.
