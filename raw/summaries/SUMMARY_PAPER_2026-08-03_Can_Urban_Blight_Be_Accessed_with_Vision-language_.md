## Summary
The paper proposes a scalable framework that uses open‑source large vision‑language models to estimate residential blight from multiple street views. By applying structured prompts, the models assess roof integrity, wall damage, and broken openings, delivering both binary labels and probabilistic estimates. The study shows that combining several views improves accuracy, that different vision‑language models have distinct strengths, and that an ensemble learner outperforms individual base models across all conditions.
