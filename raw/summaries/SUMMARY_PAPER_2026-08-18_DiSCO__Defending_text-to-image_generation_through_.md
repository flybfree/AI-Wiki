## Summary
This paper introduces DiSCO, a zero‑shot black‑box defense for text‑to‑image models that mitigates Not‑Safe‑For‑Work content without modifying the model or its weights. By using distribution‑guided suffix expansion and contrastive scoring over images generated by the target system, DiSCO reduces unsafe scores while preserving image quality.

## Key Takeaways
- DiSCO operates entirely at the prompt level as a plug‑and‑play module, requiring no retraining, fine‑tuning, or access to model internals.  
- The defense improves safety on both undefended and defended models, achieving 37.7% and 25.13% ASR reduction respectively on the I2P benchmark under red‑teaming attacks.  
- DiSCO maintains semantic fidelity and enhances image coherence despite its black‑box nature.

## Context
Current text‑to‑image safety defenses often rely on white‑box techniques that cannot be applied to proprietary models, limiting their practicality. This work addresses the gap by providing a scalable, model‑agnostic solution that can be integrated into any generative pipeline without architectural changes.

## Implications
For industry practitioners, DiSCO offers a straightforward way to embed safety checks into existing systems, reducing liability and reputational risk. The approach sets a new standard for black‑box defenses in AI, encouraging broader adoption of safe generative models across diverse applications.
