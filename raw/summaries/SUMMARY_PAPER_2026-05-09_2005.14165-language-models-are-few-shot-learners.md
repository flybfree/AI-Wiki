# Summary: 2026-05-09 2005.14165-Language-Models-Are-Few-Shot-Learners


## Summary
The paper demonstrates that scaling up language models improves few-shot performance dramatically, and we train GPT‑3, a 175‑billion‑parameter decoder‑only transformer on 45 TB of text, showing it can achieve state‑of‑the‑art results without fine‑tuning.

## Key Takeaways
- Scaling to 175 billion parameters yields dramatically better few-shot accuracy across a wide range of tasks.
- The model reaches competitive performance with prior fine‑tuned models despite having no task‑specific training, indicating that scaling alone can compensate for lack of fine‑tuning.
- Training on 45 terabytes of data enables this capability, highlighting the importance of massive corpora for unlocking such performance.

## Context
This work underscores a trend in AI research toward scaling compute and data to unlock capabilities that were previously unimaginable.

## Implications
For practitioners, it suggests that fine‑tuning may be unnecessary for many applications. For industry, deploying such models could reduce development time and lower costs.
