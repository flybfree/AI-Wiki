## Summary
This paper introduces a sociotechnical AI pipeline that converts raw ITSM ticket data into executive‑ready decision support artifacts. The pipeline uses LLM schema normalization, HDBSCAN sub‑topic clustering, and hierarchical agglomerative clustering to create Main‑topics and Sub‑topics, which were evaluated by sales engineering and customer success stakeholders.

## Key Takeaways
- The AI pipeline improves interpretability, actionability, trust, and likelihood of use, with average scores exceeding 4.0 out of 5 across four metrics.
- Trust emerges as the most consistent positive signal in stakeholder evaluations.
- The approach positions ITSM analytics as an IS problem requiring transformation, abstraction, and human‑centered design.

## Context
IT service management systems generate massive heterogeneous ticket data that remain opaque to decision makers. Conventional analytics often lack the integration of natural language processing with clustering techniques needed for actionable insights. This work bridges that gap by applying advanced AI methods within a design‑science framework to produce interpretable, usable intelligence.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt this pipeline to turn raw ITSM exports into clear executive dashboards, reducing manual analysis effort and increasing stakeholder confidence. The methodology offers a replicable template for other domain‑specific data transformation challenges in enterprise IT.
