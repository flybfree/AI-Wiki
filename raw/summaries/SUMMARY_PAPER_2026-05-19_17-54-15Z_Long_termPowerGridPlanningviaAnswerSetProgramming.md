## Summary
The paper proposes an ASP‑based framework for automating long‑term power grid planning, showing that it can handle decades of network changes while preserving topological invariants. Experiments on synthetic and real data demonstrate the approach’s expressive power and effectiveness.

## Key Takeaways
- The method encodes complex network constraints such as topology preservation and combinatorial invariants directly in ASP rules, making them concise and easy to maintain.
- The framework automates the search for feasible long‑term plans, reducing manual effort and improving solution quality.
- Experimental results confirm that the ASP model outperforms traditional planning languages on both synthetic and real‑world grid instances.
