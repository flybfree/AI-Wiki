## Summary
The paper explores how generative AI reshapes legal translation, highlighting both opportunities and risks. It proposes an AI literacy framework that extends professional competence beyond the core objectives of legal translation. Findings show that integrating AI requires a transversal competency that builds digital resilience.

## Key Takeaways
- AI does not alter the fundamental goals of legal translation but demands expanded competence through AI literacy.
- The framework consists of four dimensions: foundational, procedural, critical, and strategic, each reinforcing the others.
- Pedagogical activities are suggested to develop AI literacy in legal translator education, enabling responsible integration.

## Context
Generative AI has rapidly entered professional domains, prompting calls for specialized training. Legal translation, with its unique blend of language, law, and ethics, is particularly vulnerable to misinterpretation. This paper addresses that vulnerability by offering a structured approach.

## Implications
Practitioners must adopt this framework to maintain quality and compliance in AI‑enhanced work. Educators should embed these activities into curricula to prepare translators for future challenges. The initiative supports industry resilience against emerging risks.
