## Summary
The paper introduces Agentic Metaverse Services (AMServ) and Meta-AaaS as a new paradigm where GenAI-powered agents provide customized services in the metaverse, focusing on perception, decision-making, execution, collaboration, and content generation. It outlines their evolution, features, applications, future research trends, and highlights the synergy between GenAI and metaverse technologies, creating a fertile ground for innovative service ecosystems.

## Key Takeaways
- The shift from chatbots to agentic AI enables encapsulated abilities like perception and multi-modal interaction.
- AMServ combines these abilities into a metaverse service model for business processing.
- Meta-AaaS is the infrastructure that delivers such services as a platform, allowing scalable deployment across virtual environments.

## Context
GenAI has transformed virtual environments, allowing agents to learn autonomously and generate content, which fuels demand for integrated services in immersive spaces like the metaverse. This paper contributes by formalizing how these capabilities are packaged into service offerings within a virtual ecosystem, driven by the need for autonomous agents that can perceive their surroundings, make decisions, collaborate with other agents, generate content, and execute tasks without human intervention.

## Implications
The AMServ and Meta-AaaS models open new revenue streams for enterprises and users, accelerating adoption of AI-driven experiences in digital worlds. Practitioners can leverage this paradigm to design scalable, intelligent services that enhance user engagement and operational efficiency, potentially reshaping industry standards with new governance frameworks for AI behavior in virtual spaces.
