## Summary
The paper introduces Titans-QFWP, a hybrid reinforcement learning architecture that combines quantum fast weight programming with Titans-style memory for portfolio optimization. It achieves strong performance with median ARR 0.4260, Calmar 8.5504, and IR 0.8427.

## Key Takeaways
- Persistence supports drawdown control, enabling defensive allocation during market declines.
- Surprise contributes to return generation by adapting weights to unexpected market signals.
- Forgetting provides additional stabilization of quantum representations, smoothing model behavior.
