## Summary
This paper proposes a diagonal multi‑omics integration framework that treats biological heterogeneity as an extremal trace problem on a Laplacian defined over sets homeomorphic to the Stiefel manifold. By applying gradient ascent in functional analysis, the authors introduce a norm metric measuring the spread between maximum and minimum points across datasets.

- The study models dataset heterogeneity using the difference between the largest and smallest values as a characteristic norm, highlighting extreme value spread.

- The framework integrates heterogeneous multi‑omics data by focusing on the geometry of the Stiefel manifold, enabling better capture of biological variability.
