## Summary
The paper introduces AVISE, a modular open‑source framework for identifying vulnerabilities in AI systems and evaluating their security. It demonstrates the framework with an extended Red Queen attack on language models and an automated Security Evaluation Test that achieves high accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework AVISE provides a systematic, extensible approach for vulnerability identification and security evaluation of AI systems.
- The Security Evaluation Test (SET) includes 25 test cases and an Evaluation Language Model (ELM) that correctly identifies jailbreak attempts with 92% accuracy, F1‑score 0.91, and MCC 0.83.
- All nine evaluated language models are vulnerable to the augmented Red Queen attack, showing varying degrees of susceptibility.

## Context
AI security remains a critical concern as deployed models face exploitation risks. The lack of standardized evaluation tools hampers reproducibility and risk assessment in the field.

## Implications
AVISE equips researchers and industry with a reusable toolkit to automate AI security testing, enabling more rigorous and reproducible assessments. This could reduce the impact of high‑profile exploits in critical applications.
