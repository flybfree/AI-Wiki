## Summary
The paper introduces Indic DiarBench, a multilingual joint diarization and ASR benchmark covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages. It provides about 108 hours of audio with human‑corrected speaker transcriptions from various recording conditions. The study evaluates state‑of‑the‑art systems to set a baseline for both tasks.

## Key Takeaways
- Indic DiarBench spans the full spectrum of India’s scheduled languages, offering a comprehensive resource that was previously unavailable.
- The dataset includes near‑field, far‑field, and in‑wild recordings with time‑aligned annotations, capturing code‑mixing and speaker overlap typical of Indian speech.
- Evaluation against commercial APIs and multimodal LLMs demonstrates the need for specialized models to handle Indian linguistic nuances.

## Context
Indian language AI research has long been under‑represented compared to English or major global languages. This benchmark addresses that gap by providing a large, annotated corpus that can be used to train and test models across all 22 scheduled tongues.

## Implications
For practitioners developing inclusive speech technologies, Indic DiarBench enables better performance in real‑world Indian contexts where language diversity is high. The open release will accelerate progress toward accurate diarization and ASR for under‑served languages worldwide.
