## Summary
The paper introduces Tree-of-Ideas, a two-stage framework that reconstructs scholarly trajectories and reasons across them to generate research ideas. Experiments on six AI topics show it outperforms baselines with high novelty and groundedness scores, approaching human performance.

## Key Takeaways
- EvoTrace builds branching scholarly trajectories from citations, capturing evolving methods, resolved problems, and gaps.
- EvoAgent reasons across these trajectories to find convergent problems and complementary solutions that produce grounded ideas.
- The framework achieves the highest automatic score among six AI topics with Novelty 6.36 and Groundedness 7.00.

## Context
AI research often relies on static citation networks, which ignore how ideas branch and intersect over time. This work addresses that gap by modeling scholarly evolution as a dynamic tree rather than isolated chains.

## Implications
For researchers seeking novel directions, ToI offers an automated way to explore cross-trajectory insights. Practitioners can leverage its high groundedness score to design studies that directly address identified gaps in the field.
