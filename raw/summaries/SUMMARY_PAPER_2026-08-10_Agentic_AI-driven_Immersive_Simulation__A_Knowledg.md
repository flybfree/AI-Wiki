## Summary
This paper introduces an agentic AI‑driven immersive simulation for high dose rate vaginal cylinder brachytherapy that combines virtual reality with a knowledge‑aware assistant powered by Retrieval‑Augmented Generation. The prototype, running on a Meta Quest 3 and a local GPU backend, achieves low latency while providing accurate, complete guidance throughout complex procedures.

## Key Takeaways
- Knowledge‑aware assistance leverages RAG to ground agent responses in authoritative clinical guidelines, ensuring that every instruction is both relevant and compliant with best practices.  
- The system creates a high‑fidelity, risk‑free environment using VR and mobile computing, allowing trainees to practice without physical constraints or live radioactive sources.  
- Experimental results confirm suitable end‑to‑end latency and high context precision, answer completeness, and relevance in the RAG‑enhanced support.

## Context
The convergence of the Metaverse and large language models is reshaping autonomous, immersive education by enabling personalized pedagogical frameworks that adapt to learner needs. This work exemplifies how AI agents can deliver real‑time, guideline‑aligned guidance within a virtual setting, moving beyond static simulations toward interactive, intelligent tutoring.

## Implications
For medical training, the platform reduces procedural risk and costs while offering scalable, individualized learning experiences. Practitioners will benefit from safer skill acquisition, and institutions may adopt this technology to modernize curricula and integrate AI‑driven mentorship into routine practice.
