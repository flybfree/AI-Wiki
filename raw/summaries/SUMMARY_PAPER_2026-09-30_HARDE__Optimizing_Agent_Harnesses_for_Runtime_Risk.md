## Summary
This paper introduces HARDE, a risk-aware agent harness designed to optimize runtime safety defenses for Large Language Model (LLM) agents against injected malicious instructions and misleading information. The proposed framework integrates LLM-based monitoring with a structured execution flow comprising trigger, monitor, and feedback modules to enable targeted interventions without severely disrupting benign tasks. Experiments demonstrate that HARDE effectively improves runtime safety while preserving task utility, outperforming manually designed harnesses and naive optimization baselines across multiple attack benchmarks.

## Key Takeaways
- Existing system-level defenses often prioritize risk detection over timely prevention or rely on rigid predefined rules that lack flexibility across diverse threats; HARDE addresses this by implementing a modular harness architecture centered on trigger, monitor, and feedback components that facilitate flexible LLM-based monitoring and structured, monitor-guided execution to ensure targeted safety interventions.
