## Summary
The paper introduces SETU, an agentic ecosystem for corporate communication coaching that supports multilingual, persona‑aware interactions in two scenarios, focusing on sales pitch adaptation. It deploys specialized agents for video, audio‑speech, text relevance, scoring and reporting, with human reviewers holding final judgment. The system is evaluated only on scenario (ii) due to limited resources.

## Key Takeaways
- SETU integrates multiple modalities into a trust‑aware orchestration pipeline that produces modality‑attributed coaching reports for formative training.
- The ecosystem is designed specifically for sales pitches targeting specific audiences, with persona context guiding adaptation.
- Human reviewers retain final judgment over automated outputs to ensure auditability and explainability.

## Context
Corporate communication training requires scalable tools that can handle diverse linguistic contexts without sacrificing transparency. Current solutions often operate in silos or generate opaque scores, limiting their usefulness for human‑in‑the‑loop coaching.

## Implications
SETU demonstrates a viable path toward explainable AI in workplace training by combining modular agents with human oversight. Practitioners can adopt such ecosystems to improve multilingual sales and recruitment communication while maintaining accountability.
