## Summary
This paper addresses the limitation of traditional recommender systems that predict user interactions without explaining their reasoning. The authors fine-tune a large language model to generate personalized, faithful, and strictly non-harmful explanations for video recommendations based on viewing history. Using constrained GRPO and LLM-judge reward models, they demonstrate significant improvements in explanation quality while preserving the model's core recommendation capabilities.

## Key Takeaways
- The researchers developed a fine-tuned recommender LLM that generates personalized explanations grounded in user watching history, addressing the common industry gap where systems predict interactions but fail to justify them.
- By implementing constrained GRPO alongside two specialized LLM-judge reward models
