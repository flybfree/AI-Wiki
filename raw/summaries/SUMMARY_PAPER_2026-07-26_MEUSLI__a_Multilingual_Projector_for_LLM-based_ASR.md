## Summary
MEUSLI is a multilingual projector system that links Whisper’s speech encoder with open‑source multilingual LLMs to enable end‑to‑end ASR in 28 European languages. The project demonstrates strong performance across high‑ and low‑resource languages and can be extended to unseen languages using continual learning techniques.

## Key Takeaways
- MEUSLI provides a fully open‑science pipeline that supports 28 European languages, expanding beyond the English‑centric models typical of existing projector solutions.  
- The system achieves strong ASR results in both high‑resource and low‑resource languages by leveraging Whisper’s encoder and multilingual LLMs, showing that lightweight projectors can match large language model capabilities.  
- Continual learning enables the projector to be easily extended to new languages with only a few hours of task‑specific supervision per language.

## Context
The rapid growth of open‑source speech recognition models has highlighted the need for multilingual solutions that do not rely on proprietary data or closed APIs. MEUSLI addresses this gap by creating a scalable, community‑driven approach to multilingual speech understanding without sacrificing performance or openness.

## Implications
MEUSLI offers practitioners and researchers a ready‑to‑use foundation for building inclusive speech applications across European languages, reducing the cost of deploying multilingual ASR systems. Its continued extensibility encourages further research into low‑resource language support and broader application domains such as translation and topic identification.
