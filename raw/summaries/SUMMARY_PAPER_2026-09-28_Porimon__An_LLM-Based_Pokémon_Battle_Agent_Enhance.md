## Summary
This paper introduces Porimon, an LLM-based agent designed for Pokémon Battles that utilizes Long/Short-Term Knowledge Augmented Generation (LSTKAG) to enhance opponent-aware planning without requiring additional fine-tuning. The approach integrates external APIs for precise game mechanics and leverages both immediate task history and retrieved experience summaries from similar past scenarios to inform decision-making. Experimental results across 15,000 battles demonstrate that Porimon significantly outperforms prior LLM-based agents and rule-based heuristics, validating the efficacy of augmenting LLMs with structured knowledge retrieval mechanisms.
