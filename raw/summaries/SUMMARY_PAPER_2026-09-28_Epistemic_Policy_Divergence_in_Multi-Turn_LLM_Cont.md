## Summary
This study investigates session-level contamination in large language models, where false premises injected into conversation history are erroneously adopted as facts. The authors evaluate GPT-5.4 Mini, Gemini-3.1 Flash-Lite, and GLM-4.5-Air across five contamination protocols arranged along a source-authority gradient
