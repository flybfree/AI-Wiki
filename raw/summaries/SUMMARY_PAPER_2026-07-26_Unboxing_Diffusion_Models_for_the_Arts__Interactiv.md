## Summary
The paper introduces a hands‑on explainability framework for diffusion models in creative practice, demonstrating how artists can inspect and modify model components. By integrating an interactive interface into ComfyUIs, the authors show that bending specific pipeline layers yields predictable visual effects, offering a new material engagement with large text‑to‑image systems.

## Key Takeaways
- Artists can directly manipulate individual diffusion pipeline components through an interactive node‑based workflow, gaining insight into how each layer influences output.  
- The approach produces consistent families of visual results when specific layers are altered, enabling practical intuition about model behavior.  
- This method shifts focus from technocentric explanations to material engagement, aligning XAI with artistic practice.

## Context
Large diffusion models dominate text‑to‑image generation but remain largely opaque, limiting their use as creative tools. Explainable AI research often targets technical transparency rather than enabling artists to interact with model internals. This work bridges that gap by providing a tangible interface for inspection and modification.
