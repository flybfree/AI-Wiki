## Summary
The paper introduces MACBT, a clinician-facing AI decision-support system designed to alleviate the time burden of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy by automating pre-session preparation, documentation, and longitudinal tracking through a multi-agent framework integrated with a specialized memory module. By encoding the five-stage CBT workflow into collaborative agents and utilizing a Qwen3-14B model fine-tuned on a simulated Chinese dialogue corpus, MACBT demonstrates superior professionalism and clinical authenticity compared to existing mental health chatbots. The inclusion of CD Memory significantly enhances session quality by 12.6% and improves cross-session continuity, intervention progression, and personalization metrics.
