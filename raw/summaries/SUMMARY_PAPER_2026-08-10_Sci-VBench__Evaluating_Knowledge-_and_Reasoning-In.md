## Summary
This paper introduces Sci‑VBench, a benchmark that tests knowledge‑ and reasoning‑intensive video generation across scientific fields. The study evaluates 16 frontier models on expert‑annotated examples and shows that while visual quality scores are similar, performance on grounding prompts and causal correctness varies widely.

## Key Takeaways
- Expert human judgments align closely with MLLM‑as‑Judge scores, indicating a reliable rubric for large‑scale evaluation.  
- Automatic perceptual scores cluster tightly across models, revealing limited differences in visual realism alone.  
- Prompt grounding and scientific causal correctness show substantial gaps between proprietary and open‑source systems.

## Context
The rapid progress of generative video models has focused on photorealistic output, yet few benchmarks assess deeper scientific reasoning. Sci‑VBench addresses this gap by providing a structured set of tasks that require models to synthesize domain knowledge over time.

## Implications
For researchers, the results highlight the need for evaluation protocols that capture both visual and causal aspects of video generation. Industry practitioners should prioritize models that perform well on grounding and causality, not just those that produce realistic images.
