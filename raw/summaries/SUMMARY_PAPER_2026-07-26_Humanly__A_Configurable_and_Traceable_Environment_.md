## Summary
Humanly is a platform that records the entire writing process, allowing users to configure environments for personal or assigned tasks and draft in a workspace that logs activity and AI assistance. The system packages each session into a sealed certificate that includes configuration‑aware anomaly detection, enabling reviewers to verify whether the text came from human typing, AI generation, or mixed collaboration. By making the process itself the evidence, Humanly addresses the limitation of final text alone. User studies show it is useful across roles, and red‑team testing demonstrates its ability to distinguish human hand typing from automated typing.

## Key Takeaways
- The platform records both human and AI actions separately, allowing granular analysis of the writing workflow.
- Each session is packaged into a sealed certificate with configuration‑aware anomaly behavior review for verification.
- User studies indicate usefulness across roles, while red‑team testing shows clear distinction between human and automated typing.

## Context
In AI research, provenance of generated content is a growing concern as models become more capable and widely deployed. This approach aligns with emerging standards for digital provenance in education and research.

## Implications
For educators and certification bodies, Humanly provides verifiable evidence that can improve trust in AI‑augmented assessments. Industries can adopt Humanly to certify AI‑generated outputs, reducing fraud risk and enhancing compliance.
