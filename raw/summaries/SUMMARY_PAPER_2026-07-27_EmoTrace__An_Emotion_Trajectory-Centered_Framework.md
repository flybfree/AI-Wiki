## Summary
The paper introduces EmoTrace, a framework that generates psychological support dialogues by modeling seekers' emotional trajectories. Experiments show that the approach yields richer emotional content and higher empathy scores than prior methods.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework builds a seeker module with emotional schemas and an activation mechanism to capture dynamic emotional states.  
- A counselor module is designed to generate empathic responses guided by the identified emotional trajectory.  
- EmoTrace improves both emotional richness and empathy quality compared with existing dialogue generation techniques.

## Context
Generating high‑quality counseling dialogues remains a challenge because current data often lack genuine emotional variation and focus on problem‑solving rather than feeling. This work addresses those gaps by centering the model on the seeker’s evolving emotions, which is essential for realistic therapeutic interaction.

## Implications
For AI developers, EmoTrace offers a blueprint to embed emotion modeling into conversational agents, enhancing user support without sacrificing coherence. Practitioners can leverage these insights to create more empathetic chatbots that better meet mental‑health assistance needs.
