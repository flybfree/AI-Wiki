## Summary
The paper introduces Omni-IO Skills, a plug-and-play Agent Harness designed to make existing foundation model agents "omni-native" by supporting diverse modalities without requiring updates to the agent's core reasoning capabilities. By utilizing hierarchical Skills, a standardized execution interface, and a persistent Asset Registry, the system coordinates complex multi-asset workflows through Declare Execution Graphs that enable concurrent scheduling and cross-turn reuse of intermediate assets. Evaluations on the UniM-90 benchmark demonstrate that this harness significantly boosts input support rates to 100% for models like GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5, while substantially improving semantic quality and structural accuracy scores across seven artifact modalities.
