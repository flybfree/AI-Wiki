## Summary
The paper proposes a systems engineering framework that embeds Vision Language Models as coordination agents within the human‑UAV loop for disaster response. It integrates natural language interaction, mission‑level task coordination, software‑in‑the‑loop testing, and communication aligned with the Incident Command System. The framework was validated in simulation with three components: VLM Coordinator Agent, UAV Mission Control, and Task Allocator.

## Key Takeaways
- The VLM Coordinator Agent translates human operator inputs into structured mission commands, reducing manual translation steps.
- Integration of software‑in‑the‑loop testing ensures the system behaves predictably under varying sensor conditions.
- Human‑factors evaluation with seven participants reported lower mental demand and higher trust in AI communication.

## Context
Vision Language Models are increasingly used for situational awareness but often remain advisory tools that do not directly coordinate actions. Embedding them as active coordination agents addresses a gap between information generation and operational execution, especially under time pressure.

## Implications
This framework demonstrates scalable human‑autonomy teaming that could be applied to other high‑stakes domains such as aerospace and civil safety. Practitioners can adopt the MBSE‑driven approach to design robust AI‑enabled mission control systems.
