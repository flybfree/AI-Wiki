## Summary  
The paper introduces a scalable, context‑aware multi‑agent framework that automates the construction of “Lines and Ladders” pricing taxonomies for large retail catalogs. The 3‑agent system builds coherent price relationships by extracting attributes and applying hierarchical grouping logic.

## Key Takeaways  
- The framework achieves an F1-score of 0.83 on Lines, which is higher than single‑agent baselines because it reduces cognitive overload.  
- In the Food & Consumables domain it attains precision above 90% and recall above 75%, indicating strong matching of price lines to product variants.  
- The unstructured General Merchandise catalog shows assignment accuracy over 80.2%, showing robustness across varied data.

## Context  
This work advances AI‑driven pricing governance by replacing manual, error‑prone processes with automated multi‑agent reasoning that can handle millions of items.

## Implications  
For retailers, the system lowers operational costs and improves price consistency, supporting aggressive promotional strategies. Practitioners can adopt similar agent‑based pipelines to scale taxonomies without proportional human effort.
