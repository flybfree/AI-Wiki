## Summary
SkillSpec introduces a two-phase framework for evolving natural-language skills in LLM agents, distinguishing between validating knowledge retention and optimizing structural organization to overcome limitations of monolithic skill representations. The method employs consensus-gated evolution to ensure updates are committed only when paired evaluations demonstrate consistent improvement, followed by representation specialization that dynamically selects flat, graph, or hybrid structures based on procedural dependencies. Evaluated across six benchmarks and three models, SkillSpec achieves a 6.89% average success rate improvement over SkillOpt, demonstrating the efficacy of separating skill validation from structural adaptation
