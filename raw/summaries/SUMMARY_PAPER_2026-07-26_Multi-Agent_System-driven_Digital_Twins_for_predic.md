## Summary
The paper reviews over 547 high‑impact papers to map how Multi-Agent Systems can be combined with Digital Twins for predictive maintenance in resource‑constrained settings. It identifies three hybrid architectures and highlights that no current solution integrates embedded AI, lightweight distributed coordination, and hierarchical smart‑factory control.

## Key Takeaways
- The review shows that artificial intelligence deployment on microcontrollers remains a bottleneck because most models exceed the limited compute of embedded hardware.
- Distributed multi‑node coordination is hampered by heavy communication protocols, which increase latency and power draw in industrial settings.
- Hierarchical orchestration toward smart‑factory control has not yet achieved an integrated solution that links residual‑life estimation with explainable AI.

## Context
This work situates the research within the broader AI landscape where edge computing and federated learning aim to balance performance with resource limits. The convergence of digital twins, multi‑agent coordination, and predictive maintenance is a key driver for Industry 5.0, making this synthesis relevant for researchers seeking scalable solutions.

## Implications
For industry practitioners, the identified gaps suggest opportunities to develop compact AI modules and ultra‑lightweight communication stacks that enable real‑time twin updates without sacrificing reliability. Practitioners can leverage these insights to design resilient predictive maintenance pipelines that align with smart factory goals.
