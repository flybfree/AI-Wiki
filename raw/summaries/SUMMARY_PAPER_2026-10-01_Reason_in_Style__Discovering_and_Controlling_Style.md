## Summary
The paper investigates the unsupervised discovery and explicit control of stylistic variation in language model outputs by designing an algorithm to separate content and style representations. Validated on math reasoning tasks using over 100K traces from nine teacher models, the approach identifies six recurring styles and enables fine-tuning smaller student models to condition on these styles with importance weighting. Results demonstrate that this method improves Pass@k performance
