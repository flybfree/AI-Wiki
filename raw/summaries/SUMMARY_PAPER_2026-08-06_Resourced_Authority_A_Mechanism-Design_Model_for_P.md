## Summary
The paper proposes a formal mechanism design model for governing AI agents through resource allocation. It introduces a governance currency and compute budget system that makes authorization self‑enforcing. The framework is built as an extensive form game with sequential stakeholder contributions.

## Key Takeaways
- Governance uses a distinct governance currency to aggregate contributions into breadth weighted supports, creating a two‑threshold gate with hysteresis for binary authorization.
- Authorization releases a metered compute budget via hardware license, ensuring self‑enforcement and linking safety ceiling.
- Manipulation of the governing electorate by the governed agent is identified as the core open problem.

## Context
In AI deployment, ensuring safe and accountable behavior remains a challenge. Traditional oversight relies on external audits or strict compliance checks that can be costly and reactive.

## Implications
This mechanism offers a scalable way to embed safety directly into compute budgets, reducing reliance on manual interventions. Practitioners could adopt similar budget‑based governance for high‑risk AI systems.
