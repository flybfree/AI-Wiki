## Summary
The paper identifies a critical gap in Latin American AI development: the absence of a dedicated benchmark layer that audits systems against regional social needs and guides optimization for local contexts. It introduces EvalsHub, an open task‑first infrastructure with LatamBoard as its first instance, allowing diverse stakeholders to publish, run, compare, and maintain evaluations continuously.

## Key Takeaways
- The missing benchmark layer prevents public institutions from independently auditing foreign AI systems and hampers companies from achieving state‑of‑the‑art performance for local problems.  
- EvalsHub provides a reusable, institutionalized platform that re‑runs benchmarks whenever new models or workflows are deployed, ensuring ongoing auditability.  
- The infrastructure is incentive‑driven, encouraging universities, public bodies, professional groups, and firms to contribute and maintain the benchmark data.

## Context
Latin American AI systems often rely on global benchmarks that do not reflect regional social dynamics or economic constraints. This mismatch limits trust in deployed technologies and reduces their effectiveness where they are needed most. The paper situates EvalsHub within a growing trend of region‑specific evaluation ecosystems emerging worldwide.

## Implications
For practitioners, EvalsHub offers a practical way to embed local validation into the AI lifecycle, fostering confidence and relevance. For policymakers, it creates a transparent audit trail that can inform regulatory decisions on AI deployment in Latin America.
