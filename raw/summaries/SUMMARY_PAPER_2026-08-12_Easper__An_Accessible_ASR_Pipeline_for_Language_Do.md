## Summary
Easper is an open-source, no-code pipeline that lets field linguists iteratively fine-tune multilingual ASR models using cloud resources directly from ELAN annotations. Our experiments on three Vanuatu languages show that prioritising lexically rich narratives and boosting acoustic‑phonetic repetition improves transcription quality faster than focusing solely on clean recordings.

## Key Takeaways
- Prioritising lexically rich narratives yields the steepest Character Error Rate reduction, even when audio is noisy.
- Increasing acoustic-phonetic repetition accelerates model convergence across all three languages.
- The no-code workflow requires only ELAN annotations and cloud access, removing technical barriers for linguists.

## Context
Automatic Speech Recognition remains a bottleneck in documenting endangered languages because current models demand expert tuning. Easper addresses this by decoupling model training from specialist knowledge, enabling rapid prototyping.

## Implications
For language documentation projects, Easper can reduce transcription time and improve data quality without hiring ASR specialists. The approach also democratises access to cutting-edge AI tools for small communities and NGOs.
