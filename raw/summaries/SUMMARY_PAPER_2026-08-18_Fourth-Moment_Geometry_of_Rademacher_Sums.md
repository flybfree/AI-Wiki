## Summary
The paper studies the fourth‑moment geometry of normalized Rademacher sums and shows how higher moments depend on a fourth‑order mass term. It establishes sharp fixed‑q moment envelopes, Gaussian stability for all p≥4, and identifies the flat coefficient vector as extremizer for Lp/L4 constants. The results also confirm Jakimiuk’s quadratic estimate at p=3.

## Key Takeaways
- The normalized Rademacher sum’s fourth‑order mass uniquely determines its higher moments across all orders p≥4.
- Gaussian stability holds uniformly in p, providing a convexity threshold beyond which the bound is optimal.
- The flat coefficient vector yields the sharp finite‑dimensional Lp/L4 Khintchine constant for p≥5.

## Context
In compressed sensing and random projections, understanding moment dependence enables tighter error bounds. This work bridges theoretical limits with practical sparse coding, offering a unified framework that respects sparsity and effective dimension.

## Implications
For practitioners designing Rademacher‑based estimators or error models, the derived constants reduce over‑approximation errors and improve computational guarantees. The results also support statistical tests for random sign vectors, enhancing robustness in machine learning pipelines.
