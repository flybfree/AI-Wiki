## Summary
The paper introduces APEX-VW, an English‑Spanish post‑editing dataset derived from NHS virtual‑ward documents and professional Trados Studio workflows. It provides a benchmark for studying document‑level automatic post‑editing and the propagation of corrections across realistic CAT scenarios.

## Key Takeaways
- The corpus preserves document order and CAT‑tool context, unlike prior sentence‑level APE corpora.
- It contains seven coherent source texts totaling 42k words with four MT systems and professional post‑editing.
- It is designed to study terminology normalisation and correction propagation in human‑in‑the‑loop translation support.

## Context
This work addresses the gap between sentence‑level APE datasets and real‑world document processing, where repeated lexical corrections occur. By focusing on coherent English‑Spanish medical texts, it offers a realistic testbed for AI models that must handle terminology consistency across long passages.

## Implications
The dataset will help researchers develop post‑editing tools that understand context and propagate corrections accurately. Practitioners can leverage it to evaluate and improve CAT software for healthcare translation, reducing errors in high‑stakes medical documents.
