## Summary
The paper describes the creation and deployment of an agentic AI companion built on large language models within a security operations center over one year. Researchers observed that analysts reused the companion’s outputs in ticket closures, indicating integration beyond simple tooling. The system co‑evolved with human users as their behaviors shaped its responses.

## Key Takeaways
- Analysts reused the AI’s output in more than 90% of closing reports, showing deep adoption and trust.
- Human shaping of the companion’s behavior correlated with increased comfort and productivity gains.
- The design process embedded within SOC workflows allowed continuous co‑evolution rather than static tooling.

## Context
This work illustrates how generative AI can be embedded in high‑stakes operational environments where reliability is critical. By treating the AI as a collaborative partner rather than an external service, it addresses longstanding challenges of trust and usability in AI adoption.

## Implications
Practitioners can leverage this model to build domain‑specific agents that evolve with user feedback, reducing reliance on manual triage. The approach may inspire broader frameworks for human‑AI collaboration across complex workflows.
