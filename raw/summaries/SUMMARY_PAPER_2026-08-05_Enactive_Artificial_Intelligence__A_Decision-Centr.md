## Summary
The paper proposes Enactive AI as a decision‑centric architecture that integrates organizational and site world models through schema intelligence to enable reliable, governable AI in complex industrial systems. It demonstrates how the framework supports self‑evolving feedback loops that update both models and audit decisions. The study shows that measuring AI success by system‑aware action rather than model output is essential for enterprise deployment.

## Key Takeaways
- Enactive AI separates strategic organizational logic from physical operational execution, creating two distinct world models linked via schema intelligence.
- Decision cycles are self‑evolving, continuously updating the framework to improve reliability and auditability in real‑world settings.
- The framework shifts AI evaluation metrics toward system resilience, responsible governance, and social value rather than just model generation.

## Context
Modern AI research often focuses on generating text or images, but industrial applications require systems that can act responsibly within physical constraints. This work addresses the gap by modeling how AI decisions interact with organizational strategy and site operations, highlighting the need for decision‑centric design in complex environments.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt Enactive AI to build governance pipelines that align AI outputs with business objectives and safety standards. The approach offers a scalable model for deploying trustworthy AI across manufacturing, logistics, and other high‑stakes industries.
