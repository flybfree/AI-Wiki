## Summary
The paper introduces an AI‑accelerated end‑to‑end framework that streamlines the entire upskilling pipeline from knowledge acquisition to assessment development, aiming to reduce time and cost. It demonstrates industry validation through approval by a professional board, rapid learner success in certification exams, and generation of large risk datasets.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework integrates AI across five stages—knowledge acquisition, content creation, review/verification, teaching, and assessment—to boost both production speed and learning efficiency.
- External validation is evident as the US National Association of State Boards of Accountancy approved a program built on the framework for continuing‑professional‑education credits.
- The knowledge base produced 1,267 risk items that enable complex downstream analysis for multi‑agent AI system risk management.

## Context
Rapid workforce reskilling is critical as automation displaces jobs, yet traditional upskilling programs are fragmented and time‑consuming. This work addresses the gap by applying scalable AI to create a cohesive pipeline, aligning with industry demands for measurable outcomes.

## Implications
Practitioners can adopt this framework to cut upskilling timelines from weeks to days, securing professional credentials quickly. The generated risk datasets also support safer deployment of advanced AI systems, offering tangible benefits across education and enterprise sectors.
