## Summary
The paper proposes SciDSK, an agent‑ready representation that packages dataset knowledge and operational guidance as reusable skills. It integrates metadata such as descriptions, scientific context, file organization, usage procedures, quality checks, and provenance while keeping the raw data in its original repository. Experiments show SciDSK boosts agent‑driven discovery and yields more precise interpretation support.

## Key Takeaways
- SciDSK creates a structured skill that bundles dataset‑specific knowledge with procedural guidance, enabling autonomous agents to locate and use scientific datasets without manual intervention.
- The construction pipeline ties each SciDSK to authoritative records and supporting materials, ensuring traceability and reliability of the representation.
- Evaluation on a retrieval benchmark demonstrates improved discovery rates and more actionable interpretation outcomes compared to baseline methods.

## Context
Scientific data are becoming a core input for AI agents, yet current representations are human‑centric and fragmented across repositories. This work addresses that gap by providing an agent‑focused framework that can be reused across domains and datasets.

## Implications
For researchers and industry practitioners, SciDSK offers a scalable way to embed dataset knowledge into AI workflows, reducing reliance on manual curation. As more scientific data are integrated with AI agents, such representations will become essential for reliable, reproducible research.
