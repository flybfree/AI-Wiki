## Summary
This paper introduces AI Tour Meeting, a group travel planning framework that uses multiple Large Language Model agents with distinct personas to collaboratively generate an itinerary through natural language dialogue. The system validates its operation by running simulations and produces analytical results showing how agent interactions influence final plans.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework orchestrates multi‑person LLM discussions by allowing configurable personas, workflows, monitoring, and deployment interfaces.  
- Simulation experiments reveal that agenda setting and constraint negotiation are the most influential stages in shaping the itinerary outcome.  
- The analytical results demonstrate measurable improvements in plan diversity when agents adopt different decision‑making styles.

## Context
The work builds on the growing interest in collaborative AI systems where diverse models negotiate tasks to produce higher‑quality outputs. By simulating human‑like interactions among LLMs, it contributes to understanding emergent behaviors that could improve real‑world deployment of multi‑agent workflows.

## Implications
For practitioners, AI Tour Meeting offers a reusable template for designing coordinated LLM agents in complex planning scenarios. The insights gained can inform the development of more robust and adaptable systems across industries such as logistics, customer service, and collaborative research.
