## Summary
The paper introduces Mimir v1, a 1-billion-parameter language model built on the Hierarchical Reasoning Model architecture, trained from scratch using only permissible post‑training data. It achieves state‑of‑the‑art performance in English and Danish, matching or surpassing larger frontier models such as Qwen 3.5 4B and Gemma 4 E2B across 20 benchmarks.

## Key Takeaways
- Mimir v1 is a 1‑billion‑parameter model that can be trained from scratch using only permissible post‑training data, removing reliance on massive proprietary datasets.
- The model outperforms the original HRM‑Text 1B and competes with larger frontier models like Qwen 3.5 4B and Gemma 4 E2B across English, Math & Code, and Danish benchmarks.
- It is released openly on Hugging Face at https://huggingface.co/danish-foundation-models/DFM-Mimir.

## Context
The rapid growth of large language models has been driven by the use of huge, often non‑permissible datasets that raise ethical and accessibility concerns. This work demonstrates a viable alternative that respects data permissions while still delivering competitive performance.

## Implications
For researchers and developers, Mimir v1 shows that open‑sourced, ethically sourced models can achieve frontier results without compromising compliance. It encourages the industry to prioritize permissibility over scale when building responsible AI systems.
