## Summary
The paper proposes a methodological framework to integrate Artificial Intelligence into Product Line Engineering, aiming to streamline the reuse‑based development process. It evaluates this integration through a multi‑case study conducted in an industrial setting, demonstrating how AI can enhance cost efficiency and accelerate time‑to‑market.

## Key Takeaways
- Reuse‑based development reduces costs, improves quality, and accelerates time‑to‑market by leveraging shared assets across product families.  
- Product Line Engineering enables systematic creation, management, and customization of product lines through these reused components.  
- The integration of AI into PLE is not yet systematically explored; the proposed framework addresses this gap.

## Context
The broader AI context emphasizes the need for intelligent systems that optimize complex engineering workflows where manual decision‑making is prevalent. This paper contributes by applying AI to a domain traditionally reliant on human judgment, highlighting opportunities for automated optimization in product line design.

## Implications
