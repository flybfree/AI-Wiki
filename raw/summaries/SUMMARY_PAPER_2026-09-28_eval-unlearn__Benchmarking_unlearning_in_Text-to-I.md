## Summary
The paper introduces `eval-unlearn`, an open-source Python library designed to address the fragmented evaluation landscape of concept unlearning in text-to-image diffusion models by providing a unified, reproducible benchmarking framework. This tool integrates twelve distinct unlearning techniques and nine complementary metrics, enabling principled cross-method comparisons while supporting extensible plugin architectures and efficient streaming pipelines for both NSFW and general concepts. A public leaderboard released alongside the library
