## Summary
The paper introduces YOLOEZ, a no‑code graphical interface that enables end‑to‑end development of YOLO models for structural defect detection. The study shows that using YOLOEZ improves detection accuracy and reduces technical barriers compared to existing software. The evaluation against existing tools demonstrates consistent improvements in precision, recall, and F1‑score.

## Key Takeaways
- YOLOEZ integrates data labeling, model training, and inference into a single interface, allowing high‑performance model creation without programming.
- The tool outperforms traditional methods across most detection metrics, confirming its superior accuracy.
- By lowering adoption barriers, YOLOEZ facilitates wider use of AI‑driven monitoring for predictive maintenance and digital twins.

Modern computer vision relies on deep learning models like YOLO, but deployment often requires coding expertise. This paper addresses the gap by providing a user‑friendly solution that democratizes access to advanced defect detection.
