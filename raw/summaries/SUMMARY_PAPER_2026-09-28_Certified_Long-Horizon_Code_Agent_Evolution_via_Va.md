## Summary
This paper presents VALVE, a validated-gated framework enabling long-horizon self-evolution of code agents without requiring model weight updates. By formalizing in-context skill optimization with theoretical guarantees on convergence and performance bounds, the approach mitigates instability and drawdown issues common in extended deployments. Empirical evaluations demonstrate stable improvement across over 1,000 software engineering tasks, achieving significant gains while maintaining a compact and robust skill bank.

## Key Takeaways
- VALVE introduces a validation-gated mechanism for text-based skill optimization that ensures finite convergence and provides theoretical bounds on future-task gain and performance drawdown, addressing the instability of prior long-horizon methods.
- The framework achieves an average final gain of 14.9 points and a peak gain of 16.5 points across frontier models like GPT
