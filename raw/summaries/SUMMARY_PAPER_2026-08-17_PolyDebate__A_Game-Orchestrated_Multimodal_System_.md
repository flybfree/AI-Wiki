## Summary
PolyDebate is a game‑orchestrated multimodal system that provides English debate practice and evaluation through staged one‑on‑one interactions with an AI opponent. The abstract reports four studies demonstrating that the platform integrates interactive dialogue, visual props, and rubric‑based feedback to improve learners’ argument construction, rebuttal, oral delivery, and audience awareness.

## Key Takeaways
- PolyDebate creates a complete multimodal practice workflow where learners speak, view visual cues, and receive context‑aware opponent responses that are evaluated against structured rubrics.  
- The system’s game mechanics—skill cards, props, and coins—make persuasive strategies explicit, turning debate into an engaging, gamified experience.  
- Evaluation coverage includes both stage‑level performance and overall assessment, providing detailed feedback that aligns with traditional debate rubrics.

## Context
Current AI debate systems focus on text generation or single‑modal evaluation, leaving out the full range of skills involved in real‑world debates such as visual delivery and strategic scaffolding. PolyDebate addresses this gap by coupling language models with multimodal data capture and game‑based interaction to simulate authentic debate practice.

## Implications
For educators, PolyDebate offers a scalable tool that can be embedded into curricula without requiring extensive manual grading. In industry, the platform demonstrates how AI can support soft skills training through immersive feedback loops. Practitioners can leverage its unified workflow to evaluate both content and delivery simultaneously, enhancing learning outcomes across diverse settings.
