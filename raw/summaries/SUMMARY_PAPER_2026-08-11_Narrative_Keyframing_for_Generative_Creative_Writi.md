## Summary
The paper introduces narrative keyframing, an AI-assisted creative writing technique that lets writers place constraints at story moments using plot, character, and perspective keyframes. It demonstrates how these keyframes enable precise control over generated text while keeping the process transparent and engaging.

## Key Takeaways
- Plot keyframes define significant events in a story, allowing developers to condition AI generation on specific narrative milestones.
- Character keyframes track individual changes over time, giving fine-grained control over character development within the narrative flow.
- Perspective keyframes use first‑person narratives as an intermediary, enabling direct control of how characters experience events and focalization.

## Context
Current generative writing tools rely on high-level conditioning that often limits customization. Narrative keyframing extends this by adding granular, iterative controls that align with human storytelling workflows.

## Implications
This approach makes AI more adaptable for writers seeking precise narrative direction, potentially reshaping creative collaboration between humans and machines in publishing, game design, and interactive media.
