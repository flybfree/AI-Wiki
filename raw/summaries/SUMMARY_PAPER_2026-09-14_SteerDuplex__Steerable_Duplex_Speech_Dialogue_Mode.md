## Summary
This paper introduces SteerDuplex, a full-duplex speech dialogue model designed to address the underexplored capability of steerability—the ability to reliably adjust conversational attributes like tone, persona, and speaking rate based on user instructions. By combining supervised fine-tuning with two-stage reinforcement learning and hybrid rewards, the authors demonstrate significant performance gains over existing open baselines while introducing SteerBench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating spoken dialogue control.

## Key Takeaways
- The authors establish a novel taxonomy of text- and audio-based steerability, highlighting critical gaps in current full-duplex models that struggle to adapt vocal delivery and conversational behavior on command based on user instructions.
- SteerDuplex leverages supervised training on natural and synthetic dialogues alongside two-stage reinforcement learning with verifiable interaction checks
