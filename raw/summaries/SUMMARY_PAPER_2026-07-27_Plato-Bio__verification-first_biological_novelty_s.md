## Summary
The paper introduces Plato-Bio, a verification-first biological novelty screening framework that integrates provenance tracking and audit trails to ensure scientific validity in AI-generated biology outputs. It demonstrates that the full Python suite completed with 931 passes and no failures across multiple biology suites.

## Key Takeaways
- The workflow completed 931 passes with no failures, demonstrating robust execution of biology, genomics, evidence/citation, and adversarial-safety suites.
- In the historical rediscovery task, pre‑1986 literature ranked fish oil–Raynaud phenomenon higher than TF‑IDF, highlighting retrospective ranking bias.
- AlphaFold comparisons showed 11 human proteins had RMSD below 1 Å, while confidence masking reduced SUMO1 discrepancy from 16.61 to 2.58 Å over 74 residues.

## Context
This work addresses the gap between AI-generated biological claims and reproducible scientific validation, offering a transparent pipeline that couples workflow states with provenance records. It highlights how automated screening can produce reliable results but still requires careful audit of evidence sources.

## Implications
For researchers, Plato-Bio provides an auditable baseline for evaluating agent performance, encouraging preregistration of evaluation metrics. For industry, it supports trustworthy AI integration in genomics and structural biology pipelines.
