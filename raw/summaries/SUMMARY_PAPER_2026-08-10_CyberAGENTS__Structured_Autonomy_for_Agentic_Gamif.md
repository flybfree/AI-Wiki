## Summary
The paper introduces CyberAgents, a framework that combines generative AI with structured autonomy to deliver gamified cybersecurity learning. It demonstrates improved engagement and trust when behavioral schemas and ontology validation are active. The framework is organized around a competency‑based progression model that structures topics by difficulty and prerequisite relationships, reflecting evidence‑based scaffolded instruction.

## Key Takeaways
- The system uses an ontology to validate all AI‑generated content, ensuring domain consistency and safety.
- Behavioral schemas define operational modes for challenge, support, evaluation, and reward agents, limiting autonomy while preserving flexibility.
- Learner trust increases when feedback is clear and aligned with competency progression logic.

## Context
Generative AI agents can create adaptive learning experiences, but their unchecked behavior risks misalignment with educational goals. This work shows that embedding learning science principles can mitigate those risks and ensure pedagogical coherence. The approach aligns with the growing need for AI systems that respect pedagogical frameworks in educational technology.

## Implications
For educators and developers, CyberAgents provide a blueprint for safe, scalable AI‑driven gamified curricula in cybersecurity training. The approach balances flexibility with control, offering a model that could be adapted to other domains requiring active problem solving.
