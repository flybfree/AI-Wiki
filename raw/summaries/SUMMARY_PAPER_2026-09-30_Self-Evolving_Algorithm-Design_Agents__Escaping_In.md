## Summary
This paper introduces Population-Curated Policy Optimization (PCPO), a sample-efficient parametric self-evolution framework that enables algorithm-design agents to internalize specialized knowledge by learning from self-generated algorithms, thereby overcoming the stagnation limits of pure in-context evolutionary methods. The proposed approach leverages a global population and hybrid policy updates to retain diverse high-quality solutions, demonstrating superior performance over state-of-the-art in-context baselines on electronic design automation tasks while requiring training on only four chip cases. Additionally, PCPO achieves competitive results against frontier closed-source models like GPT-5.5, significantly reduces inference-time token costs through prompt distillation and knowledge internalization
