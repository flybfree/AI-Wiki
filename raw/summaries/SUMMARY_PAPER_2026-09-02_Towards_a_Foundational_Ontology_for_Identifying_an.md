## Summary
The paper proposes a foundational ontology called ATFOt to capture dialogue‑based contradictions in human‑robot interaction using Activity Theory and first‑order logic. It defines natural language dialogues, set‑theoretic contradictions, and three novel principles. Preliminary results include definitions and logical formulation. The ontology is built to be domain‑independent, allowing reuse across different robot platforms and human‑robot interaction scenarios.

## Key Takeaways
- Natural language definitions of dialogues and related contradictions are captured in the ontology.  
- Set theoretic definitions provide a formal representation of dialogue sequences and contradiction types.  
- First Order Logic formulation introduces three novel principles guiding human‑robot dialogue consistency.

## Context
The HRI field lacks a unified computational model for representing contradictions, limiting cross‑domain reuse. The approach aligns with broader AI initiatives that seek formal ontologies for knowledge sharing.
