## Summary
The paper introduces an ontology‑driven framework that formalizes the Robustness Validation process for automotive electrical/electronic components, enabling automated selection of analyses and data provision. By mapping mission profiles to OWL representations, the approach reduces design time and improves exhaustiveness through semantic queries. Experimental results on a power electronics case study demonstrate significant gains in validation efficiency.

## Key Takeaways
- The ontology captures formal knowledge from stress, operating, and load profiles, allowing precise semantic queries that guide the RV workflow.  
- Automating the selection of analyses based on these queries cuts manual effort and eliminates common errors in traditional manual procedures.  
- The framework integrates all relevant component data into a single structured model, enhancing exhaustiveness of validation coverage.
