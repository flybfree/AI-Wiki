## Summary
Generative AI is a subset of artificial intelligence that creates new content such as text, images, videos, or code from user prompts. It relies on neural networks trained on large datasets to generate original material quickly and at scale. The technology offers speed and versatility but also introduces challenges like hallucinations, bias, and privacy concerns.

## Key Takeaways
- Generative AI generates new media from user prompts using neural networks that have learned patterns from existing data.
- It differs from traditional AI which performs narrow tasks; GenAI produces original content rather than just processing inputs.
- Popular examples include ChatGPT for text, DALL‑E3 for images, Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini for multimodal assistance.

## Context
The rise of generative AI is driven by the ability to use natural language prompts, enabling automation across research, coding, design, and content creation. Coursera’s Prompt Engineering Specialization provides a beginner‑friendly path to learn how to harness these tools effectively.

## Implications
Because GenAI can produce inaccurate or biased outputs, human oversight and fact‑checking remain essential. Ethical frameworks must address privacy risks and ensure responsible deployment in industries ranging from marketing to healthcare.
