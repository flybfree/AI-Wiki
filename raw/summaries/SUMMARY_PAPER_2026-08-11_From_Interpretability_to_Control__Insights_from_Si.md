## Summary
This paper surveys the six years of TrustNLP workshop proceedings to trace how trust research evolved from interpretability to control in NLP. It finds that truthfulness and safety alignment have become dominant concerns while explainability shows a U-shaped pattern.

## Key Takeaways
- Truthfulness emerged as the fastest‑growing dimension, absent in early editions and now comprising 37% of papers between 2025 and 2026.  
- Fairness remains the most consistent theme across all six trust dimensions throughout the workshop series.  
- Explainability follows a U-shaped trajectory, declining as post‑hoc methods lost relevance but resurging in 2026 through mechanistic interpretability.

## Context
The TrustNLP workshop documents a shift from static model analysis to dynamic control mechanisms that align generative systems with user expectations. This evolution mirrors broader AI trends toward responsible deployment and regulatory scrutiny of large language models.

## Implications
Researchers should prioritize building trustworthy architectures that embed truthfulness and safety directly into model design rather than relying on post‑hoc explanations. Practitioners must monitor fairness continuously while leveraging mechanistic interpretability to improve transparency in 2026 releases.
