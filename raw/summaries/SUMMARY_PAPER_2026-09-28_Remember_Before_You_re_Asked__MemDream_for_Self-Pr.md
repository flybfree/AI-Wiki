## Summary
MemDream introduces a proactive framework for LLM-based agents that evolves memory through offline "dream cycles" rather than waiting for retrieval failures during real interactions. By employing specialized Dreamer, Analyst, and Consolidator agents to collaboratively probe and repair the memory graph, the system anticipates weaknesses before they impact performance. Experiments show significant improvements over reactive baselines, achieving a 4.5-point F1 gain on LoCoMo and a 9.1-point score increase on MemoryAgentBench.
