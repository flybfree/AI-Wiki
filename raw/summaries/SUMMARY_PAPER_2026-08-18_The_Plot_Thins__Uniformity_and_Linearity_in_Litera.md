## Summary  
The paper investigates how literary summaries preserve or distort the original narrative order and attention distribution. Using a dataset of 150 novel summaries mapped to source chapters, it measures linearity and uniformity. The study finds that both human and LLM annotators struggle with accurate mapping, revealing systematic breaks in plot continuity.

## Key Takeaways  
- Human annotators often reorder events, breaking linearity despite clear chapter references.  
- Automated models similarly misalign sentences with source order, indicating difficulty in preserving narrative flow.  
- Uniformity is low; summaries allocate disproportionate focus to certain chapters while omitting others.

## Context  
This work addresses a core challenge in AI-generated literary summarization: maintaining fidelity to source material. By quantifying linearity and uniformity, the study provides empirical benchmarks for evaluating summary quality beyond simple word overlap.

## Implications  
For researchers developing LLM summarizers, these findings suggest that alignment tasks must prioritize event ordering. Practitioners should incorporate manual verification steps to reduce systematic deviations in plot representation.
