## Summary
The paper introduces Agent2UCB, an autonomous system that optimizes web pages to be more likely cited or summarized by large language models. Experiments on GEO‑Bench show measurable visibility improvements while maintaining SEO quality.

## Key Takeaways
- The agent evaluates nine GEO strategies and selects the most effective one using a bandit policy that combines LLM priors with real‑time reward signals.
- A lightweight text‑only SEO readiness check assesses readability, topical coverage, and EEAT‑style credibility to monitor side effects.
- The demo lets users pick websites, watch optimization in action, and compare GEO versus traditional SEO outcomes.

## Context
Generative search engines are reshaping content discovery by favoring concise, well‑structured snippets. Optimizing for these systems requires a balance between visibility gains and user experience, which is precisely what Agent2UCB addresses.

## Implications
For web publishers, the system offers a practical tool to boost algorithmic relevance without sacrificing readability or trustworthiness. Practitioners can adopt similar feedback loops to stay competitive in an AI‑driven information landscape.
