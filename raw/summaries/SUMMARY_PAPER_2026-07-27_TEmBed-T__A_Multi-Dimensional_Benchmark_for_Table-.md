## Summary
The paper introduces TEmBed-T, a multi-dimensional benchmark that evaluates table-level embeddings across retrieval, classification, and discovery tasks. Its main finding is that no single embedding model dominates all tasks, showing the importance of task-specific quality.

## Key Takeaways
- The study confirms that retrieval performance alone does not capture overall embedding effectiveness.
- It demonstrates that different models excel in distinct downstream tasks within TEmBed-T.
- This highlights a need for multi-task evaluation rather than relying on single metrics.

## Context
Table-level embeddings are essential for many AI applications involving structured data, yet existing benchmarks often focus narrowly. This work expands the scope of TEmBed to include classification and discovery, addressing a gap in current literature.

## Implications
Practitioners will benefit from adopting task-aware evaluation frameworks that consider multiple objectives. Industry systems can design embeddings tailored to specific use cases rather than one-size-fits-all solutions.
