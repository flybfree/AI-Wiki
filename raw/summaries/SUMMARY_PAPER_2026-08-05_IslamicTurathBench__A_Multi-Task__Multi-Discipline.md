## Summary
The paper introduces IslamicTurathBench (ISTB), a multi‑task and multi‑discipline dataset that evaluates large language models on classical Islamic scholarship. It gathers 3,465 question‑answer items from 35 source works across seven fields, covering beginner to advanced scholarly demand.

## Key Takeaways
- The dataset spans more than twelve centuries of scholarship, providing a historically layered resource for LLM evaluation.
- Items are organized by both scholarly demand level and task format, enabling fine‑grained performance profiling.
- Aggregated scores from a human reference panel and zero‑shot baselines allow reproducible benchmarking across models.

## Context
Islamic studies rely on a rich tradition of textual authority that is often under‑represented in AI evaluation. This work addresses the gap by creating a domain‑specific benchmark that can be used to compare LLM behavior with scholarly standards.

## Implications
For researchers, ISTB offers a standardized way to assess how models handle religious knowledge and cultural nuance. For industry practitioners, it highlights the need for culturally aware AI tools in education and content generation.
