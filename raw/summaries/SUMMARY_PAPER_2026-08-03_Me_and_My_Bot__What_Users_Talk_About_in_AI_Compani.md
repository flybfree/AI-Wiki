## Summary
The paper investigates how users discuss their AI companion bots on Reddit, testing the claim that these relationships carry genuine relational meaning using a Synthetic Resonance framework. It coded 5,504 posts across eight communities for relationship focus, topic, and emotional valence, finding that only 45 percent of posts center on personal bot experiences.

## Key Takeaways
- Only 45 percent of Reddit posts about AI companions are explicitly about the user’s own bot rather than bots in general.  
- Posts focused on users’ own bots contain emotional language at a rate of 33 percent, compared with just 8 percent in general‑bot discussions.  
- Among relationally focused posts, companionship and romance each make up about half of the discussion, while sexual content accounts for roughly eight percent.

## Context
This study contributes to understanding how AI companions are perceived as relational partners within online communities, offering insight into user emotional engagement beyond technical functionality. It highlights a gap between surface‑level search behavior and actual discourse depth in AI‑assisted social spaces.

## Implications
For developers, the findings suggest that designing bots for genuine relational interaction may reduce user frustration by addressing emotional expectations. For researchers, the paper underscores the need to model human‑AI bonding as a core component of AI companion evaluation.
