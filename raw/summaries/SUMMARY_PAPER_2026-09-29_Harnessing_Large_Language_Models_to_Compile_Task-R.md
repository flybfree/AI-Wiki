## Summary
This paper investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be utilized to compile diverse task-relevant context into Bayesian Optimisation (BO), addressing the challenge of integrating domain knowledge and external observations. The authors introduce HarBO, a specialized harness that transforms generalised context into standard BO artefacts through a validated multi-stage workflow, supported by theoretical analysis of regret under imperfect compilation. Experimental results demonstrate that LLM harnesses achieve competitive performance against dedicated methods in familiar domains like hyperparameter optimization, though they struggle with unfamiliar, context-rich scenarios, highlighting both the potential and limitations of this approach.
