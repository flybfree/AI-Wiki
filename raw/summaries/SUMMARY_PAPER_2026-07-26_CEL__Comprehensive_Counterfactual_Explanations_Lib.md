## Summary
The paper introduces CEL, a unified library and benchmark for counterfactual explanations in AI. It provides 18 datasets and implementations of 14 methods to enable fair comparison. Quantitative evaluation across methods is shown using multiple metrics.

## Key Takeaways
- CEL offers a standardized set of 18 diverse datasets and reimplementations of 14 popular counterfactual methods to ensure consistent implementation.
- The benchmark evaluates methods using multiple complementary metrics that capture validity, coverage, sparsity, proximity, and distributional plausibility.
- This is the first comprehensive benchmark that systematically compares recent counterfactual explanation techniques within a reproducible framework.

## Context
Counterfactual explanations aim to make AI decisions interpretable by suggesting minimal input changes. However prior work lacks unified datasets and evaluation protocols leading to inconsistent results across studies. CEL addresses this gap by providing a common ground for research.

## Implications
For researchers CEL enables reproducible experiments and fair comparison of methods. For practitioners it offers a reliable toolkit to assess the quality of counterfactual explanations in production systems. This foundation supports future development of more robust and trustworthy AI.
