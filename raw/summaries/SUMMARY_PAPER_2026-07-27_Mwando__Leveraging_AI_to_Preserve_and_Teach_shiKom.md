## Summary
The paper introduces Mwando, a virtual educational assistant designed to preserve and teach shiKomori across its four dialectal variants using a knowledge base of phrases, proverbs, dictionaries, and grammar lessons. A multi‑agent architecture that combines vector search, a knowledge graph, and web search fallback enables accurate, context‑aware responses. Evaluation on 500 queries shows strong performance in vocabulary lookup and grammar explanations.

## Key Takeaways
- The system covers all four dialectal variants of shiKomori through an integrated knowledge base that includes phrases, proverbs, dictionaries, and grammar lessons.
- A multi‑agent architecture combines vector search, a knowledge graph, and web search fallback to deliver accurate, context‑aware responses.
- Evaluation on 500 queries demonstrates strong performance in vocabulary lookup and grammar explanations, while case studies reveal current limitations.
