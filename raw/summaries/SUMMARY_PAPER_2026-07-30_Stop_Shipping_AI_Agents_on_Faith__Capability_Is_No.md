## Summary
The paper argues that AI agents moving to production still rely on capability signals rather than true readiness. It introduces the ProofAgent Index (PAI) as a governance readiness index combining evaluation, context, compliance, and governance evidence. Validation in healthcare and finance shows PAI separates high‑risk from low‑risk configurations.

## Key Takeaways
- Evaluation measures observed behavior but does not guarantee production readiness.
- Context engineering strongly changes reliability yet capability alone does not determine readiness.
- Governance evidence must stay visible rather than being averaged away.

## Context
AI agents are increasingly deployed in regulated sectors where failure can have severe consequences. This work addresses a gap between technical demonstration and operational trust, offering a measurable index for deployment decisions.

## Implications
Practitioners can move beyond faith‑based releases to auditable readiness assessments. The PAI framework encourages systematic governance across evaluation, context, compliance, and governance dimensions in AI agent rollouts.
