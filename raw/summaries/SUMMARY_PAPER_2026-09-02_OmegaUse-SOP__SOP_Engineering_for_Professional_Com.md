## Summary
OmegaUse-SOP is a human‑in‑the‑loop system that transforms professional GUI demonstrations into reusable SOP skills for computer agents. The authors show that the approach improves reliability on photovoltaic simulation tasks in PVsyst 7.2, suggesting a practical path to deploying GUI agents in domain‑specific software.

## Key Takeaways
- OmegaUse-SOP records expert operations as multimodal GUI traces and abstracts them into semantic step‑level instructions for reuse.
- The system incorporates domain rules and task‑specific parameters to guide action generation and verification within live GUI environments.
- Iterative refinement of demonstrations, execution rules, and knowledge mirrors prompt engineering but focuses on SOP construction.

## Context
The rapid evolution of large language models toward autonomous agents creates a need for reliable human‑crafted workflows. Professional SOPs remain underutilized because they embed implicit domain knowledge that is hard to encode. This work bridges the gap between expert practice and automated execution, highlighting a missing link in AI‑assisted professional software.

## Implications
Practitioners can leverage OmegaUse-SOP to create reusable skill modules for GUI agents without deep coding expertise. Industries such as energy, manufacturing, or healthcare could adopt these SOP‑driven agents to automate routine tasks while maintaining compliance and accuracy.
