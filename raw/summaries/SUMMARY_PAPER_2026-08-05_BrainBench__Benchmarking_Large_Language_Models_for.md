## Summary
BrainBench introduces a unified benchmark for comprehensive EEG understanding, enabling large language models to process natural‑language instructions and generate scientifically grounded reports from EEG recordings with optional physiological signals. The study evaluates multiple LLMs across diverse tasks and execution paradigms, revealing significant variability in model performance.

## Key Takeaways
- BrainBench provides a structured framework that combines instruction decoding, signal analysis, quantitative evidence, and interpretation into a single evaluation.  
- Results differ markedly among models, task subsets, difficulty levels, and whether the system uses autonomous code or agentic analysis.  
- The benchmark includes 17 datasets with over 100K real‑data instances, offering a reproducible testbed for LLM‑based EEG research.

## Context
The paper addresses a gap in AI evaluation where most benchmarks focus on isolated decoding rather than end‑to‑end workflows. By integrating natural language and multimodal data, BrainBench aligns with broader efforts to assess AI capabilities in real‑world scientific tasks.

## Implications
BrainBench will guide developers toward more reliable LLM implementations for EEG analysis, influencing both academic research and industry applications that require accurate neurophysiological interpretation.
