## Summary
This paper conducts a long‑term analysis of Reddit discussions to trace how users perceive conversational AI system releases from Anthropic, OpenAI, Grok‑3, and DeepSeek‑R1. By linking sentiment classification with thematic concept analysis, the authors reveal that model releases act as user‑facing interventions that shift expectations, generate backlash, enable recovery, and spark excitement.

## Key Takeaways
- Anthropic’s Claude Code release is linked to a clear positive sentiment boost driven by strong product‑model fit.  
- OpenAI experiences backlash followed by recovery around GPT‑5 and GPT‑5.1 releases, indicating volatile user reactions tied to perceived quality changes.  
- Grok‑3’s perception is filtered through the provider’s identity and political discourse, while DeepSeek‑R1 blends engineering praise with worries about censorship, access restrictions, and reliability.

## Context
The rapid evolution of conversational AI models generates frequent releases that are rarely examined beyond technical metrics. This study highlights a gap in research that treats user perception as static, overlooking the dynamic impact of release events on public discourse and brand sentiment.

## Implications
For practitioners, understanding these perceptual cycles can inform product‑release strategies and risk management. The findings suggest that releasing AI models is not just an engineering milestone but a communication event that shapes market expectations and community trust.
