## Summary
This paper surveys how artificial intelligence and modeling & simulation intersect across the full lifecycle of a simulation study. It highlights that AI techniques, especially large language models, are reshaping each stage from model specification to output analysis. The authors demonstrate both supportive roles of AI in accelerating simulations and the potential for simulations to generate data for training AI systems.

## Key Takeaways
- AI can automate and enhance model specification by generating code or parameter suggestions using large language models, reducing manual effort.
- Simulations serve as dynamic data sources that feed training pipelines, enabling AI models to learn from realistic scenarios without external datasets.
- The integration creates feedback loops where AI improves simulation fidelity while simulations validate AI predictions.

## Context
The rapid rise of generative AI has prompted researchers in M&S to explore algorithmic assistance and synthetic data generation. This paper situates these advances within the broader ecosystem, emphasizing the need for interdisciplinary frameworks that bridge computational theory with practical application.

## Implications
For practitioners, this roadmap suggests prioritizing AI integration at early stages to leverage cost savings and speed. Industry adoption will depend on addressing challenges like model interpretability and computational overhead.
