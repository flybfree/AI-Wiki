## Summary
The paper introduces A-CPES, a reference framework that positions agentic AI as an outer loop controlling six‑layer CPES core while embedding authorization and accountability frames. It proposes three nested rings of governance to manage the interaction between AI agents and existing dispatch systems. The authors identify eight failure modes and six governance modules as falsifiable predictions.

## Key Takeaways
- A-CPES structures agentic AI within a six‑layer CPES core, making it an outer control loop rather than a replacement for human dispatchers.
- The framework defines three nested rings of authorization and accountability that can be tuned to close or open the loop depending on operational needs.
- It predicts eight structural failure modes and specifies six governance modules that rebuild the authorization frame before the loop begins.

## Context
The rapid integration of variable renewable energy into grids has outpaced the capacity of human‑centric dispatchers, creating a bottleneck in real‑time optimization. This work addresses the need for scalable, transparent AI control that can handle complex, multi‑party negotiations without sacrificing explainability or safety.

## Implications
For grid operators, A-CPES offers a clear blueprint to embed AI oversight while preserving human accountability and reducing operational risk. Practitioners can adopt the six governance modules to align AI deployment with regulatory expectations and ensure robust system resilience.
