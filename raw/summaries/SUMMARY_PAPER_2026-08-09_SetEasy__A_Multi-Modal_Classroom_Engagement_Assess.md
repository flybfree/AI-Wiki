## Summary
SetEasy is a framework that combines multimodal sensing with a v-Gage model to forecast and optimize classroom engagement in fixed seating grids. The study shows that the optimization raises overall engagement scores from 0.30 to 0.70, reducing prediction error and improving seat utilization.

## Key Takeaways
- Two‑week engagement forecasts are mapped onto a student‑seat utility matrix, allowing precise assignment of students to seats based on predicted affective, behavioral, cognitive, and overall dimensions.
- The CP‑SAT optimizer generates seating plans that respect visual‑access and social‑dynamics constraints while maximizing the utility derived from the v‑Gage model.
- In a four‑week deployment with 23 students across 331 classes, mean engagement increased by 0.40 points and more than two‑thirds of seats achieved high engagement levels.

## Context
This work addresses the growing need for data‑driven classroom design in an era where AI models can predict human behavior from multimodal inputs such as physiology, video, and environmental sensors. By integrating these streams into a unified v‑Gage architecture, SetEasy exemplifies how machine learning can inform spatial arrangements without requiring new hardware.

## Implications
For educators and school administrators, SetEasy provides an interpretable method to allocate seating that supports differentiated instruction and cultural responsiveness. The framework’s sustainability makes it attractive for large institutions seeking scalable solutions to improve student engagement across diverse settings.
