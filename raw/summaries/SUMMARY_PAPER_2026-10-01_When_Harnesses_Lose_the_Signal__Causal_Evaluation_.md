## Summary
This paper addresses the limitations of evaluating error recovery in large language model agents by framing it as a causal decision problem rather than relying solely on aggregate success metrics. The authors demonstrate that recovery interventions can simultaneously rescue failing trajectories and disrupt otherwise successful ones, necessitating a more nuanced approach to intervention timing.
