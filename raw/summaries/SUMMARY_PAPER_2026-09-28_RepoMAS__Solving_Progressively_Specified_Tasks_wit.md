## Summary
The paper addresses the limitation of multi-agent systems that require fully specified tasks upfront by introducing RepoMAS, a framework designed for progressively specified tasks where requirements emerge during execution. The authors propose ProgSpec, a benchmark measuring performance against both initial and evidence-based additional requirements, demonstrating that RepoMAS outperforms existing methods across multiple benchmarks through its ability to dynamically revise task specifications via structured issue tracking.
