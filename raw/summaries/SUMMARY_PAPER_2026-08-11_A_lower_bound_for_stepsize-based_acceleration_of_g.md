## Summary
This paper establishes a new lower bound for the convergence rate of gradient descent when using only predetermined nonnegative stepsize schedules. It shows that such methods cannot achieve the theoretical optimal O(T^{-2}) rate and instead are limited to Ω(T^{-1.9319}). The result is derived from rigorous analysis rather than heuristic arguments.
