## Summary
PROACT-Agent presents a framework for synthesizing high-fidelity trajectories to enable real-time safety guardrails, addressing the critical transition where AI agent risks escalate from toxic text generation to irreversible environmental harm. The authors identify a pervasive "safety drift" in existing benchmarks caused by lenient annotation paradigms that fail to enforce temporal consistency, and they introduce PROACT-Bench, a rigorous bilingual benchmark with 155,780 states labeled via multi-model adjudication. Their approach achieves substantial improvements, with the trained guard reaching a 91.46% unsafe-class F1 score and reducing attack success rates in AgentDojo from 20.82% to 0.40%.
