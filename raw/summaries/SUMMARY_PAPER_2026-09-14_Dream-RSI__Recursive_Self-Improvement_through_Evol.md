## Summary
Dream-RSI introduces a novel framework for scalable recursive self-improvement in autonomous AI agents by addressing the critical bottleneck of exploration strategy management. The system leverages accumulated discovery history to construct a lightweight replay simulator, enabling immediate and low-cost off-policy feedback for refining exploration policies without expensive online evaluations. This iterative loop continuously deploys improved strategies back into live environments, achieving competitive or superior discovery outcomes across diverse technical domains while significantly cutting computational costs.
