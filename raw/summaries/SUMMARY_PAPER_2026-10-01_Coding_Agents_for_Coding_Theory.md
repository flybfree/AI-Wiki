## Summary
This study investigates the utility of LLM coding agents in addressing open problems within coding theory, specifically targeting the maximization of four-letter word sets like DNA barcodes under edit distance constraints. Over a five-week engagement, the human researcher defined the problem scope and verification protocol while the agent autonomously generated search and verifier code. By applying classical symmetry restrictions to reduce the search space by approximately fourfold, the pipeline improved the best known lower bound for codes of length 6 with minimum edit distance 3 from 114 to 120 words and enhanced twelve additional bounds across lengths 6 to 9.

## Key Takeaways
