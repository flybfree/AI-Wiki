## Summary
This paper addresses the tendency of language models to generate homogeneous responses by formulating persona diversification as a set-level conditioning problem. The authors evaluate four methods spanning subset selection, uniform sampling, and evolutionary generation across multiple creativity benchmarks. Results demonstrate that evolutionary persona generation significantly enhances response diversity, originality, and flexibility while maintaining high validity, establishing persona-set geometry as an effective mechanism for eliciting divergent outputs.

## Key Takeaways
- Evolutionary persona generation outperforms baseline methods, increasing response diversity by 78.8%, originality by 26.1%, flexibility by 49.5%, and holistic creativity by 13.9% on the Alternative Uses Task compared to task-only prompting, all while preserving a validity rate of 98.5%.
