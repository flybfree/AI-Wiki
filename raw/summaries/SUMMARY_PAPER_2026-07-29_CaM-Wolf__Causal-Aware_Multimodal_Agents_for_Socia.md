## Summary
This paper introduces CaM‑Wolf, the first social deduction game agent that combines multimodal perception and generation to improve gameplay in games like Werewolf. By processing video inputs from other players, employing a causal‑aware Reasoner trained with reinforcement learning, and presenting itself through an animated avatar, CaM‑Wolf achieves superior performance and enhances human‑AI interaction quality.

## Key Takeaways
- CaM‑Wolf integrates multimodal perception and generation, moving beyond text‑only approaches to handle video inputs from other players.  
- It uses a causal‑aware Reasoner trained via reinforcement learning to create logical chains between observable behaviors and hidden roles.  
- Experiments demonstrate that CaM‑Wolf outperforms existing agents in gameplay and improves the quality of human‑AI interaction.

## Context
Recent advances in large language models have driven progress in text‑based social deduction agents, but these systems ignore the multimodal cues essential to human social dynamics. This paper addresses a gap by showing how combining video perception with causal reasoning can produce more realistic agents for games that rely on visual and behavioral signals.

## Implications
The work has broader implications for AI research, industry, and practitioners seeking to create agents that can navigate nuanced social interactions. By demonstrating multimodal capabilities in a game setting, CaM‑Wolf sets a foundation for future applications where human‑like behavior across multiple modalities is required.
