This study investigates how two frozen large language models from different providers can establish reliable communication without weight updates or shared tokenizers. By framing their interaction as a referential game and optimizing their prompts through isolated reflection-based agents, the researchers demonstrate that external prompt tuning can successfully generate shared coding systems.
