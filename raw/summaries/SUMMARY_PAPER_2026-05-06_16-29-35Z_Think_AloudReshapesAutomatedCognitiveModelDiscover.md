## Summary
This paper investigates how incorporating think‑aloud language traces can enhance automated discovery of cognitive models beyond the limitations imposed by behavioral data alone. The authors demonstrate that adding these process‑level constraints yields higher predictive accuracy and reveals a systematic shift in model structure from explicit comparator to integrated utility representations.

## Key Takeaways
- Think‑aloud traces provide an additional constraint that significantly improves model fit on held‑out data.
- For the majority of participants (69.4 %), models discovered with think‑aloud are of a different structural class than those found using behavior alone, moving from explicit comparator to integrated utility.
- This approach uncovers mechanisms that cannot be recovered from behavioral trajectories alone.

## Context
Current automated cognitive model discovery relies heavily on behavioral outputs, often resulting in underdetermined and potentially misleading models. By integrating process‑level language data, researchers can constrain the search space more effectively, leading to richer and more interpretable representations of mental processes.

## Implications
These findings suggest that incorporating think‑aloud traces will make AI‑driven cognitive modeling more reliable and actionable for both researchers and practitioners seeking deeper insights into decision‑making mechanisms.
