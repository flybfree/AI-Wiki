## Summary
The paper proposes a unified six‑stage closed‑loop framework for Integrated Sensing and Communication that treats AI as an agentic system. It reviews recent advances in multimodal sensing, large language models, reinforcement learning, federated learning, RIS control, UAV networks, and AI‑native network management while highlighting gaps between claimed and demonstrated agentic maturity.

## Key Takeaways
- The framework defines six sequential stages—observation, contextualization, reasoning, prediction, planning, execution, feedback—that must be integrated for true AISAC operation.
- Agentic maturity is measured at five levels, yet most studies only achieve one or two of the nine agentic evaluation criteria, indicating limited progress toward full closed‑loop capability.
- Cross‑cutting challenges such as physical‑to‑semantic grounding, real‑time PHY interaction, and resource‑efficient autonomy remain unresolved.

## Context
Current AI research often treats sensing, communication, and control as separate modules, missing the opportunity to create a seamless perception‑reasoning‑action loop. This paper bridges that gap by providing a systematic view of how these components can be orchestrated under an agentic paradigm.

## Implications
For industry, adopting this framework could enable autonomous systems that perceive, reason, act, and recover without human intervention, improving efficiency and reliability. Practitioners should focus on evaluating real‑world performance against the defined agentic criteria to avoid overstated claims.
