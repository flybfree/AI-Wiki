## Summary
The paper introduces EgoCross, a cross‑domain egocentric video question answering benchmark presented at the Third EgoVis Workshop in 2026. It evaluates multimodal large language models on first‑person videos from surgery, industrial assembly, extreme sports, and animal perspectives, providing official leaderboards and winning solutions.

## Key Takeaways
- EgoCross is a benchmark that tests whether multimodal LLMs can generalize across four distinct video domains using first‑person clips.  
- The challenge features two tracks: the Source‑Limited Track restricts participants to the baseline model and a small support set, while the Open‑Source Track allows broader model choices but forbids manual creation of target‑domain data.  
- Over 1,500 submissions were received from more than 130 teams, with 19 teams in the Open‑Source Track and 38 in the Source‑Limited Track.

## Context
This work pushes multimodal large language models beyond everyday daily‑life scenarios into specialized fields such as surgery and animal perspectives. By creating a standardized benchmark, it highlights the importance of domain generalization for real‑world applications where diverse video inputs are common.

## Implications
The EgoCross benchmark offers practitioners a clear evaluation framework to assess model robustness across unrelated domains. For industry, it encourages investment in models that can handle varied visual and textual tasks without retraining from scratch.
