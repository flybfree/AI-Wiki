## Summary
This paper evaluates Vision Language Models (VLMs) on multimodal Aristotelian persuasion tasks, focusing on detecting Logos, Ethos, and Pathos cues in image‑text pairs. The study uses the ImageArg dataset and finds that Qwen3 models achieve high F1 scores for Logos and Pathos detection, while Qwen2 performs competitively on the more challenging Ethos task.

## Key Takeaways
- Qwen3 demonstrates superior performance on Logos and Pathos tasks, indicating strong reasoning abilities in identifying logical arguments and emotional appeals.  
- Qwen2 provides competitive results for Ethos detection, showing that ethical credibility assessment is still a significant challenge despite progress.  
- The release of code supports further research into multimodal persuasion modeling, encouraging reproducibility and community contributions.

The integration of vision and language in AI has advanced rapidly, yet complex human‑centric tasks like Aristotle’s Persuasion Model remain underexplored.
