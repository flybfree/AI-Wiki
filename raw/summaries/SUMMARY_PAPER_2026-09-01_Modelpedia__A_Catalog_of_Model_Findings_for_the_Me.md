## Summary
The paper introduces Modelpedia, an automated catalog that extracts and links model findings to the corresponding models, datasets, methods, and concepts. By processing accepted ICLR papers from 2024 and 2025, it gathers over a thousand observations, treating the catalog itself as data for a meta‑analysis of how the research community investigates AI models.

## Key Takeaways
- Modelpedia creates a searchable public database that aggregates findings about foundation models extracted from hundreds of papers.  
- The framework is LLM‑assisted and automatically links each finding to its model, dataset, method, and concept for easy retrieval.  
- A meta‑analysis of the catalog reveals patterns in how researchers document and discuss model behavior.

## Context
Rapid advances in foundation models generate a flood of technical reports that remain fragmented, making it difficult for researchers to locate relevant insights. This work addresses the need for organized knowledge in AI research by providing a unified repository that can be queried across multiple publications.

## Implications
For practitioners, Modelpedia offers a quick reference to model capabilities and limitations, reducing duplication of effort. For the broader field, it supports a meta‑science of AI by making model findings accessible and reusable, fostering more informed decision‑making in industry and academia.
