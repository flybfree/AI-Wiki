## Summary
OmouAI presents an interactive deliberation system that combines large language models with computational argumentation to address sycophancy and lack of faithful explanations in high-stakes human-AI policy discussions. The framework enables users to deliberate against simulated personas representing diverse stakeholders, fostering a shared argumentation structure where humans can actively contest and refine claims while ensuring outcomes are evaluated deterministically against external goals like the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

## Key Takeaways
- OmouAI utilizes simulated personas, including domain experts, stakeholders, and devil's advocates, to generate distinct arguments that diversify the deliberation process and effectively reduce sycophantic tendencies common in standard LLM interactions.
- The system establishes a shared argumentation framework that grants users critical oversight capabilities, allowing them to contest, add, or revise arguments to ensure the deliberation remains inclusive and aligned with human judgment.
- Arguments are evaluated using deterministic argumentative semantics against external objectives such as the UN Sustainable Development Goals, providing transparent explanations of policy impacts and serving as reliable indicators for recommendation advancement or worsening.

## Context
As large language models are increasingly integrated into sensitive domains requiring public trust, addressing reliability challenges like sycophancy and opaque reasoning remains a critical research priority. This work contributes to the emerging field of argumentative AI by demonstrating how computational argumentation can structure LLM interactions to support multi-perspective analysis and transparent deliberation in complex policy environments.

## Implications
For policymakers and practitioners, OmouAI offers a mechanism to simulate stakeholder dynamics and assess policy claims against measurable global standards, potentially enhancing the robustness and accountability of public decision-making workflows. The approach also highlights a pathway for developing AI systems that provide auditable reasoning trails through deterministic evaluation, which is essential for deploying trustworthy tools in high-stakes applications where faithful explanations are mandatory.
