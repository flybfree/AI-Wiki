## Summary
This paper introduces MatEvolve, an agentic AI framework that designs crystal structures by composing them from a language of motifs. It uses a motif profile to describe recurring geometric patterns and edits the profile to generate new crystals. The approach yields new rare‑earth‑lean permanent magnet prototypes three times more often than generative models under equal validation.

## Key Takeaways
- MatEvolve designs crystals by editing a human‑readable motif profile that encodes repeating geometric patterns, allowing precise control over structure.
- The framework reaches new structural prototypes up to three times faster than standard generative models while maintaining comparable on‑target magnet rates.
- Validation is performed via first‑principles calculations and the motif profiles provide interpretable links between crystal design and material properties.

## Context
The field of data‑driven materials discovery relies heavily on interpolation rather than extrapolation, limiting the emergence of novel structure types. Recent advances in large language models enable AI to generate textual descriptions that can be mapped onto physical designs, but few have been applied directly to crystal engineering without fine‑tuning.

## Implications
This work demonstrates that interpretable motif‑based reasoning can accelerate high‑value material design for rare‑earth magnets, reducing experimental cycles. Practitioners may adopt motif profiles as a bridge between AI output and computational validation, fostering transparent and reproducible discovery pipelines.
