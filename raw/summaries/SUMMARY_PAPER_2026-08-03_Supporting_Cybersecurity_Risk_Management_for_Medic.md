## Summary
The paper introduces SECUMAN, an OWL-based ontology and SHACL constraint set that formalizes cybersecurity risk‑management documentation for medical devices. It extends the existing RISKMAN safety vocabulary to include threat scenarios, protection goals, attacker profiles, exposure levels, assets, and secure design arguments, enabling automated validation and traceability.

## Key Takeaways
- SECUMAN provides a structured OWL ontology that maps cybersecurity risk concepts to standardized terms, improving consistency in documentation.  
- SHACL constraints enforce structural completeness, allowing first‑pass checks of compliance with the intended model without manual review.  
- The ontology aligns with VDE Spec 90025 and RISKMAN, bridging safety and cybersecurity risk management for medical devices.

## Context
Medical device manufacturers must now produce documentation that satisfies both safety standards and emerging cybersecurity regulations, a dual burden that traditional semi‑structured text cannot reliably address. This work contributes to the AI research agenda by using formal ontologies to automate compliance checks, reducing human error and accelerating certification processes.

## Implications
Adopting SECUMAN can streamline regulatory submissions, lower costs associated with rework, and enhance patient safety by ensuring cybersecurity risks are systematically evaluated. Practitioners will benefit from a reusable model that integrates seamlessly with existing risk‑management workflows and supports continuous improvement in documentation quality.
