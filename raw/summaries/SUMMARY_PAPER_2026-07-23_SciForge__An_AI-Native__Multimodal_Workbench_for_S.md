## Summary
SciForge is a multimodal AI workbench that integrates scientific artifacts such as papers, code, datasets and model outputs into a single research state. It enables goal‑scoped decision governance, evidence traceability and collaborative workflows while keeping the interface for human judgment. The platform integrates desktop and mobile supervision, with future releases extending team collaboration.

## Key Takeaways
- SciForge provides modular services for search parsing model routing and workflow execution that operate behind a thin interaction layer.
- The system enforces evidence governance by linking every claim to provenance chains through an audit‑based DAG sidecar.
- It supports collaborative team science with shared workspaces, allowing multi‑role decision making. All components are designed for extensibility, allowing new scientific modules to be plugged in without altering the core architecture.

## Context
Current AI assistants lack persistent research state across heterogeneous scientific outputs. This gap limits reproducibility and teamwork in complex projects. SciForge addresses this by unifying these artifacts into a coherent audit trail.

## Implications
Researchers can now automate discovery tasks like gene screening or protein design while maintaining full traceability. The open‑source platform may accelerate AI adoption in labs and industry, fostering transparent scientific workflows.
