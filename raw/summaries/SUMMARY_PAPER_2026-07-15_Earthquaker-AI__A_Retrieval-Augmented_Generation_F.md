## Summary
The paper introduces Earthquaker-AI, a retrieval-augmented generation framework that augments the Earthquaker educational robotics project for primary‑school earthquake education. It combines Lego WeDo2 simulations with AI‑driven conversational feedback and rubric‑based assessment to promote safety knowledge and self‑regulation.

## Key Takeaways
- The system uses a two‑dimensional rubric in early grades to evaluate multiple‑choice answers on basic safety actions, providing immediate verbal feedback that guides metacognitive processing.  
- In middle grades the evaluation shifts to a three‑axis rubric assessing correct action sequences, reinforcing procedural understanding through AI‑generated prompts.  
- Upper grades employ a four‑dimensional rubric that includes clarity of expression in short written responses, encouraging verbal production and reflective learning.

## Context
This work advances retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG) applications in K‑12 education by grounding AI outputs with official safety guidelines while integrating tactile robotics. It demonstrates how hybrid human‑AI systems can support cognitive development through progressive assessment rubrics aligned with developmental stages.

## Implications
The framework offers a scalable model for emergency preparedness curricula, merging low‑cost robotics with AI to build early crisis‑management skills. Practitioners can adopt Earthquaker-AI to create engaging, data‑driven learning experiences that foster responsible digital use and self‑regulated behavior in high‑risk scenarios.
