## Summary
The paper introduces OmniScientist, an end-to-end AI system that integrates perception and three autonomous agents to conduct multidisciplinary research from raw heterogeneous evidence to a manuscript. It demonstrates full completion of the research lifecycle across 36 real-data cases and outperforms a blind variant in all evaluation dimensions.

## Key Takeaways
- OmniScientist processes raw data directly, enabling perception-driven hypothesis generation that shapes experiments and claims throughout the research pipeline.
- The system enforces novelty screening, statistical validity, execution provenance, and numerical traceability by running checks in code within each agent stage.
- Direct perception improves all seven evaluation metrics compared to a blind version that uses only precomputed scalar features.

## Context
Foundation models have enabled AI agents to automate research tasks such as coding and text generation, yet most systems lack multimodal reasoning over raw evidence. This work addresses the gap by providing an integrated pipeline that couples perception with autonomous decision-making across multiple scientific disciplines.

## Implications
The results suggest that lifecycle-wide perception is essential for truly evidence-grounded AI scientists, offering a practical blueprint for scalable, interdisciplinary research automation. Practitioners can adopt similar modular agents to handle diverse data types and improve reliability of automated scientific output.
