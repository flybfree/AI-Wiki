## Summary
Describes a two-track pipeline for Indonesian e-commerce reviews on PRDECT-ID, combining TF-IDF plus AutoML baselines with a multi-task BiLSTM for binary sentiment and five-class emotion classification. The system uses extensive text cleaning, a slang dictionary, and several model variants, and is deployed as a Gradio app.

## Key Takeaways
- Covers both sentiment and emotion labels in a shared benchmark.
- Benchmarks classical AutoML models against BiLSTM and TextCNN variants.
- Includes substantial preprocessing for slang-heavy marketplace text.

## Context
The task addresses noisy Indonesian marketplace reviews where lexicon methods struggle.

## Implications
The pipeline offers a practical baseline for multilingual sentiment and emotion analysis in e-commerce settings.
