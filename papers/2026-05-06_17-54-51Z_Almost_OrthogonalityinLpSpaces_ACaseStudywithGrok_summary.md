## Summary
This paper studies sharpened almost-orthogonality inequalities in Lp spaces and shows that a previously proposed bound fails for every p > 2. The authors identify the correct exponent constraints, prove a critical-case inequality for integer p ≥ 2, and develop a sharp three-function bound with an optimal exponent c(p). Some intermediate steps were explored with assistance from Grok.

## Key Takeaways
- Not all proposed almost-orthogonality bounds survive beyond the critical regime.
- The admissible exponent is tightly constrained by p.
- The paper derives an optimal three-function inequality with improved constants.

## Context
The work builds on Carbery-style sharpened triangle inequalities and refines earlier results by Carlen, Frank, and Lieb. It also reports that Grok helped explore some intermediate lemmas.

## Implications
The results sharpen the geometry of Lp interactions and may inform future functional-analytic bounds. The paper also serves as an example of LLM-assisted mathematical exploration.
