## Summary
This paper presents a scale-adaptive diffusion framework for joint spatiotemporal super-resolution. The same architecture is reused across spatial and temporal factors by decomposing the problem into conditional-mean prediction and residual diffusion, with factor-specific retuning of a few hyperparameters.

## Key Takeaways
- Targets joint upsampling in both space and time, not just one dimension.
- Adds an optional mass-conservation transform for precipitation data.
- Demonstrated across a wide range of spatial and temporal scaling factors.


## Context
The method is motivated by climate applications where different resolutions and cadences are needed.

## Implications
A shared architecture plus light retuning can cover many super-resolution scales.
