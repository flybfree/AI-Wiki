## Summary
Introduces Carbon-Taxed Transformers, a compression pipeline that treats efficiency and carbon cost as first-class constraints for large language models in software engineering tasks. Across code clone detection, summarization, and generation, it reports major memory, latency, and CO2 reductions while largely preserving task accuracy.

## Key Takeaways
- Applies a carbon-pricing metaphor to model compression choices.
- Evaluates encoder-only, encoder-decoder, and decoder-only models.
- Shows large resource savings with modest accuracy loss.

## Context
The work addresses the computational and environmental cost of LLMs for software engineering.

## Implications
The pipeline suggests a practical route to greener, deployment-ready SE models.
