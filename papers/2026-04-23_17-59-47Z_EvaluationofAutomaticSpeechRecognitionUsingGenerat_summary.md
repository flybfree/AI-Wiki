## Summary
The paper evaluates decoder-based generative language models as tools for automatic speech recognition assessment. It studies hypothesis selection, semantic distance, and error classification, finding that LLM judgments align well with humans and offer a more meaning-aware alternative to WER.

## Key Takeaways
- Uses generative LLMs for both pairwise selection and semantic evaluation.
- Reports 92%–94% agreement with human annotators on hypothesis selection.
- Suggests decoder-based LLM embeddings are competitive with encoder-based ones.


## Context
The work explores alternatives to word error rate for ASR evaluation.

## Implications
Generative LLMs may support more interpretable, semantics-aware ASR metrics.
