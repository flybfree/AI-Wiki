## Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of robust dexterous grasping in stochastic environments characterized by contact variability, sensing noise, and external disturbances. Traditional expected-quality objectives fail because they ignore adverse tail outcomes. The authors propose a novel framework that reformulates grasp acquisition as variational inference over latent contact parameters and object pose uncertainty. By representing the belief state using a differentiable Gaussian mixture, they enable direct, gradient-based optimization of risk metrics, specifically Conditional Value
