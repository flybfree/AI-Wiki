## Summary
Temporal taskification is presented as a structural source of instability in streaming continual learning evaluation. By varying how a single stream is split into tasks, the paper shows that benchmark conclusions can shift substantially even when the data, model, and training budget stay fixed.

## Key Takeaways
- Introduces plasticity/stability profiles, profile distance, and Boundary-Profile Sensitivity.
- Demonstrates that different valid temporal splits lead to different outcomes.
- Argues that taskification should be treated as a first-class evaluation choice.


## Context
The paper studies how converting a continuous stream into discrete tasks affects continual-learning results.

## Implications
How a stream is taskified can matter as much as the learner being evaluated.
