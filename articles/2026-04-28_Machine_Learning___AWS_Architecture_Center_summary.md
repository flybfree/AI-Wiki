- **Agentic AI Transformation**: The article emphasizes the shift toward agentic AI, where autonomous agents orchestrate data, code, and tools in real-time. This approach unlocks faster innovation cycles and new application patterns by enabling systems to adapt, optimize, and act autonomously while maintaining strict governance and reliability standards.
