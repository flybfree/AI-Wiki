## Summary  
The paper presents five novel inequalities discovered in collaboration with Grok, each strengthening existing results in combinatorial geometry and additive number theory. These discoveries address problems ranging from geometric measure theory to extremal set systems, offering both theoretical improvements and potential algorithmic applications. The authors verify each inequality through rigorous analysis and computational checks, ensuring reliability for further research.  

## Key Contributions  
- An improved lower bound on the maximal Gaussian perimeter of convex sets in ℝⁿ, tightening the classical isoperimetric estimate.  
- Sharper L₂–L₁ moment comparison inequalities on the Hamming cube {−1,1}ⁿ, providing tighter bounds for probability distributions over binary strings.  
- A strengthened autoconvolution inequality that refines the relationship between a function and its discrete Fourier transform.  
- Improved asymptotic bounds on the size of the largest g‑Sidon sets in {1,…,n}, enhancing results in additive combinatorics.  
- An optimal balanced Szarek's inequality, achieving equality under specific conditions.

These results collectively expand the frontier of known limits.
