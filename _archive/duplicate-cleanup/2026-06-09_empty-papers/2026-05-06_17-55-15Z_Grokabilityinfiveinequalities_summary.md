## Summary
This note reports five mathematical results that were developed in collaboration with Grok and later verified by the authors. The discoveries span Gaussian perimeter bounds, moment comparison inequalities on the Hamming cube, autoconvolution, bounds on g-Sidon sets, and a balanced version of Szarek's inequality. The paper is presented as a compact collection of verified inequality improvements.

## Key Takeaways
- The work spans several distinct problems in analysis and additive combinatorics.
- The results are framed as Grok-assisted discoveries that were subsequently verified.
- Each inequality improves an existing bound or sharpens a known statement.

## Context
The paper is short and note-like rather than a full technical monograph. Its emphasis is on cataloging a set of mathematically confirmed discoveries made with LLM assistance.

## Implications
The note illustrates that LLMs can participate in mathematical exploration while leaving final verification to human authors. It also suggests that model assistance may be useful across multiple unrelated inequality problems.
