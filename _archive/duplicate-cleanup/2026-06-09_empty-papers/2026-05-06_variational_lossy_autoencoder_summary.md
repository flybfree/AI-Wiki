## Summary
This entry points to the Variational Lossy Autoencoder paper, which studies compression-oriented variational modeling with lossy latent representations. The basic idea is to trade reconstruction fidelity for more compact and useful latent structure. It is a foundational reference for thinking about learned compression in generative models.

## Key Takeaways
- The model explicitly balances compression and reconstruction quality.
- Lossy latent spaces can still capture useful generative structure.
- Variational methods provide a principled framework for learned compression.
