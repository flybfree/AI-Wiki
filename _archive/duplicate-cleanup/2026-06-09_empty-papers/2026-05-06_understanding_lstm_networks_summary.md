## Summary
This entry points to Christopher Olah's visual explanation of LSTM networks. The post explains the role of gates, cell state, and recurrent information flow in a way that makes the architecture intuitive. It is primarily an educational resource rather than a research article.

## Key Takeaways
- LSTMs use gates to control memory write, read, and forget operations.
- The cell state provides a path for longer-term information retention.
- Clear visualizations make recurrent dynamics easier to understand.
