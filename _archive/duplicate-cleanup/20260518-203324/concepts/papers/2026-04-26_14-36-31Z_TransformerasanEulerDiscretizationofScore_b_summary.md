## Summary
This paper introduces Score-based Variational Flow (SVFlow), a continuous-time dynamical system designed to provide a principled theoretical foundation for representation learning, addressing the heuristic nature of the Transformer architecture. SVFlow models state evolution using a variational posterior-weighted average of conditional log-likelihood scores. The core contribution is demonstrating that applying forward Euler discretization to spherical SVFlow exactly recovers the standard Transformer structure. This unification explains the underlying dynamics and stability mechanisms of key components like multi-head attention (MHA) and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE).
