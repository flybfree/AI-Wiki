## Summary
This paper introduces a novel framework for morphological discovery in severely low-resource Bantu languages, addressing the scarcity of labeled data. The approach synergistically combines cross-lingual transfer learning with unsupervised clustering to identify grammatical features and word structures. Applied to Giriama (nyf), the method successfully discovers noun class assignments
