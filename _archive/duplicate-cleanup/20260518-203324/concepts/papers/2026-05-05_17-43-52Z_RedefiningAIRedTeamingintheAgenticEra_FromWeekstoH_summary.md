## Summary
This paper presents an agentic AI red-teaming workflow that compresses what is usually a manual, weeks-long process into hours. Built on the open-source Dreadnode SDK, the system lets operators describe goals in natural language while the agent assembles attacks, transforms, scorers, and reports.

## Key Takeaways
- Grounds workflows in 45+ adversarial attacks, 450+ transforms, and 130+ scorers.
- Unifies red teaming for traditional ML adversarial examples and generative AI jailbreaks.
- Demonstrates a case study on Meta Llama Scout with an 85% attack success rate.
- Requires zero human-developed code in the case study.

## Context
The paper responds to the growing need for red teaming in critical AI domains such as healthcare, finance, and defense. It highlights the operational burden of existing library-specific, manual workflow construction.

## Implications
The approach lowers the barrier to safety testing for complex AI systems and may make red teaming more iterative and accessible. It also suggests a shift from tool-building to goal-directed adversarial investigation.
