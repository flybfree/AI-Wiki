## Summary
This paper addresses a critical gap in multimodal affective computing by proposing a novel framework to predict video-induced pleasure. The model moves beyond simple positive emotion classification by integrating cognitive theory—specifically Cognitive Appraisal Theory—to infer underlying appraisal variables that drive pleasurable experiences. By combining advanced transformer architectures with fuzzy modeling, the approach aims to enhance both the accuracy and interpretability of predicting how visual content affects human emotional states.
