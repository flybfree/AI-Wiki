## Summary
This paper proposes a gated multimodal model for predicting building energy performance and analyzing retrofit scenarios. It combines tabular EPC data, assessor text, and GIS-derived spatial features, with learned modality gates that adapt weights per property.

## Key Takeaways
- Predicts SAP energy efficiency and Environmental Impact scores.
- Uses sample-wise gating plus an auxiliary band classification head.
- Achieves strong results in a Westminster, London case study.
- Provides interpretability through gating weights, SHAP, text occlusion, and spatial attribution.

## Context
The work targets scalable decarbonization of residential buildings, where EPCs are useful but limited by on-site inspection requirements. It aims to support city-scale assessment and retrofit planning.

## Implications
The framework may help prioritize retrofit interventions using property-specific evidence instead of one-size-fits-all scoring. It also shows how multimodal modeling can support both prediction and explanation in the built environment.
