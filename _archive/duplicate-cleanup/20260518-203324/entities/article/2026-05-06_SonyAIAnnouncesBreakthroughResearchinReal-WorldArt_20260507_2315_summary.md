The system combines nine ultra‑fast active pixel sensors with reinforcement learning and precision hardware to perceive, decide, and act faster than humans, marking the inaugural case of an AI robot achieving expert‑level performance in a competitive sport.

- **Human‑level reaction and decision making:** The robot makes split‑second choices and executes precise strokes within the same latency envelope as elite players, demonstrating true physical AI speed.  
- **Extension from virtual to physical:** Building on Sony’s Gran Turismo Sophy™ breakthrough in digital racing, Ace pushes artificial intelligence beyond simulated environments into tangible, dynamic spaces.

For decades, AI has dominated virtual domains such as chess and video games, where perception‑planning‑control loops can be optimized without physical constraints. Translating that success to the physical world remains a major hurdle because robots must integrate sensor data, compute actions, and move hardware in real time—challenges compounded by latency, noise, and safety concerns. This research directly addresses those obstacles by marrying advanced sensing with reinforcement learning.
