## Key Trends

### Open Source vs Proprietary
- **Open source models** are gaining market share
- **Self-hosted deployment** is becoming more viable
- **LM Studio** is the preferred tool for local development

### Regulatory Landscape
- **EU AI Act** is shaping global standards
- **US executive orders** are guiding policy
- **China regulations** are tightening

### Investment Patterns
- **AI infrastructure** is seeing heavy investment
- **Edge AI** is growing rapidly
- **Multimodal models** are attracting funding

### Adoption Patterns
- **Enterprise adoption** is accelerating
- **Developer tools** are improving rapidly
- **API pricing** is decreasing
