# Harness Engineering Hub

**Source**: [Meta-Harness: End-to-End Optimization of Model Harnesses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28052) · [Natural-Language Agent Harnesses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25723) · [[concepts/ai-agents/anthropic-building-effective-ai-agents.md|Anthropic: Building Effective AI Agents]] · [[concepts/ai-agents/agents-md.md|AGENTS.md]] · [Rethinking AI Agents: The Rise of Harness Engineering](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xxuxg8PcBvc)

## Summary
Harness engineering is the idea that the control layer around the model can matter as much as the model weights themselves. The harness decides what information gets stored, retrieved, presented, verified, and allowed to execute. Recent work points in two directions at once: make harness policy more explicit and inspectable, and automatically optimize harness code from prior traces and execution history.

## What the sources add
- [[concepts/ai-agents/anthropic-building-effective-ai-agents.md|Anthropic: Building Effective AI Agents]] argues that the most successful agent implementations use simple, composable patterns instead of heavyweight frameworks.
- [[concepts/ai-agents/agents-md.md|AGENTS.md]] shows the project-level version of the same idea: a predictable place for agents to read build steps, tests, and conventions.
- The YouTube survey frames harness engineering as a craft of subtraction: better agents often come from removing unnecessary structure, not adding more.
