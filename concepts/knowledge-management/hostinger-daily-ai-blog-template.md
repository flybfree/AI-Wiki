# Hostinger Daily AI Intelligence Blog Template

**Source**: [AI Research Wiki](https://github.com/flybfree/AI-Wiki/wiki)

Use this template to turn the daily AI intelligence briefing into a Hostinger blog post.
Keep the prose public-facing, but keep the source links visible in the body.

## Recommended post fields

- **Title**: `Daily AI Intelligence Briefing — YYYY-MM-DD`
- **Slug**: `daily-ai-intelligence-YYYY-MM-DD`
- **Category**: `AI Intelligence`
- **Excerpt**: 1-2 sentence summary for the blog index
- **Featured image**: optional, but useful for the blog card
- **SEO title**: short and readable
- **Meta description**: 150-160 characters, plain-English

## Copy-paste template

```markdown
# Daily AI Intelligence Briefing — YYYY-MM-DD

**Excerpt**: One to two sentences that capture the main signal of the day.

**Source**: [AI Research Wiki](https://github.com/flybfree/AI-Wiki/wiki)

[Optional featured image caption or intro note]
```

## Writing rules

- Lead with the strongest signal, not the longest list.
- Keep sections short enough to scan on mobile.
- Link source titles inline where the claim appears.
- Put the source block at the bottom even if links already appear in the body.
- Prefer one post per day instead of mixing days together.
- Rewrite the wiki briefing for a public audience; do not paste raw internal notes.
