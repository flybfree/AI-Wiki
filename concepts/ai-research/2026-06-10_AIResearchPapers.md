## Key Papers

### Qwen-AgentWorld
- **Title**: "Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents"
- **arXiv**: [2606.24597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24597)
- **Key Contribution**: First language world models for agentic environment simulation across 7 domains
- **Implications**: Useful as a reusable simulator and warm-up for downstream agentic RL

### Llama 4 Scout Architecture
- **Title**: "Llama 4 Scout: A 256B Sparse MoE Model"
- **Authors**: Meta AI
- **Key Contribution**: Efficient sparse MoE architecture
- **Implications**: Strong performer for self-hosted agent loops

### DiffusionGemma
- **Title**: "DiffusionGemma: 26B MoE Model with Discrete Diffusion"
- **Authors**: Google DeepMind
- **Key Contribution**: Block-autoregressive text generation
- **Implications**: 4x faster inference, good for agent loops

### SmolAgents
- **Title**: "SmolAgents: Code-First Agents from Hugging Face"
- **Authors**: Hugging Face
- **Key Contribution**: CodeAgent vs ToolCallingAgent architecture
- **Implications**: Simple, effective agent framework

## Research Trends

- **Sparse MoE** architectures are dominating
- **Discrete diffusion** is emerging for text
- **Code-first agents** are gaining traction
- **Self-hosted deployment** is becoming mainstream
