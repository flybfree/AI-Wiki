## Harness-1

**Last Updated**: 2026-06-10

**Description**: A 20B search agent trained with reinforcement learning inside a stateful retrieval harness that externalizes state management from the policy to the environment. Maintains recoverable search state and makes semantic decisions about what to search, which documents to inspect or curate, what claims to verify, and when the evidence is sufficient.

## Core Idea

**State-externalizing harness**: Instead of training a search agent as a policy over growing transcripts (where the model must handle both semantic decisions AND bookkeeping), Harness-1 splits the responsibilities:

| Component | Responsibility |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Harness (environment)** | Maintains state: candidate pool, curated set, evidence links, verification records, compressed observations, budget-aware context |
| **Policy (20B model)** | Makes semantic decisions: what to search, which docs to keep/discard, what to verify, when to stop |

The argument: reinforcement learning is forced to optimize both semantic search decisions and recoverable bookkeeping when state lives in the transcript. Externalizing state to the environment lets the policy focus on semantics.
