## Architecture

Harness-1 operates through a **stateful retrieval harness** that maintains:

1. **Candidate documents** — retrieved during search trajectory
2. **Curated evidence** — selected documents relevant to the query
3. **Evidence links** — connections between claims and supporting documents
4. **Verification records** — tracking which claims have been verified
5. **Budget-aware context** — managing token/search budget limits

The **policy** (the 20B model) makes semantic decisions at each step:

- What to search next
- Which documents to inspect or curate
- What claims to verify
- When the evidence is sufficient to answer

This is a **long-horizon agent** — it doesn't just retrieve once and answer, but iteratively builds evidence through multiple search steps.

## Evaluation Metrics

- **recall** — recall of evidence documents in the final curated set
- **final_answer_recall** — recall over evidence tied to the final answer
- **trajectory_recall** — evidence recall anywhere in the search trajectory
- **precision** — precision of the final curated set
