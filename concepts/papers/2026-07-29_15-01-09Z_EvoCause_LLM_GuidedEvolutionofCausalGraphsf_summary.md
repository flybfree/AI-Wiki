## Summary  
The paper presents EvoCause, a framework that evolves causal graphs for root‑cause analysis by leveraging large language model (LLM) guidance while respecting expert constraints. It introduces TeleRCA, an expert‑annotated benchmark of 485 681 alarm events across 194 types and 5 621 resources. EvoCause refines the graph generated by PC causal discovery using LLM‑proposed edits that are validated for node identity and acyclicity, producing a final graph that can be used directly for predictions without further LLM calls. The approach aims to close the loop between expert diagnoses and algorithmic learning in telecommunication fault cascades.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] EvoCause improves root‑cause identification by iteratively evolving causal graphs with LLM‑guided edits that respect expert‑provided source constraints.  
- [Finding 2] On synthetic data, the evolved graph outperforms the baseline PC algorithm: Node F1 rises to +11.59 %, Case EM improves by +9.40 %, Graph F1 increases by +4.59 %, while nSHD drops by 0.2379.  
- [Finding 3] Alarm‑name information is essential for graph refinement; anonymizing titles reduces Node F1 by 6.12 % and Case EM by 8.04 % on the TeleRCA benchmark.

## Methodology  
EvoCause starts with a PC causal discovery output that may contain incorrect source nodes. Expert labels constrain which alarms should be considered sources but do not specify edge changes. An LLM is prompted to propose semantically plausible graph edits (adding or removing edges) that satisfy those constraints. A deterministic validation script checks node identities and acyclicity, retaining the best‑aligned graph on a labeled set. At inference time only the final graph is used for prediction.

## Results  
The synthetic experiments show substantial gains: Node F1 +11.59 %, Case EM +9.40 %, Graph F1 +4.59 % versus PC alone, and nSHD reduced by 0.2379. On the real TeleRCA dataset, replacing alarm titles with anonymous identifiers lowers Node F1 by 6.12 % and Case EM by 8.04 %, confirming that human‑readable names aid graph refinement.

## Significance  
EvoCause bridges expert knowledge and automated causal discovery, delivering transparent, accurate root‑cause predictions without requiring costly LLM calls at runtime. By quantifying the impact of alarm metadata, it highlights a critical data source for improving telecommunication fault analysis and could be applied to other domains where labeled causal graphs are needed.
