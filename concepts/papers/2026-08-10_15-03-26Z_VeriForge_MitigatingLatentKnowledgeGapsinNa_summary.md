## Summary  
VeriForge is a mixed‑initiative writing system designed to expose latent knowledge gaps that authors encounter while drafting fictional narratives, thereby enabling richer, more authentic prose without imposing the author’s explicit queries. By integrating proactive highlighting, dual‑stream querying with source‑anchored Knowledge Cards, and a spatial Knowledge Canvas supported by graph‑based retrieval‑augmented generation, VeriForge lets the AI assume initiative over domain discovery while preserving the writer’s control over narrative synthesis. The system is evaluated in a controlled cold‑start writing task with twelve participants, showing measurable improvements in gap detection, creative exploration, and perceived domain grounding.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] Mixed‑initiative scaffolding reveals previously overlooked knowledge gaps that authors cannot articulate.  
- [Finding 2] Proactive inline highlighting paired with dual‑stream querying and Knowledge Cards enables direct fact extraction from source material.  
- [Finding 3] The spatial Knowledge Canvas, combined with graph‑based retrieval‑augmented generation, organizes discovered knowledge into a coherent narrative scaffold.

## Methodology  
The authors grounded their approach in nine semi‑structured interviews with fiction writers to identify pain points in AI‑assisted drafting. They then designed VeriForge as a system that divides cognitive labor: the AI handles domain discovery through graph‑based retrieval, while the author retains initiative over narrative synthesis. The three mechanisms—proactive highlighting, dual‑stream querying/Knowledge Cards, and spatial Knowledge Canvas—are implemented within a unified pipeline that leverages domain‑specific source graphs.

## Results  
A within‑subjects user study (N = 12) demonstrated that participants recognized more knowledge gaps than control groups, engaged in deeper creative exploration, and produced passages rated higher by expert raters for domain grounding. The cold‑start writing task showed a statistically significant increase in the proportion of sentences containing accurate, source‑anchored details compared to baseline AI assistance.

## Significance  
VeriForge addresses a longstanding limitation of current AI writing tools: they either require explicit queries or produce homogeneous output that lacks authentic voice. By surfacing hidden knowledge gaps and preserving author agency, VeriForge enhances narrative authenticity and encourages richer world‑building without sacrificing creative control.
