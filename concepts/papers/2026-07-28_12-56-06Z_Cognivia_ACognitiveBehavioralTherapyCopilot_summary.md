## Summary  
The paper introduces **Cognivia**, an artificial‑intelligence therapist that automatically detects cognitive distortions and generates rational, evidence‑based CBT responses. It bridges the gap between large language models (LLMs) and mental‑health care by combining expertly curated CBT texts with structured prompting and fine‑tuning. The framework includes a hierarchical evaluation system validated through both automated metrics and human experts. This work offers scalable support that can complement or replace limited therapist availability, especially for early cognitive‑distortion interventions.

## Key Contributions  
- Cognivia integrates automatic cognitive distortion detection with rational response generation using authoritative CBT literature.  
- It introduces a hierarchical quality evaluation framework that assesses LLM outputs via lexical metrics, complementary LLM judges, and expert ratings.  
- The method demonstrates superior performance over baseline approaches in both recognition accuracy and response‑generation quality.

## Methodology  
The authors assembled an augmented dataset comprising core CBT reference texts and mental‑health question‑answer pairs. They employed multi‑stage prompting and structured generation strategies guided by behavioral‑science experts to fine‑tune a lightweight LLM, yielding Cognivia. Evaluation combined lexical metrics, two complementary LLM judges, and human evaluation from ten behavioral science specialists.

## Results  
Cognivia achieved an 84 % accuracy in identifying cognitive distortions versus 67 % for baselines (e.g., standard LLMs). Its rational responses scored a mean hierarchical rating of 0.82 compared to 0.65 for the baseline, and nine out of ten human experts rated its clinical relevance as high. These results confirm that Cognivia outperforms existing methods across all evaluation criteria.

## Significance  
By delivering evidence‑based CBT support at scale, Cognivia can alleviate therapist shortages, improve early intervention for cognitive distortions, and enhance accessibility to mental‑health care without compromising therapeutic quality.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), cognitive distortions, large language models (LLMs), hierarchical evaluation framework, evidence‑based AI therapy, fine‑tuning of LLMs on domain‑specific data.
