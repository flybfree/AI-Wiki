## Summary  
The paper proposes an AI‑assisted script management system for requirements elicitation interviews, aiming to integrate real‑time topic tracking with adaptive question generation. It evaluates this workflow against a training‑only approach using a quasi‑experimental design. The goal is to show how AI scaffolding changes interview dynamics and generated artifacts. The contribution is empirical evidence that AI‑assisted scripts improve coverage efficiency and depth; the study also highlights the practical implications for interview design.

## Key Contributions  
- Finding 1: AI‑generated scripts scored higher (92.8 vs 74.8) on a best‑practice rubric.  
- Finding 2: AI‑assisted interviews covered fewer topics (9.6 vs 14.5) but asked more follow‑ups per topic (3.43 vs 1.15).  
- Finding 3: Participants rated topic tracking as the most useful feature (86% agreement).

## Methodology  
The authors designed a between‑subjects quasi‑experimental study with two conditions: no‑training AI‑assisted and training AI‑unassisted. Interviewers were randomly assigned to conditions and recorded their scripts for analysis. The script generation combined theory‑grounded business goals with live coverage monitoring, while follow‑up questions were generated on demand using an AI model.

## Results  
The AI‑assisted condition produced more refined goal models (lowest‑level goal fraction 0.653 vs 0.598). It also increased the proportion of scripted questions (86% vs 69%) and follow‑up rate per topic. These differences suggest that AI can streamline the elicitation process while preserving quality.

## Significance  
These findings demonstrate that AI can act as a scaffold, altering interview trajectories toward more focused coverage without sacrificing depth, offering a template for future AI‑enhanced elicitation research.
