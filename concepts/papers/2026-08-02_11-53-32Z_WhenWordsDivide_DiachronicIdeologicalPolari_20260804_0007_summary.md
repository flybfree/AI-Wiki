## Summary  
The paper investigates how ideological polarization evolves over time in political discourse on Reddit, moving beyond single‑point cross‑sectional studies that focus only on affective sentiment. By constructing temporally aligned word embeddings for opposing communities, the authors quantify the semantic divergence between political concepts across the study period. Their longitudinal analysis reveals a measurable increase in both concept‑level and topic‑level polarization, offering a novel computational framework for tracking ideological change over time.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] Ideological polarization has increased substantially during the study period at both the concept‑ and topic‑level.  
- [Finding 2] The authors capture the evolution of ideological differences in semantic framing rather than only affective intensity at a single moment.  
- [Finding 3] Their method provides a scalable, reproducible framework for studying temporal dynamics of polarization in large‑scale social media discourse.

## Methodology  
The researchers gather Reddit posts from two opposing political communities and generate community‑specific word embeddings that are aligned to the same time windows. They then compute the semantic distance between corresponding political concepts across these embeddings, treating each passage as a vector representation of its ideological stance. By aggregating these distances over time, they obtain a continuous measure of polarization growth.

## Results  
The experimental results show a clear upward trend in the average semantic divergence metric from early to late 2025, indicating that opposing communities are using increasingly distinct language to express their political ideas. The increase is observed not only for isolated concepts but also for broader topics, confirming that polarization is both fine‑grained and topic‑wide.

## Significance  
This work matters because it shifts the focus from static snapshots of online conflict to a dynamic process that can be measured quantitatively. By providing a scalable method, researchers can monitor how ideological gaps widen or narrow as social media environments evolve, informing policy, platform design, and public discourse strategies.
