## Summary  
The paper introduces **MMArch**, a benchmark designed to test multimodal reasoning in architecture and civil engineering by requiring models to integrate multiple figures with domain‑specific principles. Its goal is to move beyond simple image recognition tasks toward genuine evidence‑based inference, which is essential for real‑world engineering problems. The authors claim that MMArch reveals the current limits of large language models (LLMs) when they must combine distributed visual evidence and apply architectural rules. By comparing model performance against human experts, the study demonstrates a substantial gap between automated systems and expert judgment.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] MMArch spans ten engineering subdomains and contains 1 212 short‑answer items generated by a decoupled planner‑writer pipeline, each demanding evidence from multiple figures.  
- [Finding 2] Human experts achieve an average accuracy of 95 %, whereas the strongest open‑source MLLM reaches about 30 % and the best proprietary system only 52 %.  
- [Finding 3] Error analysis shows that failures concentrate on applying principles and combining evidence across figures rather than locating it, indicating room for improvement in reasoning integration.

## Methodology  
The authors constructed MMArch entirely from figures sourced from peer‑reviewed architecture and civil engineering papers. A two‑stage pipeline first produces the visual evidence (planner) and then generates a concise short‑answer item (writer). The dataset is screened automatically, subjected to a blind adversarial audit, and finally validated by domain experts to ensure that no textual shortcuts are possible; answering must rely solely on perceiving relevant evidence, identifying governing principles, and applying them.

## Results  
An evaluation of 18 open‑weight and proprietary MLLMs against the MMArch panel yields the reported accuracies: the top open‑source model scores ~30 %, while the leading proprietary system reaches 52 %. This places both models well below human performance, highlighting a significant performance gap that persists across different model families.

## Significance  
MMArch provides a rigorous benchmark that quantifies how far current multimodal reasoning systems can advance in engineering contexts. By exposing systematic weaknesses in principle application and evidence synthesis, the study motivates future research into better integration of visual data with domain knowledge, ultimately aiming to close the gap between automated models and expert judgment.
