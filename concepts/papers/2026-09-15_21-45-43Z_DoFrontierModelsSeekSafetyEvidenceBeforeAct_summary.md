## Finding
The SAFE benchmark tests whether frontier models acquire safety-relevant evidence before acting. Across GPT-5.5, o3, Claude Opus 4.8, and Claude Sonnet 4.6, inspection varies by model and responds strongly to severity and retrieval cost, while stated probability has a weaker effect. The authors also report a mismatch between decisions and stated rationales: evidence framing can change borderline decisions without being acknowledged.
