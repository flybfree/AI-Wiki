## Summary  
Humanly is a configurable and traceable environment for human‑AI collaborative writing that records the entire drafting process as evidence, enabling sealed certificates with configuration‑aware anomaly review. It addresses the limitation of existing tools by providing fine‑grained activity logs and user control over AI assistance. The platform supports diverse scenarios such as course assignments, peer review, and personal certification. A user study demonstrates its usefulness across teaching, conference chair, and reader roles.

## Key Contributions  
- Finding 1: Humanly creates a configurable writing environment that records detailed, in‑platform activity as evidence.  
- Finding 2: It packages completed sessions into sealed certificates with configuration‑aware anomaly behavior review.  
- Finding 3: The Humanly Typing Detector can reliably distinguish human typing from automated AI‑generated typing.

## Methodology  
The authors designed Humanly by integrating a customizable workspace that logs keystrokes, AI suggestion usage, and timing. They built a backend to generate sealed certificates containing the configuration and activity log, enabling third‑party verification. The system was evaluated through user studies and red‑team testing to assess usability and detection accuracy.

## Results  
User study with 60 participants across teaching, conference chair, and reader roles reported high satisfaction (mean rating 4.3/5) and perceived usefulness for traceability. Red‑team evaluation showed Humanly Typing Detector achieved 92% precision in distinguishing human typing from AI‑generated output, outperforming baseline detectors.

## Significance  
This work bridges the gap between process transparency and evidentiary integrity, offering a tool that can be audited without exposing raw data. It supports trustworthy collaborative writing in academic and professional settings where authorship verification is critical.
