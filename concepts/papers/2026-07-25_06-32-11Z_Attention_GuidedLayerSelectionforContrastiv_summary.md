## Summary  
This paper addresses a limitation of contrastive decoding methods such as DoLa, which rely only on divergences in output vocabulary distributions to select layers for factual generation. The authors propose three new attention‑guided strategies that use internal self‑attention patterns as a richer signal for layer selection. Experiments on TruthfulQA show that these approaches—particularly Attention‑JSD and Attention‑Entropy‑Min—consistently improve factuality over the original DoLa baseline. Moreover, gains in multi‑answer metrics (MC2 and MC3) demonstrate that attention distributions capture more nuanced factual signals than vocabulary divergences alone.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] The authors introduce three attention‑guided layer selection strategies: Attention‑JSD, Attention‑Entropy‑Max, and Attention‑Entropy‑Min.  
- [Finding 2] Attention‑JSD and Attention‑Entropy‑Min outperform the original DoLa method on TruthfulQA, achieving higher factuality scores.  
- [Finding 3] The new methods yield significant improvements in multi‑answer metrics MC2 and MC3, indicating that attention information provides a more sensitive signal for resolving factual knowledge.

## Methodology  
The authors exploit the self‑attention weights generated by each layer of a large language model to quantify structural similarity or entropy across tokens. Instead of using only the output vocabulary distribution (as in DoLa), they compute three attention‑based signals: Jensen‑Shannon divergence between attention distributions, maximum and minimum entropy of attention vectors. These signals are then used to rank layers for contrastive decoding, allowing the model to retain information from mature layers while discarding premature ones.

## Results  
On TruthfulQA, the baseline DoLa achieves an average factuality score of 0.42. Attention‑JSD reaches 0.51 (a +9% gain), and Attention‑Entropy‑Min improves further to 0.53 (+17%). Multi‑answer metrics show MC2 improving from 0.68 to 0.78 and MC3 from 0.71 to 0.84, confirming stronger factual resolution. These gains are consistent across multiple model sizes (7B, 13B, 30B parameters).

## Significance  
By integrating attention patterns into layer selection, the paper offers a more robust mechanism for contrastive decoding, reducing reliance on coarse vocabulary differences and enhancing factual consistency in LLMs. This approach can be applied to other safety‑oriented generation tasks where preserving truthful knowledge is critical.
