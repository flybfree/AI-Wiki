## Summary  
The paper reviews how Multi‑Agent Systems can be integrated with digital twins to enable predictive maintenance in resource‑constrained industrial settings, aiming to create an integrated embedded‑distributed hierarchical architecture for Industry 5.0. It synthesizes over 547 high‑impact papers to identify existing hybrid architectures, pinpoint technological bottlenecks, and formulate three open research questions. The contribution is a taxonomy of these solutions and a set of challenges that remain unsolved.  

## Key Contributions  
- A comprehensive taxonomy of hybrid Multi‑Agent System–Digital Twin architectures for predictive maintenance.  
- Identification of three persistent technological bottlenecks: AI deployment on microcontrollers, lightweight distributed coordination, and hierarchical orchestration with explainable AI.  
- Formulation of three open research questions addressing these bottlenecks within an Industry 5.0 framework.  

## Methodology  
The authors conducted a systematic literature review of 547 peer‑reviewed papers from IEEE Transactions, Nature, Elsevier, and MDPI, extracting architecture descriptions, technology stacks, and performance metrics to construct the taxonomy and pinpoint gaps.  

## Results  
The analysis reveals that no current system integrates embedded AI inference, ultra‑lightweight communication, and hierarchical control with residual life estimation and explainability. The identified bottlenecks are confirmed across domains, highlighting a lack of holistic solutions.  

## Significance  
This work clarifies the state of the art in Multi‑Agent driven digital twins for predictive maintenance, guiding future research toward scalable, low‑power, and explainable systems that enable autonomous smart factories.  

## Related Concepts  
- Digital Twin: real‑time virtual replica of physical assets.  
- Multi‑Agent System (MAS): collection of autonomous agents communicating to achieve common goals.  
- Predictive Maintenance: forecasting equipment failures using data analytics.  
- Industry 5.0: human‑centric, sustainable manufacturing with AI integration.  
- Explainable AI (XAI): providing interpretable model outputs.  
- Microcontroller constraints: limited compute and memory in embedded devices.
