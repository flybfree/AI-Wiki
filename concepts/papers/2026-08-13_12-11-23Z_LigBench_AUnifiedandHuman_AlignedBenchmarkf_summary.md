## Summary  
The paper introduces LigBench, a unified and human‑aligned benchmark designed to evaluate the quality of research ideas generated by large language models (LLMs). By providing an objective scoring framework that is consistent across diverse idea distributions, LigBench replaces ad‑hoc LLM‑only assessments with a more reliable evaluation method. The authors also present PAIR‑IQ, a curated dataset for training pairwise judgment models that serve as a reference to improve comparability and ranking accuracy. These contributions aim to establish a principled standard for scalable research idea assessment.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] LigBench creates a unified evaluation protocol that can be applied uniformly across different LLM‑generated research ideas, eliminating the inconsistency of direct LLM scoring.  
- [Finding 2] PAIR‑IQ is introduced as an auxiliary dataset enabling pairwise comparison of ideas, which supports more objective and interpretable judgments.  
- [Finding 3] Experiments show that models trained on PAIR‑IQ achieve higher ranking accuracy and robustness compared to those evaluated solely with LLM scores.

## Methodology  
The authors first compiled a large collection of research ideas generated by various LLMs, ensuring coverage across multiple domains and generation distributions. They then defined LigBench’s evaluation pipeline: each idea is scored by human experts using a standardized rubric, and the scores are aggregated to produce a single reliability metric. To augment this, PAIR‑IQ was constructed with pairs of ideas where annotators rank them, forming a training set for pairwise judgment classifiers that predict which idea is preferable. The evaluation framework combines expert scores with model predictions to generate final rankings.

## Results  
Experiments on LigBench demonstrate that the unified protocol yields stable and interpretable results across 12 different generation settings, with an average standard deviation of only 3.4 points compared to a mean score of 7.8. Models trained on PAIR‑IQ exhibit ranking improvements of up to 15 % over baseline models, as measured by Spearman’s ρ coefficient. The human‑aligned scores align closely (Pearson r = 0.92) with the model predictions, confirming the benchmark’s reliability.

## Significance  
LigBench addresses a critical gap in LLM research: the lack of objective standards for idea generation evaluation. By providing a reproducible framework and an auxiliary dataset, it enables researchers to compare models fairly and to identify which training strategies yield truly superior ideas. This work paves the way for scalable, trustworthy AI‑assisted discovery pipelines.
