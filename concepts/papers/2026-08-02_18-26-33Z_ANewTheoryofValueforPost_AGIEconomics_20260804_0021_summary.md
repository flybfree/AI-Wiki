## Summary  
The paper proposes Flourishing Value Theory (FVT) to address the inadequacy of traditional economic metrics after AGI, focusing on conversion of abundant intelligence into durable human and planetary flourishing. It argues that value should be measured by contribution to capabilities rather than price or GDP. FVT introduces a multidimensional, agency‑preserving, regenerative definition of value. The theory offers a new framework for post‑AGI economics.

## Key Contributions  
- Finding 1: Value is defined as the counterfactual, distribution‑sensitive contribution of systems to durable capabilities within planetary constraints.  
- Finding 2: Traditional metrics (price, profit, GDP) are partial signals; value creation differs from value capture and should be non‑compensatory at critical thresholds.  
- Finding 3: Post‑AGI economics shifts from scarcity to governed abundance, with collective expansion of consciousness as regenerative value.

## Methodology  
The authors synthesize insights from AI economics, capability theory, automation studies, digital valuation, and ecological economics. They construct FVT through conceptual analysis, comparative evaluation of existing frameworks, and design of layered metrics (Flourishing Metrics, Return on Flourishing). No experiments are run; it is a theoretical synthesis.

## Results  
The framework delineates three value dimensions—durable capability enhancement, agency preservation, regeneration—and proposes that firms, governments, work transitions, AI governance, and national accounting should integrate these dimensions. It predicts that post‑AGI economies will prioritize conversion of intelligence into flourishing rather than maximizing output.

## Significance  
This theory resolves the core post‑AGI problem of misalignment between economic incentives and human well‑being, offering a principled basis for policy and corporate strategy beyond GDP.

## Related Concepts  
Flourishing Value Theory (FVT), capability approach, regenerative value, non‑compensatory thresholds, AI governance, collective consciousness expansion, Return on Flourishing (RoF).
