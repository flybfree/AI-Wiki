## Summary  
The paper introduces **CyberAGENTS**, a framework that provides structured autonomy for AI agents in gamified cybersecurity education, ensuring that generative outputs are pedagogically sound and safe. It combines ontology‑driven validation, schema‑governed behavioral control, and competency‑based progression to balance adaptive flexibility with pedagogical alignment. The system deploys four specialized agents—challenge, support, evaluation, and reward—each governed by predefined schemas while a cybersecurity ontology validates all generated content before it is shown to learners.

## Key Contributions  
- Presents CyberAGENTS as an integrated framework that merges structured autonomy with generative AI for gamified cybersecurity learning.  
- Introduces an ontology‑driven validation pipeline and schema‑governed agent behavior that explicitly bounds autonomous actions without eliminating flexibility.  
- Demonstrates, through classroom trials, improved student engagement, clearer feedback interpretation, and higher learner trust compared to an unconstrained baseline.

## Methodology  
The authors built CyberAGENTS around a competency‑based progression model that orders topics by difficulty and prerequisite relationships, reflecting evidence‑based scaffolding. The learning loop is decomposed into four agents, each equipped with behavioral schemas that dictate operational modes and progression logic. Before any AI response is displayed, it passes through a cybersecurity ontology to enforce domain consistency and safety constraints, thereby grounding the system in learning science.

## Results  
In a controlled classroom experiment with undergraduate students, CyberAGENTS yielded significantly higher engagement scores (p < 0.05), more accurate feedback interpretation, and increased confidence in AI‑generated answers than the unconstrained control group. Expert evaluations confirmed that structured schemas reduced hallucinations and aligned outputs with pedagogical objectives, reinforcing the value of ontology validation.

## Significance  
By grounding generative agents in learning science through ontology validation and schema governance, CyberAGENTS offers a scalable blueprint for safe, effective gamified cybersecurity education. It mitigates risks such as inconsistent or unsafe AI behavior while preserving adaptive flexibility, paving the way for future pedagogically grounded agentic systems.
