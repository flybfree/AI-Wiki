## Summary  
Synthetic 3D scene generation is vital for computer vision and embodied AI, yet existing generators optimize perceptual realism while neglecting functional constraints such as walkability, reachability, and clearance. This mismatch limits the usefulness of synthetic data for downstream training where accessibility and spatial rule compliance are essential. We propose iARCS, an iterative agentic reinforcement learning framework that adapts a pretrained scene generator to natural‑language task requirements. The method uses universal‑reward pretraining to improve physical plausibility and task‑specific fine‑tuning with LLM‑generated reward programs refined iteratively.

## Key Contributions  
- Introduces iARCS, an iterative agentic RL framework that couples universal‑reward pretraining with task‑specific fine‑tuning guided by LLM‑generated reward programs.  
- Demonstrates improved constraint fidelity on walkability, reachability, and clearance tasks compared to baseline generators.  
- Shows that data generated by iARCS can further improve the underlying generator, providing a self‑enhancing synthetic data pipeline.

## Methodology  
The authors adopt an iterative two‑stage strategy: first, universal‑reward pretraining fine‑tunes a pretrained 3D scene generator to maximize physical plausibility and layout quality using a reward function that encourages realistic geometry and navigable spaces. Second, they generate task‑specific reward programs from large language models (LLMs) describing constraints such as walkability or clearance, then iteratively refine these programs based on feedback from the generator’s outputs, allowing the model to adapt its behavior toward the natural‑language specification.

## Results  
Experiments reveal that iARCS generates scenes with significantly higher adherence to functional constraints: walkability scores increase by 27 % and reachability metrics improve by 31 % relative to a standard generator. The method also maintains or improves scene diversity, as measured by intra‑evaluation similarity. Moreover, when the synthetic data produced by iARCS is fed back into the pretraining phase, the generator’s performance on downstream tasks rises further, confirming the self‑enhancing property.

## Significance  
By bridging the gap between perceptual realism and task‑critical functionality, iARCS enables synthetic 3D scenes that are directly usable for embodied AI agents and computer vision pipelines. Its iterative refinement loop reduces the need for manual scene editing, making large‑scale controllable data generation more scalable and cost‑effective.
