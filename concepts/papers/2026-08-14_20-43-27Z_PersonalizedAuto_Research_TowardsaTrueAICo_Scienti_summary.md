# Summary: Personalized Auto-Research: Towards a True AI Co-Scientist

**Original paper:** [arXiv: 2608.14881](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14881v1)

## Summary
Personalized auto-research conditions each stage of AI-driven scientific work on the context of an individual researcher. The graph-grounded framework spans retrieval, hypothesis generation, experimentation, writing, and review, arguing that novelty, value, and feasibility are researcher-specific.

## Why it matters
A co-scientist that produces the same research agenda for every user is not genuinely collaborative. Personal context and tacit knowledge become first-class inputs to automated research systems.
