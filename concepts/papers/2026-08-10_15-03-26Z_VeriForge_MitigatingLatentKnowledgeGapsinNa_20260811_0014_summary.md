## Summary  
VeriForge is a mixed‑initiative writing system designed to uncover latent knowledge gaps that authors encounter while drafting fiction, thereby improving the verisimilitude of their narratives. The system proactively flags missing information, extracts facts from domain‑specific Knowledge Cards, and lets writers organize these insights on a spatial Knowledge Canvas before generating prose. By separating domain discovery (taken over by the AI) from narrative synthesis (retained by the author), VeriForge preserves creative agency while enhancing factual grounding. The approach is grounded in formative interviews with fiction writers and supported by a graph‑based retrieval‑augmented generation pipeline.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] A mixed‑initiative framework that combines proactive inline highlighting, dual‑stream querying with source‑anchored Knowledge Cards, and a spatial Knowledge Canvas to surface and organize missing domain facts.  
- [Finding 2] A graph‑based retrieval‑augmented generation pipeline that pulls information from curated domain sources to power the system’s suggestions.  
- [Finding 3] Within‑subjects user study (N=12) shows that VeriForge helps authors recognize previously overlooked knowledge gaps, supports creative exploration, and produces passages with stronger domain grounding in a cold‑start writing task.

## Methodology  
The authors approached the problem through three complementary mechanisms. First, proactive inline highlighting flags potential knowledge gaps as the author drafts. Second, dual‑stream querying pairs conversational responses with source‑anchored Knowledge Cards to extract facts directly from the AI’s knowledge base. Third, a spatial Knowledge Canvas lets the author visually organize and connect discovered knowledge across the narrative. All mechanisms are powered by a graph‑based retrieval‑augmented generation pipeline that leverages domain‑specific source materials.

## Results  
A within‑subjects user study with twelve fiction writers demonstrated that VeriForge’s paradigm enables authors to notice previously overlooked gaps, encourages creative exploration of unfamiliar facts, and is judged by expert raters to generate passages with stronger domain grounding in a controlled cold‑start writing task. The results indicate measurable improvements in both awareness and quality of narrative content.

## Significance  
This work matters because it addresses a core limitation of current AI writing tools: they either require explicit queries that authors cannot formulate or produce prose that homogenizes voice. By revealing hidden knowledge gaps while keeping the author fully in control, VeriForge fosters richer, more authentic narratives and opens a path toward truly collaborative human‑AI writing.
