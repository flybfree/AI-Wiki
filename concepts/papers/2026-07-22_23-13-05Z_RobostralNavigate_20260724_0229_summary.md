## Summary  
[Robostral Navigate is an 8B vision‑language model designed to enable navigation across diverse robotic platforms using only monocular RGB images, thereby minimizing hardware assumptions and deployment cost. It predicts waypoints by pointing to the next location within the current camera view, operating entirely in image space. The approach removes reliance on depth sensors or pre‑built maps, making it scalable for wheeled, legged, and aerial robots without recalibration. Training is accelerated using a prefix‑caching recipe that packs entire episodes into single sequences.]  

## Key Contributions  
- [The model operates purely in image space, allowing natural robustness to camera intrinsics and scene scale changes across different robot bodies.]  
- [A tree‑based attention mask is used during training to condition predictions only on the current visual input, preventing reliance on previous ground‑truth actions.]  
- [Prefix‑caching reduces training tokens by 22× and cuts total training time from months to days.]  

## Methodology  
[The authors trained Robostral Navigate on 2.4 million trajectories generated across 350k simulated scenes, using reinforcement learning to refine exploration and recovery while the tree mask encourages visually grounded action prediction.]  

## Results  
[On the Room‑to‑Room Continuous Environments (R2R-CE) benchmark, Robostral Navigate achieves a 77.4% success rate, surpassing monocular baselines by 10.5 points and outperforming depth‑ or multi‑camera methods by 5.3 points; on the Room‑Across‑Room Continuous Environments (RxR-CE) it reaches 75.1%, setting a new state of the art.]  

## Significance  
[This work demonstrates that high‑performance navigation can be achieved with minimal sensor hardware, reducing deployment cost and enabling broader robot adoption across heterogeneous platforms.]  

## Related Concepts  
[Vision‑language models, monocular perception, prefix caching, tree attention masks, reinforcement learning for navigation, and scalable simulation pipelines.]
