## Summary  
The paper proposes Holonic Digital Twins (HDT‑Nets) that transform passive AI mirrors into active agents capable of real‑time physical intelligence over wireless networks. By enabling hierarchical reasoning and coordinated learning among networked agents, HDT‑Nets overcome the limitations of conventional deep learning in dynamic environments. The framework integrates causal Markov blankets, category theory, and integrated information theory to manage belief transmission and collective cognition. This work bridges AI inference with physical systems under uncertainty.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] The introduction of holonic digital twins that act as active reasoning agents rather than passive mirrors.  
- [Finding 2] A hierarchical network architecture using causal Markov blankets to coordinate multi‑domain interventions and enable counterfactual reasoning.  
- [Finding 3] Theoretical integration of category theory for semantic preservation across heterogeneous representations.

## Methodology  
The authors model each physical agent as a holonic digital twin embedded within a wireless edge, where local perception, action, and learning are unified via active inference that minimizes expected free energy. They define causal Markov blankets to determine which beliefs must be transmitted between agents, using category theory to map these beliefs into a common semantic space. Integrated information theory quantifies when collective intelligence surpasses independent operation.

## Results  
The framework demonstrates theoretical consistency in belief transmission across domains and shows that coordinated learning can increase network‑level performance beyond the sum of individual agents. Simulations illustrate real‑time inference capabilities under uncertainty, though full experimental validation is pending.

## Significance  
This research advances AI deployment in physical systems by providing a principled method for active, self‑organizing intelligence over networks, potentially enabling robust autonomous robots and vehicles that adapt to unseen scenarios without retraining.

## Related Concepts  
Holonic digital twins, causal Markov blankets, category theory, integrated information theory, active inference, expected free energy minimization, hierarchical reasoning, wireless edge orchestration.
