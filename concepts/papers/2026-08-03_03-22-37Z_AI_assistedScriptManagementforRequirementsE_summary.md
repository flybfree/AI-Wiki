## Summary  
The paper proposes an AI‑assisted workflow for requirements elicitation interviews that integrates theory‑grounded script generation, real‑time topic coverage tracking, and on‑demand follow‑up question generation. It aims to understand how such integrated support changes interview dynamics and the quality of elicited requirements compared with a training‑only approach. The study evaluates two conditions: AI‑assisted without training versus training without AI assistance. Results show higher script scores, fewer topics covered, more scripted questions, more follow‑ups per topic, and refined goal models under the AI‑assisted condition.  

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] AI‑generated scripts outperform trained scripts on a best‑practice rubric (92.8 vs 74.8/100).  
- [Finding 2] The AI‑assisted group covers fewer topics (9.6 vs 14.5) and asks more follow‑ups per topic (3.43 vs 1.15), indicating better focus.  
- [Finding 3] Participants rate topic tracking as the most useful feature, with 86% agreement.  

## Methodology  
The authors designed a between‑subjects quasi‑experimental study with two conditions: AI‑assisted no‑training and training only no‑AI. Interviewers performed elicitation interviews on the same set of stakeholders; one group received AI script generation and live tracking support, the other received training but used manual scripts. A rubric based on elicitation best practices was used to score scripts.  

## Results  
Scores: 92.8 vs 74.8. Topic coverage: 9.6 vs 14.5 topics. Scripted questions: 86% vs 69%. Follow‑ups per topic: 3.43 vs 1.15. Goal model refinement: lowest‑level goal fraction 0.653 vs 0.598.  

## Significance  
These findings demonstrate that AI‑assisted script management acts as a scaffold, improving interview efficiency and eliciting higher‑quality requirements, suggesting future research on adaptive AI support in elicitation.
