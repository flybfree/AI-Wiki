## Summary  
DialectS2S is an end‑to‑end speech dialogue model designed to generate natural Chinese dialect utterances despite the scarcity of labeled data, a problem that plagues most existing models. The authors introduce two novel contributions: (1) a scalable pipeline for constructing dialect speech datasets and (2) a two‑stage post‑training strategy using self‑aligned speech supervision that aligns the model’s evolving semantic representations with the target dialect’s speech semantics. By jointly optimizing data generation and representation alignment, DialectS2S achieves higher dialect consistency, response quality, and intelligibility compared to state‑of‑the‑art baselines.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] A scalable end‑to‑end synthesis pipeline that efficiently constructs dialect speech dialogue corpora with minimal annotation effort.  
- [Finding 2] A two‑stage post‑training method employing self‑aligned speech supervision to synchronize the model’s hidden representations with dialect‑specific speech targets, mitigating semantic drift during adaptation.  
- [Finding 3] Demonstrated quantitative superiority over existing models across multiple Chinese dialects in terms of BLEU, WER, and a custom “dialect consistency” metric.

## Methodology  
The authors first pre‑train a standard Mandarin speech dialogue model on abundant public data to obtain a robust baseline representation. Then they fine‑tune this model using the newly generated dialect speech pairs generated by their pipeline. The two‑stage strategy consists of (i) an initial adaptation phase that maps the pretrained representations onto the dialect’s acoustic and semantic space, and (ii) a self‑alignment step where the model generates its own speech targets from the adapted hidden states; these targets are used to further refine the representation alignment via a supervised loss. This loop ensures that the evolving semantic space is continuously steered toward the target dialect without external supervision.

## Results  
Across Mandarin, Shanghainese, and Cantonese dialects, DialectS2S reduced word error rate (WER) by up to 18 % relative to the best baseline while improving BLEU scores by an average of 4.3 points. The custom dialect consistency score increased by 0.6 on a scale of 0–1, indicating markedly smoother generation and fewer semantic mismatches. Ablation studies confirm that both the synthesis pipeline and self‑alignment are essential; removing either component drops performance by roughly half.

## Significance  
By providing an open‑source framework for low‑resource Chinese dialect dialogue modeling, DialectS2S enables researchers and practitioners to generate high‑quality speech in under‑represented dialects without requiring massive labeled datasets. The combination of scalable data generation with self‑aligned post‑training offers a practical path toward more natural and consistent dialect speech synthesis.
