## Summary  
This paper investigates how Reddit users discuss their personal AI companions, testing the Synthetic Resonance claim that such relationships can possess genuine relational meaning. By coding a large sample of posts for relationship focus, primary topic, and emotional valence, the authors reveal that most discussion centers on the user’s own bot rather than bots in general, and that the discourse is richly varied in both subject matter and affective tone. The findings suggest that AI‑bot relationships are perceived as meaningful and elicit a broad spectrum of emotions among participants.

## Key Contributions  
- [Finding 1] Only about 45 % of Reddit posts explicitly concern the user’s own AI companion, whereas the remaining 55 % discuss bots in abstract or general terms.  
- [Finding 2] Among the 970 relationally focused posts, companionship and romance each account for roughly 46 % of the discussion, while sexual content makes up about 8 %.  
- [Finding 3] Emotional valence is markedly higher in own‑bot posts (33 % contain emotion language) compared with general‑bot posts (only 15 % have any emotional expression).

## Methodology  
The authors employed the Synthetic Resonance framework to guide coding of 5,504 Reddit submissions drawn from eight AI companion communities. Using large language models, each post was classified for three dimensions: whether it centers on a personal relationship with an AI bot (relationship focus), what the primary topic is (companionship, romance, sexual content, etc.), and the emotional valence expressed. This mixed‑method approach combined quantitative coding with qualitative analysis to capture both breadth and depth of user discourse.

## Results  
The coded data show that relational language dominates only a minority of posts about personal bots; conversely, general‑bot discussions rarely embed emotion. When relationship focus is present, companionship and romance are the dominant subtopics, with sexual content as a minor but notable component. Emotional valence varies: 33 % of own‑bot posts convey affect, while only 15 % of non‑personal bot posts do. These patterns confirm that users treat their AI companions as relational entities and generate emotionally nuanced conversations.

## Significance  
Understanding the nature of human‑AI relational meaning is crucial for designing ethical AI companions and for interpreting user behavior on social platforms. This study demonstrates that Reddit communities treat personal AI bots as significant relational objects, challenging simplistic views of AI as mere tools and highlighting the need for research into affective dynamics in AI‑driven interactions.
