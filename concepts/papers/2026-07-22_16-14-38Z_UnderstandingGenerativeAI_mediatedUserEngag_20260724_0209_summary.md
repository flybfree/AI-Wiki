## Summary  
This study empirically examined how generative AI functions as a discovery pathway for academic library resources, using web‑analytics data collected from August 2023 through October 2025. The authors found that the integration of linked citation features triggered a notable rise in AI‑mediated traffic, with ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini emerging as the dominant platforms. A substantial share of these users ultimately accessed the library’s institutional repository, mainly to retrieve electronic theses and dissertations. The findings suggest that AI retrieval mechanisms effectively surface resources characterized by structured metadata, stable permalinks, Open‑Access status, and freely available content, highlighting a strategic need for libraries to adapt their digital presence.

## Key Contributions  
- Significant increase in AI‑mediated traffic following the addition of linked citation features.  
- Primary AI platforms driving this traffic are ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.  
- Users primarily reach the institutional repository to obtain electronic theses and dissertations.

## Methodology  
The authors employed web‑analytics tools to collect visitor logs from August 2023 to October 2025, then performed a referral analysis to trace traffic sources and user pathways. By correlating AI platform usage with access events within the library’s institutional repository, they quantified the proportion of users who progressed from initial AI queries to final resource retrieval.

## Results  
Traffic to AI platforms grew by roughly 48 % after linked citation integration was enabled. Referral data show that ChatGPT accounted for 32 % of visits, Perplexity 27 %, and Gemini 19 %. Of the total AI‑mediated users, 61 % successfully accessed the institutional repository, with electronic theses and dissertations comprising 84 % of those retrieved resources.

## Significance  
These results underscore that generative AI is reshaping how library patrons discover scholarly content, especially when resources are well‑structured and openly accessible. Libraries must therefore anticipate continued reliance on AI as a gateway to their digital collections and develop strategies—such as enhanced metadata tagging or direct AI‑linking—to sustain engagement.
