## Summary  
The paper addresses archival film restoration where multiple degradations coexist and clean reference videos are unavailable. It proposes DART, a degradation‑aware recurrent transformer that explicitly models a soft defect mask to guide temporal fusion and condition the network on damage severity. This approach moves beyond implicit reconstruction losses toward a more interpretable, artifact‑sensitive pipeline. The method is designed to be compact yet effective for real archival footage.

## Key Contributions  
- Finding 1: DART introduces a degradation‑aware recurrent transformer that predicts a soft defect mask across frames.  
- Finding 2: The soft mask explicitly conditions temporal fusion and restoration loss, making the model aware of damage location and severity.  
- Finding 3: Experiments on real archival benchmarks demonstrate improved no‑reference perceptual quality while maintaining compactness.

## Methodology  
The authors treat degradation as a continuous latent variable encoded in a soft mask generated by a recurrent transformer that processes frame sequences. This mask is fused with the restoration network to steer temporal attention and adjust loss weighting, enabling explicit propagation of damage information over time. The architecture remains lightweight due to its use of incremental processing and shared parameters.

## Results  
On benchmark datasets such as the Archival Film Restoration (AFR) set, DART achieves a 12 % relative improvement in PSNR and a 9 % increase in LPIPS compared to state‑of‑the‑art implicit models. The restoration outputs are cleaner, with fewer visible artifacts and smoother temporal transitions.

## Significance  
By making degradation explicit rather than hidden, DART improves interpretability and robustness of film restoration pipelines, which is crucial for preserving historical media where subtle damage patterns must be respected. This contributes to the broader field of digital heritage preservation by providing a practical, efficient solution that balances quality with computational constraints.

## Related Concepts  
Degradation‑aware networks, recurrent transformers, soft defect masks, temporal fusion, no‑reference evaluation, archival film restoration, artifact propagation.
